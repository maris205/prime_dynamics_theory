\begin{abstract}
TPC-51 identifies the correct representation space for the inherited
Mellin/residue synthesis: the source space must be quotiented by the
kernel of the physical synthesis map.  TPC-112 then separates the
determinant--zero compatibility cone from the strict physical loss
ledger.  The missing interface is an invariant measurement of the
declared quotient conditioning itself and a proof of whether the
actual H9 operator chain pays that conditioning.

For one finite literal packet let
\[
 \Syn_X:\C^{\Theta_X}\longrightarrow\mathcal H_X,\qquad
 \Syn_Xc=\sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}c_\theta A_{X,\theta},
\]
where every column retains its physical coefficient, support and
provenance, and let
\(\mathcal K_X=(\ker\Syn_X)^\perp\).  For the declared literal source
norm, we prove that the exact quotient condition number is
\[
 \boxed{\;
 \kappa_{\rm q}(X)
 =
 \frac{\sigma_{\max}(\Syn_X)}
      {\sigma_{\min}^{+}(\Syn_X)}
 =
 \sqrt{\frac{\lambda_{\max}^{+}(G_X)}
              {\lambda_{\min}^{+}(G_X)}} ,
 \qquad G_X=\Syn_X^*\Syn_X .\;}
\]
The Moore--Penrose inverse gives the exact optimal recovery constant
on \(\operatorname{ran}\Syn_X\); quotienting removes kernel
zero-directions, while the positive spectrum still depends on the
declared literal presentation.  More precisely,
\(\kappa_{\rm q}\) is the sharp uniform envelope
\[
 \sup_{\|Q\|\le1}
 \|\Syn_XQ\Syn_X^\dagger\|=\kappa_{\rm q}
\]
for an arbitrary intervening operator \(Q\) on the quotient source.
It is not an inevitable round-trip loss:
\(\Syn_X\Syn_X^\dagger\) is the identity on the physical range and
has norm one.  The actual H9 cost is the norm of its literal
composite (possibly in a different norm), not
\(\kappa_{\rm q}\) automatically.

We also prove a sharp two-column subframe obstruction without
rescaling the physical columns.  If their norms are
\(\alpha,\beta>0\) and their normalized coherence is \(\rho\), the
two-column Gram has exact eigenvalues
\[
 \lambda_\pm=\frac{\alpha^2+\beta^2\pm
 \sqrt{(\alpha^2-\beta^2)^2+4\alpha^2\beta^2\rho^2}}2,
\]
and hence
\[
 \kappa_{{\rm q},{\rm pair}}^2=\frac{\lambda_+}{\lambda_-}
 \ge\frac{1+\rho}{1-\rho}.
\]
Equality in the lower bound occurs for equal column norms.
This obstruction transfers to the complete frame only when the pair
is a reducing block, or when a full-Gram Rayleigh certificate proves
the same costly direction.  Under that explicit inheritance
condition, \(1-\rho\le X^{-2\chi+o(1)}\) forces the uniform quotient
envelope to be at least \(X^{\chi+o(1)}\).  This alone does not stop
the strict physical endpoint: a stop additionally requires a lower
bound for the actual unavoidable H9 composite or a proof that it
aligns the extremal singular directions.  The
theorem is exact \(\Lzero\) frame algebra with a conditional
\(\Lone\) bridge.  We do not estimate the smallest
positive Gram eigenvalue for the growing TPC carrier, obtain an
\(\Ltwo\) fixed-shift saving, or claim a parity or twin-prime
breakthrough.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:}
canonical synthesis; quotient Gram; physical reassembly; condition
number; fixed shift; endpoint obstruction.

\noindent\textbf{Literal-frame convention.}
The column set below is the declared physical column set after exact
provenance aggregation.  A column may not be rescaled, duplicated or
deleted merely to improve a frame constant.  One prescribed
\(h_0\ne0\), the actual active support and the inherited global
normalization remain fixed throughout.

\section{The inherited interface}

TPC-51 constructs a canonical synthesis from residue/Mellin
provenance and proves that a lower frame bound on the full source
space is impossible whenever the source has a nontrivial kernel
\citep{WangTPC51}.  Its correct reduced space is
\[
 \mathcal K_X=(\ker\Syn_X)^\perp
 =\operatorname{ran}\Syn_X^*.
\]
The actual coefficient is a distinguished physical direction, not a
freely optimized representation.  Related TPC-49--TPC-52 results
show why an arbitrary projective presentation or a fixed-complexity
subpacket cannot replace the inherited full synthesis
\citep{WangTPC49,WangTPC52}.

TPC-112 proves that any physical cost is independent of the
determinant reserve unless an explicit map-specific intertwining is
supplied \citep{WangTPC112}.  The present paper measures one candidate
round-trip condition number.  It does not identify that number with
the forward H9 synthesis norm, import a determinant exponent, or
spend determinant reserve.

\section{Canonical quotient cost}

Let \(\Theta\) be a finite provenance set, let \(\mathcal H\) be a
finite-dimensional Hilbert space, and let
\[
 \Syn:\C^\Theta\to\mathcal H,\qquad
 \Syn c=\sum_{\theta\in\Theta}c_\theta A_\theta .
\]
Zero columns are allowed, because an inherited mask may delete a
source coordinate.  Put
\[
 G=\Syn^*\Syn,\qquad
 \mathcal K=(\ker\Syn)^\perp.
\]

\begin{definition}[Declared quotient and recovery constants]
If \(\Syn=0\), set both costs to zero.  Otherwise define
\[
 C_{\rm rec}(\Syn)=\|\Syn^\dagger\|
 =\frac1{\sigma_{\min}^{+}(\Syn)}
\]
and
\[
 \kappa_{\rm q}(\Syn)
 =\|\Syn\|\,\|\Syn^\dagger\|
 =\frac{\sigma_{\max}(\Syn)}
        {\sigma_{\min}^{+}(\Syn)}.
\]
Here \(^{\dagger}\) is the Moore--Penrose inverse and \(+\) means the
smallest positive singular or spectral value.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact quotient-frame reassembly]
\label{thm:quotient}
For every \(c\in\mathcal K\),
\[
 \sigma_{\min}^{+}(\Syn)\|c\|_2
 \le\|\Syn c\|_{\mathcal H}
 \le\sigma_{\max}(\Syn)\|c\|_2.
\]
For every \(y\in\operatorname{ran}\Syn\), the unique least-norm
coefficient is \(c_y=\Syn^\dagger y\), and
\[
 \|c_y\|_2\le C_{\rm rec}(\Syn)\|y\|_{\mathcal H}.
\]
Both constants are optimal.  If \(\Syn\ne0\), moreover
\[
 \boxed{\;
 \kappa_{\rm q}(\Syn)^2
 =
 \frac{\lambda_{\max}^{+}(G)}
      {\lambda_{\min}^{+}(G)} .\;}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Restrict \(\Syn\) to \(\mathcal K\).  It is injective and has the same
nonzero singular values as \(\Syn\).  The singular-value
decomposition gives the two-sided inequality and the least-norm
inverse.  Singular vectors at the extremal singular values show
optimality.  The positive eigenvalues of \(G=\Syn^*\Syn\) are the
squares of the nonzero singular values.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the quotient is compulsory]
The raw Gram always has a zero eigenvalue when exact redundant
coordinates are retained.  Declaring its ordinary condition number
infinite confuses a representation kernel with a physical
obstruction.  Conversely, silently deleting a nearly dependent but
nonredundant column can erase real conditioning.  The positive
spectrum is canonical only relative to the declared literal source
metric.  It is invariant under unitary source changes, but not under
arbitrary redundant expansions or nonunitary reparameterizations.
For example, \(I_2\) has quotient condition number \(1\), whereas
the same physical range presented by columns
\([e_1,e_1,e_2]\) has positive singular values
\(\sqrt2,1\) and quotient condition number \(\sqrt2\).
\end{remark}

\section{Uniform conjugation envelope and the actual H9 norm}

\begin{proposition}[Sharp uniform conjugation envelope]
\label{prop:square}
Assume \(\Syn\ne0\).  For operators
\(Q:\mathcal K\to\mathcal K\),
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \sup_{\|Q\|\le1}
 \|\Syn Q\Syn^\dagger\|_{\operatorname{ran}\Syn\to
                         \operatorname{ran}\Syn}
 =\kappa_{\rm q}(\Syn).
 \quad}
\]
Consequently
\(\|\Syn Q\Syn^\dagger\|
\le\kappa_{\rm q}\|Q\|\), and the corresponding uniform squared-norm
envelope is \(\kappa_{\rm q}^2\|Q\|^2\).  However,
\[
 \Syn I_{\mathcal K}\Syn^\dagger
 =I_{\operatorname{ran}\Syn},
\]
so the identity intermediate operator has amplitude cost one, not
\(\kappa_{\rm q}\).  If
\[
 \kappa_{\rm q}(X)=X^{\chi+o(1)},
\]
then \(\chi\) (or \(2\chi\) for squared norms) is a safe uniform
envelope only for a matching composite; it is not an automatic H9
token.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Submultiplicativity gives the upper bound.  Let
\(v_{\min},v_{\max}\in\mathcal K\) be right singular vectors for
\(\sigma_{\min}^+\) and \(\sigma_{\max}\), and take the norm-one
rank-one operator \(Qv_{\min}=v_{\max}\), zero on
\(v_{\min}^\perp\).  Applied to the corresponding left singular
vector, \(\Syn Q\Syn^\dagger\) has norm
\(\sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}^+=\kappa_{\rm q}\), proving sharpness.
The identity formula is the Moore--Penrose range-projection identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Actual direction versus uniform frame]
TPC-51 also defines an actual-direction ratio.  A good bound for that
one direction can be sufficient if every subsequent operation
preserves it exactly.  A theorem applied uniformly to a family must
instead use the reduced Gram on the declared family.  This paper does
not replace one quantifier by the other.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Not automatically the H9 norm]
The MVP1 endpoint controls a literal map from packet amplitudes to a
scalar, naturally with a max-packet input norm.  Depending on the
proved factorization, that map may pay only \(\|\Syn\|\), only
\(\|\Syn^\dagger\|\), the actual composite
\(\|\Syn Q\Syn^\dagger\|\), or a different
\(\ell^\infty\)-to-scalar norm.  The uniform bound
\(\kappa_{\rm q}\|Q\|\) can certify an upper cost, but a large
\(\kappa_{\rm q}\) does not certify a lower cost.  No entry may be
placed in the H9 ledger until that operator chain and its norm
conversions are displayed.
\end{remark}

\section{A sharp physical coherence obstruction}

For each nonzero physical column write
\[
 \alpha_\theta=\|A_\theta\|,\qquad
 u_\theta=\frac{A_\theta}{\alpha_\theta}.
\]
The directions are used only to define coherence; the synthesis
itself retains the literal column norms.

\begin{theorem}[Exact two-column obstruction]
\label{thm:coherence}
Let two declared nonzero physical columns have norms
\(\alpha,\beta>0\) and normalized coherence
\[
 \rho=|\langle u_\theta,u_{\theta'}\rangle|<1.
\]
Let \(\Syn_{\rm pair}:\C^2\to\mathcal H\) be the synthesis consisting
of precisely these two literal columns.  Put
\[
 \Delta=\sqrt{(\alpha^2-\beta^2)^2
                 +4\alpha^2\beta^2\rho^2}.
\]
Then
\[
 \lambda_\pm(\Syn_{\rm pair}^*\Syn_{\rm pair})
 =\frac{\alpha^2+\beta^2\pm\Delta}{2}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \kappa_{\rm q}(\Syn_{\rm pair})^2
 =\frac{\alpha^2+\beta^2+\Delta}
        {\alpha^2+\beta^2-\Delta}
 \ge\frac{1+\rho}{1-\rho}.
 \quad}
\]
The inequality is an equality if and only if \(\alpha=\beta\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
After multiplying one column by a unit phase, the principal
two-column literal Gram is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
  \alpha^2&\alpha\beta\rho\\
  \alpha\beta\rho&\beta^2
 \end{pmatrix},
\]
whose eigenvalues are the displayed \(\lambda_\pm\).  Moreover
\[
 \Delta^2-\rho^2(\alpha^2+\beta^2)^2
 =(1-\rho^2)(\alpha^2-\beta^2)^2\ge0.
\]
Thus \(\Delta/(\alpha^2+\beta^2)\ge\rho\), which gives the ratio
bound after applying the increasing function
\(t\mapsto(1+t)/(1-t)\).  Equality holds exactly when
\(\alpha=\beta\).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Polynomial near-collinearity envelope]
Suppose an actual pair in the declared physical family
satisfies
\[
 1-\rho_X\le X^{-2\chi+o(1)},
\]
and suppose either:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2em]
\item its two-dimensional source coordinate space is a reducing block
  of the full Gram; or
\item an independent full-Gram certificate proves
  \(\kappa_{\rm q}(\Syn_X)\ge
    \kappa_{\rm q}(\Syn_{{\rm pair},X})\).
\end{enumerate}
then
\[
 \kappa_{\rm q}(X)\ge X^{\chi+o(1)}.
\]
This quotient lower bound alone is not a route stop.  If a separate
literal certificate proves that the actual unavoidable H9 composite
has amplitude norm at least \(X^{\chi+o(1)}\), for example by forced
alignment of the relevant singular directions, then
\(\chi\ge1/400\) is incompatible with the strict physical endpoint
for that route.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By \cref{thm:coherence}, the pair quotient condition number is at least
\((1-\rho_X)^{-1/2}\).  Either inheritance hypothesis transfers this
condition number to the complete frame.  The extra actual-composite
lower bound then invokes the strict endpoint rule of TPC-MVP1
\citep{WangMVP1}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[A lower bound must be literal]
An abstract pair of near-collinear vectors is only an \(\Lzero\)
countermodel.  Adding further columns does not generically preserve
the condition number of a principal two-column Gram block: the pair
direction can mix with the kernel or the rest of the positive
spectrum.  The exact two-column formula above retains the inherited
column norms, but a physical full-frame stop certificate must
additionally prove one of the inheritance hypotheses.  No such
growing full-frame certificate is produced here.
\end{remark}

\section{Finite exact regression certificate}

The script
\texttt{experiments/tpc113\_frame\_certificate.py} uses exact rational
arithmetic to check:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Gram and Moore--Penrose identities for several rectangular
  full-row-rank synthesis matrices;
\item the quotient projection and its idempotence;
\item the nonzero singular-value ledger through \(SS^*\);
\item the exact equal-norm two-column eigenvalues \(1\pm\rho\) for
  rational Pythagorean pairs;
\item the sharp condition-number ratio
  \((1+\rho)/(1-\rho)\); and
\item the identity-intermediate cost one and a swap intermediate that
  attains the sharp uniform conjugation envelope.
\end{enumerate}
The committed JSON is a finite algebra certificate.  It is not
numerical evidence for a lower frame bound on the growing TPC
coefficient.

\paragraph{Established.}
\Cref{thm:quotient,prop:square,thm:coherence} give an exact declared
quotient condition number, its sharp uniform conjugation envelope and
a sharp coherence obstruction.  The finite algebra is \(\Lzero\);
attachment to H9 is only a conditional \(\Lone\) bridge requiring the
actual operator factorization and norm conversion.

\paragraph{Not established.}
No asymptotic lower bound for the smallest positive physical Gram
eigenvalue is proved.  No actual polynomial obstruction is exhibited.
No equality between \(\kappa_{\rm q}\) and the H9 forward synthesis
or actual composite norm is asserted, and no lower bound for the
actual H9 composite follows from a large quotient condition number.
No residual grouping, native-Gram comparison, fixed-\(h_0\)
localization, high-frequency return or full-block reassembly is
closed.  There is no \(\Ltwo\) fixed-power arithmetic saving, parity
breakthrough, Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound or
twin-prime theorem.  Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).
