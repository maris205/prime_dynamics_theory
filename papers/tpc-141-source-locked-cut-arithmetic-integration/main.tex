\begin{abstract}
TPC-133--140 pursue two branches left open by the MVP3 route audit.
The first makes the native entrance executable and follows every
atom through a boundary-complete dyadic split to the first carrier
not supported by the TPC-17/18 block compiler.  The second imports
proved fixed-data logarithmic Chowla information and a newly proved
small-polylog affine estimate outside a small logarithmic-density
exceptional set, isolates a quantitative shift-one non-transfer, and
formulates the remaining actual-family and selector estimates needed
by the literal determinant-two packet.  We integrate these branches
without promoting a restricted arithmetic shadow to the physical
carrier.

The archive branch gives the exact cut decomposition
\[
 B_{h_0,\delta}(X)
 =
 S_{\rm soft}(X)+S_{\rm elig}(X)+S_{\rm front}(X).
\]
The first term is returned as \(o(X)\), the eligible tail is sent to
the arithmetic corridor, and the frontier remains explicit.  Thus a
future eligible-tail saving yields only
\[
 |B_{h_0,\delta}(X)|
 \le o(X)+|S_{\rm front}(X)|
 +X^{1-\sigma_{\rm elig}+\Lambda_{\rm elig}+o(1)}.
\]
It cannot close the original packet until the frontier is totalized
or separately bounded.

We give a content-addressed manifest, a typed import/export calculus,
an exact terminal and tail cover, and an occurrence-level endpoint
registry.  The MVP1 physical threshold remains the strict amplitude
inequality \(\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400\).  Unknown costs are not set to
zero, determinant reserve is not endpoint slack, and a conservative
upper bound at or above the threshold is not a route obstruction
without a lower certificate.  In the frozen snapshot the first
missing record is \(\mathsf{H1.frontier\_totalization}\), the physical
registry is incomplete, and no positive \(\Ltwo\) estimate is
present.  This is an \(\Lzero/\Lone\) integration theorem, not a
prime-pair theorem.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} fixed shift; source-locked manifest;
native archive; unsupported carrier; arithmetic shadow; endpoint
certificate.

\section{The inherited target and the two new branches}

Fix a prescribed nonzero even \(h_0\), a fixed rational
\(0<\delta<1/2\), and a fixed compactly supported smooth weight
\(W\).  The imported TPC-15 identity writes the weighted fixed-shift
defect as
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_{h_0,W}(X)
 =
 \Bhard(X)+O_A\!\left(X(\log X)^{-A}\right)
\label{eq:anchor}
\end{equation}
for every fixed \(A>0\) \citep{WangTPC15}.  The task is therefore to
bound the literal \(\Bhard\), not a support-only or averaged-shift
surrogate.

TPC-MVP3 reported the requirement for a complete actual-carrier
native archive as the first missing object \citep{WangTPC132}.
TPC-133--136 now move that pointer forward, but do not yet satisfy the
complete-carrier requirement.  TPC-133 gives a canonical tuple
generator for the opened atoms; TPC-134 gives an exact dyadic
prefix--tail path archive; TPC-135 partitions the archive into the
domain eligible for the TPC-17/18 compiler and a geometric frontier;
and TPC-136 reconnects the resulting three cut-terminal classes
exactly
\citep{WangTPC133,WangTPC134,WangTPC135,WangTPC136}.

In parallel, TPC-137--140 test whether proved two-point Chowla
information can feed the determinant-two arithmetic packet
\citep{WangTPC137,WangTPC138,WangTPC139,WangTPC140}.  The formal
fixed-affine logarithmic baseline is supplied by Tao's two-point
theorem \citep{Tao2016}.  Quantitative shift-one results of
Helfgott--Radziwi{\l}{\l} and Pilatte are stronger in their stated
scope \citep{HelfgottRadziwill2021,Pilatte2025}, but a shift-one
bound is not imported as an estimate for the active odd
determinant-two progression.  TPC-138/139 additionally record the
Tao--Ter\"av\"ainen small-polylog affine theorem at almost all scales.
That theorem is a genuine positive arithmetic shadow, but its
exceptional-scale and reduced-component scope is retained exactly.

\section{Source-locked records}

\begin{definition}[Literal scope]
A scope record is
\[
 {\mathfrak s}
 =
 (h_0,\delta,W_{\rm env},{\mathfrak q},\Omega,\nu,{\mathfrak a}),
\]
where \(W_{\rm env}\) is the effective support envelope,
\({\mathfrak q}\) records all growth, scale, prefix, modulus and
weight quantifiers, \(\Omega\) is the carrier, \(\nu\) is the single
normalization, and \({\mathfrak a}\) is the archive identity.  A
minimal nonzero support is not inferred from an effective envelope.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Export and import]
An export record contains a statement hash, source bundle hash,
status, evidence level, direction, coverage, scope, carrier and
normalization.  A proof import is callable only when those object
fields agree exactly or a separately sourced crosswalk theorem is
listed.  A shadow-only import may deliberately have a different
scope, but it has no proof edge to the actual target.
\end{definition}

Tuple hashes are integrity checks only.  The native tuple itself is
the key, and injectivity is checked on the tuples rather than inferred
from collision resistance.

\begin{theorem}[Source-locked non-escalating merge]
\label{thm:merge}
Let a finite collection of paper exports and imports pass the
following checks:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item every proof import has one exact export or a theorem-backed
  crosswalk;
\item the logical dependency graph is acyclic and every primitive
  node has an independent source;
\item no primitive source assumes the hard-packet target or an
  equivalent target gate;
\item terminal leaves, tail occurrences and physical occurrences
  have exact nonoverlapping covers; and
\item the declared status and evidence level of every merged node are
  no stronger than those of its source.
\end{enumerate}
Then the merged root implication is a valid consequence of the
source theorems in their recorded scopes.  The merge cannot turn a
conditional interface, logarithmic shadow, finite regression or
scope-mismatched theorem into a proved growing physical estimate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Exact import matching makes every logical edge a typed invocation.
Induction along the topological order proves each nonprimitive node
from its already justified parents.  The primitive-source firewall
excludes target-as-premise circularity.  Exact covers prevent omitted
or duplicated terms, while source-level monotonicity prevents an
administrative merge from strengthening a theorem.  A shadow-only
edge is excluded from the logical graph and hence cannot justify the
actual target.
\end{proof}

\section{The complete cut and the frontier}

Let \(\mathcal A_X\) be the support-envelope atom archive generated at
the native entrance.  The TPC-134 dyadic partition and deterministic
prefix cut assign every atom to exactly one terminal type:
\[
\mathsf{eligible\mbox{-}prefix},\qquad
\mathsf{eligible\mbox{-}tail},\qquad
\mathsf{frontier}.
\]
Column conservation through the cut gives the exact identity
\begin{equation}
 \Bhard(X)
 =
 S_{\rm soft}(X)+S_{\rm elig}(X)+S_{\rm front}(X).
\label{eq:cut}
\end{equation}
The imported TPC-17 theorem, attached by TPC-136 to the complete
eligible-prefix outer sum, gives
\begin{equation}
 S_{\rm soft}(X)=o(X).
\label{eq:soft}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Eligible-only compiler obstruction]
\label{prop:frontier}
The TPC-135 low-\(L\) witness is a formal support-envelope native
column in the frontier for all sufficiently large \(X\), and its
dyadic path-matrix column has sum one.  Its full arithmetic
coefficient may nevertheless vanish.  Consequently the eligible-only
coefficientwise compiler is not a complete cover of \(\mathcal A_X\).
\end{proposition}

This is a scoped \(\Lone\) negative result.  It refutes neither the
scalar estimate \(\Bhard=o(X)\) nor a frontier-augmented signed route.
In particular, \(S_{\rm front}\) cannot be called soft merely because
the eligible-prefix sum satisfies \eqref{eq:soft}.

\begin{definition}[Frontier totalization]
The node \(\mathsf{H1.frontier\_totalization}\) is satisfied if every
frontier terminal has complete \(Q_D,Q_Z,G\), fixed-\(h_0\), cover,
reconnection and physical-occurrence records, or if one separate
theorem bounds the complete original-scale frontier sum by \(o(X)\).
\end{definition}

\section{What the arithmetic shadow proves}

TPC-137 combines the formal fixed-affine logarithmic theorem with a
fixed periodic/squarefree closure.  Its quantifier order freezes the
affine forms, periodic modulus and auxiliary cutoff before the
asymptotic limit.  This is an unconditional arithmetic theorem in a
restricted scope, but it is not the growing physical H3 family.

TPC-138 records quantitative shift-one information and proves a
determinant/parity non-transfer for the active odd determinant-two
carrier.  Thus no direct edge
\[
 \text{quantitative shift one}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{actual determinant-two H3}
\]
appears in the proof DAG.  TPC-139 then names the missing uniform
envelope in coefficient height, CRT cutoff, modulus and period.  It
also records a proved power-of-log estimate for positive nonparallel
affine coefficients and constants of sufficiently small
polylogarithmic height, outside a small logarithmic-density
exceptional set.  This restricted result has no automatic edge to the
actual carrier: containment of every reduced CRT component, complete
squarefree/periodic reassembly, and exceptional-set control remain
unproved.  TPC-140 separately proves that an almost-scale theorem does
not control a prescribed sequence of actual prefixes, formulates the
remaining pointwise-or-selector gate, and gives a conditional
raw-saving ledger.  It also records the exact global-to-window
firewall
\[
 m_{x,\omega}(\mathcal E)
 \ll
 \min\left\{1,\frac{(\log x)^{1-c_0}}{\log\omega}\right\}.
\]
Thus a cumulative exceptional-set estimate cannot simply be copied
as the same saving on every actual terminal window.

\begin{proposition}[No shadow teleportation]
\label{prop:shadow}
A fixed-data logarithmic estimate, a quantitative shift-one estimate,
or a small-polylog affine estimate outside a small
logarithmic-density exceptional set does not supply the actual H3
packet estimate without, respectively, a growing-family uniformity
theorem, a determinant-two transfer, and proved actual-component
containment plus squarefree/periodic reassembly and an independent
local exceptional-window estimate together with a selector or
all-prefix theorem.
\end{proposition}

The power-ledger value of both the qualitative logarithmic shadow and
the new power-of-log almost-scale shadow is zero on the fixed
\(X\)-power scale.  This means only that neither supplies a fixed
power of \(X\); it is not a lower bound showing that the actual family
cannot have a positive power saving.

\section{Cut-aware synthesis}

\begin{theorem}[Cut-aware eligible-corridor bound]
\label{thm:cut-aware}
Assume that the eligible nonsoft packets admit an amplitude estimate
\[
 \max_{b\in\mathcal B_X^{\rm elig}}|\mathcal P_b|
 \le X^{1-\sigma_{\rm elig}+o(1)}
\]
and that their theorem-backed physical synthesis satisfies
\[
 \left|\mathscr S_X^{\rm elig}(\{\mathcal P_b\})\right|
 \le
 X^{\Lambda_{\rm elig}+o(1)}
 \max_b|\mathcal P_b|.
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 |\Bhard(X)|
 \le
 o(X)+|S_{\rm front}(X)|
 +X^{1-\sigma_{\rm elig}+\Lambda_{\rm elig}+o(1)}.}
\]
In particular, \(\sigma_{\rm elig}>\Lambda_{\rm elig}\) closes the
eligible corridor but does not imply \(\Bhard=o(X)\) unless
\(S_{\rm front}=o(X)\) or the frontier is included in a complete
joint packet theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Insert \eqref{eq:soft} into \eqref{eq:cut}; apply the triangle
inequality and then the two assumed eligible-corridor bounds.  The
frontier is an independent summand and remains in the conclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Relation to TPC-131]
TPC-131 proves the complete-carrier formula
\[
 |B_{h_0,\delta}(X)|
 \le o(X)+X^{1-\sigma_{\rm raw}+\Lambda_{\rm phys}+o(1)}
\]
under a complete archive and registry \citep{WangTPC131}.
\Cref{thm:cut-aware} is its deliberately incomplete cut-level
specialization: it displays the term that prevents invoking that
complete theorem.
\end{remark}

\section{The nonduplicated \texorpdfstring{\(1/400\)}{1/400} ledger}

The fixed MVP1 physical contract is
\[
 \sigma_{\rm target}=\frac1{400}
 \quad\text{on the amplitude scale},\qquad
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}<\frac1{400}.
\]
Every physical occurrence is identified by path and stage.  An
energy exponent \(e\) enters the amplitude ledger as \(e/2\).
A joint theorem replaces a disjoint exact cover of primitive
occurrences; it is not retained together with its dependencies.

Four residual namespaces remain distinct:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf{H3.squarefree\_tail},&
\mathsf{H5.content\_remainder},\\
\mathsf{Phys.high\_ultra\_boundary},&
\mathsf{Archive.frontier\_totalization}.
\end{array}
\]
The selector cost, if any, must be assigned once to either the raw
arithmetic crosswalk or the physical synthesis map according to its
actual domain and codomain.

\begin{proposition}[Endpoint certificate directions]
\label{prop:endpoint}
For a fully specified route cell:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2em]
\item a theorem-backed complete upper certificate
  \(U<1/400\) is a strict physical pass;
\item a theorem-backed lower or exact dual certificate
  \(L\ge1/400\) stops that route cell, with equality included; and
\item an unknown token, an incomplete cell cover, or merely an upper
  bound \(U\ge1/400\) without a lower obstruction is not a stop
  certificate.
\end{enumerate}
The determinant--zero reserve is not a negative physical token.
\end{proposition}

The current frontier, cover, localization, final reconnection and
outer packet-census occurrences are unknown.  Consequently
\[
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}^{\rm upper}
 =
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}^{\rm lower}
 =
 \text{not certified},
\]
and the endpoint state is \textsc{incomplete}.  Assigning zero to
those occurrences would change the route.

\item four explicit scope records, including fixed physical,

The manifest identity is its canonical content, not a self-referential
repository commit.  Source hashes detect drift but do not prove a

\section{Deterministic audit and claim boundary}

The standard-library script
\texttt{experiments/tpc141\_batch\_integration\_audit.py} writes the
frozen manifest and an audit report by default.  Its
\texttt{--check} mode performs no writes and compares both artifacts
byte for byte.  Mutation regressions reject:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item a proof import with a mismatched shift, carrier or
  normalization;
\item a duplicate or omitted terminal role;
\item assigning the frontier to the proved-soft destination;
\item merging two tail namespaces;
\item duplicate, self-dependent or overlapping joint tokens;
\item filling an unknown endpoint token with zero;
\item subtracting determinant reserve from the physical loss;
\item a positive-\(\Ltwo\) label on a fixed/logarithmic/almost-scale
  export; and
\item a direct or indirect H9 physical-registry dependency on an
  arithmetic node.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Established.}
The source-locked merge theorem, exact cut-aware synthesis,
certificate language, and endpoint decision directions are
\(\Lzero/\Lone\) results.  The integrated source records also include
the restricted small-polylog arithmetic shadow and the scoped
eligible-only and shift-one non-transfer obstructions.

\paragraph{Not established.}
The paper proves no frontier bound, complete actual-carrier H1,
growing H3/H5 input, physical H6--H9 conjunction, strict endpoint,
\(\Bhard=o(X)\), Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower
bound, or twin-prime theorem.  Its first missing node is
\[
 \boxed{\mathsf{H1.frontier\_totalization}}.
\]

\section{Conclusion}

TPC-133--140 advance the route in two honest ways.  The native
archive is no longer generically absent: it reaches an exact,
reconnected cut and identifies the first unsupported carrier.
Separately, known arithmetic cancellation now occupies a precisely
typed fixed or small-polylog almost-scale shadow scope, with the
actual-family containment, reassembly and selector gaps exposed.
Neither result is promoted to the complete physical coefficient.

The remaining frontier term and unknown occurrence tokens force an
incomplete endpoint.  The correct next action is to totalize or bound
the frontier before describing the program as being at a purely
arithmetic frontier.  If that succeeds, the same manifest can be
refrozen and passed to the MVP4 decision calculus without changing
the evidence rules.
