# Roadmap after RH-366

RH-366 adds an independent Möbius-correlation theorem edge without changing
the physical route coordinate

    actual_same_clock_unnormalized_head_transport_open.

Its central separation is exact:

    fixed periodic and Parry-typical points -> Möbius orthogonality
    offline Möbius-adapted point           -> correlation 4/pi^2.

The positive result is an encoding theorem.  It does not construct a
canonical arithmetic observable, operator trace, determinant, or zeta factor.

The shortest genuine continuations are:

1. **Capacity-limit route.** Prove or disprove existence of `lim K_N/N` for
   the distance-two constraint.  Pair correlations alone do not control this
   nonlinear maximum-weight functional.
2. **Nonadaptive-measure route.** Identify geometrically or dynamically
   selected invariant measures, beyond the Parry measure, for which a
   quantitative Möbius theorem holds without reading the arithmetic sequence.
3. **General constraint-graph route.** Classify which mixing subshifts admit
   explicit arithmetic-adapted points and compute graph-dependent capacity
   brackets.
4. **Cyclic-Ulam route.** Audit the independent aligned block/sign-mode
   theorem and isolate any rigorous leakage statement.  Finite square-root-
   like slopes remain numerical observations until a continuum perturbation
   theorem exists.
5. **Return-bouquet routes.** Retain composite primitive-divisor, sharp
   radius, finite entropy-data, and intrinsic globalization problems from
   RH-365.
6. **Original physical route.** Return to same-clock unnormalised head
   transport, typed `q/E_off`, the complete direct prefix, or the RH-241
   moving noisy envelope/coefficient bridge.

Do not turn an optimizer that reads Möbius into a spontaneous coupling.  Do
not promote the finite `0.492...` plateau to a limit.  Do not identify a
Möbius-weighted orbit average with a von-Mangoldt trace.  Gates A--E remain
false/open.
