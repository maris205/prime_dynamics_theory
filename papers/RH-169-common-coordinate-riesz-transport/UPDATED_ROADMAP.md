# Roadmap after RH-169

Fixed-rank transport is now exact once common coordinates and summable
operator defects are supplied.  It cannot be applied wholesale to the
moving cloud because its degree and Riesz rank grow.

The next paper must prove the rank-change obstruction explicitly and replace

```text
Pi_j -> Pi in operator norm
```

by stabilization of every fixed spectral shell.  That distinction decides
the correct meaning of the all-level `R` interface.
