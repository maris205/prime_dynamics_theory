\begin{abstract}
TPC--324 replicated a finite signed spectral-profile pattern at new source
locations.  We next ask whether the pattern survives a controlled change of
source cardinality.  Using the same literal deleted-diagonal centered
prime-shell blocks, we freeze a new source origin and a nested four-rung
ladder with source counts $160,320,640,1280$.  Across
$Q\in\{24,36,54,80\}$ and exponents $s\in\{1,2\}$, the all-plus coherent
normalized profile majorizes the direct profile on all $32$ rows.  The
outward lower envelope of profile total variation and the outward upper
envelope of the coherent energy ratio both decrease strictly across the four
rungs.  These are finite numerical certificates and observations, not an
asymptotic theorem, an arithmetic cancellation estimate, or a twin-prime
result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and claim boundary}

Let $I_N=[12001,12000+N/2]\cap\mathbb Z$ for
$N\in\{320,640,1280,2560\}$.  Thus the four actual source counts are
$N/2=160,320,640,1280$, and the intervals are nested.  For a prime
$p\in(Q,2Q]$, define the literal block
\begin{equation}
B_{p,N}^{(s)}(u,t)=p\frac{H^{2s}}{(H^2+(u-t)^2)^s}
\left({\bf 1}_{p\mid u-t}-\frac1{p-1}\right)
{\bf 1}_{u\ne t}{\bf 1}_{p\nmid u}{\bf 1}_{p\nmid t},
\qquad H=66.
\end{equation}
The direct and signed Grams are
\begin{equation}
G_0(N)=\sum_p B_p^*B_p,\qquad
G_e(N)=\left(\sum_p e_pB_p\right)^*
                 \left(\sum_p e_pB_p\right).
\end{equation}
We use four predeclared laws: all-plus, index-alternating, the mod-$4$
character, and half-split signs.

For a positive-trace Gram $G$, let
\[
\pi(G)=\left(\frac{\lambda_1(G)}{\operatorname{tr}G},\ldots,
 \frac{\lambda_n(G)}{\operatorname{tr}G}\right),
 \qquad \lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_n.
\]
We say that the signed profile majorizes the direct profile when every
interior partial-sum difference is nonnegative and at least one is positive.
The principal claim is deliberately finite:
\begin{quote}
\textbf{NUMERICALLY CERTIFIED FINITE:} all-plus majorization on $32/32$
ladder rows, with a strict descending four-rung diagnostic envelope.
\end{quote}

\section{Exact structure and protocol}

Every Gram above is of the form $A^*A$ (or a finite sum of such matrices), so
positive semidefiniteness and positive spectral-profile typing are exact finite
facts (standard spectral facts are summarized in
\cite{horn2013matrix}).  Also, for $a>0$, $\pi(aG)=\pi(G)$ while
$\operatorname{tr}(aG)=a\operatorname{tr}(G)$.  Energy ratio and normalized
shape are therefore different observables; one cannot be substituted for the
other.

The scale intervention is source cardinality only: the origin, height, shell
anchors, exponents, kernel, and sign menu are held fixed.  The producer is
provenance-locked to the TPC--324 literal engine.  It records forward SciPy,
forward NumPy, and reverse NumPy accumulation/spectral paths.  A separate
checker rebuilds the blocks in reverse order with an independent einsum
implementation.  A stress suite verifies strict nesting, disjointness from
all prior frozen panels, a nontrivial residue-mask perturbation, the envelope
trend, and the claim firewall.

\section{Interpretation and obstruction}

The result advances the route from source-location replication to a
fixed-origin finite scale audit.  It does not establish that the four-rung
envelopes have a limit, or that a profile law is uniform for arbitrary source
intervals.  In particular, the nested intervals change the Hilbert-space
dimension, so their comparison is not an identity under unitary translation.
The imported literal engine also contains no Möbius or von Mangoldt weighting.

Accordingly the arithmetic status is
\begin{equation*}
\texttt{ARITHMETIC\_ADVANCE = NO},\qquad
\texttt{FIXED\_POWER\_CREDIT = 0},\qquad
\texttt{FULL\_GATE\_B = OPEN}.
\end{equation*}
The next minimal tests are a disjoint second scale ladder and a source-native
signed arithmetic $L^2$ estimate.  The Session-named official Route-A and
Route-B evaluator files are absent from this checkout; the local Bridge-B
record is therefore fail-closed and makes no official-pass claim.
