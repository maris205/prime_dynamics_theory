\section{Joint feasible sets and unconditional containment}

Let $A$ be finite.  For $h\in A$, fix $c_h,\lambda_h\in\C$ and $r_h\ge0$.
Let $\mathcal J$ be a nonempty set of jointly feasible tuples
$z=(z_h)_{h\in A}$ satisfying
\[
 z_h\in c_h+r_h\Disk.
\]
Define
\[
 \Cagg=\sum_{h\in A}\lambda_hc_h,
 \qquad
 \Ragg=\sum_{h\in A}|\lambda_h|r_h,
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal A_\lambda(\mathcal J)
 =\left\{\sum_{h\in A}\lambda_hz_h:z\in\mathcal J\right\}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Coupled-family enclosure]\label{prop:containment}
For every such joint family,
\[
 \mathcal A_\lambda(\mathcal J)
 \subseteq \Cagg+\Ragg\Disk.
\]
No product-realizability assumption is needed.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $z\in\mathcal J$, write $z_h=c_h+e_h$ with $|e_h|\le r_h$.
Then
\[
 \left|\sum_h\lambda_hz_h-\Cagg\right|
 \le \sum_h|\lambda_h|\,|e_h|
 \le \Ragg.
\]
\end{proof}

The nonemptiness premise matters: an unrealizable local TPC-245 branch makes
the full joint family empty, even when its scalar weight happens to vanish.
