\section{Exact weighted-disk reassembly}

\begin{theorem}[Weighted Minkowski disk identity]\label{thm:disk}
Assume blockwise product realizability,
\[
 \mathcal J=\prod_{h\in A}(c_h+r_h\Disk).
\]
Then
\[
 \mathcal A_\lambda(\mathcal J)=\Cagg+\Ragg\Disk.
\]
The identity includes an empty index set, zero weights, and zero radii.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:containment} gives one inclusion.  If $\Ragg=0$, every
nonnegative summand $|\lambda_h|r_h$ vanishes, so every weighted deviation is
zero and the aggregate is the singleton $\{\Cagg\}$.

Suppose $\Ragg>0$ and fix $d\in\C$ with $|d|\le\Ragg$.  Define
\[
 e_h=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle
 \frac{\overline{\lambda_h}}{|\lambda_h|}
 \frac{r_h}{\Ragg}\,d,&\lambda_h\ne0,\\[7pt]
 0,&\lambda_h=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then $|e_h|=r_h|d|/\Ragg\le r_h$ whenever $\lambda_h\ne0$, while
\[
 \sum_h\lambda_he_h
 =\frac d{\Ragg}\sum_h|\lambda_h|r_h=d.
\]
Thus $z_h=c_h+e_h$ is a jointly feasible product choice whose aggregate is
$\Cagg+d$.  This proves the reverse inclusion.  For $A=\varnothing$, the
empty product has one empty tuple and the empty sum gives $\{0\}$, agreeing
with the formula.
\end{proof}

The formula uses arbitrary complex weights only as abstract covariance
scalars.  A common multiplier in both Hilbert-space lanes gives the narrower
weight $|\gamma_h|^2$.  Different lane multipliers $a_h,d_h$ would instead
give $\overline{a_h}d_h$ and require separate norm budgets.
