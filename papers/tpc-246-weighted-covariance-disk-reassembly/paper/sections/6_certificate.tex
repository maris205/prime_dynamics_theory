\section{Exact finite certificate}

The machine-readable certificate uses three Gaussian-rational local disks and
complex weights of moduli $1,1,2$.  It recomputes
\[
 \Cagg=\frac{61}{65}-\frac{97}{65}i,\qquad \Ragg=2,
\]
and uses the proof's reverse formula to realize aggregate zero and a boundary
point exactly.  A second fixture has coefficient center $7$, radius $3/4$,
and window leakage $1/4$, giving the strict lower margin $6$.

The independent checker redoes the Gaussian-rational algebra without importing
the producer and rejects sixteen digest-rebound semantic mutations.  The stress
suite checks 175 forward product points, 27 reverse targets (including a
zero-radius system), and a circle-annulus control.  These are finite
reproduction checks only; the theorem is symbolic and no asymptotic claim is
inferred from the fixtures.
