\section{Common multipliers and hard-window inflation}

For each block, let TPC-245 supply a full local disk
\[
 \ip{w_h}{b_h}\in c_h+r_h\Disk,
 \qquad
 c_h=\overline{w_h^{\parallel}}b_h^{\parallel},
 \quad r_h=\sqrt{E_{B,h}E_{W,h}},
\]
where every positive-radius active transverse space has complex dimension at
least two.  If these choices have blockwise product realizability, then the
TPC-244 common multipliers $\gamma_h$ give the exact coefficient disk with
\[
 \Cagg=\sum_h|\gamma_h|^2c_h,
 \qquad
 \Ragg=\sum_h|\gamma_h|^2r_h.
\]

Assume additionally that both coefficient lanes lie in one finite
$\delta$-separated frequency space and are synthesized by the common TPC-243
map $T$ on $N$ consecutive integers.  Write
\[
 \epsilon=\frac{\delta^{-1}H_{\lfloor1/(2\delta)\rfloor}}{N},
 \qquad
 Q_I=N^{-1}\ip{TW}{TB}.
\]
With fixed local moments and energies, the common-multiplier direct sum has
fixed norm budgets
\[
 L_B^2=\sum_h|\gamma_h|^2
       (|b_h^{\parallel}|^2+E_{B,h}),\qquad
 L_W^2=\sum_h|\gamma_h|^2
       (|w_h^{\parallel}|^2+E_{W,h}).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Window-inflated margin]\label{thm:window}
Under the stated attachment, with $\Ewin=\epsilon L_WL_B$,
\[
 Q_I\in\Cagg+(\Ragg+\Ewin)\Disk
\]
and
\[
 |Q_I|\ge\max\{|\Cagg|-\Ragg-\Ewin,0\}.
\]
In particular, $|\Cagg|>\Ragg+\Ewin$ implies uniform nonvanishing.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
TPC-243 gives the correctly oriented one-transfer estimate
\[
 |Q_I-\ip{W}{B}|\le\epsilon\norm{W}\norm{B}=\Ewin.
\]
The coefficient covariance lies in $\Cagg+\Ragg\Disk$.  The triangle and
reverse-triangle inequalities prove the claims.
\end{proof}

If the norms vary over admissible realizations, $L_WL_B$ must be replaced by a
uniform supremum.  The displayed physical set is only contained in the
inflated disk: the transfer theorem does not realize every error phase.  The
strict margin is sufficient, not necessary, and only one factor of $\epsilon$
is paid for this single coefficient-to-window comparison.
