\section{Conclusion}

Local covariance disks now have an exact global calculus.  Their weighted
product image is one disk, and its center--radius gap is the complete
coefficient-space nonvanishing margin.  Hard-window synthesis spends an
additional explicit radius.  This isolates the next useful target sharply:
construct the source-native two-lane block object and prove a longitudinal
margin that dominates both transverse freedom and window leakage.  Until that
interface is supplied, the result remains a structural obstruction and
conditional bridge rather than an arithmetic advance.
