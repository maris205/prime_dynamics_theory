\begin{abstract}
TPC-93 exports the complete low-frequency reconstruction window of
the matched fixed-shift shell to primitive affine M\"obius fibers.
TPC-94 proves that every surviving literal phase has conductor
exactly \(1\) or the strong no-wrap prime \(q_X\).  The conductor-one
branch was left as a potentially full-mass obstruction.  We prove
that the entire conductor-one sector is already affordable in the
literal packet.

The harmless auxiliary prime can be chosen, still with
\(q_X\asymp Q_X\), larger than every physical row slope, every row
difference, and the orbit diameter.  A resolved key has
phase slope
\[
 \Omega_\xi=\ell_\xi v_\xi\sigma_\xi B_\xi .
\]
Exact common-target provenance gives
\[
 b_\xi\mid M_\xi-n_\xi,\qquad B_\xi\mid b_\xi.
\]
The rows are distinct and their difference has modulus below
\(q_X\), so \(B_\xi<q_X\).  The other native factors
\(\ell_\xi,v_\xi\) are smaller than \(q_X\), hence on every active
literal key
\[
 q_X\mid\Omega_\xi
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 q_X\mid\sigma_\xi.
\]
The opened-divisor relation
\[
 m_\alpha j+h_0=u\sigma_\xi
\]
then forces \(q_X\mid m_\alpha j+h_0\).  Since
\(q_X\nmid m_\alpha\) and the actual orbit interval has diameter
less than \(q_X\), each moving row admits at most one such orbit
point.

Using the literal row \(\ell^1\) bounds, the subpower divisor
envelopes of both matched prefixes, the exact-content expansion mass,
the row-gcd projector mass, and the bounded \(\ell^1\) mass of the
low-window reconstruction multiplier, we obtain
\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal L^{\rm const}_{K,R,X}|
 \ll_{K,h_0,\mathscr D}
 X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.}
\]
At the critical scales this is
\(X^{o(1)}N_{0,X}/J_X\), exactly the required amplitude scale.
The result removes the complete conductor-one sector from the list
of possible polynomial obstructions.  It does not bound the
remaining short nonconstant, resonant, or high-conductor generic
sectors and proves
no new signed M\"obius cancellation theorem or prime-pair result.
\end{abstract}
