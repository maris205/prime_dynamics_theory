\section{Claim boundary}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\subsection*{Proved here}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The auxiliary no-wrap prime may be enlarged by a fixed factor,
  still with \(q_X\asymp Q_X\), to dominate every relevant row and
  orbit diameter.
\item Exact common-target provenance gives
  \(B_\xi\le b_\xi\le|M_\xi-n_\xi|<q_X\), so the apparent
  \(q_X\mid B_\xi\) alternative is absent.
\item Every literal conductor-one key satisfies
  \(q_X\mid\sigma_\xi\), hence
  \(q_X\mid m_\alpha j+h_0\).
\item Each moving row supports at most one constant-sector orbit point.
\item After restoring both polarizations, literal row weights, masks,
  projector children, exact-content children, and low-frequency
  multiplier mass, the full conductor-one contribution is
  \(O(X^{o(1)}Q_X^2)\).
\end{itemize}

The finite divisibility statements are \(\Lzero\).  Their exact
attachment and the growing literal exceptional-sector upper bound
are \(\Lone\) progress.  The proof is absolute and uses no new
signed-correlation estimate.

\subsection*{Not proved here}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item No bound is proved for the complete short sector or the
  nonconstant resonant and generic sectors.
\item No natural post-bin mass or determinant-energy lower bound is
  proved.
\item No fixed-form two-point M\"obius theorem, parity breakthrough,
  Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime
  conclusion is proved or implied.
\item Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).  The shift remains one
  prescribed nonzero integer throughout.
\end{itemize}

Thus this paper closes one literal exceptional branch but does not
claim \(\Ltwo\) progress on the full growing fixed-shift coefficient.
