\section{Exact finite regression}
\label{sec:regression}

The script
\texttt{experiments/tpc98\_certificate.py} checks the finite
divisibility mechanism independently of asymptotic notation.  It
enumerates nonzero signed shifts, primes, primitive rows, orbit
intervals shorter than the prime, opened factorizations
\(mj+h_0=u\sigma\), content steps \(B\), and native factors
\(\ell,v\).  It verifies:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item every orbit congruence has at most one point in the interval;
\item literal common-target provenance gives
  \(B\le b\le|m-n|<q\);
\item on every active literal key,
  \(q\mid\ell v\sigma B\) is equivalent to \(q\mid\sigma\);
\item every enumerated constant key comes from the unique orbit
  congruence \(mj+h_0\equiv0\pmod q\); and
\item the absolute exact-content multiplicity is bounded by
  \(\tau(G)^2\).
\end{enumerate}

The regression is deliberately finite.  It tests exact logic,
signs of \(h_0\), both row orientations, and consequences of the
imported primitive-content provenance; it does
not test the asymptotic row \(\ell^1\) estimate, divisor bounds, or
any M\"obius cancellation theorem.
