\section{Endpoint and route ledger}
\label{sec:endpoint-ledger}

At the critical scales,
\[
 Q_X^2
 =\frac{N_{0,X}}{J_X}
 =X^{-133/400+o(1)}N_{0,X}.
\]
Consequently \cref{thm:literal-constant-bound} places the entire
conductor-one sector exactly at the target amplitude scale.  It carries
no fixed-power loss, but also supplies no additional fixed-power
reserve.

\begin{corollary}[Removal as a polynomial obstruction]
\label{cor:constant-removed}
The conductor-one sector cannot be responsible for a failure of
every strict amplitude saving below \(133/400\).  Any remaining
polynomial obstruction in the TPC-93 low window must lie in the
short sector, the nonconstant \(q_X\)-conductor resonant sector, the
generic arithmetic sector, or their outer reassembly.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
For every fixed \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
 X^{o(1)}Q_X^2
 \ll_\varepsilon
 N_{0,X}X^{-133/400+\varepsilon}.
\]
Apply \cref{thm:literal-constant-bound}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Independent ledgers}

This sector estimate is not entered twice.  In particular:
\begin{align}
 \lambda_D&\le2\eta_Z,
\label{eq:determinant-ledger}\\
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}&<\frac1{400}
\label{eq:physical-ledger}
\end{align}
remain logically separate requirements.  The first relates a future
determinant-energy lower bound to zero-mode cancellation.  The
second counts all other literal fixed-power losses.  The soft factor
in \eqref{eq:main-constant-bound} creates no new fixed exponent in
either ledger.

TPC-18 full-block reassembly and primitive generic determinant
dispersion also remain independent.  Eliminating one exceptional
low-frequency sector proves neither.

\subsection{The next arithmetic target}

The literal conductor gap now leaves a cleaner split:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{short fibers}: occupancy must be summed across native
  keys without replacing the actual weights by unit coefficients;
\item \emph{nonconstant resonant fibers}: the phase has conductor
  \(q_X\) but too little archimedean variation on its own interval;
\item \emph{generic fibers}: a weighted two-form M\"obius estimate
  and coherent outer reassembly are still required.
\end{enumerate}
The next paper should attack the resonant incidence geometry before
attempting a generic correlation theorem.
