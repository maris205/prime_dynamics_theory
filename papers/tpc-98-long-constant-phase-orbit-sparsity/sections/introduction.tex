\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

On the high-\(\beta\) endpoint row, the physical scales are
\[
 Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad
 J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad
 N_{0,X}=J_XQ_X^2.
\]
The zero-mode route isolated in TPC-85--TPC-88 asks for the
amplitude scale
\begin{equation}
 X^{o(1)}Q_X^2
 =
 X^{o(1)}\frac{N_{0,X}}{J_X}.
\label{eq:target-amplitude}
\end{equation}
TPC-93 gives an exact low-frequency export of the literal matched
shell \citep{WangTPC93}.  After exact-content resolution, one
surviving affine key \(\xi\) has two primitive forms of determinant
\(h_0\), a finite interval of affine parameters, and additive phase
\[
 e_{c_\xi q_X}\!
 \left(-r_\xi\varepsilon_\xi
       \Omega_\xi z\right),
 \qquad
 \Omega_\xi
 =\ell_\xi v_\xi\sigma_\xi B_\xi.
\]
TPC-94 proves that literal common-target provenance gives
\(c_\xi\mid\Omega_\xi\); since \(q_X\) is prime and
\(0<|r_\xi|<q_X\), the reduced conductor is exactly \(1\) or
\(q_X\) \citep{WangTPC94}.  Conductor \(1\) is equivalent to
\begin{equation}
 q_X\mid\Omega_\xi.
\label{eq:constant-condition}
\end{equation}
The reconstruction filter gives this branch its full low-frequency
multiplier mass, so Fourier decay alone supplies no discount.

The present paper uses a different feature: the no-wrap modulus is
auxiliary.  It may be enlarged by a fixed factor without changing a
single physical coefficient.  Once it dominates all rows, row
differences, and orbit diameters, exact common-target provenance
makes the progression step divide a nonzero row difference below
\(q_X\).  The remaining native factors except the complementary
divisor \(\sigma_\xi\) are also below \(q_X\).  Consequently
\eqref{eq:constant-condition} forces \(q_X\mid\sigma_\xi\), and the
original orbit target occupies one residue class modulo \(q_X\).
The actual orbit interval is shorter than \(q_X\), hence at most one
orbit point survives for each moving row.

\subsection{What is new}

The main result, \cref{thm:literal-constant-bound}, is a literal
growing-packet upper bound, not merely a finite-dimensional model.
It uses no cancellation of M\"obius signs: orbit sparsity alone
replaces the usual \(O(J_X)\) orbit count by \(O(1)\) on the entire
conductor-one branch.  The two row \(\ell^1\) bounds then give
\(X^{o(1)}Q_X^2\).

This is a one-sector closure.  In particular:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the apparent \(q_X\mid B_\xi\) alternative is proved absent on
  active literal support, rather than discarded;
\item no estimate is inferred for a nonconstant phase merely from
  its conductor \(q_X\);
\item the bound has only soft reserve at
  \eqref{eq:target-amplitude}; and
\item the determinant compatibility
  \(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\) and the strict physical budget
  \(\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400\) remain separate.
\end{itemize}
