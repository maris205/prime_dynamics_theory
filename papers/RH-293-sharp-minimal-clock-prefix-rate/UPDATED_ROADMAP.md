# Roadmap after RH-293

At the shortest bridge clock, a uniform coefficient theorem of the form

    max error = O(sigma^beta)

would be enough when beta is at least 0.9433582098747317.  Constants may not
depend uncontrollably on the moving order.  Coefficientwise O_n(sigma^2)
alone is not this theorem.

The direct contour and root-transport alternatives remain admissible.
