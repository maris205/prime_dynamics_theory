\begin{abstract}
TPC-175 extracts the largest production local occurrence-edge family
supported by explicit theorem claims in the frozen TPC-133--172
corpus.  That family is empty.  We apply the TPC-165 local-to-global
interface only to proved local edges and record two exact ledgers.
Of 2,988 source-declared production cut addresses, zero are covered,
zero duplicated, and 2,988 unmatched.  Separately, there are zero
eligible actual carriers and hence zero covered, duplicated, and
unmatched eligible carriers.  The unmatched cuts are not promoted to
actual physical carriers, and neither ledger proves physical totality.

The nonempty-local-family hypothesis of the TPC-165 production gate
is therefore unsatisfied, so its formal gluing conclusion is not
invoked.  The source-extraction cell is stopped in the exact frozen
corpus scope, while the occurrence-augmented H1 architecture remains
\(\NT\).  The result is an \(\Lone\) scoped interface obstruction,
not an actual-support theorem, canonicality theorem, fixed-phase
estimate, program-positive \(\Ltwo\), or twin-prime result.
\end{abstract}

\section{The admissible input}

Let \(\mathcal E_{175}\) be the maximal family certified by TPC-175.
Membership requires a production claim to pass the source, locator,
semantic, derivation, and witness-schema gates of TPC-173 and
TPC-174.  TPC-175 proves the scoped identity
\[
  |\mathcal E_{175}|=0                                             \tag{1}
\]
for the explicitly frozen corpus.  Equation (1) is not a
mathematical nonexistence theorem.  It says that the declared source
inventory contains no qualifying claim.

We define the eligible carrier domain by the carrier identifiers of
proved edges:
\[
  \mathcal C_{\rm elig}
  =\{c(e):e\in\mathcal E_{175}\}.                                  \tag{2}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal C_{\rm elig}=\varnothing\).  Definition (2) is
deliberately narrow.  It does not identify \(\mathcal C_{\rm elig}\)
with the 2,988 frozen cut paths, nor with an unenumerated universe of
physical carriers.

\section{The TPC-165 gate}

TPC-165 proves a finite descent theorem under supplied nonempty local
row families and exact compatible overlap bijections
\cite{WangTPC165}.  The theorem is formal and conditional on those
inputs.  It does not construct them.

\begin{proposition}[No production invocation on empty input]
The TPC-165 production gluing gate is not triggered by
\(\mathcal E_{175}\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The local nonemptiness hypothesis fails by (1).  Applying the
set-theoretic quotient operation to an empty family would yield an
empty set, but that tautology is not the conclusion of the TPC-165
production theorem and proves no coverage statement.  We therefore
record \emph{not invoked}, rather than ``glued totality with zero
rows.''
\end{proof}

This distinction prevents a familiar vacuity error:
\[
  \forall e\in\varnothing,\ P(e)
  \quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
  \exists e,\ P(e).                                                \tag{3}
\]

\section{Exact coverage ledgers}

Let \(\mathcal K_{\rm cut}\) be the 2,988 source-declared production
cut addresses imported by TPC-175.  For \(k\in\mathcal K_{\rm cut}\),
let \(n(k)\) be the number of proved local-edge rows above \(k\).  The
exact classes are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal K_{\rm cov}&=\{k:n(k)=1\},\\
 \mathcal K_{\rm dup}&=\{k:n(k)>1\},\\
 \mathcal K_{\rm miss}&=\{k:n(k)=0\}.
\end{aligned}                                                     \tag{4}
\]
TPC-175 computes
\[
 (|\mathcal K_{\rm cov}|,|\mathcal K_{\rm dup}|,
   |\mathcal K_{\rm miss}|)=(0,0,2988).                           \tag{5}
\]
These are cut addresses, not actual physical carriers.

For the separately declared eligible carrier domain (2), let
\(\mathcal C_{\rm cov},\mathcal C_{\rm dup},\mathcal C_{\rm miss}\)
be the analogous multiplicity classes.  All three are empty.

\begin{theorem}[TPC-176 coverage ledger]
The exact frozen-cut and eligible-carrier ledgers are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (|\mathcal K_{\rm cov}|,|\mathcal K_{\rm dup}|,
   |\mathcal K_{\rm miss}|)&=(0,0,2988),\\
 (|\mathcal C_{\rm cov}|,|\mathcal C_{\rm dup}|,
   |\mathcal C_{\rm miss}|)&=(0,0,0).
 \end{aligned}                                                    \tag{6}
\]
No production totality conclusion follows.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equation (1) gives \(n(k)=0\) for all 2,988 declared cuts, proving the
first line of (6).  It also makes (2) empty, proving the second line.
The final sentence follows because a cut address has not been proved
to be an actual occurrence carrier, and the physical carrier universe
has not been asserted to equal either declared domain.
\end{proof}

\section{Scoped stop versus architecture status}

The extraction-and-gluing cell has exhausted its declared input:
\[
 \texttt{STOP\_SCOPED\_EMPTY\_PROVED\_LOCAL\_EDGE\_FAMILY}.        \tag{7}
\]
Its scope is the explicit TPC-133--172 source corpus and its declared
data substrate.  Enlarging that corpus, or proving a new source claim,
creates a new audit instance.

The selected occurrence-augmented architecture is not stopped by
(7).  It may still be supplied by new mathematics or an explicitly
enlarged source inventory.  Its current status remains
\[
 \mathsf{NOT\_TESTABLE}.                                          \tag{8}
\]
This separation is essential: a stopped extraction method cell is
not a proof that the complete architecture is infeasible.

\section{Reproducible audit and claim boundary}

The standard-library audit source-locks the TPC-165 formal theorem
and the TPC-175 maximal-family export, recomputes (2)--(6), and
mutation-tests the boundary.  It rejects a fabricated positive edge,
invocation of the gluing theorem on empty input, promotion of the
2,988 unmatched cuts to actual physical carriers, a production-totality
claim, and a global architecture stop.  Hashes carry integrity
semantics only.

TPC-176 consumes no literal physical coefficient, named phase,
deterministic endpoint, normalization, or fixed-\(h_0=2\) arithmetic
estimate.  Consequently it supplies no actual active support,
canonical physical representative, program-positive \(\Ltwo\), or
strict \(1/400\) endpoint gain.

\section{Conclusion}

The correct output of an empty proved local-edge family is an exact
empty-domain ledger plus a scoped stop, not a vacuous totality
certificate.  TPC-176 closes the frozen-corpus extraction cell while
leaving all three H1 structural roots visible.  TPC-177 next asks
what an actual active-support audit can and cannot say when its
eligible carrier domain is empty.
