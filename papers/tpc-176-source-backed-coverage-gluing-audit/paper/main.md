# Exact coverage ledgers

Let $\mathcal K_{\rm cut}$ be the 2,988 source-declared production cut addresses imported by TPC-175. For $k\in\mathcal K_{\rm cut}$, let $n(k)$ be the number of proved local-edge rows above $k$. The exact classes are $$\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal K_{\rm cov}&=\{k:n(k)=1\},\\
 \mathcal K_{\rm dup}&=\{k:n(k)>1\},\\
 \mathcal K_{\rm miss}&=\{k:n(k)=0\}.
\end{aligned}                                                     \tag{4}$$ TPC-175 computes $$(|\mathcal K_{\rm cov}|,|\mathcal K_{\rm dup}|,
   |\mathcal K_{\rm miss}|)=(0,0,2988).                           \tag{5}$$ These are cut addresses, not actual physical carriers.

For the separately declared eligible carrier domain (2), let $\mathcal C_{\rm cov},\mathcal C_{\rm dup},\mathcal C_{\rm miss}$ be the analogous multiplicity classes. All three are empty.

> **Theorem: TPC-176 coverage ledger** The exact frozen-cut and eligible-carrier ledgers are $$\begin{aligned}
>  (|\mathcal K_{\rm cov}|,|\mathcal K_{\rm dup}|,
>    |\mathcal K_{\rm miss}|)&=(0,0,2988),\\
>  (|\mathcal C_{\rm cov}|,|\mathcal C_{\rm dup}|,
>    |\mathcal C_{\rm miss}|)&=(0,0,0).
>  \end{aligned}                                                    \tag{6}$$ No production totality conclusion follows.

> **Proof** Equation (1) gives $n(k)=0$ for all 2,988 declared cuts, proving the first line of (6). It also makes (2) empty, proving the second line. The final sentence follows because a cut address has not been proved to be an actual occurrence carrier, and the physical carrier universe has not been asserted to equal either declared domain.
