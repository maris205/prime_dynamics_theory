\title{\textbf{A Fiber Phase-Coherence Certificate for Post-Bin
Determinant Energy}\\
\large Exact Mean-Fluctuation Splitting, Sector Lower Bounds, and the
Remaining Arithmetic Gate}
\author{Liang Wang}
\date{July 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
TPC-110 identified the post-bin determinant energy as the squared
norm of the literal coefficient after summation on equal-determinant
fibers.  Its kernel-angle factorization is exact but does not say what
coefficient-specific fact could force a positive angle.  We give two
such refinements.  First, the pre-bin energy splits orthogonally into
fiber-mean coherence and within-fiber fluctuation, while the
determinant energy is exactly the fiber-size-weighted coherent part.
Second, a phase-sector certificate lower-bounds a fiber sum when a
good absolute-mass component lies in one half-plane and the remaining
absolute mass is smaller than the projected good mass.  The missing
amplification is measured by the effective participation ratio of the
good mass.  At the growing scale, a diagonal mass exponent
\(\lambda_E\), participation gain \(\gamma_R\), and phase-margin
exponent \(\lambda_\phi\) imply
\[
 \lambda_D^{\rm cert}
 =(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+.
\]
Combining this with the TPC-112 compatibility cone gives an explicit
three-input requirement.  None of the growing inputs or the zero-mode
saving is proved here.  The result turns an opaque kernel angle into
literal coefficient tests; it is an exact \(\Lzero/\Lone\) gate, not
an \(\Ltwo\) arithmetic advance.
\end{abstract}

\section{Literal post-bin fibers}

Fix the prescribed nonzero shift \(h_0\).  Import the inverse-aggregated
actual carrier \(\cT_X\), determinant label
\(\nu:\cT_X\to\cN_X\), and literal coefficients
\(u=(u_t)_{t\in\cT_X}\) from TPC-110 \citep{WangTPC110}.  For every
nonempty fiber
\[
 F_n=\{t\in\cT_X:\nu(t)=n\},\qquad m_n=|F_n|,
\]
put
\[
 A_X(n)=\sum_{t\in F_n}u_t,\qquad
 E_X=\sum_{t\in\cT_X}|u_t|^2,\qquad
 D_X=\sum_{n\in\cN_X}|A_X(n)|^2.
\]
All coefficients, determinant labels, and fibers are literal.  No
random phases, ambient support, or averaged shift is inserted.

For each fiber define
\[
 \mu_n=\frac1{m_n}\sum_{t\in F_n}u_t,\qquad
 C_n=m_n|\mu_n|^2,\qquad
 V_n=\sum_{t\in F_n}|u_t-\mu_n|^2.
\]
Here \(C_n\) is the squared norm of the constant-on-fiber projection
and \(V_n\) is its orthogonal complement.

\begin{theorem}[Exact mean-fluctuation splitting]
\label{thm:splitting}
One has
\[
 \boxed{E_X=C_X+V_X,}
 \qquad
 C_X=\sum_n C_n,\quad V_X=\sum_nV_n,
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{D_X=\sum_nm_nC_n.}
\]
If \(C_X>0\), the coherent effective multiplicity
\[
 \overline m_X^{\rm coh}
 =\frac{\sum_nm_nC_n}{\sum_nC_n}
\]
satisfies
\[
 m_{\min}\le\overline m_X^{\rm coh}\le m_{\max},
 \qquad
 D_X=C_X\overline m_X^{\rm coh}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On \(F_n\), the constant vector \((\mu_n)_{t\in F_n}\) is orthogonal
to \((u_t-\mu_n)_{t\in F_n}\), because the latter sums to zero.
Pythagoras gives
\[
 \sum_{t\in F_n}|u_t|^2=m_n|\mu_n|^2+V_n.
\]
Also \(A_X(n)=m_n\mu_n\), hence
\[
 |A_X(n)|^2=m_n^2|\mu_n|^2=m_nC_n.
\]
Summation proves both identities.  The effective multiplicity is a
weighted average of the nonempty \(m_n\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is equivalent to the TPC-110 kernel-angle identity, but exposes
the missing input locally: a determinant lower bound requires the
literal coefficient to have substantial fiber means, not merely
many collisions or large pre-bin energy.
\end{remark}

\section{A coefficient-specific phase certificate}

The next lemma is deterministic.  It is stated so that every term can
be checked on an actual fiber.

\begin{lemma}[Good-sector minus bad-mass bound]
\label{lem:sector}
Let \(F=G\sqcup B\) be one fiber and let \(u_t\in\C\).  Suppose that
there are a unit complex number \(\zeta\), a number
\(\kappa\in[0,1]\), and \(\theta\ge0\) such that
\[
 \Re(\overline\zeta u_t)\ge\kappa|u_t|
 \quad(t\in G),
\]
and
\[
 \sum_{t\in B}|u_t|
 \le\theta\sum_{t\in G}|u_t|.
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \left|\sum_{t\in F}u_t\right|
 \ge(\kappa-\theta)_+
 \sum_{t\in G}|u_t|.}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \left|\sum_{t\in F}u_t\right|^2
 \ge(\kappa-\theta)_+^2
 \left(\sum_{t\in G}|u_t|\right)^2
 \ge(\kappa-\theta)_+^2
 \sum_{t\in G}|u_t|^2.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Project the sum onto \(\zeta\):
\[
 \Re\left(\overline\zeta\sum_{t\in F}u_t\right)
 \ge
 \kappa\sum_{t\in G}|u_t|
 -\sum_{t\in B}|u_t|.
\]
The first inequality follows.  The second uses
\((\sum_{G}|u_t|)^2\ge\sum_G|u_t|^2\).
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Certified phase mass]
For a nonempty family of fibers \(\cG_X\subseteq\cN_X\), choose literal
partitions \(F_n=G_n\sqcup B_n\) and sector data
\((\zeta_n,\kappa_n,\theta_n)\) satisfying
\cref{lem:sector}.  Put
\[
 E_X^{\rm cert}=\sum_{n\in\cG_X}\sum_{t\in G_n}|u_t|^2,
 \qquad
 M_X^{\rm cert}
 =\sum_{n\in\cG_X}
 \left(\sum_{t\in G_n}|u_t|\right)^2,
 \qquad
 \rho_X=\min_{n\in\cG_X}(\kappa_n-\theta_n)_+.
\]
If \(E_X^{\rm cert}>0\), define the effective participation
\[
 R_X^{\rm cert}=\frac{M_X^{\rm cert}}{E_X^{\rm cert}}.
\]
It satisfies
\[
 1\le R_X^{\rm cert}\le
 \max_{n\in\cG_X}|G_n|.
\]
If \(E_X^{\rm cert}=0\), set \(R_X^{\rm cert}=0\).
For the empty certified family, set
\(E_X^{\rm cert}=M_X^{\rm cert}=\rho_X=R_X^{\rm cert}=0\).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Literal determinant lower certificate]
\label{thm:certificate}
Every certified family satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 D_X\ge\rho_X^2M_X^{\rm cert}
 =\rho_X^2R_X^{\rm cert}E_X^{\rm cert}.}
\]
The certificate is invariant under a common phase rotation on each
fiber and uses the actual post-bin sums.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the first squared conclusion of \cref{lem:sector} on each
certified fiber and sum.  Discarding uncertified fibers is legitimate
because their contributions to \(D_X\) are nonnegative.  The
participation bounds follow from
\[
 \sum_G|u_t|^2\le
 \left(\sum_G|u_t|\right)^2
 \le |G|\sum_G|u_t|^2.
\]
\end{proof}

\section{Exponent transfer and compatibility}

Recall the high-\(\beta\) scales
\[
 Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad
 J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)}.
\]
TPC-112 requires a lower bound
\[
 D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2
\]
and a zero-mode exponent \(\eta_Z\) satisfying
\(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\) \citep{WangTPC112}.

\begin{corollary}[Three-input exponent ledger]
\label{cor:ledger}
Fix one predeclared \(X\)-indexed selection rule for the certified
family, including its sets \(G_{X,n},B_{X,n}\) and sector data.
Assume nonnegative
exponents \(\lambda_E,\lambda_\phi,\gamma_R\) and future literal
estimates
\[
 E_X^{\rm cert}
 \ge X^{-\lambda_E+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,
\qquad
 R_X^{\rm cert}\ge X^{\gamma_R+o(1)},
 \qquad
 \rho_X\ge X^{-\lambda_\phi+o(1)}.
\]
Then
\[
 D_X\ge
 X^{-(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)+o(1)}
 Q_X^3J_X^2.
\]
Thus one admissible nonnegative recorded exponent is
\[
 \boxed{
 \lambda_D^{\rm cert}
 =(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+.}
\]
This certificate is compatible with a separately proved zero-mode
exponent precisely when
\[
 \boxed{
 \lambda_D^{\rm cert}
 \le2\eta_Z.}
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Insert the two assumed estimates into \cref{thm:certificate}, then
apply the TPC-112 cone.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[No double charging]
The phase loss occurs twice because the determinant energy is a
squared magnitude.  The participation gain is used once and may not
be recycled as physical synthesis slack.  Conversely, equality in
the determinant cone is permitted with zero determinant reserve,
while the independent physical condition remains strictly
\(\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400\).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Noncircular invocation]
The three estimates in \cref{cor:ledger} must be proved on the same
predeclared certified family and without invoking the target lower
bound for \(D_X\).  In particular, defining either
\(\gamma_R\) or \(\lambda_\phi\) from the already aggregated value
\(D_X/E_X\) would merely rename the desired conclusion.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[Participation is indispensable below loss one]
\label{cor:participation}
The imported diagonal envelope
\[
 E_X^{\rm cert}\le E_X
 \ll X^{-1+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2
\]
shows that a certificate with
\(\lambda_D^{\rm cert}<1\) must obtain a positive
power from effective participation after paying the phase loss.  More
precisely, the three-input ledger requires
\[
 \gamma_R> \lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-1.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The diagonal ceiling forces every matching lower exponent
\(\lambda_E\) to be at least \(1\), up to \(o(1)\).  Rearrange
\(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R<1\).
\end{proof}

\section{Sharp limitations}

\begin{proposition}[Magnitudes do not certify a phase margin]
\label{prop:roots}
For every \(m\ge2\) there are \(m\) unit-modulus coefficients on one
fiber whose sum is zero.  Hence active support, equal magnitudes,
fiber multiplicity, and diagonal energy alone do not imply
\(\rho_X>0\), \(C_X>0\), or \(D_X>0\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use the \(m\)-th roots of unity.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A half-plane condition is genuinely additional]
If all coefficients of a fiber lie in an angular sector of width
\(\Delta<\pi\), then \cref{lem:sector} applies with \(B=\varnothing\)
and
\[
 \kappa=\cos(\Delta/2)>0.
\]
No sector of width \(\pi\) or more gives a uniform positive lower
bound, since opposite phases can cancel.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Rotate the sector midpoint to the positive real axis.  Every
coefficient then has real projection at least
\(\cos(\Delta/2)\) times its modulus.  Opposite unit vectors prove
the endpoint obstruction.
\end{proof}

The literal phases contain the fixed \(h_0\), reconstruction
multiplier, affine character, and arithmetic signs.  Establishing a
growing phase margin for them may be as hard as controlling the
signed arithmetic directly.  The certificate is a target, not a
random-phase heuristic.

\section{Certificate and claim boundary}

The deterministic script
\texttt{experiments/tpc121\_phase\_coherence\_audit.py} verifies:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the mean-fluctuation and weighted-coherence identities;
\item the sector lower bound on a nonsymmetric complex example;
\item exact cancellation by equal-modulus roots of unity; and
\item the participation ratio, a two-fiber minimum-margin test, the
diagonal-ceiling requirement, the determinant-zero compatibility
check, and the exponent ledger
\[
\lambda_D^{\rm cert}
=(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+.
\]
\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.42\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Statement & Level and status\\
\midrule
Mean-fluctuation splitting and sector lemma
& Exact finite-dimensional theorem (\(\Lzero\)).\\
Attachment to TPC-110 actual determinant fibers
& Literal fixed-\(h_0\) interface (\(\Lone\)).\\
Growing lower bound for \(E_X^{\rm cert}\)
& Open arithmetic input.\\
Growing participation gain \(R_X^{\rm cert}\)
& Open equal-determinant coherence input.\\
Growing positive phase margin \(\rho_X\)
& Open coefficient-specific arithmetic input.\\
Fixed-\(h_0\) polynomial saving
& Not proved; no \(\Ltwo\) advance.\\

TPC-121 therefore advances the route by decomposing a sufficient lower
certificate for the opaque coherent product
\(D_X=C_X\overline m_X^{\rm coh}\) into three independently auditable
inputs: certified diagonal mass, effective participation growth, and
a literal fiber phase margin.  It does not show that any of them holds.
TPC-122 treats the other side of the compatibility cone: conversion
of ordered signed-prefix cancellation into a distinguished zero-mode
exponent.

\paragraph{Explicit exclusions.}
No statement proves H3, a Möbius or Liouville cancellation estimate,
a positive prime-pair lower bound, a Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, a
parity breach, or the twin-prime conjecture.  Nothing is averaged over
or specialized to a substitute for the prescribed \(h_0\).
