\section{An unnormalized weighted Bessel compiler}

Let $\mathcal V$ be a Hilbert space and let
$v_{h,q}\in\ell^2(\mathbb Z/h\mathbb Z;\mathcal V)$ be arbitrary rows supported on
$S_{h,q}$.  Their values may include the full profile in \eqref{eq:physical-row}; no
equality of amplitudes or row norms is assumed.

\begin{corollary}[Physical fixed-fiber Bessel bound]\label{cor:bessel}
For arbitrary complex coefficients $c_q$,
\begin{equation}
 \left\|\sum_qc_qv_{h,q}\right\|^2
 \le B_h\sum_q|c_q|^2\|v_{h,q}\|^2,
 \qquad
 B_h\le\frac{4Q^2}{H}+\frac{4hQ}{H}
      \le\frac{8Q^2}{H}.                              \label{eq:bessel}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
At a fixed residue $a$, at most $R_h(a)$ rows contribute.  Cauchy--Schwarz at that
coordinate and summation over $a$ give \eqref{eq:bessel}, with
$B_h=\max_aR_h(a)$.  Theorem~\ref{thm:gcd-envelope} and $g\ge1$ give the displayed
uniform bounds.
\end{proof}

The estimate immediately preserves the physical divisor weights in the orthogonal
pre-reassembly direct sum:
\begin{equation}
 \sum_h|C_h|^2\left\|\sum_qc_{h,q}v_{h,q}\right\|^2
 \le B_{\rm phys}
 \sum_{h,q}|C_h|^2|c_{h,q}|^2\|v_{h,q}\|^2,           \label{eq:weighted-direct-sum}
\end{equation}
where $B_{\rm phys}=max_hB_h$.  Nothing has been absorbed into a row-dependent
normalization.

If one common bounded linear map $T_h$ is subsequently applied to every packet output
at denominator $h$, the right side acquires only
$\sup_h\|T_h\|^2$.  Because the map is common and linear, the four-phase polarization
identity remains valid.  This is the precise repair of the normalization obstruction:
conditioning is paid by an explicit multiplicity factor rather than by independently
renormalizing packet outputs.
