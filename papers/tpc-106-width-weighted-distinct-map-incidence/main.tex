\begin{abstract}
TPC-105 aggregates identical literal affine multiplier maps while
retaining their input profiles and complete provenance.  This paper
analyzes the remaining genuinely distinct-map term.  Let
\(G=\F_q^\times\), let
\(\phi(j)=A_\phi(m_\phi j+h_0)\) be the canonical maps at one
resonance width, and let \(f_\phi:G\to[0,\infty)\) be the exact
pushforward of the opened-atom input profile.  For
\(M_\phi=\sum_yf_\phi(y)\), the ordered distinct-map excess is
\[
 \YH
 =
 \sum_{\phi\ne\psi}
 \left\{
  \sum_{y\in G}f_\phi(y)f_\psi(y)
  -\frac{M_\phi M_\psi}{q-1}
 \right\}.
\]
We prove the exact multiplicative-character identity
\[
 \YH
 =
 \frac1{q-1}\sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}
 \sum_{\phi\ne\psi}
 \widehat f_\phi(\chi)
 \overline{\widehat f_\psi(\chi)}
 =
 \|a_H^\circ\|_2^2
 -\sum_\phi\|f_\phi^\circ\|_2^2,
\qquad a_H=\sum_\phi f_\phi.
\]
Thus the exact missing datum is cross-map character coherence, not
the number of distinct formulas.  If
\(\mathcal E_H=\sum_\phi\|f_\phi^\circ\|_2^2\) and
\(\kappa_H=|\YH|/\mathcal E_H\), then
\[
 0\le\kappa_H\le |\Phi_H|-1,
\qquad
 \sum_Hg_q(H)\sqrt{|\YH|}
 =
 \sum_Hg_q(H)\sqrt{\kappa_H\mathcal E_H}.
\]
The upper endpoint is sharp even inside the physical map shape
\(A(mj+h_0)\): distinct maps supported at one input can all hit one
output.  Hence map distinctness, no-wrap injectivity, and width
weighting alone cannot prove the TPC-106 target.  We give a
two-ledger sufficient criterion and an exact deterministic
certificate, but do not estimate the actual growing profiles.
The identities and obstruction are \(\Lzero\); their literal
crosswalk is \(\Lone\).  No \(\Ltwo\) fixed-\(h_0\) arithmetic
estimate, parity breakthrough, or prime-pair result is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} fixed-shift affine carrier;

\section{Scope and imported interface}

TPC-99--TPC-102 reduce the positive nonconstant resonance sector to
a principal ledger, an opened-atom diagonal ledger, and a
width-weighted cross-cell ledger
\citep{WangTPC99,WangTPC101,WangTPC102}.  TPC-105 then quotients
identical affine maps without replacing an input profile by its
total mass \citep{WangTPC105}.  The remaining proposed estimate is
\begin{equation}
 \Xdist
 :=
 \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)
 \sqrt{|\YH|}
 \ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,
\label{eq:tpc106-target}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 g_q(H)
 =
 \sqrt{\frac{2H(q-1-2H)}{q-1}}.
\]
The square root contains an absolute value because the centered
ordered cross term need not be nonnegative.
Even a proof of \eqref{eq:tpc106-target} closes the full positive
cross ledger only together with the independent TPC-105 conditions
\[
 U_X\le X^{o(1)},\qquad
 \mathfrak P_X\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.
\]
Those conditions return the identical-map ledger and are not
reproved or assumed true here.

We analyze exactly what information can imply
\eqref{eq:tpc106-target}.  The paper does not assert that the actual
TPC profiles satisfy the resulting coherence bounds.

\section{Canonical maps and exact pushforwards}

Fix an odd prime \(q\), put \(G=\F_q^\times\), and fix
\(h_0\in G\).  At one active width \(H\), let \(\Phi_H\) be the
canonical set of pairwise distinct maps
\begin{equation}
 \phi(j)=s_\phi j+t_\phi
 =A_\phi(m_\phi j+h_0),
 \qquad A_\phi,m_\phi\in G.
\label{eq:physical-map}
\end{equation}
The identity with the right-hand expression is part of the literal
fixed-\(h_0\) interface.

For each \(\phi\), let \(J_\phi\subseteq\F_q\) be its actual active
input support, with \(\phi(J_\phi)\subseteq G\), and let
\(\nu_\phi:J_\phi\to[0,\infty)\) be the provenance-preserving
opened-atom profile.  Define
\begin{align}
 f_\phi(y)
 &=
 \sum_{j\in J_\phi}
 \nu_\phi(j)\one_{\{\phi(j)=y\}},
\label{eq:pushforward}\\
 M_\phi&=\sum_{j\in J_\phi}\nu_\phi(j)
       =\sum_{y\in G}f_\phi(y),
\qquad
 a_H=\sum_{\phi\in\Phi_H}f_\phi.
\label{eq:masses}
\end{align}
Since \(s_\phi\ne0\), each map is injective on \(\F_q\); nevertheless
different maps may have arbitrarily aligned pushforwards.

\begin{definition}[Ordered distinct-map excess]
\label{def:distinct-excess}
Set
\begin{equation}
 \YH
 =
 \sum_{\substack{\phi,\psi\in\Phi_H\\\phi\ne\psi}}
 \left[
  \sum_{y\in G}f_\phi(y)f_\psi(y)
  -\frac{M_\phi M_\psi}{q-1}
 \right].
\label{eq:distinct-excess}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

The ordered convention agrees with expanding the squared
\(\ell^2(G)\) norm.  Each unordered pair therefore occurs twice.

\section{Exact character-coherence theorem}

For \(f:G\to\C\), use
\[
 \widehat f(\chi)
 =
 \sum_{y\in G}f(y)\overline{\chi(y)},
\qquad
 f^\circ=f-\frac1{q-1}\left(\sum_yf(y)\right)\one_G.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Three exact forms of the distinct-map ledger]
\label{thm:three-forms}
For every family in \cref{eq:physical-map}--\cref{eq:masses},
\begin{align}
 \YH
 &=
 \sum_{\phi\ne\psi}
 \langle f_\phi^\circ,f_\psi^\circ\rangle,
\label{eq:gram-form}\\
 &=
 \frac1{q-1}\sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}
 \sum_{\phi\ne\psi}
 \widehat f_\phi(\chi)
 \overline{\widehat f_\psi(\chi)},
\label{eq:character-form}\\
 &=
 \|a_H^\circ\|_2^2
 -\sum_{\phi\in\Phi_H}\|f_\phi^\circ\|_2^2.
\label{eq:energy-difference}
\end{align}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The bracket in \eqref{eq:distinct-excess} is exactly
\(\langle f_\phi^\circ,f_\psi^\circ\rangle\), giving
\eqref{eq:gram-form}.  Multiplicative Parseval on \(G\) deletes the
principal character and gives \eqref{eq:character-form}.  Finally,
expand
\[
 \left\|\sum_\phi f_\phi^\circ\right\|_2^2
 =
 \sum_\phi\|f_\phi^\circ\|_2^2
 +\sum_{\phi\ne\psi}
 \langle f_\phi^\circ,f_\psi^\circ\rangle.
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why a map total is insufficient]
The character vector \(\widehat f_\phi(\chi)\), not merely
\(M_\phi=\widehat f_\phi(\chi_0)\), determines the nonprincipal
cross term.  This is the precise reason that the canonical quotient
must retain the complete input profile and provenance bag.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Internal energy and coherence]
\label{def:coherence}
Put
\[
 \mathcal E_H
 =
 \sum_{\phi\in\Phi_H}\|f_\phi^\circ\|_2^2,
\qquad
 \kappa_H
 =
 \begin{cases}
 |\YH|/\mathcal E_H,
   &\mathcal E_H>0,\\
 0,&\mathcal E_H=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Sharp universal coherence range]
\label{prop:coherence-range}
If \(K_H=|\Phi_H|\), then
\begin{equation}
 0\le\kappa_H\le K_H-1.
\label{eq:coherence-range}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The lower bound is definitional.  By Cauchy--Schwarz,
\begin{align*}
 |\YH|
 &\le
 \sum_{\phi\ne\psi}
 \|f_\phi^\circ\|_2\|f_\psi^\circ\|_2\\
 &=
 \left(\sum_\phi\|f_\phi^\circ\|_2\right)^2
 -\sum_\phi\|f_\phi^\circ\|_2^2
 \le(K_H-1)\mathcal E_H.
\end{align*}
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact two-ledger reduction]
\label{cor:two-ledger}
The TPC-106 left side has the exact form
\begin{equation}
 \Xdist
 =
 \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{\kappa_H\mathcal E_H}
\label{eq:exact-width-ledger}
\end{equation}
and obeys
\begin{equation}
 \Xdist
 \le
 \left(\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)^2\kappa_H\right)^{1/2}
 \left(\sum_H\mathcal E_H\right)^{1/2}.
\label{eq:two-ledger-bound}
\end{equation}
In particular, bounds
\[
 \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)^2\kappa_H
 \le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,
\qquad
 \sum_H\mathcal E_H
 \le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2
\]
are sufficient for \eqref{eq:tpc106-target}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The identity follows from \cref{def:coherence}; the inequality is
Cauchy--Schwarz in \(H\).
\end{proof}

This reduction separates the amount of centered mass carried by
each map from the alignment of distinct maps.  Neither ledger is
estimated here for the actual growing coefficient.

\section{Sharp physical-shape alignment obstruction}

The next result shows that the word ``distinct'' has no quantitative
dispersion content by itself.

\begin{theorem}[Distinct physical maps can align perfectly]
\label{thm:sharp-alignment}
Let \(q\ge5\), choose \(h_0,j_0,y_0\in G\), and let
\(\mathcal M\subseteq G\setminus\{-h_0j_0^{-1}\}\) have cardinality
\(K\).  For \(m\in\mathcal M\), set
\[
 A_m=\frac{y_0}{mj_0+h_0},
\qquad
 \phi_m(j)=A_m(mj+h_0).
\]
Then the \(\phi_m\) are pairwise distinct maps of the literal shape
\eqref{eq:physical-map}.  If every profile is the singleton
\(\nu_m=w\one_{\{j_0\}}\), \(w>0\), then
\begin{align}
 f_m&=w\one_{\{y_0\}},\\
 \mathcal E_H
 &=K w^2\frac{q-2}{q-1},\\
 \YH
 &=K(K-1)w^2\frac{q-2}{q-1},\\
 \kappa_H&=K-1.
\end{align}
Thus \eqref{eq:coherence-range} is sharp.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every denominator defining \(A_m\) is nonzero and
\(\phi_m(j_0)=y_0\).  If \(\phi_m=\phi_{m'}\), equality of the
constant terms gives \(A_mh_0=A_{m'}h_0\), hence \(A_m=A_{m'}\);
equality of slopes then gives \(m=m'\).  The centered norm of
\(w\one_{\{y_0\}}\) on a set of size \(q-1\) is
\(w^2(1-1/(q-1))\).  Every ordered distinct pair has the same
centered inner product, so the formulas follow.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No geometry-only TPC-106 theorem]
\label{cor:no-geometry-only}
No bound with subpower coherence can follow solely from:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item pairwise distinctness of the canonical maps;
\item the fixed-\(h_0\) form \(A(mj+h_0)\);
\item injectivity of each map on its no-wrap input support; and
\item the resonance width \(H\).
\end{itemize}
Additional information about the actual profiles, weights, or
arithmetic signs is necessary.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[What the countermodel does not prove]
The construction is an exact finite obstruction to a
coefficient-blind argument.  It is not asserted to occur with
polynomial mass inside the literal TPC coefficient.  Therefore it
does not prove that the actual positive route fails.
\end{remark}

\section{Literal audit and route decision}

For the actual coefficient, a proof-carrying TPC-106 certificate
must list, at every active \(H\):
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item every canonical map
\(\phi(j)=A_\phi(m_\phi j+h_0)\);
\item the recoverable provenance bag and exact profile
\(\nu_\phi(j)\);
\item the actual support condition \(\phi(j)\ne0\) and all inherited
masks;
\item one copy of the physical opened-atom weight under the native
global normalization;
\item the exact values of \(\mathcal E_H\) and either
\(\YH\) or the character vectors in
\eqref{eq:character-form}; and
\item the width-weighted sum in \eqref{eq:exact-width-ledger}.
\end{enumerate}
Ambient map counts or a random-map model cannot replace these data.

\begin{proposition}[Stop--go statement]
\label{prop:stop-go}
The following conclusions have different strengths.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item A verified bound \eqref{eq:tpc106-target} for the actual
growing profiles is a go certificate for the distinct-map part of
the positive resonance route.
\item A verified polynomial lower obstruction in the actual
\(\Xdist\), at its native normalization and with no compensating
signed term, stops this positive termwise route and triggers signed
filtering before majorization.
\item \Cref{cor:no-geometry-only} already stops every proof based
only on map distinctness, but does not decide (a) or (b).
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\section{\texorpdfstring{\hspace{0.35em}}{}Deterministic regression certificate}

The script \texttt{experiments/tpc106\_certificate.py} checks, over
several small primes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item equality of the direct centered-Gram form and the
energy-difference form in \cref{thm:three-forms};
\item the sharp family in \cref{thm:sharp-alignment}; and
\item the coherence bound \(\kappa_H\le K_H-1\).
\end{itemize}
It uses exact rational arithmetic.  The character form in
\cref{thm:three-forms} is proved analytically by multiplicative
Fourier inversion; it is not separately tested by this script.  The
certificate verifies finite algebra, not asymptotic arithmetic
cancellation.

\section{Claim boundary}

\subsection*{Proved}

The canonical distinct-map ledger has the exact character and Gram
forms in \cref{thm:three-forms}.  Its width-weighted target is
exactly the energy--coherence ledger
\eqref{eq:exact-width-ledger}.  The universal coherence factor is at
most \(K_H-1\), and this endpoint is achieved by pairwise distinct
maps of the literal fixed-\(h_0\) shape.  These are \(\Lzero\)
results.  Importing the TPC-105 quotient profiles without changing
support, coefficients, or normalization gives the stated
\(\Lone\) interface.

\subsection*{Not proved}

This paper does not prove:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \eqref{eq:tpc106-target} for the actual growing profiles;
\item a subpower bound for \(\mathcal E_H\) or \(\kappa_H\);
\item that the sharp alignment countermodel has substantial mass in
the physical packet;
\item closure or failure of the full positive resonance route;
\item a signed affine M\"obius estimate, determinant/zero-mode
compatibility, full-block return, or the strict physical endpoint;
or
\item a parity breakthrough, Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic,
prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem.
\end{itemize}
Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).

\subsection*{Verdict}

TPC-106 makes a genuine route decision at the theorem-design level:
canonical quotienting removes artificial identical-map duplication,
but distinctness alone supplies no dispersion.  The remaining
positive door is precisely a weighted character-coherence theorem
for the actual profiles.  If that theorem cannot be proved or is
falsified on the literal coefficient, the mathematically correct
fallback is to retain the signs before taking absolute values, as
developed in TPC-107.
