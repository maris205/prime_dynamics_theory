\begin{abstract}
TPC-294 found strong cancellation in an ambient equal-sign cube for the
exactly weighted Gram form, but did not determine whether its favorable sign
targets could be produced by a source-side correlation map.  This paper
isolates that finite linear-algebra question.  Let the physical shell
vectors be the columns of a rational matrix $A$ and let
$C=A^{\mathsf T}$ map source coordinates to prime correlations.  We prove
that nonsingularity of $G=A^{\mathsf T}A$ makes $C$ surjective and gives the
explicit witness $h=AG^{-1}b$ for every target $b$.  Two independently
replayed modular determinant certificates establish full column rank on all
18 inherited rows, covering 1,380 shell edges.  Consequently every
TPC-294 weighted minimizer, unit-edge max-cut target, and all-positive target
has an exact witness in the declared unrestricted finite rational source
space.  This closes only the broad finite image question: admissibility in
the frozen Mobius/comparison profile, witness-norm growth, arithmetic
$L^2$, and the twin-prime endpoint remain open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Position on the route}

The project studies one literal prime-shell dynamical family throughout.
Earlier releases separated physical prime components and measured their
cross-prime Gram interaction \cite{tpc287,tpc288,tpc289}.  Adaptive
nonnegative weighting encountered a coherence wall \cite{tpc290}; signed
pair cancellation was then tested \cite{tpc291}.  Three-prime parity and
the complete signed-shell max-cut layer exposed combinatorial obstructions
\cite{tpc292,tpc293}.  Restoring the true Gram magnitudes in TPC-294 showed
that the weighted sign optimizer can be substantially better than the
unit-edge surrogate \cite{tpc294}.

That last result left a precise question.  The signs in TPC-294 were chosen
in an ambient coefficient cube.  Are they even in the image of a source
perturbation?  The present paper answers the finite question for an
explicitly declared broad source space.  The answer is positive because
the inherited physical columns have full Gram rank.  The qualification
``broad'' is part of the theorem: no assertion is made here about the
frozen arithmetic source profile.

\section{Finite physical shell and source map}

Let $I=I_N$ be the frozen integer interval.  For an odd prime $q$, height
$H$, and exponent $s$, write
\[
 K_{H,s}(v)=\frac{H^{2s}}{(H^2+v^2)^s},\qquad
 B_q(u,t)=\mathbf 1_{q\nmid u}\mathbf 1_{q\nmid t}
 \left(\mathbf 1_{u\equiv t\pmod q}-\frac1{q-1}\right).
\]
The deleted-diagonal physical component inherited from the frozen source
vector is
\[
 (g_q)_u=\sum_{t\in I,\ t\ne u}
 qK_{H,s}(u-t)B_q(u,t)\,\beta_t,
 \qquad u\in I.
 \label{eq:physical}
\]
For a finite shell $S=\{q:Q<q\le 2Q\}$, set
\[
 A=[g_q]_{q\in S}\in\Q^{I\times S},\qquad
 G=A^{\mathsf T}A.
 \label{eq:matrix}
\]
Thus $G$ is exactly the Gram matrix used by TPC-294.  The source-side
correlation map is
\[
 C=A^{\mathsf T}:\Q^{I}\longrightarrow\Q^{S},\qquad
 C(h)=\bigl(\ip{h}{g_q}\bigr)_{q\in S}.
 \label{eq:correlation}
\]
The source space in this release is the full finite rational coordinate
space $\Q^I$.  Restricting it to a parameterized arithmetic profile is a
different problem and is not silently built into $C$.

\section{Exact image theorem}

The basic observation is elementary but decisive for the route map.

\begin{theorem}[Full Gram rank gives a full finite source image]
Let $A\in\Q^{n\times m}$ and $G=A^{\mathsf T}A$.  If $G$ is nonsingular,
then $C=A^{\mathsf T}:\Q^n\to\Q^m$ is surjective.  For every
$b\in\Q^m$, one exact preimage is
\[
 h_b=AG^{-1}b.
 \label{eq:witness}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Nonsingularity gives $G^{-1}$ over $\Q$.  Substitution yields
\[
 A^{\mathsf T}h_b=A^{\mathsf T}AG^{-1}b=GG^{-1}b=b.
\]
Hence every target has a preimage.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The displayed witness is the least-norm witness]
Under the hypotheses of the theorem, the same vector $h_b$ is the unique
minimum-$\ell^2$ solution of $A^{\mathsf T}h=b$ over $\R^n$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The vector $h_b$ belongs to $\operatorname{col}(A)$, while every difference
of two solutions belongs to $\ker(A^{\mathsf T})$.  These two subspaces are
orthogonal.  Therefore every real solution has the form $h_b+v$ with
$v\perp h_b$, and $\norm{h_b+v}^2=\norm{h_b}^2+\norm{v}^2$.
\end{proof}

The proposition identifies the next quantitative obstruction: exact
attainability does not imply that the least source cost is small enough for
an analytic argument.  In particular, the theorem pays no asymptotic power
credit.

\section{Modular rank certificate}

All entries in \eqref{eq:physical} are rational on the declared finite grid.
For a prime modulus $p$ avoiding their denominators, reduce $G$ entrywise
to $G_p$.  We use the following denominator-clearing fact.

\begin{lemma}[Nonzero modular determinant]
If every entry denominator is invertible modulo $p$ and
$\det(G_p)\ne0$ in $\mathbb F_p$, then $\det(G)\ne0$ over $\Q$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose a common denominator $D$ with $p\nmid D$.  The integer
$D^m\det(G)$ reduces to $\det(G_p)$.  A nonzero reduction cannot come from
the zero integer, so $\det(G)\ne0$.
\end{proof}

The producer accumulates each $g_q$ target-first, forms the rational Gram
matrix, and performs Gaussian elimination modulo
\[
 p_1=1{,}000{,}000{,}007,\qquad p_2=998{,}244{,}353.
\]
The independent replay accumulates the same columns source-first and does
not import the producer.  Target residuals for the all-positive label, the
TPC-293 max-cut label, and the TPC-294 minimum label are also checked modulo
both primes.  These residual checks are a replay guard; exact feasibility
itself follows from Theorem 1 once the determinant certificate is accepted.

\section{What this does and does not close}

The finite atlas closes a genuine branch of the map: an ambient signed
target cannot be rejected merely because the finite linear correlation map
has too small an image.  This is useful because TPC-294's favorable
weighted directions are now known to possess explicit finite source
preimages.

It also sharpens the obstruction.  There are at least three remaining
filters between this theorem and a twin-prime result:

\begin{enumerate}
\item the witness must belong to the native Mobius/comparison profile or a
      rigorously justified enlargement of it;
\item the least-norm cost $\norm{AG^{-1}b}$ must be bounded in a growing
      shell with the correct normalization;
\item the resulting source construction must feed the arithmetic $L^2$
      estimate and the Route-B endpoint without spending more power than it
      gains.
\end{enumerate}

The first issue is an image-of-a-profile problem, not a rank problem.  The
second is a conditioning problem: full rank can coexist with a very large
inverse Gram matrix.  The third is the original arithmetic bottleneck.
Accordingly, the result is deliberately recorded as
\[
\texttt{PROVED\_EXACT\_FINITE}\quad+\quad
\texttt{NUMERICALLY\_CERTIFIED\_FINITE},
\]
with no asymptotic exponent credit and no twin-prime conclusion.

\section{Reproducibility and conclusion}

From the project directory, run:
\begin{verbatim}
export PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1
python -B code/tpc295_source_correlation_image_certificate.py --check
python -B experiments/tpc295_independent_checker.py
python -B experiments/tpc295_source_image_stress.py
\end{verbatim}
The optimized versions use Python's \texttt{-O} flag and must have
byte-identical standard output and empty standard error.  The canonical
finite record is
\texttt{results/tpc295\_certificate.json}.

The Session-named Route-A/Route-B evaluator files are absent from this
checkout.  Therefore this paper reports the local proof package, exact
canonical certificate, independent replay, stress test, and Bridge-B
checker as the available fail-closed evidence; it does not declare an
official evaluator pass.

The next route experiment is quantitative rather than another ambient sign
search: evaluate the least-norm witness cost, compare it with admissible
source-profile spans, and test whether any normalized budget can reach the
arithmetic $L^2$ interface.  This is the
\texttt{ROUND2\_CLUE} recorded by the certificate.
