\section{Claim boundary and next gate}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\begin{theorem}[Audited conclusion]
\label{thm:audited-conclusion}
Let \(P\) be an orthogonal projection on a prescribed finite
actual-support frame, let \(B=PA\), and let \(R=(I-P)A\).
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item If \(P\one=\one\), then
  \[
  Z(B)=Z(A),\qquad
  D(A)=D(B)+D(R),
  \]
  and every nonzero projected branch satisfies
  \[
  \rho(A)\le\rho(B),\qquad
  \Flat(A)\le\Flat(B).
  \]
  The zero projected branch forces \(Z(A)=0\).
\item For arbitrary \(P\),
  \[
  Z(B)-Z(A)
  =
  -\langle R,(I-P)\one\rangle
  \]
  and
  \[
  |Z(B)-Z(A)|
  \le
  \sqrt S\,\kappa(P)\|R\|_2
  \]
  with optimal constant.
\item If \(B\ne0\), the critical structure-free condition is
  \[
  \kappa(P)
  \frac{\|R\|_2}{\|B\|_2}
  =O(J^{-1}).
  \]
  It is vacuous for an exactly constant-preserving projection and
  returns the TPC-89 \(J^{-1}\) residual order when
  \(\kappa(P)\asymp1\).
\item Every unsafe \(P\) has the unique minimal constant completion
  \(P^\sharp\) from \eqref{eq:completed-projection}.
\end{enumerate}
For the literal fixed-\(h_0\) TPC coefficient, a zero-mode-safe
orthogonal surrogate meeting the projected critical angle bound
certifies
\[
 \Flat_X\le X^{1/400+o(1)}
\]
without a relative approximation hypothesis.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine
\cref{thm:safe-projection-law,thm:sharp-zero-defect,thm:critical-sharpness,thm:minimal-completion,thm:literal-safe-certificate}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What is proved at \(\Lzero\).}
The projection ledger, exact monotonicity, equality cases, partition
variance law, nested hierarchy, weighted compression theorem,
unweighted counterexample, sharp defect bound, product threshold,
and rank-one completion are unconditional finite-dimensional
theorems.

\paragraph{What is proved at \(\Lone\).}
Once an explicitly constructed operator is verified on the literal
post-bin frame, the paper gives a proof-carrying certificate that
transfers projected zero-mode and energy bounds to the physical
coefficient in the correct directions.  The checklist in
\cref{prop:promotion-checklist} is part of this interface.

\paragraph{What is not proved at \(\Ltwo\).}
No constant-preserving projection with a small projected flatness is
constructed for the growing arithmetic family.  No projected
determinant-energy lower bound, low-window cancellation estimate,
affine M{\"o}bius-correlation theorem, full-block return, or
fixed-shift asymptotic is proved.

\paragraph{No free averaging.}
Block averaging cannot manufacture a smaller physical flatness when
it is performed as a same-frame constant-preserving orthogonal
projection.  It can manufacture apparent cancellation if block
multiplicities are deleted, if the operator misses the constant
mode, or if an ensemble/frequency average is substituted for the
physical bins.  Those operations are outside the safe theorem.

\paragraph{No contradiction with the accuracy barrier.}
The TPC-89 barrier concerns arbitrary error directions.
Constant-preserving orthogonal projection removes precisely the
zero-mode component of the error.  In the defective case, the sharp
product \(\kappa r\) restores the same critical order.  These are
complementary statements.

\paragraph{Arithmetic boundary.}
Nothing here specializes the prescribed nonzero \(h_0\) to \(2\),
proves a growing fixed-shift M{\"o}bius or Liouville correlation,
crosses the sieve parity barrier, proves a Hardy--Littlewood
asymptotic, gives a prime-pair lower bound, or proves the twin-prime
conjecture.

\paragraph{Next gate.}
The most useful continuation is a literal projection census:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item define candidate blocks from the native TPC-32/TPC-93 keys;
\item verify their exact inverse map, global weights, and
  \(P_X\one=\one\);
\item compute the projected zero mode and energy without deleting
  any sector;
\item prove or disprove
  \[
  \rho(P_XA_X)\le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X}
  \]
  for one growing family; and
\item if constants are not preserved, test the sharp fallback
  \[
  \kappa_Xr_X
  \le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X}.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
A positive estimate would promote the projection route.  A lower
bound showing every admissible coarse projection has excessive
flatness would be a precise negative certificate and would redirect
the program toward the low-window/Tauberian or full-block routes.
