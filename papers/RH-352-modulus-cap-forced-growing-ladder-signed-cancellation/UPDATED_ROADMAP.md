# Roadmap after RH-352

RH-352 closes the route coordinate

    actual_growing_lower_even_signed_remainder_open

at the normalized local natural scale.  The actual direct coefficient
`p=tau-a` is exponentially small after division by `2 H_m x^m`, uniformly
on every RH-350 window `J_k->infinity`, `J_k=o(k)`.  Therefore the actual
signed remainder tracks `S-P`, and its normalized aggregate is bounded away
from zero.  RH-350's aggregate small-Y hypothesis is false for the actual
physical coefficients.

The next theorem edge is not another abstract completion.  The modulus cap
used here is too weak on the unnormalized selected-prefix scale because

    lambda^2(qR)^2 > 1.

A positive continuation must therefore obtain cancellation or finer
spectral information beyond the separate absolute bounds on `tau` and `a`.
Narrow candidates are:

1. an actual complex-phase or signed trace theorem for `tau_(sigma,2m)-a_(2m)`
   with unnormalized root below one on a growing selected window;
2. a direct weighted aggregate estimate for the selected lower-even prefix,
   stronger by an additional factor `x^(-k)` than the present modulus cap;
3. same-clock actual completion theorems at the critical and first-lower
   orders, followed by odd- and upper-alias control;
4. a direct annular theorem for the full physical modulus complement that
   controls the complete prefix rather than this selected ladder.

Even a successful unnormalized lower-even theorem would not by itself close
the full `E_off` aggregate.  RH-288 requires one common physical determinant
data type with the direct prefix and both analytic tails.  Gate A separately
requires a canonical intrinsic determinant, and Gates B--E remain
independent.  No current result constructs a Hilbert--Polya operator,
identifies Riemann zeros, proves a von Mangoldt trace, proves completed-zeta
divisor equality, or proves RH.
