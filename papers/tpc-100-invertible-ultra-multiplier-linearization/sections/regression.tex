\section{Exact provenance regression}
\label{sec:regression}

The script
\texttt{experiments/tpc100\_certificate.py} models the literal order
\[
 \beta=(\theta,c,\kappa)
 \longrightarrow
 (\beta,z)
 \longrightarrow
 \text{source inverse}
 \longrightarrow
 \operatorname{del}_j\text{ cells}.
\]
It constructs \(4{,}096\) resolved branches,
\(6{,}851\) opened atoms, and \(6{,}851\) fine cells over small
no-wrap primes.  Using exact integer and rational arithmetic, it
checks:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the affine determinant identity and the source equality
  \(m_{\beta,z}j_\theta+h_0=\sigma_\theta u_{\beta,z}\);
\item the exact mass equality
  \(w_\beta=\sum_z w_{\beta,z}\) and equality of the branch and atom
  multiplier censuses;
\item reconstruction of \((\beta,z)\) and its \(r\)-free source from
  the fine cell together with \(j\), and the stronger fact that the
  retained physical source coordinates force every fine cell to be
  a singleton;
\item inheritance of \(H_q(N_\beta)\), including explicit examples
  where it differs from \(H_q(|J_C|)\);
\item both finite-field coefficients, atomwise linearization, and
  fine-cell injectivity;
\item forced cross-cell collisions between different \(z\)'s of one
  branch;
\item exact resonance preimages, cross-cell congruences and partner
  counts; and
\item uncentered and centered opened-atom energy identities.
\end{enumerate}
The certificate completes \(208{,}811\) exact checks, including both
polarization signs.  Its SHA-256
hash is
\[
\texttt{EF308431ED63AC09D93B3E45C4B8915FDD851D7C32237AF192CB48DAA9A36E4F}.
\]
No randomness or floating-point comparison is used.  The
certificate verifies finite provenance algebra only; it is not
evidence for any asymptotic gate.
