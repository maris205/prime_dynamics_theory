\section{The corrected TPC-99 crosswalk}
\label{sec:native-crosswalk}

TPC-99 proves for the full branch census
\begin{align}
 \mathcal I_H(a_H,b_X)
 ={}&
 \frac{2H}{q_X-1}\|a_H\|_1\|b_X\|_1
 +\mathcal R_H,
\label{eq:correct-imported-incidence}\\
 |\mathcal R_H|
 \ll_K{}&
 \sqrt{q_X}\log q_X\,\|a_H^\circ\|_2,
\label{eq:correct-imported-remainder}
\end{align}
with \(\|b_X\|_1+\|b_X\|_2\ll_K1\)
\citep{WangTPC99}.  The exact atomization gives
\[
 a_H=a_H^{q\mid u}+a_H^{\rm inv}.
\]
The positive incidence form is linear in \(a_H\).  The first term is
returned directly by \cref{thm:opened-q-u-bound}; no centered-energy
estimate for it is needed.

\begin{theorem}[Corrected sufficient gates for the invertible
submass]
\label{thm:corrected-three-gates}
Assume the literal atomization and source inverse in
\cref{thm:lossless-atom-cells}.  After the
\(q_X\mid u_p\) return, the remaining nonconstant resonant carrier
is \(O(X^{o(1)}Q_X^2)\) if
\begin{align}
 \sum_H\frac{H}{q_X}M_H
 &\ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,
\label{eq:correct-principal-gate}\\
 \sum_H\sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}
 &\ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^{3/2},
\label{eq:correct-diagonal-gate}\\
 \sum_H\sqrt{|\mathfrak X_H^\circ|}
 &\ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^{3/2}.
\label{eq:correct-cross-gate}
\end{align}
Here every \(H\) is \(H_q(N_\beta)\), and every mass in
\(M_H,\mathfrak D_H^\circ,\mathfrak X_H^\circ\) is the opened
coefficient \eqref{eq:atom-weight}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply \eqref{eq:correct-imported-incidence} to
\(a_H^{\rm inv}\).  The principal term is controlled by
\eqref{eq:correct-principal-gate}.  From
\eqref{eq:correct-centered-energy},
\[
 \|(a_H^{\rm inv})^\circ\|_2
 \le
 \sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}
 +\sqrt{|\mathfrak X_H^\circ|}.
\]
The last two gates therefore give
\[
 \sum_H\|(a_H^{\rm inv})^\circ\|_2
 \ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^{3/2}.
\]
Since \(q_X\asymp Q_X\), the factor in
\eqref{eq:correct-imported-remainder} returns
\(O(X^{o(1)}Q_X^2)\).  Add the already controlled
\(q_X\mid u_p\) atom submass.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[No diagonal theorem is hidden]
Fine-cell injectivity identifies the diagonal term but does not
bound its atom weights.  In particular, splitting \(w_\beta\) into
\(w_{\beta,z}\) does not reduce the original point mass at
\(\omega_\beta\): all cross terms between its \(z\)-atoms reappear
in \(\mathfrak X_H^\circ\).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Sufficient positive route only]
Neither \eqref{eq:correct-principal-gate},
\eqref{eq:correct-diagonal-gate}, nor
\eqref{eq:correct-cross-gate} is proved here.  A proof-carrying
polynomial obstruction in the corresponding termwise bounds would
stop this sufficient principal-plus-absolute-remainder
implementation, not the original signed M\"obius sum, which retains
phase constants and cancellation across frequencies and branches.
\end{remark}
