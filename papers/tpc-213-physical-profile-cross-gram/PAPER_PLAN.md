# TPC-213 Paper Plan

## Research question

What is the exact operator obtained when the literal V46 profile at every
divisor is coupled to its reciprocal emitter through one common physical
sequence, before any direct-sum Cauchy estimate or outer absolute value?

## Claim ceiling

The paper proves a finite operator identity and exact finite Gram formulas.  It
does not prove an asymptotic estimate for the shifted-prime sequence, the smooth
`psi`, the prime shell, or Gate B.

## Theorem sequence

1. Define the residue lift `C_d`, the additive transform, the emitter `A_d`,
   and the pulled-back kernel `K_d`.
2. Prove the affine common-source pullback identity for the V46 residual,
   including the divisor-dependent profile correction.
3. Prove the gcd/lcm aliasing identity for `C_d C_e^*` on complete periods.
4. Prove the frequency-intersection formula for the Hermitian Gram of `K_d`
   and `K_e`.
5. Show that direct-sum physical identification is a scoped false replacement,
   while the literal asymptotic Gram bound remains open.

## Finite certificate

Use the squarefree band `d={5,7,35}`, common support `{0,...,34}`, cutoff
`z=3`, reciprocal primes `{11,13,17}`, and `H=40`.  The fixture respects
`2|m|<d` for every nonempty reciprocal row.  It checks:

- three exact Euler profile zero-axis/zero-mean rows;
- three CRT/lcm residue-lift identities;
- joint lift rank `35` with twelve codomain dependencies;
- three emitter rows and three cross-Gram pairs;
- nonzero pullback cross-Grams `560` and `770` for `5,35` and `7,35`.

## Route decision

Route A is not applicable to this analytic prime/twin-prime session.  Route B
advances structurally because the literal coupling map is now explicit.  The
direct-sum replacement is refuted in scope, but no arithmetic `L2` credit or
fixed-atom credit is earned.

## Deliverables

The deliverable is one standalone structural paper with a proof package,
producer, independent checker, route evaluation, and compiled PDF.  The next
research question is a joint pullback estimate in the literal V46 range.
