\begin{abstract}
The preceding signed-shell study treated every nonzero Gram cross term as a
unit edge.  That sign layer can identify compatible cuts, but it does not
measure the physical quadratic form.  This paper restores the exact Gram
magnitudes and studies the trace-normalized quotient over equal coefficient
signs.  We prove the diagonal-plus-cross-term identity, prove that a
common-denominator exhaustive enumeration is globally optimal modulo global
sign reversal, and certify the resulting finite atlas with exact rational
arithmetic.  On the inherited 18-row literal grid, all 18 globally optimized
quotients are below one, all 18 all-positive quotients are above one, and the
weighted optimizer differs from the unit-edge max-cut optimizer on all 18
rows.  The result is a finite weighted structural diagnostic: source-image
feasibility, growing-shell control, arithmetic $L^2$ bounds, and the
twin-prime endpoint remain open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Position on the twin-prime route}

This release stays inside the same literal prime-shell dynamical family.  The
earlier papers separated physical prime components and measured their Gram
coherence \cite{tpc287,tpc288,tpc289}.  Adaptive nonnegative weighting found a
coherence wall \cite{tpc290}; a signed pair direction was then isolated in
TPC-291 \cite{tpc291}.  TPC-292 showed that three pairwise preferences obey a
cycle-parity obstruction \cite{tpc292}, and TPC-293 lifted that sign question
to a complete signed shell graph \cite{tpc293}.  The present paper performs
the smallest missing test: put the Gram magnitudes back into the objective.

The distinction is essential.  A unit-weight max-cut label optimizes a
combinatorial surrogate.  The physical norm weights each cross term by its
actual magnitude and also carries a fixed diagonal contribution.  We compare
the two optimizers directly rather than identifying them by assumption.

\section{Physical shell}

Let $I=I_N$ be the frozen integer interval and let $\beta$ be the frozen
rational source vector.  For an odd prime $q$, height $H$, and exponent $s$,
write
\[
 K_{H,s}(h)=\frac{H^{2s}}{(H^2+h^2)^s},\qquad
 B_q(u,t)=\mathbf 1_{q\nmid u}\mathbf 1_{q\nmid t}
 \left(\mathbf 1_{u\equiv t\pmod q}-\frac1{q-1}\right).
\]
The deleted-diagonal physical component is $g_q=A_q\beta$, where
\[
 (A_q)_{u,t}=qK_{H,s}(u-t)\mathbf 1_{u\ne t}B_q(u,t).
 \label{eq:model}
\]
For a finite prime shell $S=\{q:Q<q\le 2Q\}$, define the exact Gram matrix
\[
 G_{q,r}=\ip{g_q}{g_r},\qquad
 \operatorname{tr}G=\sum_{q\in S}G_{q,q}.
 \label{eq:gram}
\]
All quantities on the declared grid are rational.

\section{Weighted sign quotient}

For an equal-coefficient sign vector $a\in\{-1,+1\}^{S}$, define
\[
 R(a)=\frac{a^{\mathsf T}Ga}{\operatorname{tr}G}.
 \label{eq:rayleigh}
\]
The diagonal part is fixed, and symmetry gives the following exact identity.

\begin{theorem}[Trace-normalized signed quadratic identity]
If $G$ is symmetric with positive trace, then
\[
 R(a)=1+\frac{2\sum_{q<r}a_q a_rG_{q,r}}
 {\operatorname{tr}G}.
 \label{eq:identity}
\]
Moreover $R(a)\ge0$ when $G$ is a Gram matrix.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expand $a^{\mathsf T}Ga$.  Since $a_q^2=1$, its diagonal contribution is
$\sum_qG_{q,q}=\operatorname{tr}G$, and symmetry combines the two copies of
each upper-triangular cross term.  This proves \eqref{eq:identity}.  If $G$
is a Gram matrix, then $a^{\mathsf T}Ga=\norm{\sum_q a_qg_q}^2\ge0$.
\end{proof}

The unit-edge objective of TPC-293 keeps only the signs of $G_{q,r}$.  In
contrast, \eqref{eq:identity} retains the weights $|G_{q,r}|$ and minimizes
their signed sum.  This is the precise layer at which the two routes can
separate.

\section{Exact finite optimization}

Let $D$ be the least common multiple of all denominators in $G$ and put
$M=DG$.  Then $M$ is an integer symmetric matrix and
\[
 R(a)=\frac{a^{\mathsf T}Ma}{\operatorname{tr}M}.
 \label{eq:integer}
\]
Global reversal $a\mapsto-a$ leaves this value unchanged.  Thus fixing one
coordinate, say $a_0=+1$, leaves exactly $2^{|S|-1}$ representatives.

\begin{theorem}[Global finite sign search]
For rational $G$ with positive trace, exhaustive enumeration of those
$2^{|S|-1}$ representatives returns the global minimum and maximum of
$R(a)$ over all sign vectors.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Multiplication by the positive $D$ preserves ordering.  Every sign vector is
either represented by exactly one vector with first coordinate $+1$ or by its
global reversal, and the quadratic form is invariant under that reversal.
The restricted enumeration therefore contains one representative of every
equivalence class, so its extrema are the unrestricted extrema. \\qed
\end{proof}

For the producer, a Gray traversal updates an integer quadratic value after a
single label flip.  If
$F_v=\sum_{j\ne v}M_{v,j}a_j$ and the old label is $a_v$, then
\[
 (a')^{\mathsf T}Ma-a^{\mathsf T}Ma=-4a_vF_v,\\qquad
 F'_u-F_u=-2a_vM_{u,v}\quad(u\ne v).
 \label{eq:gray}
\]
The independent checker uses direct reflected-binary enumeration and a
source-coordinate-first physical accumulation, so the certificate does not
depend on one traversal or one summation order.

\section{Interpretation and route boundary}

The finite result closes a genuine missing branch of the map.  The
sign-frustration atlas did not by itself say whether a favorable edge pattern
carried enough mass.  TPC-294 answers that finite question affirmatively for
the ambient equal-sign cube, while simultaneously showing that unit-edge
max-cut is not the right physical optimizer.

There is a second, equally important boundary.  The signs $a_q$ were
optimized in the ambient coefficient-sign cube; they were not required to be
images of a coefficient vector generated by the frozen source map.  The
source-native image may be much smaller, and no surjectivity or approximation
theorem is proved here.  Nor does an 18-row atlas imply a growing-shell
estimate.  Thus the result pays no fixed-power credit and leaves arithmetic
$L^2$, Gate B, and the twin-prime endpoint open.

The next natural experiment is not another unconstrained sign search.  It is
an image audit: compute the coefficient/sign patterns induced by admissible
source perturbations, compare them with the weighted minimizers, and measure
the least attainable weighted quotient.  This is recorded as the
\texttt{ROUND2\_CLUE} in the certificate.

\section{Reproducibility and conclusion}

From the project directory, run:
\begin{verbatim}
export PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1
python -B code/tpc294_magnitude_weighted_signed_rayleigh_certificate.py --check
python -B experiments/tpc294_independent_checker.py
python -B experiments/tpc294_magnitude_weighted_stress.py
\end{verbatim}
The canonical result is
\texttt{results/tpc294\_certificate.json}.  Normal and optimized invocations
are required to have empty standard error and byte-identical standard output.

The Session-named Route-A/Route-B evaluator files are absent from this
checkout; the proof package, exact certificate, independent replay, stress
test, and Bridge-B checker provide the available local fail-closed evidence.
