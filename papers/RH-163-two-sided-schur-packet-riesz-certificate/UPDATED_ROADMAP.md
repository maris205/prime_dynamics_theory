# Roadmap after RH-163

The finite rank part of `R` no longer needs the symmetric product
`M epsilon`.  Its sharper directed gate is

```text
(ambient realization X) -> a,d,b,c -> kappa=a*d*b*c < 1.
```

The next layer should balance the two couplings by a diagonal similarity.
That gives an optimized RH-161-style Neumann certificate and exposes the
conditioning price paid when the packet graph is measured back in the
original norm.
