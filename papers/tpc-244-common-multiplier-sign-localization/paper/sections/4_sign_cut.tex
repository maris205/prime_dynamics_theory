\section{Nonorthogonal reassembly as a sign cut}

The direct-sum identity identifies the principal block term.  To locate every
possible failure of phase blindness, let $J_h:\cH_h\to\cK$ be linear maps and,
for real $C_h$ and $s_h\in\{-1,1\}$, define
\[
W(s)=\sum_hs_hC_hJ_hw_h,
\qquad
B(s)=\sum_hs_hC_hJ_hb_h,
\qquad Q(s)=\ip{W(s)}{B(s)}.
\]
Put
\[
M_{hk}=\ip{J_hw_h}{J_kb_k},\qquad
D=\sum_hC_h^2M_{hh},\qquad
S_{hk}=C_hC_k(M_{hk}+M_{kh})\quad(h<k).
\]

\begin{theorem}[exact sign-cut localization]
For every sign pattern $s$,
\begin{align}
Q(s)&=D+\sum_{h<k}s_hs_kS_{hk},\label{eq:walsh}\\
Q(s)-Q(\one)&=-2\sum_{\substack{h<k\\s_h\ne s_k}}S_{hk}.\label{eq:cut}
\end{align}
Moreover, $Q$ is constant on the complete sign cube if and only if
$S_{hk}=0$ for every unordered pair.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expand $Q(s)$ over ordered pairs.  The diagonal has $s_h^2=1$.  Pairing the
two directed terms for $h<k$ yields \eqref{eq:walsh}.  Relative to the
all-positive pattern, precisely the edges joining opposite signs reverse, so
\eqref{eq:cut} follows.

If all $S_{hk}$ vanish, \eqref{eq:walsh} is constant.  Conversely, fix
$a<b$, multiply \eqref{eq:walsh} by the Walsh character $s_as_b$, and average
over all $2^{|\cA|}$ patterns.  Orthogonality of distinct sign characters gives
\[
2^{-|\cA|}\sum_sQ(s)s_as_b=S_{ab}.
\]
The left side is zero when $Q$ is constant.  Hence every $S_{ab}$ vanishes.
\end{proof}

The edge coefficients may be complex; the theorem locates sensitivity and
does not assign a real saving sign.  Vanishing of $S_{hk}$ only requires the
two directed terms to cancel in their sum.  For complex baseline multipliers,
the correct paired edge is
\[
\overline{C_h}C_kM_{hk}+\overline{C_k}C_hM_{kh},
\]
not $C_hC_k(M_{hk}+M_{kh})$.
