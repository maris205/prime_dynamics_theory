# Roadmap after Volume II

The typed assembly architecture is explicit, but its physical inputs are not
closed. The endpoint coordinate is

```text
physical_riesz_cloud_assembled_moving_noisy_trace_envelope_open
```

Admissible next inputs are:

1. a physical packet-to-Riesz contour/coupling theorem on the moving clock;
2. an actual cloud coefficient and deterministic pole-ledger identification;
3. a uniform common-space `S1` or `S2` complement limit;
4. schedule, packet-gauge, reset, and cutoff independence;
5. directed marked-trace convergence;
6. a moving noisy all-order trace envelope and no-over-extraction bridge.

Fixed orders 2--12, finite jets, local packet fits, and synthetic clouds do
not satisfy item 6. Gates A--E remain false/open.
