# RH-217: transverse shape quenching

For the coefficient map `Phi(u,eta)=(c2,c3,c4)`, the uniform Lipschitz
constant in `eta` is
`2(1-u)sqrt(4u+(1-u)^2)`, hence transverse coefficient sensitivity vanishes
linearly as `u -> 1`, while axial sensitivity remains at least four because
`dc2/du=-4`.  The observed transverse-to-axial Jacobian ratio falls from at
most `0.3752` at `sigma=0.02` to `0.2389` at the finest level.
