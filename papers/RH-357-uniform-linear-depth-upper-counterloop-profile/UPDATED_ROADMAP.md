# Roadmap after RH-357

RH-357 closes the full deterministic strict upper band.  The endpoint is
geometrically localized uniformly even when (L) is proportional to (k):
the denominator contributes (1/(1+\alpha)), while the finite-radius drift
restores (C_M^{-\alpha}) in (B_k(L)/A_k).  Thus RH-356's cancellation of
(C_M) is genuinely mesoscopic and cannot be extrapolated to linear depth.

The integer phase is now structural.  For (L=\lfloor\alpha k+c\rfloor),
(\theta_k=\{\alpha k+c\}) remains in the leading constant.  Rational
slopes produce finite periodic clusters; irrational slopes produce the full
closed phase image interval.  Any scalar selection of a linear depth must
state its phase subsequence or use the phase-safe normalization.

The endpoint α=1 with (L=k-1) exactly reproduces RH-355's complete strict
upper burden.  The endpoint α=0 remains the RH-356 boundary: bounded depth
keeps (1-x^{-L}), and deletion is valid only for (L\to\infty), (L=o(k)).

The next read-only candidate is RH-358, **terminal-lag geometric
localization**.  It should reindex the same upper band by
(q=k-1-L), quantify the terminal geometric tail for fixed or slowly growing
lag, and audit what survives when the endpoint is approached from below.  This
is a deterministic counterloop question only; it does not pre-authorize an
actual-head theorem.

For physical transfer, the first blocker is unchanged: prove the original
unnormalized same-clock leaf (D_{4k}(R)\to0), or prove a typed direct/full-trace
theorem with the correct (q/E_{\rm off}) budget.  RH-241 and RH-288 remain
open/inactive, Gates A--E remain false/open, and no statement about Riemann
zeros or RH follows.
