# Roadmap after RH-336

RH-336 identifies the exact moving scale required to suppress a frozen
projector-density parity cell:

```text
pi_sigma(J) = o((beta R)^(-2k))
             = o(sigma^kappa_proj (beta R)^(-2 eta_sigma)),
kappa_proj = log(beta R)/log(lambda).
```

This is a projector-gauge mass threshold, not the RH-325 adapted-norm
stability threshold.  The two exponents must remain separate:

```text
kappa_proj = 0.463406944517002...   projector-cell mass scale
gamma_*    = 0.3503698834605293...  sufficient Duhamel weight ceiling
```

Their order is certified exactly, not by those decimals:
`kappa_proj>gamma_*` follows from the RH-334 polynomial for `lambda`, the
bound `lambda<17/10`, and the positive rational gap
`(196/85)^2-(17/10)^3=116783/289000`.

For every fixed finite partition, some parity cell is super-target in
normalized magnitude.  The maximizing cell may change with noise, and only a
subsequence identifies a recurring fixed cell.  More importantly, this
parity-gauge conclusion does not identify the cell with the physical
boundary/sibling aggregate.  The localized raw term and the globally
subtracted alias packet can still cancel it.

The exact positive Markov similarity family proves that even all global power
traces and the full spectrum do not determine corrected singleton cells.
This closes only a finite algebraic identification route.  It does not create
two physical noisy quadratic operators or a moving physical counterexample.

The next target-scale parity--alias paper must therefore keep one exact clock
and answer one of the following without substituting finite algebra:

1. prove a rigorous physical remainder estimate aligning parity and alias on
   the same bounded phase; or
2. prove that a proposed comparator uses a different exponential clock and
   therefore cannot test common-phase cancellation; or
3. produce a genuine physical signed obstruction in the corrected RH-334
   observation data type.

Until then, physical `Delta_B+Delta_S` cancellation is `NOT_TESTABLE`.  A
large parity gauge cell alone is not a full-trace obstruction, and failure of
one local allocation does not exclude signed cancellation across raw, local,
parity, alias, and far terms.

No result closes moving-order replacement, the far remainder, off-alias
background, noisy head/counterloop gluing, or any Gate A--E condition.
