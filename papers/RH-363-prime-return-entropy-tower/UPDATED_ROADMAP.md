# Roadmap after RH-363

RH-363 adds a second theorem edge to the independent arithmetic-dynamical
branch opened by RH-362.  It leaves the four-volume foundation and the
physical route unchanged.

The new structural lesson is exact.  Encoding the return ranks in the
pairwise-coprime moduli `p^(m r_p)` produces a tower whose periodic-orbit
zeta functions all collapse to `(1-z)^-1`.  A single Artin--Mazur zeta is
therefore blind to every rank.  In contrast, the normalized entropy samples

```text
E_m(P) = Z_P(m)^(-1)
```

at all positive integers, and the complete sequence recovers every rank by
multiples-Mobius inversion followed by moment peeling.

This result supports three genuine continuations.

1. **Natural bouquet-height route.** Combine a primitive-divisor coefficient
   anchor with a height-controlled positive analytic radius and an all-order
   trace envelope for the unweighted return-cycle bouquet.  The source lock
   must distinguish a proved height theorem from finite return-rank data.
2. **Weighted-Hénon prime-lift route.** For the exact four-state survivor
   matrix, determine the Schatten/Fredholm region of the common-clock prime
   lift and audit the first arithmetic trace defect.  The locked finite-state
   ledger starts

   ```text
   (F_1,F_2,F_3) = (1,1,4).
   ```

   Thus primes and prime squares match after the unique scalar normalization,
   while prime cubes carry excess weight.  This is a candidate theorem and
   scoped obstruction, not a von Mangoldt bridge.
3. **Finite entropy-data route.** Determine whether a finite initial segment
   `E_1,...,E_M`, together with arithmetic rank bounds, gives certified
   recovery or exclusion of a finite prime/rank prefix.  Any result must be
   quantitative and cannot promote numerical conditioning to exact recovery.
4. **Intrinsic pressure-operator route.** Seek one source-backed transfer
   operator or groupoid object whose pressure data generate the entropy
   tower without inserting every modulus by hand.  The object must retain
   prime labels and clocks and must state its trace ideal and domain.
5. **New-candidate breadth route.** Continue breadth-first scans of the
   cyclic-Ulam, weighted-Henon, Mobius-correlation, and global-dynamics
   repositories.  Evaluate every candidate separately by Route A
   (standalone theorem value) and Route B (RH data-type compatibility and
   first fatal mismatch).

The shortest Route B blocker is unchanged: topological entropy samples are
not von Mangoldt-weighted signed prime-power traces of a canonical physical
operator.  The exact periodic zeta collapse strengthens this boundary rather
than crossing it.

Do not infer analytic continuation of `Z_P` from its positive-integer values,
do not call the admissible shifts finite-field Hasse--Weil objects, and do not
promote finite approximants to an all-prime distribution theorem.  Gates
A--E remain false/open and the physical coordinate remains

