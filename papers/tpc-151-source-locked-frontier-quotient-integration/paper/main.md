## Abstract

TPC-143–150 pursue the two openings left by the MVP4 audit. The structural branch asks what must be added after the complete cut archive in order to define determinant and zero-mode quotients, physical grouping, and the prescribed-shift selector. The arithmetic branch replaces a Boolean squarefree expansion by an exact quotient lift and reaches a genuine fixed-two Möbius periodic core in a small-polylogarithmic almost-scale corridor.

We integrate both branches without escalating either result. The common structural object is a conservative row-separated matrix $$L_X:\mathbb C^{\mathcal C_X^{\rm ns}}
 \longrightarrow \mathbb C^{\mathcal O_X},
 \qquad {\bf 1}^{T}L_X={\bf 1}^{T},$$ defined on every eligible-tail and frontier cut path. This occurrence lift is absent. The four downstream maps are consequently untestable on the actual carrier, although their abstract kernel/fiber and commuting-square criteria are proved.

On the arithmetic side, the exact identities $$t=aD(z),\qquad t+2=sV(z),\qquad
 \mu(D(z))\mu(V(z))=G_a(t)G_s(t+2)$$ place a bounded periodic core in the quantitative affine corridor. This is real arithmetic progress, classified as $\mathrm{L1}_{\rm actual\mbox{-}core}$. It is not positive $\mathrm{L2}$: physical weights, generic phases, all-prefix selection and the complete four-sign return remain missing, and its fixed-$X$-power exponent is zero.

A canonical cross-platform source manifest, a typed proof DAG, a minimal-missing-antichain algorithm, a role-based H9 firewall and separate arithmetic, physical and full-synthesis endpoint ledgers freeze the conclusion. The first missing node is $\mathsf{H1.frontier\_occurrence\_lift}$; no $1/400$ endpoint, hard-packet estimate or prime-pair theorem is obtained.

# The occurrence lift and four-map contract

TPC-143 distinguishes the cut selector from every downstream selector and proves $$\mathsf{H1.cut\_shift\_selector}=\textnormal{\textsc{proved}}_{\mathrm{L1}},
 \qquad P_{h_0}^{\rm cut}=I$$ `\citep{WangTPC143}`. It also proves a field-descent criterion: a downstream label descends through the current forgetting projection if and only if it is constant on every projection fiber. Legal completions with the same cut record and different determinant, zero-mode, grouping or shift-lineage labels show that the four labels do not descend within the maximal formal completion class constrained only by the currently archived fields and identities. This is a current-schema route obstruction, not a theorem that the unknown actual-support carrier admits two such completions or that an augmented construction is impossible.

> **Definition: Occurrence lift** An occurrence lift is a sparse row-separated matrix $$L_X:\mathbb C^{\mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{ns}}}\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{\mathcal O_X}.$$ One cut column may have several occurrence rows. Each nonzero edge records exact source, target, occurrence, parent, stage and local-edge identifiers, its multiplier AST, canonical-parent lineage, native lineage, source and target shift tags, normalization, support status, mask and boundary data. It is conservative when $${\bf 1}^{T}L_X={\bf 1}^{T}.$$

The one-to-many nature is essential: a single canonical parent may have several computational or physical occurrences. Conversely, every target occurrence must have a unique recorded canonical-parent lineage. Formal-envelope, canonical-parent and actual-active support statuses are different namespaces.

TPC-144 gives abstract determinant and zero-mode quotient interfaces `\citep{WangTPC144}`. For surjective completed maps $Q_D,Q_Z$, a unique isomorphism $J$ with $JQ_D=Q_Z$ exists exactly when $$\ker Q_D=\ker Q_Z.$$ Literal fiber relabeling additionally requires equality of the occurrence equivalence relations and exact edge multipliers. Equality of one aggregated scalar is insufficient.

TPC-145 gives the occurrence-resolved prescribed-shift square `\citep{WangTPC145}`. On an aggregated matrix $G$, $$P_{\rm phys}G=GP_{\rm src}$$ may vanish by cancellation between two leaking edges. On the row-separated lift, commutation is equivalent to edgewise preservation of the predicate “shift equals $h_0$.” Thus $P_{h_0}^{\rm cut}=I$ cannot be substituted for a downstream selector.

The old phrase “the endpoint” hides two different tasks. We record $$\begin{array}{ll}
\text{arithmetic target:}&
 \sigma_{\rm raw}\ge 1/400,\\[2mm]
\text{physical loss target:}&
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400.
\end{array}$$ The first may remain open at the arithmetic frontier; the second must be certified from a complete H9 registry independently of H2–H5. Full synthesis additionally requires strictly positive net slack.

> **Proposition: Directional endpoint calculus** For a complete physical route cell, a theorem-backed upper certificate $U<1/400$ passes the physical-loss endpoint. A theorem-backed lower or exact dual certificate $L\ge1/400$ stops that cell. An upper certificate $U\ge1/400$ without such a lower certificate proves neither pass nor stop. Unknown costs are not zero, energy exponents are halved exactly once, joint tokens replace exact disjoint covers, and determinant reserve is not a physical token.

The actual-core logarithmic saving is stored separately with fixed-$X$-power exponent $0$. It cannot be subtracted from $\Lambda_{\rm phys}$ or used to pass either $1/400$ contract.
