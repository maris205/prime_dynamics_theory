\begin{abstract}
The positive TPC resonance route takes absolute values before the
low-window reconstruction filter.  This is safe, but TPC-106 shows
that distinct affine maps can remain perfectly aligned.  We return
to the exact TPC-93 expansion and move the filter ahead of
majorization.

For the completely opened, frequency-free literal atoms \(p\), let
\(a_p\) retain the source coefficient, both M\"obius signs, the
extracted \(\mu(B)\), all masks and weights, and the one physical
normalization.  Let \(\psi_p(r)\) be the exact unit phase,
\(\omega_p\) the native multiplier, and \(H_p\) the inherited
resonance width.  With the signed reconstruction multiplier
\(\mathfrak m_X(r)\), the resonant contribution is exactly
\[
 \mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}
 =
 \sum_r\mathfrak m_X(r)
 \sum_p a_p\psi_p(r)
 \one_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p).
\]
Finite reordering gives the filter-first identity
\[
 \mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}
 =
 \sum_pa_p\mathcal K_p,\qquad
 \mathcal K_p
 =
 \sum_r\mathfrak m_X(r)\psi_p(r)
 \one_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p).
\]
Consequently the filtered-atom majorant
\(\sum_p|a_p||\mathcal K_p|\) is always no larger than the
pre-filter positive carrier.

We also identify the canonical unfiltered signed frequency energy
\[
 \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}
 =
 \sum_r\left|
 \sum_pa_p\psi_p(r)\one_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p)
 \right|^2
\]
and prove its exact positive-semidefinite \(TT^*\) Gram formula.
Thus
\[
 |\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}|
 \le\|\mathfrak m_X\|_2
 \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}^{1/2}.
\]
Two sharp examples delimit the result.  Signed atoms can cancel
completely while the positive carrier is nonzero; conversely, a
phase gauge can saturate the positive carrier.  Hence filtering
before majorization preserves a real cancellation channel but does
not manufacture cancellation.  The reconstruction and inequalities
are \(\Lzero\), and their imported literal dictionary is \(\Lone\).
The growing fixed-\(h_0\) bound for
\(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}\) is a new \(\Ltwo\) arithmetic gate
and is not proved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why the order of operations matters}

TPC-93 exports the complete low window into fixed-determinant affine
fibers while retaining the two M\"obius signs, content extraction,
both polarizations, exact phases, and provenance
\citep{WangTPC93}.  TPC-99--TPC-102 then construct a positive
resonance carrier by taking absolute values and derive exact
finite-group bounds for it \citep{WangTPC99,WangTPC102}.  This route
is sufficient, not equivalent to the signed low window.

TPC-105 removes identical-map duplication, while TPC-106 proves
that pairwise distinct maps can still align perfectly
\citep{WangTPC105,WangTPC106}.  The prescribed fallback in
TPC-MVP1 is therefore
\[
 \text{signed filter}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{arithmetic estimate}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{majorization},
\]
with no early deletion of phases or M\"obius signs
\citep{WangTPCMVP1}.  The purpose of this paper is to make that
fallback exact.

\section{The frequency-free literal atom dictionary}

Let \(q=q_X\) and \(G=\F_q^\times\).  Extend every frequency weight
by zero outside the actual low window.  Let \(\mathcal P_X\) be the
finite set of fully opened, frequency-free atoms in the unresolved
\(q_X\)-invertible nonconstant carrier with inherited branch length
\(1\le N_p\le q_X\).  Branches with \(N_p>q_X\) have no resonant
nonzero frequency and remain in the separate generic return.  The
conductor-one and
\(q_X\mid u\) pieces may be absent only because their disjoint exact
returns were already recorded in TPC-98 and TPC-100
\citep{WangTPC102}.  An atom
\(p\in\mathcal P_X\) retains:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the source row, polarization, opposite row, interval
component, content \(c\), projector \(\kappa\), extracted factor
\(B\), and resolved point \(z\);
\item the two reduced affine values
\[
 D_p=d_p+s_pz,\qquad V_p=u_p+a_p'z,
 \qquad s_pu_p-a_p'd_p=h_0\ne0;
\]
\item the factors
\(\mu(\kappa)\mu(B)\mu(D_p)\mu(V_p)\),
\(\one_{(B,V_p)=1}\), every inherited mask, and the exact physical
weight;
\item the native multiplier
\(\omega_p\in G\), inherited width \(H_p\), and every
\(r\)-dependent unit phase.
\end{itemize}

To avoid a collision of notation, write \(\alpha_p\in\C\) for the
complete \(r\)-independent signed coefficient of the atom, including
one copy of the global normalization.  Write
\(\psi_p:G\to\C\) for its exact phase, with
\(|\psi_p(r)|\in\{0,1\}\); a zero records an inactive source
condition.  In the TPC-93 notation, the additive part of this phase
contains
\[
 e_{cq_X}(-r\varepsilon_\theta\Omega_pz),
\]
and the remaining \(r\)-dependent source phase is retained rather
than set to one.

Equivalently, the allowed atom set and factor split may be recorded
as
\[
 \mathcal P_X=
 \left\{(\theta,c,\kappa,z):
 \begin{array}{l}
 z\in I_{\theta,c,\kappa}^{\ast},\
 1\le N_{\theta,c,\kappa}\le q_X,\
 q_X\nmid u_p,\\
 \omega_p\in G,\ \text{and every inherited literal mask is active}
 \end{array}\right\},
\]
and
\begin{align*}
 \alpha_p
 &=
 \mathcal N_Xc_{\theta,X}
 \mu(\kappa_p)\mu(B_p)\mu(D_p)\mu(V_p)
 \one_{(B_p,V_p)=1}\mathcal W_p^{(0)},\\
 \psi_p(r)
 &=
 \zeta_{p,r}
 e_{c_pq_X}(-r\varepsilon_\theta\Omega_pz)
 \mathcal W_p^{(r)}.
\end{align*}
Here \(\mathcal N_X\) is the single inherited global normalization,
\(\mathcal W_p^{(0)}\) is the product of all remaining
\(r\)-independent literal weights and masks, and
\(\mathcal W_p^{(r)}\) is the product of the remaining
\(r\)-dependent unit phases or zero source masks.  This is a
partition of the TPC-93 master coefficient, not a new
renormalization.

Let
\[
 \rho_p(r)
 =
 \one_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p),
\qquad
 \mathfrak m_X(r)=\mathfrak m_{K,X}(r)
 \one_{\{0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R_X\}}.
\]
The filter is signed and complex; the previously used positive
weight is \(|\mathfrak m_X(r)|\).

\begin{theorem}[Exact signed resonant reconstruction]
\label{thm:literal-reconstruction}
Under the TPC-93 source-to-child inverse and exact-content
crosswalk, the complete signed resonant contribution is
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}
 =
 \sum_{r\in G}\mathfrak m_X(r)
 \sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}
 \alpha_p\psi_p(r)\rho_p(r).}
\label{eq:signed-frequency-form}
\end{equation}
Every literal opened atom occurs once.  No polarization, content
factor, phase, mask, or fixed-\(h_0\) determinant is changed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Start with the finite TPC-93 sector master formula, split the
complete outer coefficient into the signed reconstruction
multiplier and the remaining source phase, and open the resolved
fiber point \(z\).  The source-to-child inverse makes
\((\theta,c,\kappa,z)\) a frequency-free atom, while the resonance
test supplies \(\rho_p(r)\).  Reindexing finite sums gives
\eqref{eq:signed-frequency-form}.  The exact content extraction is
the step that retains determinant \(h_0\) and the factor
\(\mu(B)\one_{(B,V_p)=1}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope]
\Cref{thm:literal-reconstruction} imports the previously proved
literal dictionary.  It is not a new estimate for the inner
M\"obius sum.
\end{remark}

\section{Filter first, majorize second}

\begin{definition}[Atomwise filtered kernel]
\label{def:atom-kernel}
For \(p\in\mathcal P_X\), put
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal K_p
 =
 \sum_{r\in G}
 \mathfrak m_X(r)\psi_p(r)\rho_p(r).
\label{eq:atom-kernel}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Filter-before-majorant identity and domination]
\label{thm:filter-first}
One has
\begin{align}
 \mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}
 &=
 \sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}\alpha_p\mathcal K_p,
\label{eq:filter-first-identity}\\
 |\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}|
 &\le
 \underbrace{\sum_p|\alpha_p||\mathcal K_p|}
 _{\mathcal C_{\mathrm{filt},X}}
 \le
 \underbrace{\sum_p|\alpha_p|
 \sum_r|\mathfrak m_X(r)|\rho_p(r)}
 _{\mathcal C_{\mathrm{preabs},X}}.
\label{eq:majorant-chain}
\end{align}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Interchange the two finite sums in
\eqref{eq:signed-frequency-form}.  The first inequality is the
triangle inequality after filtering.  For the second, apply the
triangle inequality inside \eqref{eq:atom-kernel} and use
\(|\psi_p(r)|\le1\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Relation to the positive census]
After the literal fiber mass is opened into the atoms \(p\), the
last term of \eqref{eq:majorant-chain} is the corresponding
pre-filter positive carrier.  If one first replaces the signed
fiber by a uniform absolute mass, it may be still larger.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[The separation can be complete]
\label{prop:complete-separation}
There exist two signed atoms with identical
\((\psi,\rho)\) and coefficients \(\alpha_1=1\),
\(\alpha_2=-1\) for which
\[
 \mathcal S_{\mathrm{res}}=0,\qquad
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{filt}}=2|\mathcal K_1|,
\qquad
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{preabs}}
 =2\sum_r|\mathfrak m(r)|\rho_1(r).
\]
If \(\mathcal K_1\ne0\), both positive quantities are nonzero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The two atom contributions cancel at every frequency before the
filter is applied.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[No automatic phase saving]
\label{prop:gauge-saturation}
Given any nonzero filter \(\mathfrak m\) and nonempty resonance
support \(\rho\), there is a unit phase \(\psi\) on that support such
that
\[
 \left|
 \sum_r\mathfrak m(r)\psi(r)\rho(r)
 \right|
 =
 \sum_r|\mathfrak m(r)|\rho(r).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On points where \(\mathfrak m(r)\ne0\), choose
\(\psi(r)=\overline{\mathfrak m(r)}/|\mathfrak m(r)|\); choose any
unit value elsewhere.
\end{proof}

The last proposition is a coefficient-blind sharpness statement.
The literal phases are not freely chosen, but no theorem may infer a
saving merely from their unit modulus.

\section{\texorpdfstring{\hspace{0.35em}}{}The canonical signed energy and exact
\texorpdfstring{\(TT^*\)}{TT-star}}

Define the unfiltered signed frequency profile
\begin{equation}
 F_X(r)
 =
 \sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}
 \alpha_p\psi_p(r)\rho_p(r)
\label{eq:signed-profile}
\end{equation}
and its energy
\begin{equation}
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}
=
\sum_{r\in G}|F_X(r)|^2.
\label{eq:signed-energy}
\end{equation}
This is canonical for the full finite frequency group, not an
extremal minimum: restricting the sum to
\(\operatorname{supp}\mathfrak m_X\) can only decrease it and gives
another valid sufficient bound.

\begin{theorem}[Exact signed Gram identity]
\label{thm:signed-gram}
Let
\begin{equation}
 \Gamma_{p,p'}
 =
 \sum_{r\in G}
 \psi_p(r)\overline{\psi_{p'}(r)}
 \rho_p(r)\rho_{p'}(r).
\label{eq:gram-kernel}
\end{equation}
Then \(\Gamma\) is positive semidefinite and
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}
 =
 \sum_{p,p'}
 \alpha_p\overline{\alpha_{p'}}
 \Gamma_{p,p'}.}
\label{eq:signed-ttstar}
\end{equation}
Moreover,
\begin{equation}
 |\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}|
 \le
 \|\mathfrak m_X\|_2
 \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}^{1/2}.
\label{eq:energy-bound}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expand \eqref{eq:signed-energy} using
\eqref{eq:signed-profile} to obtain
\eqref{eq:signed-ttstar}.  The matrix is the Gram matrix of the
vectors \(r\mapsto\psi_p(r)\rho_p(r)\).  Finally,
\(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}=\langle\overline{\mathfrak m_X},
F_X\rangle\) up to the chosen inner-product convention, so
Cauchy--Schwarz gives \eqref{eq:energy-bound}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A precise signed replacement gate]
\label{cor:signed-gate}
Since the inherited low-window filter satisfies
\(\|\mathfrak m_X\|_2\ll_K1\), the bound
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}^{1/2}
 \ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^2
\label{eq:signed-gate}
\end{equation}
is sufficient to return the signed resonant contribution at the
native TPC-102 scale.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Why this remains arithmetic]
The off-diagonal entries of \eqref{eq:signed-ttstar} retain products
of the actual M\"obius signs, source phases, and masks.  Bounding
\eqref{eq:signed-gate} is therefore not finite spectral algebra.  It
is a growing fixed-\(h_0\) arithmetic estimate.
\end{remark}

\section{What information survives}

The signed profile in \eqref{eq:signed-profile} preserves
simultaneously:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the signed reconstruction multiplier;
\item the \(r\)-dependent source and determinant phases;
\item both M\"obius signs and the extracted \(\mu(B)\);
\item the coprimality mask \(\one_{(B,V)=1}\);
\item the actual moving affine row and fixed determinant \(h_0\);
\item both polarizations and all outer keys; and
\item the one physical post-aggregation normalization.
\end{enumerate}
Replacing any item by an independent model variable changes the
Gram matrix.  Conversely, identical signed atoms may be combined
before absolute values provided their complete provenance remains
recoverable.

\par\medskip
\begin{proposition}[Positive failure does not imply signed failure]
\label{prop:route-logic}
A polynomial obstruction in the principal or distinct-map term of a
positive carrier does not yield a lower bound for
\(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}\) or
\(|\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}|\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\Cref{prop:complete-separation} gives an exact finite example with a
nonzero positive carrier and zero signed profile.  Therefore no
implication exists without additional literal information.
\end{proof}

\section{Claim boundary and route verdict}

\subsection*{Proved}

The exact literal signed resonant sum can be filtered before
majorization.  The resulting atomwise majorant never exceeds the
corresponding pre-filter positive carrier.  The canonical unfiltered
signed frequency energy has the positive-semidefinite \(TT^*\) identity
\eqref{eq:signed-ttstar}, and
\eqref{eq:signed-gate} is a sufficient replacement gate.  Sharp
finite examples show both complete improvement and complete
saturation.  These are \(\Lzero\) statements; the imported
source-to-atom crosswalk is \(\Lone\).

\subsection*{Not proved}

This paper does not prove:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \eqref{eq:signed-gate} for the growing physical coefficient;
\item a M\"obius or Liouville cancellation theorem on the actual
fixed-\(h_0\) carrier;
\item that the literal phases realize either sharp finite example;
\item closure of the generic affine sector, high-frequency return,
full-block reassembly, or the strict physical endpoint; or
\item a parity breakthrough, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime
theorem.
\end{itemize}
Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).

\subsection*{Verdict}

TPC-107 opens the signed fallback corridor exactly, but it does not
walk through the parity-facing door.  Its gain is architectural:
cancellation discarded by the positive route is now represented by
one explicit Gram energy.  TPC-108 must determine what arithmetic
input could control the analogous literal generic-affine
M\"obius sums without disguising an unproved Chowla-type estimate.
