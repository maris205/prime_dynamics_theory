# Claim Firewall

Maximum supported claim:

```text
PROVED_STRUCTURAL_L1_LITERAL_V59_DECLARED_BLOCK_LONGITUDINAL_TRANSVERSE_MARGIN_COMPILER
```

Mandatory firewall:

```text
TPC251_HARD_PARTITION=MODELING_CHOICE_EXHAUSTIVE_NONEMPTY
TPC251_BLOCK_FLAT_DIRECTION=MODELING_CHOICE_RELATIVE_TO_DECLARED_BLOCK
TPC251_TPC243_EXTERNAL_ERROR=CONDITIONAL_INPUT_NOT_AUTOMATIC
TPC251_ACTUAL_V59_PROJECTED_COHERENCE_ASYMPTOTIC=OPEN
TPC251_PAYABLE_LONGITUDINAL_DOMINANCE=OPEN
TPC251_ARITHMETIC_ADVANCE=NO
TPC251_FIXED_ATOM_CREDIT=0
TPC251_L2=NONE
TPC251_FULL_GATE_B=OPEN
TPC251_FULL_GATE_B_STRICT_1_OVER_400=UNPAID_GLOBAL
TPC251_TWIN_PRIME_RESULT=NONE
```

Allowed claims are the finite literal source-operator decomposition, the
declared-block orthogonal projection, projected Gram subtraction, coherence
upper radius, and conditional external margin.  Forbidden claims include a
nonexhaustive or empty block, a V59-canonical direction, identity with the
TPC-219 object, unprojected coherence, an automatic TPC-243 error, equality
implying nonzero, a fixed-data disk image, arithmetic promotion, Route A
progress, Gate-B closure, or a twin-prime result.
