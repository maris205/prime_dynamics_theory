# Claim firewall

Allowed: exact weighted vector contraction, Gram support radius, independent
and global ball images, explicit reverse realization, declared affine
translation, exact zero/margin calculus, triangle comparison, and equality
classification.

Forbidden: presenting affine balls as source-forced; estimating actual V59
Gram forms; claiming primitive-frequency attachment, arithmetic `L2`,
fixed-atom credit, strict `1/400`, full Gate B, or twin primes.

Maximum claim:
`PROVED_STRUCTURAL_L1_SHARP_WEIGHTED_SHARED_LANE_CONTRACTION`.
