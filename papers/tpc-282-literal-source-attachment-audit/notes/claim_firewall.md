# TPC-282 claim firewall

The source, shell, cutoff schedule, block projection, and comparison-weight
intervals are inherited from the hash-locked TPC-275/TPC-268 finite model.
`NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED_FINITE` means that the executable certificate and its
independent replay agree on the declared rows.  It does not mean a theorem
uniform in `X`.

The sign change is reported, not repaired.  The smallest normalized attachment
is a diagnostic for the next stability paper; it is not converted into an
exponent.  `FIXED_POWER_CREDIT` remains zero and `FULL_GATE_B` remains open.
