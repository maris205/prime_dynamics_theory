# TPC-282 paper plan

## Question

Does the actual V59 comparison-weight readout attach nontrivially to the
projected packet output used by the TPC-275 signed reassembly?

## Claim-driven contributions

1. Define the physical projected source functional and its scalar attachment.
2. State the exact finite source-lock protocol and its interval semantics.
3. Certify the twelve registered rows, including signs and the normalized
   attachment coefficient.
4. Use the weakest row to identify the precise missing asymptotic theorem.

## Non-claims

The paper does not prove a uniform source lower bound, a literal arithmetic
`L2` estimate, a fixed-power gain, full Gate B, or the twin-prime conjecture.
