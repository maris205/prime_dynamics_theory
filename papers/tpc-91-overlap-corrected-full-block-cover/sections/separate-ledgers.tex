\section{Separate feasibility, determinant, and physical ledgers}
\label{sec:separate-ledgers}

Several losses can occur near the same endpoint, but they measure
different objects.  We record them separately to prevent an incidence
gain from being counted as arithmetic cancellation.

\subsection{The cover ledger}

The exact feasibility entry is binary:
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{w_X\in B_X\mathscr C_X.}
 \label{eq:cover-ledger}
\end{equation}
When \(B_X\mathscr C_X\) is closed, this is equivalent to
\(\eps_{\cov}(B_X,w_X;\mathscr C_X)=0\).  The original asymptotic
TPC-18 representation also admits a distinct route in which the
prescribed coefficient leaves \(r_X\ne0\) but a new theorem proves
\(\mathcal T_X(r_X)=o(X)\).
When it holds, the quantitative reassembly cost is the exact product
\[
 \sum_j|c_{j,X}||E_{j,X}|,
\]
minimized over allowed exact covers if there is a choice.  If
\(\|c_X\|_1=X^{\lambda_{\cov}+o(1)}\) with
\(\lambda_{\cov}>0\), that amplification is a physical-interface loss
only when it is not already present in the block error.  It is never a
zero-mode saving.

\subsection{The determinant/zero-mode ledger}

On a separate conditional post-bin branch, suppose
\[
 S_X=X^{o(1)}Q_X,\qquad
 |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,\qquad
 D_X\ge
 X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2.
\]
These are hypotheses about a literal coefficient, not conclusions of
the incidence model.  Its principal angle
\[
 \rho_X=\frac{|Z_X|}{\sqrt{S_XD_X}}
\]
then satisfies
\[
 \rho_X
 \le
 \frac{X^{\lambda_D/2-\eta_Z+o(1)}}{J_X}.
\]
Thus the recorded estimates certify the critical angle scale under
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z.}
 \label{eq:determinant-ledger}
\end{equation}
This is the inherited determinant/zero-mode compatibility rule
\citep{WangTPC89}.  Solving \(B_Xc_X=w_X\) neither improves
\(\lambda_D\) nor proves \(\eta_Z>0\).

\subsection{The strict physical-loss ledger}

Let \(\Lambda_{\phys}\) be the sum of fixed-power losses actually
incurred by the full physical reduction and downstream comparison,
excluding every loss already represented in
\(\lambda_D-2\eta_Z\).  The route remains viable only under the strict
rule
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\Lambda_{\phys}<\frac1{400}.}
 \label{eq:physical-loss-ledger}
\end{equation}
Equality or excess is a stop condition.  If a polynomial
exact-cover amplification is newly incurred, it is charged here once.
It may not also be inserted into \(\lambda_D\) or subtracted from
\(\eta_Z\).

\begin{theorem}[No automatic conversion among ledgers]
\label{thm:no-ledger-conversion}
None of the following implications follows from the results of this
paper:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item \(w_X\in B_X\mathscr C_X
  \Longrightarrow\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\);
\item \(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z
  \Longrightarrow w_X\in B_X\mathscr C_X\);
\item bounded overlap
  \(\Longrightarrow\Lambda_{\phys}=0\);
\item a small Euclidean cover residual
  \(\Longrightarrow\) a saving in \(\eta_Z\);
\item determinant-compatible column metadata
  \(\Longrightarrow\) the TPC-18 generic determinant estimate.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The cover ledger concerns membership of one target vector in an
allowed linear image.  The determinant ledger compares a zero mode
with an energy of a literal post-bin coefficient.  The physical
ledger accumulates losses in arithmetic reassembly, and the TPC-18
generic estimate bounds a fixed-shift correlation inside each
compatible column.  The counterexamples in
\cref{sec:counterexamples} already separate support overlap from
linear feasibility, while \cref{prop:residual-remainder} separates
Euclidean residual size from a physical statistic.  No theorem
identifies the remaining quantities, so no listed conversion follows.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[TPC-18 gate independence]
The all-block representation in the first row is still its own gate.
The present paper makes the exact zero-remainder route falsifiable by
an exact residual or Farkas vector, but does not declare the original
asymptotic gate closed: a separate controlled-residual route may
remain.  Even if the representation gate were closed, the last row
would remain open.
\end{remark}
