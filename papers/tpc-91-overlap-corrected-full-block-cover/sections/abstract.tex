\begin{abstract}
The all-block cover hypothesis in TPC-18 is a physical asymptotic
representation with a controlled \(o(X)\) remainder, not a
consequence of bounded overlap.  We formulate a stronger exact
sufficient certificate on the literal finite atom space.  The columns
of a weighted incidence matrix \(B\) are the exact candidate block
coefficients, and the zero-remainder route is the constrained
synthesis equation \(Bc=w\), where \(w\) is the physical target
coefficient.  For
unrestricted coefficients, the orthogonal residual
\((I-BB^\dagger)w\) is the unique least-squares residual and, when
nonzero, yields a sharp obstruction through an exact dual variational
formula.  For nonnegative coefficients, the
corresponding cone projection gives both a quantitative residual and
a Farkas certificate.  More general convex coefficient rules whose
attainable images are closed have an analogous support-function dual.

Two explicit families show why overlap bookkeeping alone is
insufficient.  Even a partition of all atoms into disjoint two-point
blocks can stay a fixed relative distance \(1/\sqrt{10}\) from the
target, and a two-column system may admit a signed exact cover while
having nonnegative distance \(1/\sqrt2\).  We also prove that a cover
after collapsing native keys need not lift to a literal cover.
Least-squares conditioning, residual stability, and the exact
coefficient-amplification cost are recorded.  Most importantly, only
an exact literal identity transports the original global
normalization without an additional remainder theorem.

Applied to TPC-18, the framework is an \(\mathrm{L1}\) certificate
schema for the exact zero-remainder route through the still-unproved
all-block representation gate.  It neither constructs that exact
certificate nor controls a nonzero physical remainder, and it does not
prove the primitive determinant-dispersion estimate.
The determinant/zero-mode condition
\(\lambda_D\le 2\eta_Z\), the strict physical-loss rule
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400\), and the TPC-18 all-block
representation gate remain independent.  No \(\mathrm{L2}\)
fixed-shift arithmetic estimate,
parity breakthrough, or prime-pair conclusion is claimed.
\end{abstract}
