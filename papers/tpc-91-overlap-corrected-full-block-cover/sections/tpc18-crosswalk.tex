\section{An exact sufficient certificate for the TPC-18 cover gate}
\label{sec:tpc18-crosswalk}

TPC-18 proves a stopping dichotomy inside an admissible symmetric tail
block and states a conditional global implication under two additional
inputs \citep{WangTPC18}: a primitive generic determinant-dispersion
estimate and an all-block representation of the upstream hard packet
up to \(o(X)\) in the physical statistic.  The present section gives
an exact zero-remainder certificate for the second input and records
the distinct controlled-residual route.

\subsection{The physical incidence system}

For a fixed nonzero \(h_0\), let \(w_X\) be the literal atomic
coefficient of the hard packet inherited from the upstream normal
form.  Construct \(\Omega_X\) from the union of its active atoms and
the atoms of every proposed TPC-18 block.  Let \(b_{j,X}\) be the
literal coefficient of the \(j\)-th candidate block, including both
the prefix and complementary-tail pieces, and form
\[
 B_X=(b_{1,X}\ \cdots\ b_{m_X,X}).
\]
The upstream derivation also determines an allowed coefficient set
\(\mathscr C_X\).  It may be a singleton, a partition-of-unity
polytope, or another explicitly derived set.

\begin{definition}[Exact TPC-18 full-block certificate]
\label{def:tpc18-certificate}
An exact full-block cover certificate at scale \(X\) consists of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the complete literal row list \(\Omega_X\), with native keys and
  zero target coefficients retained on every leakage row;
\item the exact weighted incidence matrix \(B_X\);
\item an allowed coefficient \(c_X\in\mathscr C_X\) satisfying
  \[
  B_Xc_X=w_X;
  \]
\item a proof-producing exact verification of the equality, together
  with a rigorous bound for the coefficient amplification in the
  physical normalization.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[The original unit cover]
If the upstream normal form requires the unweighted sum of all
candidate blocks, then
\(\mathscr C_X=\{\one\}\), and the certificate is the single identity
\[
 B_X\one=w_X.
\]
Finding some \(c_X\in\R^{m_X}\) is not a substitute.  Allowing
different coefficients changes the decomposition unless that freedom
has been derived upstream.  Failure of the exact identity
\(B_X\one=w_X\) does not by itself refute the original TPC-18
hypothesis: the separate controlled-residual route may still prove
\(\mathcal T_X(w_X-B_X\one)=o(X)\).
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional exact-cover transfer]
\label{thm:tpc18-cover-transfer}
Assume an exact TPC-18 full-block certificate in the sense of
\cref{def:tpc18-certificate}.  Let the normalized physical functional
be \(\mathcal T_X\), and suppose each certified block has a decomposition
\[
 \mathcal T_X(b_{j,X})=M_{j,X}+E_{j,X}.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal T_X(w_X)
 =
 \sum_{j=1}^{m_X}c_{j,X}M_{j,X}
 +
 O\!\left(
 \sum_{j=1}^{m_X}|c_{j,X}||E_{j,X}|
 \right).
 \label{eq:tpc18-exact-transfer}
\end{equation}
Consequently, if the upstream calibrated main term equals
\(\sum_jc_{j,X}M_{j,X}\) and the displayed weighted error is
admissible, the all-block reassembly step is valid with no overlap
correction beyond \(B_Xc_X=w_X\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This is \cref{thm:exact-reassembly} applied on the literal TPC-18
frame.  The hypotheses about the calibrated main term and error are
stated separately because they are not consequences of incidence
feasibility.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Controlled-residual route]
\label{prop:tpc18-controlled-residual}
Let \(c_X\) be the coefficient prescribed by the upstream
decomposition, put \(r_X=w_X-B_Xc_X\), and retain the original
physical functional \(\mathcal T_X\).  Then
\[
 \mathcal T_X(w_X)
 =
 \sum_jc_{j,X}\mathcal T_X(b_{j,X})
 +
 \mathcal T_X(r_X).
\]
Thus a theorem proving \(\mathcal T_X(r_X)=o(X)\), together with the
required block estimates and the calibrated main term, can verify the
original asymptotic all-block hypothesis even when \(r_X\ne0\).
Euclidean smallness of \(r_X\) alone does not supply this theorem.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The identity is \cref{prop:residual-remainder}.  The remaining
statements distinguish an assumed physical remainder estimate from
the Euclidean incidence calculation.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A checkable failure certificate]
\label{cor:tpc18-failure-certificate}
If unrestricted synthesis is permitted and
\[
 r_X=(I-B_XB_X^\dagger)w_X\ne0,
\]
then \(r_X/\|r_X\|_2\) certifies that the proposed block family cannot
exactly cover the TPC-18 hard packet.  If nonnegative synthesis is required,
any Farkas vector from \cref{thm:farkas-cone} certifies failure of that
allowed exact cover.  Such a certificate is a rigorous negative
\(\Lone\) result for the zero-remainder route; it does not rule out a
controlled physical residual and is not an arithmetic cancellation
theorem.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{What this does not supply}

\begin{proposition}[Independent downstream inputs]
\label{prop:independent-downstream-inputs}
Even an exact, well-conditioned full-block certificate does not imply:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the TPC-18 primitive generic determinant-dispersion estimate;
\item a determinant-energy lower bound or distinguished zero-mode
  cancellation estimate for a later post-bin coefficient;
\item the prefix estimate for a block outside the published TPC-17
  geometry;
\item an admissible bound for an approximate-cover residual;
\item a fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The certificate is the coefficient identity \(B_Xc_X=w_X\).
Items (i)--(iii) are analytic estimates on the columns, item (iv) is
an estimate on a vector outside their exact synthesis, and item (v)
requires the full upstream and downstream theorem chain.  None is a
linear-algebraic consequence of column-space membership.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Present status]
\label{rem:present-tpc18-status}
This paper does not enumerate the full physical atom list of the
TPC-16/TPC-18 normal form, exhibit an allowed exact \(c_X\), or bound
the physical image of a nonzero residual.  Therefore the TPC-18
all-block representation remains an independent open gate.  The
result here is a rigorous format in which a future exact certificate,
controlled-residual theorem, or negative exact-route certificate can
be stated and checked.
\end{remark}
