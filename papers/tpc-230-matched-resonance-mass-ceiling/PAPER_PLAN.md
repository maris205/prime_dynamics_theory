# TPC-230 paper plan

## Question

Even if every matched resonance block cancels perfectly, how much global AP energy can
the first `3--7` matching remove?

## Theorem edge

Prove that global saving is at most the diagonal mass on matched vertices, with a sharp
anti-aligned extremizer, and convert the mass requirement into an edge-density toll
under row-mass comparability.

## Deliverables

1. Exact unmatched-energy floor and matched-mass ceiling.
2. Sharpness and necessary `M/D>=delta` criterion.
3. `2*kappa*E/P` comparability bound.
4. Literal aligned row-weight range `2..8`, hence `kappa<=4`.
5. Explicit `1/3200` edge-density toll for a `1/400` saving.
6. Exact finite census and adversarial controls.

## Exit condition

The theorem, certificate, PDF and bridge checker must agree while leaving asymptotic
edge density and actual V59 source concentration open.
