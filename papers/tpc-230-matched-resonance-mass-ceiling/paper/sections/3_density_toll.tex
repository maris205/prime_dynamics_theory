\section{From matched mass to edge density}

Suppose every row mass lies in $[d_{\min},d_{\max}]$ and put
$\kappa=d_{\max}/d_{\min}$.  If $P$ is the number of rows and $E$ the number of
matching edges, then
\[
 M\le2E d_{\max},\qquad D\ge P d_{\min}.
\]
Therefore
\begin{equation}
 \frac MD\le2\kappa\frac EP.
 \label{eq:density-bound}
\end{equation}

\begin{corollary}[Necessary density toll]
Under the preceding comparability hypothesis, a $\delta$-saving requires
\begin{equation}
 \frac EP\ge\frac{\delta}{2\kappa}.
 \label{eq:density-toll}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

For equal row mass, $M/D=2E/P$ exactly.  The loss in
\eqref{eq:density-bound} records only possible concentration within the declared
comparability class.
