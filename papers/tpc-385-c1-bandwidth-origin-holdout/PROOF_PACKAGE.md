# TPC-385 proof package

## Finite proposition

For the predeclared five-origin panel, the producer constructs exactly
`5 origins x 2 Q x 4 laws x 2 bandwidths x 2 normalizations = 160` rows.
The three calibration origins alone determine each pooled training scalar; the
two holdout origins do not enter it. The canonical certificate records every
row, all 32 role-separated cells, the four locked parent forecast errors, and
the exact rational anchor.

## Verification

The independent checker does not import the producer. It re-sieves the prime
shell, accumulates in reverse shell order, rebuilds the masks and training-only
pooled scalars, and compares all metrics, cells, forecasts, and exact-anchor
digests. The mutation suite attacks schema, selection, role, row, phase, and
claim-firewall fields. The local Bridge-B locks all project artifacts and runs
producer, independent replay, and stress checks in normal and optimized modes.

## What is proved and what is not

The finite protocol, coordinate separation, role split, rational anchor, and
the recorded numerical replay are the only paid claims. The four under-one-
percent all-plus forecast errors establish only a finite, declared holdout
transfer. They do not prove bandwidth monotonicity, law/origin/count
uniformity, source-valid normalization, a growing operator bound, arithmetic
(L^2), power saving, Route-B reassembly, or the twin-prime conjecture.
