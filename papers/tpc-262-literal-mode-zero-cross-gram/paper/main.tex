\begin{abstract}
TPC-261 identified a strict fixed-power gap of \(1/400\) between the current
V59 baseline and target, while TPC-260 identified a four-packet reassembly
datum.  We now freeze the literal reduced-residue signed operator at each
additive phase.  With the actual residue synthesis \(S_{q,v}\), unit mask
\(P_q\), and outer prime weight retained, the operator is
\(J_{q,v}=S_{q,v}^{*}C_qS_{q,v}-(q-2)(q-1)^{-1}P_q\).  We prove its exact
quadratic remainder identity and the associated four-phase polarization.
We also prove that aggregate packet mode zero and the nontrivial polarization
characters are distinct.  An exact finite operator-image certificate on
\(\{5,7,11,13\}\) gives identical diagonals with mode-zero energies
\(16\norm{Y}^2\) and zero.  This is a finite structural obstruction, not a
growing-shell counterexample or an estimate for the source-frozen packets.
\end{abstract}

\section{The literal reduced-residue fiber}
For an odd prime \(q\), write \(m_q=q-1\) and let
\({\bf 1}_q\in{\mathbb R}^{m_q}\) be the all-ones vector.  Define
\begin{equation}
C_q=I_{m_q}-\frac{1}{m_q}{\bf 1}_q{\bf 1}_q^{\mathsf T}.
\label{eq:C}
\end{equation}
The rows correspond to the nonzero residue classes; no ordinary residue
class is inserted.

\begin{lemma}[unit-class projection]
For every odd prime \(q\), \(C_q\) is symmetric and idempotent, is positive
semidefinite, has rank \(q-2\), and has kernel
\({\mathbb R}{\bf 1}_q\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Since \({\bf 1}_q^{\mathsf T}{\bf 1}_q=m_q\), direct multiplication gives
\(C_q^2=C_q\).  For \(v\in{\mathbb R}^{m_q}\),
\begin{equation}
v^{\mathsf T}C_qv
=\norm{v}^2-\frac{|{\bf 1}_q^{\mathsf T}v|^2}{m_q}
=\frac{1}{m_q}\sum_{r<s}(v_r-v_s)^2\geq0.
\label{eq:psd}
\end{equation}
The constant line is the kernel and its orthogonal complement is fixed, so
the rank is \(m_q-1=q-2\).
\end{proof}

For a finite set \(\Qp\) of odd primes, a packet source is
\(a=(a_q)_{q\in\Qp}\), \(a_q\in{\mathbb R}^{m_q}\).  Its zero-fiber output is
\begin{equation}
Y(a)_q=C_qa_q,\qquad
\ip{Y(a)}{Y(b)}_{\Qp}
:=\sum_{q\in\Qp}q\,\ip{C_qa_q}{C_qb_q}.
\label{eq:weighted}
\end{equation}
The prime weight is inside the inner product, so no unrecorded normalization
change is made.

\subsection{The signed remainder operator}
Let \(I\) be a finite interval and \(v\in\mathbb R\).  For a coefficient
vector \(a\in\mathbb C^I\), define the physical residue synthesis
\begin{equation}
(S_{q,v}a)_r=\sum_{\substack{n\in I\\n\equiv r\pmod q}}
a_n e(vn/H),\qquad r\in\mathbb F_q^\times.
\label{eq:synthesis}
\end{equation}
Let \(P_q\) be the diagonal projection onto \(q\)-units.  The signed
reduced-residue remainder is represented exactly by
\begin{equation}
J_{q,v}=S_{q,v}^{*}C_qS_{q,v}
-\frac{q-2}{q-1}P_q.
\label{eq:J}
\end{equation}
The first term is positive semidefinite, but \(J_{q,v}\) is a signed
Hermitian operator.  Direct expansion gives
\begin{equation}
V_q^\times(a;v)-D_q^\times(a;v)
=\ip{a}{J_{q,v}a},\quad
V_q^\times(a;v)=\norm{C_qS_{q,v}a}^2,\quad
D_q^\times(a;v)=\frac{q-2}{q-1}\norm{P_qa}^2.
\label{eq:remainder}
\end{equation}
Consequently the common-clock operator
\begin{equation}
\mathcal J_x=\int_{\mathbb R}\psi_+(v)
\sum_{q\in\mathcal Q_x}qJ_{q,v}\,dv
\label{eq:Jglobal}
\end{equation}
satisfies the exact V59 polarization identity
\begin{equation}
\mathfrak C_x=\frac14\sum_{j=0}^3\ii^j
\ip{a^{(j)}}{\mathcal J_xa^{(j)}},
\qquad a^{(j)}=\beta+\ii^jw.
\label{eq:polarizedJ}
\end{equation}
This is an exact factorization, not an estimate.

\section{Mode zero is a signed cross-Gram}
Let \(Y_0,Y_1,Y_2,Y_3\) be four vectors in \eqref{eq:weighted}.  Put
\begin{equation}
\Gamma_{jk}=\ip{Y_j}{Y_k}_{\Qp},\qquad
D=\sum_{j=0}^3\Gamma_{jj},\qquad
R=\sum_{0\leq j<k\leq3}\operatorname{Re}\Gamma_{jk}.
\label{eq:gram}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[literal cross-Gram mode-zero identity]
With
\begin{equation}
\widehat Y_k=\frac12\sum_{j=0}^3\ii^{-jk}Y_j,\qquad 0\leq k\leq3,
\label{eq:dft}
\end{equation}
one has
\begin{align}
\norm{Y_0+Y_1+Y_2+Y_3}_{\Qp}^2&=D+2R,\label{eq:modegram}\\
\sum_{k=0}^3\norm{\widehat Y_k}_{\Qp}^2&=D,\label{eq:parseval}\\
\norm{Y_0+Y_1+Y_2+Y_3}_{\Qp}^2
&=4\norm{\widehat Y_0}_{\Qp}^2.\label{eq:modezero}
\end{align}
Thus diagonal mass alone does not determine mode zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Expanding the first left side gives the diagonal terms and each off-diagonal
term with its conjugate, hence \(D+2R\).  Orthogonality of the four roots of
unity gives Parseval for \eqref{eq:dft}; setting \(k=0\) gives the last
identity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The phase-character firewall}
The packet index has its own Fourier characters.  For two vectors \(X,Y\)
write
\begin{equation}
E_j=\norm{X+\ii^jY}^2,\qquad
F_k=\frac14\sum_{j=0}^3\ii^{kj}E_j.
\label{eq:phase}
\end{equation}
With the conjugate-linear-first-slot convention, direct expansion yields
\begin{equation}
F_0=\norm{X}^2+\norm{Y}^2,\qquad
F_1=\ip{Y}{X},\qquad
F_2=0,\qquad
F_3=\ip{X}{Y}.
\label{eq:phasechars}
\end{equation}
Thus aggregate packet mode zero and the nontrivial character selected by the
V59 polarized scalar are different observables.  A theorem must name the
character it estimates; a mode-zero bound cannot be silently substituted for
the \(F_1\) or \(F_3\) coupling.

The identity is compatible with the V59 four-packet construction: whenever
the packet outputs are produced by one literal linear map, every
\(\Gamma_{jk}\) is a sum over the same primes and reduced-residue unit masks.
The theorem does not replace the smooth kernel or prove a favorable sign.

\begin{corollary}[endpoint translation]
The current V59 ledger is
\begin{equation}
E_0=\frac53=\frac{2000}{1200},\qquad
E_*=\frac{1997}{1200},\qquad E_0-E_*=\frac1{400}.
\label{eq:gap}
\end{equation}
If \(D+2R\) has effective fixed-power saving \(\sigma\) after all paid
losses, the finite-lane endpoint compiler closes when

\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
This is the exact exponent substitution into TPC-261's strict finite-lane
compiler; strictness absorbs the arbitrary \(x^\varepsilon\) loss.
\end{proof}

\section{A finite literal operator-image adversary}
Take the actual finite prime shell
\begin{equation}
\Qp=\{5,7,11,13\}.
\label{eq:shell}
\end{equation}
Let \(e\) be the first coordinate in the \(q=5\) fiber and zero elsewhere,
and let \(Y=Y(e)\).  Equation \eqref{eq:psd} gives \(Y\neq0\), and
\begin{equation}
\norm{Y}_{\Qp}^2
=5\left(\left(\frac34\right)^2+3\left(\frac14\right)^2\right)
=\frac{15}{4}.
\label{eq:probe}
\end{equation}
Define two families in the same literal operator image:
\begin{equation}
Y_j^+=Y,\qquad Y_j^-=(-1)^jY.
\label{eq:adversary}
\end{equation}
Every packet in both families has diagonal \(15/4\), and both have total
packet energy \(15\), but
\begin{equation}
\norm{\sum_jY_j^+}^2=60,\qquad
\norm{\sum_jY_j^-}^2=0.
\label{eq:endpoints}
\end{equation}
The corresponding \(R\)'s are \(45/2\) and \(-15/2\).  The difference is
entirely in the signed cross-Gram, not in the diagonal.

This is a finite structural adversary.  Its source probes are freely chosen
and are not identified with \(\beta+\ii^jw\) on the growing V59 interval.
It refutes only the shortcut that literal diagonal and PSD information imply
mode-zero saving.  It does not refute a future theorem using the arithmetic
of the actual source packets.

\section{Route status and conclusion}
TPC-262 pays one structural interface: the literal signed reduced-residue
operator, its deleted diagonal, and its packet-index phase typing are now
written exactly on one common finite object.  It also identifies the exact
failure mode of a diagonal-only argument after actual prime unit matrices are
retained.  The aggregate mode-zero identity is useful, but it is not silently
identified with the nontrivial V59 polarization character.

The next experiment is a census of cross-Gram entries on a growing prime
shell, restoring the smooth kernel, deleted diagonal, unit masks, and the
source-frozen \(\beta,w\) inputs.  A positive result must prove negative
signed cross-Gram cancellation with effective credit strictly larger than
\(1/400\).  A finite census may instead reveal another obstruction; neither
outcome is an asymptotic theorem by itself.
