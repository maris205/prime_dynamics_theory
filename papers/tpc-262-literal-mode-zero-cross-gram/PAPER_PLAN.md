# TPC-262 paper plan

## Question

What exact datum is missing after TPC-260--261, when the four packets are
formed on the literal V59 prime shell rather than in an abstract Hilbert
space?

## Contribution

1. Define the zero-frequency reduced-residue operator using the actual prime
   weights and unit masks.
2. Prove its centered unit-class matrix is an exact projection and derive the
   four-packet mode-zero/cross-Gram identity.
3. State the endpoint criterion as one signed scalar estimate after all losses.
4. Give an exact finite operator-image adversary showing that packet diagonal
   data and PSD bounds alone cannot supply that estimate.

## Ceiling

This is a structural finite-fiber paper. It does not estimate the growing
V59 kernel, prove arithmetic `L2`, pay the `1/400` gap, or prove twin primes.
