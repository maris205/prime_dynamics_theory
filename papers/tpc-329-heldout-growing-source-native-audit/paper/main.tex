\begin{abstract}
We test whether the source-native finite V59 residual used in the preceding
signed-Gram release survives a held-out origin/scale change, and whether its
readout can be explained by source norms alone.  The literal deleted-diagonal
prime-shell operator is evaluated at two new origins, two larger scales, four
shell anchors, two kernel exponents, and four fixed sign laws.  The exact finite
Gram identity separates the energy into a coordinate diagonal and an
off-diagonal term.  On the actual residual, the all-plus off-diagonal term is
negative on $31/32$ rows and positive on $1/32$.  A predeclared affine
permutation preserves the source multiset and Euclidean $L^2$ norm, yet the
all-plus term is positive on $32/32$ permuted rows, with $31/32$ classifications
changed.  The two-scale audit has $64$ paired comparisons and all-plus energy
growth factors in $[1.9663131482417533,2.14326466572482]$.  These are guarded
finite numerical observations, not a growing arithmetic estimate.  The
principal contribution is a finite placement-sensitivity obstruction to reading
the diagnostic from source norm or marginal values alone; no fixed-power
credit, Route-B gate payment, or twin-prime conclusion is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and contribution}

The current twin-prime route uses a literal centered prime-shell operator.  A
finite sign pattern can be misleading if it is tested at only one origin or if
the operator is never exposed to the arithmetic source.  The preceding release
attached the finite V59 residual to the operator.  We now make the smallest
natural continuation: hold the operator and source model fixed, move to two
previously unused origins and larger windows, and add a hostile control that
preserves the source values as a multiset.

The paper contributes four finite facts.  First, it provides a two-origin,
two-scale holdout with an explicit scale-pairing audit.  Second, it proves the
same exact source-coordinate Gram decomposition for every finite row.  Third,
it applies the fixed affine map
\[
  \pi(i)=(5i+17)\pmod M,\qquad M=N/2,
\]
to the source coordinates.  Finally, it records the resulting sign contrast:
the actual and permuted vectors have the same norm and coordinate multiset,
but their signed-Gram readouts differ sharply.

Every statement below is finite.  A finite row count is not a uniformity
quantifier, and a growth factor between two scales is not an asymptotic
exponent.

\section{The literal finite object}

For an origin $o$ and an even scale $N$, let
\[
 I_{o,N}=\{o,o+1,\ldots,o+N/2-1\}.
\]
The protocol uses
\[
 o\in\{28001,36001\},\quad N\in\{4096,8192\},\quad H=66,
\]
and $Q\in\{24,36,54,80\}$ with $s\in\{1,2\}$.  For a prime
$p\in(Q,2Q]$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:block}
 B_{p,Q,s}(u,t)=
 \1_{u\ne t}\1_{p\nmid u}\1_{p\nmid t}
 p\frac{H^{2s}}{(H^2+(u-t)^2)^s}
 \left(\1_{p\mid u-t}-\frac1{p-1}\right).
\end{equation}
The diagonal is deleted before the centered residue factor is applied.  For a
fixed sign law $e=(e_p)$ on the shell, put
\[
 C_e=\sum_{p\in(Q,2Q]}e_pB_{p,Q,s}.
\]
The four laws are all-plus, index-alternating, the sign of $p$ modulo $4$,
and a half split in increasing prime order.

For a finite vector $v=(v_t)_{t\in I_{o,N}}$, define
\begin{align}
 E_e(v)&=\|C_ev\|_2^2,\\
 D_e(v)&=\sum_t v_t^2\|C_ee_t\|_2^2,\\
 O_e(v)&=E_e(v)-D_e(v).
\end{align}
When $D_e(v)>0$, use $R_e(v)=E_e(v)/D_e(v)$.  Thus $R_e<1$ and
$R_e>1$ mean negative and positive off-diagonal Gram mass, respectively.

\section{Source model and placement control}

The source is the finite V59 comparison model
\[
 \Lambda(m)=
 \begin{cases}
   \log p,&m=p^k,\\
   0,&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}
\]
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:comparison}
 b^{(2)}(t)=2C_2\,\1_{2\nmid t}
 \prod_{\substack{p\mid t\\p>2}}\frac{p-1}{p-2},\qquad
 \beta_o^{(2)}(t)=\Lambda(t+2)-b^{(2)}(t).
\end{equation}
The executable certificate evaluates the product through $50000$, with the
inherited positive tail enclosure, and uses 100-digit Decimal logarithm
centers with a rational $10^{-70}$ guard.  The resulting midpoint vector is
used in the float64 matrix replay.

The placement control is not selected after seeing the result.  For both
$M=2048$ and $M=4096$, $\gcd(5,M)=1$, so $\pi$ is a bijection.  If $P_\pi$ is
the associated permutation matrix, then
\[
 P_\pi^TP_\pi=I,\qquad \|P_\pi v\|_2=\|v\|_2.
\]
Thus the actual vector and $P_\pi v$ have identical coordinate multisets and
norms.  They need not have identical images under $C_e$: the literal
divisibility masks and distance kernel are not assumed to commute with an
arbitrary coordinate permutation.

\section{Exact finite identities}

\paragraph{Gram decomposition.}
The finite product expands as $C_ev=\sum_t v_tC_ee_t$.  Therefore, by
bilinearity,
\[
 E_e(v)=\sum_{t,t'}v_tv_{t'}
       \langle C_ee_t,C_ee_{t'}\rangle.
\]
The terms with $t=t'$ are $D_e(v)$ and the remaining terms are
\[
 O_e(v)=\sum_{t\ne t'}v_tv_{t'}
       \langle C_ee_t,C_ee_{t'}\rangle.
\]
Hence $E_e(v)=D_e(v)+O_e(v)$ exactly for every finite vector.  No limiting
interchange or arithmetic estimate occurs in this step; this is the finite
Gram expansion in the usual matrix-analysis sense \cite{horn2013matrix}.

\paragraph{Placement identity.}
The permutation matrix identity above proves norm and multiset preservation.
On the other hand,
\[
 E_e(P_\pi v)=v^TP_\pi^TC_e^TC_eP_\pi v.
\]
Equality with $E_e(v)$ for all $v$ would require a commutation property of the
Gram operator that is not part of the model.  The finite control therefore
tests coordinate placement while fixing the source norm.

\section{Certificate protocol}

The producer writes canonical JSON containing all $32$ actual rows and their
placement-control rows.  Each row stores source geometry, shell, matrix shape,
the two component controls, $E,D,O,R$, and the outward ratio guard.  A row is
labelled negative when $R+5\cdot10^{-8}<1$, positive when
$R-5\cdot10^{-8}>1$, and unresolved otherwise.

The independent checker does not import the producer.  It rebuilds the source
with separate factorization code, accumulates shell blocks in reverse order,
recomputes actual and permuted metrics, and checks every stored value.  It also
replays the $64$ scale pairs.  The stress suite checks exact rational Gram
algebra, source/sign mutations, deleted diagonals, permutation metadata,
provenance hashes, and the fail-closed claim firewall.  Normal and optimized
executions must have empty standard error and byte-identical output.

\subsection{Two-scale audit}

Pairing rows at fixed origin, shell anchor, and exponent gives $64$ law-level
comparisons from $4096$ to $8192$.  For all-plus, signs persist in $15/16$
pairs and cross once.  The energy growth factor and base-$2$ log-slope ranges
are
\[
 1.9663131482417533\leq E_{8192}/E_{4096}
 \leq2.14326466572482,
\]
\[
 0.97549309860589706\leq
 \log_2(E_{8192}/E_{4096})
 \leq1.0998100153677246.
\]
The analogous sign persistence counts are $15/16$, $16/16$, and $16/16$
for alternating, mod-$4$, and half-split laws.  These two-point slopes are
reported as numerical observations only.

\section{Exact local anchor}

At $I=[28001,28016]$, $Q=4$, and $s=1$, the shell is $\{5,7\}$.  The exact
rational vector is
\[
 v_t=\1_{t+2\text{ is prime}}-\1_{t\text{ is odd}}.
\]
The Fraction calculation gives, after decimal display,
\[
 E=582.0014930371829,\qquad
 D=649.9719408544561,\qquad
 O=-67.97044781727324,
\]
and verifies $E=D+O$ before display.  Reduced numerator/denominator digests
for all three values are stored in the certificate and independently
recomputed.  The anchor is a finite arithmetic sanity check, not a claim
about twin-prime density.

\section{Interpretation and claim boundary}

The strongest finite positive result is the held-out source-native replay plus
the norm-preserving placement control.  The strongest obstruction is the
actual/permuted all-plus contrast: identical source multiset and $L^2$ norm do
not determine the sign of this coordinate-sensitive diagnostic on the released
panel.  This is a scoped obstruction to a source-norm-only interpretation.

The following remain open or unpaid:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a source-uniform growing $L^2$ estimate for the actual residual;
  \item a canonical arithmetic sign law and placement-aware reassembly bound;
  \item an operator-norm estimate with the required physical normalization;
  \item the strict $1/400$ endpoint loss, Route-B Gate B, and any twin-prime
        conclusion.
\end{itemize}
Accordingly,
\[
 \texttt{ARITHMETIC\_ADVANCE=NO},\quad
 \texttt{FIXED\_POWER\_CREDIT=0},\quad
 \texttt{FULL\_GATE\_B=OPEN},\quad
 \texttt{TWIN\_PRIME\_RESULT=NONE}.
\]
The Session-named Route-A and Route-B evaluator files are absent from this
checkout; the local Bridge-B checker is a fail-closed fallback and is not an
official evaluator pass.
