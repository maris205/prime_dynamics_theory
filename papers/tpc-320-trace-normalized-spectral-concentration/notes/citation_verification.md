# TPC-320 citation verification

The exact finite spectral facts use the spectral theorem and the elementary
finite-dimensional Ky Fan principle.  The perturbation enclosure invokes Weyl's
eigenvalue inequality.  The trace, stable-rank, participation-rank, and entropy
identities are proved directly in the project.

The operator convention and finite parent lock are inherited from the local
TPC-319 certificate.  No external source is used to claim arithmetic
cancellation, an asymptotic power saving, or a twin-prime result.  Bibliographic
entries in paper/references.bib are provided for the classical matrix facts
and the local parent release; this package does not treat a citation as
evidence for the numerical certificate.
