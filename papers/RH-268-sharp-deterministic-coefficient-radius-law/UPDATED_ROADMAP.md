# Updated roadmap after RH-268

The deterministic target coefficient problem is now sharp: the certified base
`q_*` cannot be improved.  Further progress must not spend papers fitting a
smaller target rate.  The remaining route is structural: a legal head, a
cloud-to-target bridge, and a uniform quotient theorem.  RH-269 records a
precise sufficient criterion for the quotient part and audits which hypotheses
are absent from the repository.
