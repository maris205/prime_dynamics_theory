\section{Weighted-prime aggregation}
\label{sec:aggregation}

Substitute Lemma~\ref{lem:riemann} into \eqref{eq:direct-energy}.  Since
$|C_p|^2=(\log p)^2/p^2$, define
\[
 A_U=\sum_{p\in\Ptop}\frac{(\log p)^2}{p},\qquad
 E_U=\sum_{p\in\Ptop}\frac{(\log p)^2}{p^2},\qquad
 S_Q=\sum_{q\in\Qshell}q,
\]
and write $P=|\Qshell|$.  Then
\begin{equation}
 \Dtop=\frac{\kappa_\psi}{H}A_US_Q+O_\psi(PE_U).
 \label{eq:aggregate}
\end{equation}

The accumulated row error is genuinely lower order.  On the top shell,
$p>U/2$, so $E_U\leq2A_U/U$.  Every $q\in\Qshell$ exceeds $Q$, hence
$P\leq S_Q/Q$.  Relative to the positive main product in
\eqref{eq:aggregate}, these inequalities give
\begin{equation}
 \frac{HPE_U}{A_US_Q}
 \leq\frac{2H}{UQ}
 =O\!\left(x^{-23/2400}\right).
 \label{eq:relative-error}
\end{equation}
The fixed factor $\kappa_\psi^{-1}\leq2$ can be absorbed into the implied
constant.

We next evaluate the two positive weighted sums.  Partial summation from the
prime number theorem gives \cite{MontgomeryVaughan2007}
\begin{align}
 S_Q&=\left(\frac32+o(1)\right)\frac{Q^2}{\log Q},
 \label{eq:q-pnt}\\
 A_U&=(\log2+o(1))\log U.
 \label{eq:p-pnt}
\end{align}
For completeness, the main integral in \eqref{eq:q-pnt} is
\[
 \int_Q^{2Q}\frac{t}{\log t}\,dt
 =\left(\frac32+o(1)\right)\frac{Q^2}{\log Q}.
\]
The main integral in \eqref{eq:p-pnt} is
\[
 \int_{U/2}^{U}\frac{\log t}{t}\,dt
 =\log2\,\log U-\frac{(\log2)^2}{2}.
\]

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Lemma~\ref{lem:kappa} proves \eqref{eq:kappa-range}.  Combining
\eqref{eq:aggregate}--\eqref{eq:p-pnt} with
\eqref{eq:relative-error} gives
\[
 \Dtop=\left(
 \frac32\,\kappa_\psi\log2\,
 \frac{\log U}{\log Q}+o_\psi(1)\right)\frac{Q^2}{H}.
\]
The frozen scales satisfy
\[
 \frac{\log U}{\log Q}=\frac{133/400}{1/3}=\frac{399}{400}.
\]
Thus the rational multiplier is
$(3/2)(399/400)=1197/800$.  Finally,
\[
 \frac{Q^2}{H}=x^{2/3-21/32}=x^{1/96}.
\]
This proves \eqref{eq:main-asymptotic}, with the fixed-profile quantifier
stated in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
\end{proof}

Because the leading constant is positive, Theorem~\ref{thm:main} immediately
rules out $\Dtop=o(Q^2/H)$.  More strongly, for this exact object no estimate
of the form $\Dtop\ll_\psi x^{-\delta}Q^2/H$ can hold with fixed
$\delta>0$.
