# Roadmap after RH-371

RH-371 closes the exact finite run-frequency reduction and the cyclic
two-point-ledger obstruction for the RH-366 distance-two capacity.  It does
not prove that the Mobius run-frequency envelope has a natural density.

The remaining candidates are:

1. a source-backed theorem for the alternating eight-run envelope (or a
   rigorous nonexistence result in the Mobius data type);
2. a new fractional/tower-adapted strong space for the RH-367 Ulam route;
3. a nonadaptive invariant-measure theorem or a general constraint-graph
   arithmetic classification;
4. the composite primitive-divisor, radius, finite-entropy, globalization,
   and original same-clock physical routes.

The periodic words in this paper are ternary synthetic witnesses.  Their
cyclic pair ledger must not be presented as an open-prefix ledger or as a
Mobius sequence.  A finite capacity scan, a fitted limit, or a restatement of
the RH-366 dynamic program is not a reopening theorem.
