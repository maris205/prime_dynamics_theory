\begin{abstract}
TPC-173--175 freeze every TPC-133--172 manuscript and the declared
data substrate, define a source-locked production local-edge witness
contract, and extract the maximal qualifying family.  The family is
empty in that corpus.  TPC-176 sends only proved edges to the
TPC-165 gluing interface and obtains an exact empty-domain coverage
ledger without asserting totality.  TPC-177 shows that active support
is vacuous on the empty eligible domain and cannot close its
existential root.  TPC-178 shows that no physical representation
class is eligible for a canonicality or minimality audit and preserves
the archive-key firewall.

We integrate these results without turning a scoped source-extraction
stop into global architecture infeasibility.  The three independent
H1 structural roots all remain \(\NT\); the selected first missing
object is the source-backed local occurrence-edge family.  The
current architecture verdict remains \(\mathsf{NOT\_TESTABLE}\).
The result is an \(\Lone\) structural integration supported by
\(\Lzero\) executable audits, not fixed-phase or fixed-\(h_0\)
arithmetic progress and not program-positive \(\Ltwo\).
\end{abstract}

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{cell}&\text{type}&\text{status}\\ \hline
\text{TPC-133--172 source extraction}&
\text{SOURCE\_EXTRACTION\_METHOD}&\mathsf{STOP\_SCOPED}\\
\text{occurrence-augmented H1 path}&
\text{ARCHITECTURE\_ROUTE}&\mathsf{NOT\_TESTABLE}.
\end{array}                                                       \tag{8}
\]

The following are valid next actions:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item enlarge the source corpus explicitly and rerun the inventory,
      schema, family, coverage, support, and representation chain;
\item prove a new production local-edge theorem satisfying the

calling the architecture stopped because one extraction cell is
empty, is invalid.

The TPC-133--172 production source corpus is exhausted under an
explicit witness contract, and its local-edge extraction cell is
properly stopped.  The architecture is not closed: its three
independent structural roots remain \(\NT\).  This sharpened status

atom audits.  No fixed-phase or fixed-\(h_0\) arithmetic conclusion
