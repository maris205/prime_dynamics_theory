\begin{abstract}
We freeze the exact finite interface for a pair-native route beginning after
the TPC-18 Cauchy/TT-star step.  The ordered bilinear row-pair carrier is
source-backed, and the TPC-93 source--child inverse is exact once a retained
source atom is supplied; its coefficient reassembly retains the physical
squarefree and target-primitive support hypotheses.  However, the declared
TPC-205 registry/source-lock corpus contains no production pair record and no
theorem-backed pair-to-source-atom crosswalk.
Moreover, a TT-star bilinear pair term is not the linear, coefficientwise
conservative cut-to-occurrence edge required by H1.  We give a 42-field
fail-closed registry contract, two strictly finite non-production fixtures,
and a complete normalization/loss firewall.  The pair-native route therefore
survives only as an open architecture-reroute candidate.  This is an L0/L1
interface theorem, not a structural reopen, an L2 estimate, or a prime-pair
theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{The ordered post-TT-star carrier}

Fix one TPC-18 opened-\(D\) packet.  Write
\(\alpha=(\ell_\alpha,d_\alpha)\),
\(\gamma=(\ell_\gamma,d_\gamma)\), and
\(N_\alpha(j)=\ell_\alpha d_\alpha j+h_0\).
TPC-18 gives \citep{WangTPC18}
\begin{equation}
 |\mathcal T_D|^2
 \ll_W J\bigl(\mathcal E_D+\mathcal C_D^{\mathrm{off}}\bigr),
 \label{eq:ttstar}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal C_D^{\mathrm{off}}\) is summed over ordered triples
\((\alpha,\gamma,j)\) in the same packet, with \(\alpha\ne\gamma\).
Its displayed coefficient carrier is
\begin{equation}
 \mu(d_\alpha)\mu(d_\gamma)
 (\log\ell_\alpha)(\log\ell_\gamma)
 r_R(N_\alpha(j))r_R(N_\gamma(j))
 B_\alpha(j)B_\gamma(j).
 \label{eq:carrier}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Exact carrier and its boundary]
\label{thm:carrier}
The ordered summation domain in \eqref{eq:carrier}, and the eight displayed
coefficient factors, are source-backed.  The second row \(\gamma\) is
generated by the TT-star square expansion, not by a singleton cut.
The diagonal is the separate energy \(\mathcal E_D\).  The factors
\(B_\alpha,B_\gamma\) remain source aliases whose full literal expansion is
unsupplied; membership in the formal summation domain does not prove an
active or nonzero occurrence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
These statements are the literal domain, coefficient, and diagonal split of
the TPC-18 opened-\(D\) formula.  Its support restrictions are stated as
``understood,'' so replacing the two \(B\)-aliases by products of archived
single-row ASTs would add data not present in the source.
\end{proof}

TPC-32 supplies a separate matched-shell parent \citep{WangTPC32}:
\begin{align}
 w_{\alpha,\gamma,j}^{\mathrm{sh}}
 &=
 \gamma_\alpha^{(1)}\gamma_\gamma^{(2)}
 \mathfrak A_{\alpha,\gamma}(j)
 \mathcal K_{\alpha,\gamma}^{\mathrm{sh}}(j),\\
 \mathcal K_{\alpha,\gamma}^{\mathrm{sh}}(j)
 &=C_{m_\alpha}(j)\mathsf H_{m_\gamma;T,U_0}(j)
   +\mathsf H_{m_\alpha;T,U_0}(j)C_{m_\gamma}(j).
 \label{eq:polarization}
\end{align}
Opening \(C_{m_\alpha}\) produces
\(T<u\le U_0,\ u\mid N_\alpha(j)\); the right polarization is symmetric.
Thus \(u\) is not a field of the parent \((\alpha,\gamma,j)\).  TPC-32 also
states that no complex conjugation is implicit.  A Hermitian rewrite would
therefore have to record the second-side conjugation explicitly.  Nothing in
these formulae identifies the TPC-18 \(B\)-carrier with the TPC-32 literal
parent.

\section{Conditional source--child composition}

Given a retained TPC-93 source atom
\[
 \omega=(L/R,\alpha,\gamma,j,u),
\]
and a divisor \(v\mid(d_\alpha,d_\gamma)\), TPC-93 constructs a unique child
\((\theta,t)\) and gives an explicit inverse back to \(\omega\)
\citep{WangTPC93}.  Its projector identity is
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{v\mid d,e}\lambda_{G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}(v)
 =\mathbf 1_{(d,e)\le G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}.
 \label{eq:projector}
\end{equation}
A single child does not restore the source.  The projector identity is a
finite identity.  On TPC-93's physical squarefree and target-primitive
support, each child retains its sign and coefficient, and the weighted sum in
\eqref{eq:projector} restores the actual-row-gcd-masked source coefficient.
Both polarizations occur once and no new fiber normalization is introduced.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional pair-native composition]
\label{thm:conditional}
Suppose a future source-backed record supplies a fully materialized ordered
pair, the actual joint mask and packet schedule, \(T,U_0\), a polarization,
all normalization stages, and a theorem identifying that record with a
retained \(\omega\) on TPC-93's physical squarefree and target-primitive
support.  Then the TPC-93 source--child inverse and projector-weighted
coefficient reassembly apply exactly.  In the declared TPC-205 corpus the
premise is not instantiated: the production pair-to-\(\omega\) crosswalk is
absent.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The positive conclusion is precisely the TPC-93 inverse and
\eqref{eq:projector}, evaluated on the supplied source.  Neither theorem
constructs \(\omega\) from a TPC-18 pair.  Hence the stated premise cannot be
deleted or supplied by renaming indices.
\end{proof}

\section{Normalization and physical-loss firewall}

The source-backed TPC-18 object in \eqref{eq:ttstar} is unnormalized.  The
archived string \code{nu_X} is only a scope label and not a scalar
definition.  If a future theorem supplies a multiplicative scalar \(c_X\),
then and only then one may write
\begin{equation}
 |c_X\mathcal T_D|^2
 \ll_W |c_X|^2J
 \bigl(\mathcal E_D+\mathcal C_D^{\mathrm{off}}\bigr).
 \label{eq:normalization}
\end{equation}
The square in \(|c_X|^2\) and the four normalization stages are mandatory.

The ledger retains the source-backed prime-power error, bounded-overlap
dyadic partition, Cauchy factor \(J\), diagonal
\(\mathcal E_D\ll X^{1+\varepsilon}\), and the same/near/large-gcd bounds
from TPC-18.  It separately retains the TPC-25 zero, principal, drift and
polylogarithmic terms \citep{WangTPC25}, the TPC-32 drift and large-content
bounds, and the TPC-93 Fourier tail.  Three terminal rows remain:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\text{generic remainder}&=&\text{\code{UNCONTROLLED_HARD_REMAINDER}},\\
\text{square-root return}&=&\text{\code{MISSING_UNSUPPLIED}},\\
\text{full-block/endpoint return}&=&\text{\code{MISSING_UNSUPPLIED}}.
\end{array}
\]
Every retained bound remains conditional on its own source theorem's
hypotheses; the ledger does not compose these rows onto a production TPC-18
pair.
Consequently no target positive exponent, strict \(1/400\) credit, or L2
credit is available.

\section{H1 type separation and final verdict}

The H1 occurrence lift is linear and coefficientwise conservative
\citep{WangTPC143}:
\begin{equation}
 L_X:\mathbb C^{\mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{ns}}}
 \longrightarrow\mathbb C^{\mathcal O_X},
 \qquad
 \mathbf 1_{\mathcal O_X}^{T}L_X
 =\mathbf 1_{\mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{ns}}}^{T}.
 \label{eq:h1}
\end{equation}
Its conceptual entries may be signed or complex.  The finite TPC-174
contract further uses nonzero exact-rational weights with per-cut column sum
one, but its committed fixture is synthetic \citep{WangTPC174}.  By contrast,
the TPC-18 object is a
\code{TTSTAR_BILINEAR_PAIR_TERM} generated after a quadratic inequality.
There is no theorem inverse-aggregating it to every original cut
coefficient.

\begin{theorem}[Typed interface and first missing]
\label{thm:verdict}
In the declared source-locked TPC-205 registry corpus, the pair and H1
relation types are distinct, the number of production pair records is zero,
and the first production missing node is
\begin{center}
\small
\code{SOURCE_LOCKED_POST_TTSTAR_ORDERED_PAIR_REGISTRY_WITH_}\\
\code{COMPLETE_PAIR_COEFFICIENT_AND_GLOBAL_NORMALIZATION}.
\end{center}
Thus \(H1.E\) is not repaired.  The pair-native formula gate passes, while
its production and structural reopen triggers fail.  The pair-native
architecture-reroute candidate remains open.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorems~\ref{thm:carrier} and \ref{thm:conditional} establish the formula
carrier and the conditional inverse.  The declared source-locked registry has no row
containing a joint mask, full literal coefficient, pair nonzero status,
pair-to-\(\omega\) theorem, and complete normalization return.  Equation
\eqref{eq:h1} has a different source type and no inverse-aggregation bridge.
The conclusion follows without asserting a global impossibility theorem.
\end{proof}

TPC-179's independent roots for local edges \(E\), actual active support
\(A\), and canonical/minimal representation \(M\) remain
\(\NT\) \citep{WangTPC179}.  Both O161 pointwise parents, H1, and the global
architecture remain open.  The scoped cell
\begin{center}
\small
\code{TPC18_TPC93_POST_TTSTAR_PAIR_DIRECT_COMPOSITION_V1=STOP_SCOPED}
\end{center}
stops only direct renaming of the current formula carrier as a production
source atom or H1 edge; it does not stop a new registry, theorem crosswalk,
or architecture.

\section{Machine certificate and trust boundary}

The active certificate locks 17 sources, four relation types, 42 required
registry fields, two finite fixtures, 17 loss rows, and 23 gates.  A separate
L0 schema accepts only \code{ROW_ONLY} and
\code{DERIVED_L0_ONLY}, never a production mode.  The independent checker
imports neither builder nor materializer; it reselects the four archive rows
by semantic ID, recomputes their integrity hashes and integer arithmetic,
reconstructs the derived affine child, freezes the complete nested contract
by deep strict-type comparison, and independently executes 12 active-schema,
37 regenerated-schema semantic, and six Boolean/integer-confusion mutations.
The manifest is a repository review pin, not an external signature or theorem
source.
