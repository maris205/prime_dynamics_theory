\begin{abstract}
TPC-173 freezes and completely reviews the contiguous TPC-133--172 theorem
corpus, finding zero qualifying local actual-occurrence edge claims.
TPC-174 proves a finite witness-verifier interface, but its only positive
fixture is synthetic \(\Lzero\) and its production witness is absent.  We
now form the largest defensible production local-edge family relative to
these exact sources.

An admissible family member must resolve to a qualifying TPC-173 claim and
belong to a production candidate accepted by TPC-174.  Since the qualifying
source universe is empty, the largest family is exactly empty.  It covers
zero of the \(2988\) frozen cut rows; all \(2988\) are unmatched.  In
particular the nonempty-local-fibre hypothesis of the TPC-165 gluing theorem
is not instantiated.  The conclusion is maximal only inside the frozen
declared corpus.  It is a scoped \(\Lone\) obstruction, not mathematical
nonexistence, not actual active support, not canonical minimality, and not
arithmetic \(\Ltwo\) progress.
\end{abstract}

\section{Admissible universe}

Let \(\mathcal Q_{173}\) be the TPC-173 qualifying claim set and let
\(\operatorname{Verify}_{174}(W)\) mean that a production-mode witness
passes the TPC-174 verifier \citep{WangTPC173,WangTPC174}.

\begin{definition}[Defensible local edge]\label{def:edge}
A local edge \(e\) is defensible in the current evidence universe if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item its source claim belongs to \(\mathcal Q_{173}\); and
\item it belongs to a production witness \(W\) with
 \(\operatorname{Verify}_{174}(W)=\mathrm{PASS}\).
\end{enumerate}
The family \(\mathcal E_{175}\) contains every such edge and no other row.
\end{definition}

Synthetic fixtures, shadow rows, archive addresses, formal gluing
hypotheses and actual-core arithmetic statements without a cut crosswalk are
therefore excluded by definition.

\section{Scoped empty-family theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Largest declared-corpus family]\label{thm:empty}
For the source-locked TPC-173 and TPC-174 artifacts,
\[
 |\mathcal Q_{173}|=0,\qquad
 |\mathcal E_{175}|=0.
\tag{1}\label{eq:empty}
\]
Moreover \(\mathcal E_{175}=\varnothing\) is the unique largest defensible
family in the evidence universe of \cref{def:edge}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
TPC-173 has an empty qualifying claim list.  Every defensible edge must map
to one member of that list.  Therefore no edge satisfies the first
predicate in \cref{def:edge}, independently of the second.  The family of
all admissible members is consequently empty and contains every other
admissible family, proving maximality in the declared universe.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Closed-world boundary]\label{prop:boundary}
Equation \eqref{eq:empty} proves neither mathematical nonexistence of local
occurrences nor emptiness after a declared corpus enlargement.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The admissible universe explicitly quantifies only over the finite
TPC-133--172 corpus and the current committed production witness set.  No
completeness theorem for all mathematical sources is supplied.
\end{proof}
