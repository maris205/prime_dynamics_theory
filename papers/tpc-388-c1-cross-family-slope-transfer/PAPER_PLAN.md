# TPC-388 paper plan

## Research question

Does the finite `512 -> 768` spectral slope learned on TPC-387 transfer to a
new, coordinate-disjoint origin family when it is frozen before the new
family's endpoint response is read?

## Claim-sized contributions

1. A response-blind second origin family with the same three-level count role
   split as TPC-387.
2. A genuinely out-of-family forecast using the 32 parent slopes without
   refitting, plus a same-family recalibration control.
3. A complete 256-row certificate, exact rational anchor, descending-shell
   independent replay, and 25-mutation firewall.
4. A finite result: both parent transfer and local control pass all 32 cells
   under the 3% endpoint cap, with 40 inherited spectral diagnostics still
   above the separate 0.64 cap.

## Non-claims

The transfer is finite and scoped.  It is not an origin-uniform theorem, a
count-uniform law, a source-valid normalization result, a growing operator
bound, an arithmetic `L2` estimate, an official Route-A/Route-B verdict, or a
twin-prime theorem.
