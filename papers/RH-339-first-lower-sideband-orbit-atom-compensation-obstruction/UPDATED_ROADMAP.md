# Roadmap after RH-339

RH-339 proves that every one-alias off-background contains a mandatory
first-lower-sideband boundary-orbit atom with divergent isolated weighted
size.  Off-alias closure therefore requires signed compensation at

    n = 2k-2

to relative precision o((beta R)^(-2(k-1))).  The result closes only the
separate-absolute orbit/complement route.  It does not prove a positive lower
bound for the fully signed coefficient and does not decide E_off.

The next determinant-gluing stage cannot activate while any of the following
remain open:

1. the critical signed packet, including diffuse far compensation;
2. the mandatory lower-sideband signed compensation and the rest of E_off;
3. synchronized noisy-head/counterloop transport on the identical clock and
   Hardy coefficient type.

An admissible RH-340 paper may audit the head/counterloop synchronization and
prove an exact mismatch or inactive-condition theorem.  It may activate
gluing only if all three budgets close, which the current repository does not
support.  Rounded C_M signs, finite sideband tables, RH-329's wrong clock, or
separate absolute majorants are not reopening inputs.

No Gate A--E condition changes.
