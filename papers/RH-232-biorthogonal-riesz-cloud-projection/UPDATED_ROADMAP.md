# Roadmap after RH-232

The finite Riesz construction is algebraically straightforward, but the
left/right angle collapses as the noise decreases.  RH-233 tests whether the
positive radial shell gaps of RH-222 can nevertheless control this angle.  If
not, RH-234 switches to a projection-free spectral factorization of `det_2`.

No Riesz projection is interval-certified here, and Gates A--E remain open.
