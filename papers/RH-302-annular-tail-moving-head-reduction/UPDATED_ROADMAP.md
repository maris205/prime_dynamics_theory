# Roadmap after RH-302

The annular route is no longer blocked by high-order tails.  The sole direct
input is a norm estimate for

    sum_(2 <= n < m_sigma) (tau_(sigma,n)-a_n) z^n/n.

The next audit asks what fixed-order transport and mass growth any such
estimate would necessarily entail.  More tail estimates do not reopen the
route.
