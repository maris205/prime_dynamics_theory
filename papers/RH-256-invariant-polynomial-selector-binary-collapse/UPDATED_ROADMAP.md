# Roadmap after RH-256

Complex coefficients of a polynomial in `A` are not by themselves a new
selector class.  Idempotence collapses them to binary choices on algebraic
root spaces.  For real, conjugate-closed selectors supported on the resolved
RH-254 window, the expanded-window shell obstruction therefore still applies.
The conclusion does not exclude a complex mask selecting only one member of a
conjugate pair or a selector using unresolved root spaces.

The next live class must be non-idempotent at the loop/moment level while
remaining forced by an exact quotient identity.  RH-257 should formulate that
class and test whether its coefficients are stable and operator-realizable,
not merely least-squares weights.
