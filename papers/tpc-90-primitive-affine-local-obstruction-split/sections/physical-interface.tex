\section{The literal growing fixed-\texorpdfstring{\(h_0\)}{h0}
correlation}
\label{sec:physical-interface}

We now index the forms by a scale \(X\) and an outer key
\(\theta\in\Theta_X\):
\begin{equation}
 L_{1,\theta,X}(r)
 =d_{\theta,X}+s_{\theta,X}r,\qquad
 L_{2,\theta,X}(r)
 =u_{\theta,X}+a_{\theta,X}r.
\label{eq:growing-affine-family}
\end{equation}
The literal branch requires
\begin{equation}
 s_{\theta,X}u_{\theta,X}
 -a_{\theta,X}d_{\theta,X}
 =h_0\ne0
\label{eq:growing-fixed-determinant}
\end{equation}
with the same prescribed \(h_0\) for every active key.  It also
requires individual primitivity, or the exact content audit of
\cref{prop:content-factorization}.

Let \(I_{\theta,X}\) be the actual translated interval on which both
forms are positive.  Combine the physical smooth weight,
coprimality and residue masks, joint mask, polarization, and every
other local multiplier into one literal coefficient
\[
 W_{\theta,X}:I_{\theta,X}\to\C.
\]
No assumption of positivity or translation invariance is made.

\begin{definition}[Literal parity correlation]
\label{def:literal-parity-correlation}
Define
\begin{align}
 C_{\theta,X}
 &=
 \sum_{r\in I_{\theta,X}}
 W_{\theta,X}(r)
 \mu(L_{1,\theta,X}(r))
 \mu(L_{2,\theta,X}(r)),
\label{eq:literal-mobius-correlation}\\
 \Qsf_{\theta,X}(r)
 &=
 \mu^2(L_{1,\theta,X}(r))
 \mu^2(L_{2,\theta,X}(r)).
\label{eq:literal-squarefree-support}
\end{align}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact literal local-parity split]
\label{thm:literal-local-parity-split}
For every active key,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 C_{\theta,X}
 =
 \sum_{r\in I_{\theta,X}}
 W_{\theta,X}(r)\Qsf_{\theta,X}(r)
 \lambda(L_{1,\theta,X}(r))
 \lambda(L_{2,\theta,X}(r)).}
\label{eq:literal-local-parity-split}
\end{equation}
At each fixed \(X,\theta\), the local sets and root counts of
\(\Qsf_{\theta,X}\) are given exactly by
\cref{thm:prime-power-root-count}.  In particular, all simultaneous
root primes divide \(h_0\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{thm:two-form-split} to the literal interval and weight.
The local classification follows from
\cref{thm:gcd-confinement,thm:prime-power-root-count}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The actual unresolved estimate}

In the determinant packet introduced in TPC-32
\citep{WangTPC32}, suppose the complete raw distinguished coefficient
has an exact outer reassembly
\begin{equation}
 Z_X
 =
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}
 c_{\theta,X}C_{\theta,X}
 +E_{\mathrm{content},X},
\label{eq:literal-outer-reassembly}
\end{equation}
where all native keys and both polarizations occur once, the
\(c_{\theta,X}\) are the literal outer coefficients, and
\(E_{\mathrm{content},X}\) contains only explicitly audited content
or bounded-fiber terms.

\begin{definition}[Growing fixed-determinant parity gate]
\label{def:growing-parity-gate}
The \emph{literal growing fixed-\(h_0\) weighted parity gate} is the
problem of obtaining the required saving for
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}c_{\theta,X}
 \sum_{r\in I_{\theta,X}}
 W_{\theta,X}(r)\Qsf_{\theta,X}(r)
 \lambda(L_{1,\theta,X}(r))
 \lambda(L_{2,\theta,X}(r)),
\label{eq:hard-growing-correlation}
\end{equation}
uniformly in the actual active family and with every physical loss
and completion term included.
\end{definition}

Equation \eqref{eq:hard-growing-correlation}, not the Euler product,
is the remaining arithmetic object.  A strong fiberwise theorem
sufficient for many partial-summation or dyadic-reassembly arguments
would control every relevant prefix \(I_{\theta,X}(M)\):
\begin{equation}
 \sup_{\substack{\theta\in\Theta_X\\
                 M\text{ an active prefix}}}
 \left|
 \sum_{r\in I_{\theta,X}(M)}
 W_{\theta,X}(r)\Qsf_{\theta,X}(r)
 \lambda(L_{1,\theta,X}(r))
 \lambda(L_{2,\theta,X}(r))
 \right|
 \le
 X^{-\eta+o(1)}
 \mathcal M_{\theta,X}(M),
\label{eq:uniform-prefix-target}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 \mathcal M_{\theta,X}(M)
 =
 \sum_{r\in I_{\theta,X}(M)}
 |W_{\theta,X}(r)|\Qsf_{\theta,X}(r).
\]
Here \(\eta>0\) is only a schematic desired saving.
Equation \eqref{eq:uniform-prefix-target} is not asserted to be
necessary: cancellation may also occur in the exact outer sum
\eqref{eq:literal-outer-reassembly}.  The minimal requirement is the
calibrated bound on that complete sum.

\begin{proposition}[Why fixed-form local information is insufficient]
\label{prop:fixed-form-insufficient}
The results proved in
\cref{sec:determinant-algebra,sec:local-root-counts,sec:squarefree-support,sec:parity-split}
do not imply either \eqref{eq:uniform-prefix-target} or a power saving
for \eqref{eq:hard-growing-correlation}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Those results determine the zero set of \(\Qsf_{\theta,X}\), its
finite periodic densities, and the exact placement of square
divisors.  They contain no estimate for the signs
\(\lambda(L_{1,\theta,X})\lambda(L_{2,\theta,X})\).  Even the
fixed-form density theorem is unsigned and asymptotic in an interval
whose length tends to infinity with fixed coefficients.  It supplies
neither uniformity in the growing coefficients nor control of
translated short prefixes and literal weights.  Therefore the stated
signed conclusions do not follow.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Comparison with available correlation theorems}

Tao's logarithmically averaged two-point theorem fixes the affine
coefficients before the main scale tends to infinity
\citep{Tao2016}.  The averaged Chowla theorem of
\citet{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015} averages over a shift box whose side
length \(H=H(X)\) tends to infinity with the scale.  Neither
quantifier pattern gives
\eqref{eq:hard-growing-correlation}: the physical problem prescribes
one determinant, lets the slopes and intercepts grow, retains
ordinary local weights and masks, and then performs a specific outer
sum.  Moreover, replacing a weight \(1/(r_0+r)\) by \(1/r\) after
translating a fiber requires a uniform partial-summation theorem; it
is not a formal relabeling.  These distinctions are exactly the
Tauberian and literal-coefficient gaps isolated in TPC-87
\citep{WangTPC87}.

\begin{remark}[No specialization]
\label{rem:no-specialization}
The determinant in \eqref{eq:growing-fixed-determinant} remains an
arbitrary prescribed nonzero integer.  None of the local identities
or conditional targets selects \(h_0=2\), produces prime values, or
proves a fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic.
\end{remark}
