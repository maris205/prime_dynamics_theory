\begin{abstract}
Let
\[
 L_1(r)=d+sr,\qquad L_2(r)=u+ar,
 \qquad su-ad=h_0\ne0,
\]
with \(\gcd(d,s)=\gcd(u,a)=1\).  We give an exact local-obstruction
audit for this primitive affine pair.  The two determinant identities
\[
 \gcd(a,s)\mid h_0,\qquad
 \gcd\bigl(L_1(r),L_2(r)\bigr)\mid h_0
\]
show that all simultaneous value divisors are confined to the fixed
prime set dividing \(h_0\).

For every prime \(p\) and \(k\ge1\), let \(\nu_{p,k}\) be the number
of residues modulo \(p^k\) on which \(p^k\mid L_1(r)\) or
\(p^k\mid L_2(r)\), and set
\[
 \epsilon_{1,p}=\ind_{p\nmid s},\qquad
 \epsilon_{2,p}=\ind_{p\nmid a}.
\]
We prove the exact formula
\[
 \boxed{\;
 \nu_{p,k}
 =\epsilon_{1,p}+\epsilon_{2,p}
 -\epsilon_{1,p}\epsilon_{2,p}\ind_{p^k\mid h_0}.
 \;}
\]
Thus a common root modulo \(p^k\) exists exactly when both slopes are
units modulo \(p\) and \(p^k\mid h_0\).  In particular, the
simultaneous-squarefree local factor is
\[
 1-\frac{\nu_{p,2}}{p^2}>0,
\]
and
\[
 \Mloc(L_1,L_2)
 :=\prod_p\left(1-\frac{\nu_{p,2}}{p^2}\right)
 \ge\prod_p\left(1-\frac{2}{p^2}\right)>0.
\]
We prove that this Euler product is the natural simultaneous
squarefree density for every fixed primitive pair.  We also give the
exact square-divisor expansion: for squarefree \(q_1,q_2\), the system
\[
 q_1^2\mid L_1(r),\qquad q_2^2\mid L_2(r)
\]
is soluble precisely when
\[
 (q_1,s)=(q_2,a)=1,\qquad
 \gcd(q_1,q_2)^2\mid h_0,
\]
and then defines one residue class modulo
\(\lcm(q_1^2,q_2^2)\).

On every interval on which both forms are positive,
\[
 \mu(L_1(r))\mu(L_2(r))
 =
 \underbrace{\mu^2(L_1(r))\mu^2(L_2(r))}_{\text{squarefree/local support}}
 \underbrace{\lambda(L_1(r))\lambda(L_2(r))}_{\text{Liouville parity}}.
\]
This separates all square-power local obstructions from the remaining
two-sign problem without solving the latter.  The unresolved
arithmetic target is uniform cancellation for the literal growing
fixed-\(h_0\) weighted correlation, with its actual origins, masks,
prefixes, polarizations, and outer reassembly.  The algebra, root
counts, Euler product, and divisor expansion are \(\Lzero\).  Their
attachment to the complete physical packet gives a conditional
\(\Lone\) certificate schema whose literal verification remains
open.  The required growing-family Liouville cancellation is
\(\Ltwo\) and is not proved.  The determinant compatibility ledger
\(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\) and the independent physical-loss rule
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400\) remain separate.  No prime-pair
lower bound, parity breach, or twin-prime conclusion is claimed.
\end{abstract}
