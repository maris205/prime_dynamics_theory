# TPC-283 paper plan

## Question

How much source-direction uncertainty is enough to erase the actual scalar
attachment measured in TPC-282?

## Contributions

1. Prove the distance formula for the zero-attachment hyperplane.
2. Give the unique minimum-norm zeroing perturbation.
3. Apply the formula to the twelve source-locked rows and certify strict
   `3/10` and `1/10` radius thresholds.
4. State why this obstruction does not by itself describe an admissible literal
   source perturbation.

## Non-claims

No physical perturbation class, asymptotic nondegeneracy theorem, arithmetic
`L2` estimate, or twin-prime result is claimed.
