\begin{abstract}
The corrected RH-334 observation map defines an actual physical signed far
slot at the first alias.  We isolate within that slot a real deterministic
boundary-orbit atom.  For the primitive period-$2k$ boundary cycle, all but
one of its folded marked points lie in the frozen far set for every fixed
window parameter and all sufficiently small noise.  The resulting set has
$2k-1$ points.  Its noisy localized trace is exactly zero because a finite
set has zero multiplication operator on $L^2$, whereas its corrected
deterministic mass is
\[
 D_k^{\rm orb}=r_H^{-2k}\frac{2k-1}{1+|M_k|}.
\]
Thus its signed far subledger is $-D_k^{\rm orb}$.  The physical multiplier
law gives $D_k^{\rm orb}/\mathcal A_{k,2k}\to1$ and
$D_k^{\rm orb}/H_k\to+\infty$.  This is an alias-sized physical obstruction
to proving the far estimate by taking separate absolute values after the
orbit/rest split.  It is not an aggregate far nonvanishing theorem.  If the
full far slot is $o(H_k)$, the complementary diffuse ledger must compensate
the orbit atom as $D_k^{\rm orb}+o(H_k)$, requiring relative precision
$o((\beta R)^{-2k})$.  No moving-order estimate for that diffuse term is
available, so aggregate far closure and aggregate nonvanishing both remain
not testable.  No determinant or Riemann-hypothesis conclusion follows.
\end{abstract}

\section{Frozen far observation and boundary orbit}

Let $u_c\in(1,2)$ solve
\begin{equation}\label{eq:u-cubic}
 u_c^3-2u_c^2+2u_c-2=0
\end{equation}
and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-constants}
 f(x)=1-u_cx^2,
 \qquad r=u_c-1,
 \qquad \lambda=2u_cr>1,
 \qquad b=u_c^{-1/2}.
\end{equation}
Then $f(r)=r$, $f(1)=-r$, and the two-step component map is $S=f^2$.
The inverse branch used by the primitive boundary word is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:h}
 h(x)=\sqrt{\frac{1-\sqrt{(1-x)/u_c}}{u_c}},
 \qquad h(1)=b,
\end{equation}
with $h$ increasing and $h(x)<x$ on $(r,1)$
\citep{WangBoundaryMonodromy2026}.

Fix $A>0$.  RH-334 freezes the boundary-owned windows before evaluating any
trace:
\begin{align}
 J^-_{\sigma,k,A}&=[0,1]\cap[b-A\sqrt\sigma,b),\label{eq:Jminus}\\
 J^+_{\sigma,k,A}&=[0,1]\cap[b,b+A\sqrt\sigma],\label{eq:Jplus}\\
 F_{\sigma,k,A}&=[0,1]\setminus(J^-_{\sigma,k,A}\cup J^+_{\sigma,k,A}).
 \label{eq:F}
\end{align}
For measurable $J\subset[0,1]$ and $n\ge2$, let
\begin{align}
 L_{\sigma,n}(J)&=\Tr(M_JK_\sigma^n),\label{eq:L}\\
 P_n^{\rm abs}(J)&=
 \sum_{\substack{f^n(x)=x\\ |x|\in J}}
 \frac1{|1-(f^n)'(x)|}.
 \label{eq:Pabs}
\end{align}
Here $K_\sigma$ is the RH-334 folded backward-observable noisy operator; the
multiplication operator marks the cyclic basepoint rather than a forward
probability event.
The corrected actual far slot is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R-full}
 \cR_k:=r_H^{-2k}
 \{L_{\sigma,2k}(F_{\sigma,k,A})
       -P_{2k}^{\rm abs}(F_{\sigma,k,A})\},
 \qquad r_H=\frac{17}{20}.
\end{equation}
Absolute-value folding is a multiplier-preserving bijection between the
signed and folded fixed points, including marked-point multiplicity and
minimal period \citep{WangObservation2026}.

Let $p_k=p_{2k}$ be the primitive boundary point coded by
$CA(CB)^{k-1}$.  RH-17 proves that its $S$-orbit is the exact ordered chain
\begin{equation}\label{eq:S-chain}
 p_k, h^{k-1}(p_k), h^{k-2}(p_k),\ldots,h(p_k),
\end{equation}
and that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:multiplier-law}
 M_k:=(f^{2k})'(p_k)=(S^k)'(p_k)
 =-C_M\lambda^k\{1+o(1)\},
 \qquad C_M>0.
\end{equation}
The word is primitive, so all $2k$ signed and folded marked points are
distinct \citep{WangLongCycle2026,WangBoundaryMonodromy2026}.

We work on the exact physical natural clock
\begin{equation}\label{eq:natural-clock}
 k=\frac{\log(1/\sigma)}{2\log\lambda}+O(1),
 \qquad \sigma\to0,
\end{equation}
though the containment argument below needs only $k\to\infty$ and
$\sigma\to0$.

\section{The physical far-orbit atom}

For the signed orbit $x_j=f^j(p_k)$, define the folded marked set with one
point deleted:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Omega}
 \Omega_k=
 \{|x_j|:0\le j<2k,\ j\ne2k-2\}.
\end{equation}
The deleted point is the final even component point
$|x_{2k-2}|=h(p_k)$.  Its distance from $b$ is itself on the critical
$\lambda^{-k}$ scale, so no fixed-$A$ membership is asserted for it.

\begin{theorem}[Eventual far containment of $2k-1$ marked points]
\label{thm:containment}
For every fixed $A>0$, along \eqref{eq:natural-clock} and all sufficiently
large $k$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Omega-in-F}
 \Omega_k\subset F_{\sigma,k,A},
 \qquad |\Omega_k|=2k-1.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
RH-17 proves that $p_k$ increases with $k$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:right-gap}
 p_k\ge p_1>b.
\end{equation}
Thus the endpoint $p_k$ lies to the right of the shrinking windows by the
fixed gap $p_1-b>0$.

The even marked points are the $S$-chain \eqref{eq:S-chain}.  Except for
$h(p_k)$, every internal even point has the form $h^m(p_k)$ with $m\ge2$.
Monotonicity and $p_k<1$ give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:even-left-gap}
 h^m(p_k)\le h^2(1)=h(b)<b.
\end{equation}
Hence these points lie to the left by the fixed gap $b-h(b)>0$.

Every point in the positive $S$-chain lies in $[r,1]$.  Since $f$ decreases
from $f(r)=r$ to $f(1)=-r$ on this interval, every odd folded point satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:odd-left-gap}
 |f(S^j(p_k))|\le r<b,
\end{equation}
with fixed gap $b-r>0$.  Choose $k$ large enough that
\begin{equation*}
 A\sqrt\sigma<min\{p_1-b,\ b-h(b),\ b-r\}.
\end{equation*}
Equations \eqref{eq:right-gap}--\eqref{eq:odd-left-gap} then place every
marked point except $h(p_k)$ in $F_{\sigma,k,A}$.  Primitivity and the
folding bijection give distinctness, so deleting one of $2k$ points leaves
cardinality $2k-1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact signed far-orbit subledger]
\label{thm:far-atom}
For all $k$ in the range of \cref{thm:containment}, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:D-orb}
 D_k^{\rm orb}
 =r_H^{-2k}\frac{2k-1}{1+|M_k|}>0.
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R-orb}
 \boxed{
 \cR_k^{\rm orb}:=r_H^{-2k}
 \{L_{\sigma,2k}(\Omega_k)-P_{2k}^{\rm abs}(\Omega_k)\}
 =-D_k^{\rm orb}.}
\end{equation}
Moreover the full actual far slot has the exact decomposition
\begin{equation}\label{eq:atom-rest}
 \cR_k=\cR_k^{\rm orb}+\cR_k^{\rm rest},
\end{equation}
where $\cR_k^{\rm rest}$ is the same localized ledger on
$F_{\sigma,k,A}\setminus\Omega_k$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A finite set has indicator zero almost everywhere, so $M_{\Omega_k}=0$ as
an operator on $L^2[0,1]$.  Therefore
$L_{\sigma,2k}(\Omega_k)=0$ exactly.  The derivative of $f^{2k}$ is the same
cyclic product at every marked point of one orbit.  By
\eqref{eq:multiplier-law} it is negative for large $k$, and the RH-334
folding theorem preserves it.  Each of the $2k-1$ folded marked points
therefore has deterministic weight
\begin{equation*}
 \frac1{|1-M_k|}=\frac1{1+|M_k|}.
\end{equation*}
This proves \eqref{eq:R-orb}.  Additivity of the localized noisy trace and of
the deterministic fixed-point sum across the disjoint partition
$F=\Omega_k\sqcup(F\setminus\Omega_k)$ proves \eqref{eq:atom-rest}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The windows remain the frozen observation]
$\Omega_k$ is an analytic subpartition of the already-frozen far set; it is
not promoted to a new canonical physical window or a fitted observation
coefficient.  Its dependence on the known deterministic orbit is used only
to expose one exact signed constituent of $\cR_k$.  The aggregate far slot
and all claims about it retain the original RH-334 windows.
\end{remark}

\section{Alias scale and signed compensation}

Retain
\begin{equation}\label{eq:beta-H}
 \beta=\frac1{r_H\sqrt\lambda},
 \qquad H_k=kR^{-2k},
 \qquad R=\frac75.
\end{equation}
The physical first-alias packet of RH-326 \citep{WangFirstAlias2026} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:alias}
 \cA_{k,2k}=(2k-2)\beta_k^{2k}+2\beta^{2k},
 \qquad
 \beta_k^{2k}=\frac{r_H^{-2k}}{|M_k|}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[The far atom is alias-sized and super-target]
\label{thm:scales}
As $k\to\infty$,
\begin{align}
 D_k^{\rm orb}
 &=\frac{2k}{C_M}\beta^{2k}\{1+o(1)\},
 \label{eq:D-asymptotic}\\
 \frac{D_k^{\rm orb}}{\cA_{k,2k}}
 &\longrightarrow1,
 \label{eq:D-over-A}\\
 \frac{D_k^{\rm orb}}{H_k}
 &=\frac{2}{C_M}(\beta R)^{2k}\{1+o(1)\}
 \longrightarrow+\infty.
 \label{eq:D-over-H}
\end{align}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Insert \eqref{eq:multiplier-law} into \eqref{eq:D-orb} and use
$\beta^{2k}=r_H^{-2k}\lambda^{-k}$ to obtain
\eqref{eq:D-asymptotic}.  The same multiplier law in \eqref{eq:alias}
gives
\begin{equation*}
 \cA_{k,2k}=\frac{2k}{C_M}\beta^{2k}\{1+o(1)\},
\end{equation*}
which proves \eqref{eq:D-over-A}.  Division by $H_k$ gives the first
identity in \eqref{eq:D-over-H}.  RH-336 certifies $\beta R>1$ exactly,
without relying on a rounded decimal \citep{WangProjectorMass2026}; hence the
limit is infinite.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Necessary diffuse compensation]
\label{cor:compensation}
The aggregate far estimate $\cR_k=o(H_k)$ holds if and only if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:compensation}
 \cR_k^{\rm rest}=D_k^{\rm orb}+o(H_k).
\end{equation}
Relative to the orbit mass, the required precision is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:relative-precision}
 o\!\left(\frac{H_k}{D_k^{\rm orb}}\right)
 =o\!\left((\beta R)^{-2k}\right).
\end{equation}
In particular, taking separate absolute values after the atom/rest split
cannot prove $\cR_k=o(H_k)$, because
\begin{equation}\label{eq:absolute-obstruction}
 \frac{|\cR_k^{\rm orb}|+|\cR_k^{\rm rest}|}{H_k}
 \ge\frac{D_k^{\rm orb}}{H_k}\longrightarrow+\infty.
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Substitute $\cR_k^{\rm orb}=-D_k^{\rm orb}$ into
\eqref{eq:atom-rest}.  This proves the equivalence.  The reciprocal of
\eqref{eq:D-over-H} gives \eqref{eq:relative-precision}, up to a harmless
positive constant.  Inequality \eqref{eq:absolute-obstruction} is immediate.
\end{proof}

This corollary does not say that signed compensation is absent.  It says
precisely what an aggregate positive theorem must prove.  RH-19's local tail
analysis explicitly leaves genuine bulk excursions to a missing resolvent
or moving-order theorem \citep{WangBoundaryTail2026}; the repository contains
no estimate of $\cR_k^{\rm rest}$ at the scale in
\eqref{eq:relative-precision}.  Accordingly both
$\cR_k=o(H_k)$ and aggregate nonvanishing remain
\texttt{NOT\_TESTABLE}.  After the clock obstruction of RH-337 there is also
no valid hatted actual/model pair typing a physical $\Delta\cR_k$
\citep{WangClockDrift2026}.  The result here concerns the actual physical far
slot itself, not RH-330 transfer \citep{WangTransfer2026}.

\section{Reproduction and claim boundary}

The artifact solves the physical algebraic constants and boundary inverse
word at high Decimal precision.  For $k=2,4,8,16,32$ and the diagnostic
choice $A=1/4$, it checks the counts $3,7,15,31,63$, orbit closure, multiplier
sign, approach of $D_k^{\rm orb}/\cA_{k,2k}$ to one, and growth of
$D_k^{\rm orb}/H_k$.  These rows reproduce the analytic formulas; they are
not interval certificates and do not prove the moving theorem.

The maximum rigorous conclusion is the exact physical orbit subledger,
its alias-scale asymptotic, and the necessary signed compensation law.  The
paper does not bound the diffuse rest, prove aggregate far vanishing or
nonvanishing, close the parity--alias or off-alias packets, transport the
noisy head, glue a determinant, or prove full-trace divergence.  No
Hilbert--Polya operator, Riemann-zero identification, von Mangoldt trace,
completed-zeta divisor equality, or Riemann-hypothesis proof is obtained.
Gates A--E remain false/open.
