# Roadmap after RH-338

RH-338 proves that the corrected physical far slot contains a canonical
boundary-orbit subledger of alias size:

    R_orb,k = -D_orb,k,
    D_orb,k / A_(k,2k) -> 1,
    D_orb,k / H_k -> +infinity.

This kills only the atom/rest separate-absolute proof route.  It does not
decide the aggregate signed far slot.  Any positive aggregate theorem must
prove the compensating diffuse law

    R_rest,k = D_orb,k + o(H_k)

with relative precision o((beta R)^(-2k)).  Any negative aggregate theorem
must instead show that this compensation fails by a nonzero H_k-normalized
amount.  The repository presently has neither estimate.

The original RH-339 punctured one-alias background route therefore cannot
assume that the critical packet is closed.  The next admissible paper must
remain theorem-backed and may take one of two forms:

1. a signed physical compensation or obstruction theorem for R_rest,k on the
   exact natural clock; or
2. an independent neighboring-sideband theorem that isolates a genuine
   positive lower obstruction without presupposing critical far closure.

Finite orbit enumeration, separate absolute tail sums, RH-329's wrong clock,
or an unidentified model remainder are not reopening inputs.  No actual/model
Delta_R, off-alias closure, noisy-head synchronization, determinant gluing,
or Gate A--E condition is active.
