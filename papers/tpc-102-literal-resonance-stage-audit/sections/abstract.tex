\begin{abstract}
TPC-93 through TPC-101 develop a literal low-frequency route for one
prescribed nonzero shift \(h_0\).  The sequence contains genuine
branch closures, exact finite-group reductions, and several sharp
warnings against false shortcuts.  This paper audits the combined
proof state and gives one consolidated inequality for the remaining
positive resonant carrier.

\(M_H^{\mathrm{inv}}=\|a_H^{\mathrm{inv}}\|_1\) be the remaining
invertible frequency-free multiplier census, let
\[

 \sqrt{\frac{2H(q-1-2H)}{q-1}},

\]

from TPC-100.  If \(W_X\) is the maximum normalized opened-atom

Thus the positive resonance path now has three explicit literal

nonconstant \(q_X\mid u\) branch are already returned at

We give proof-carrying stop criteria and a next-batch order:
