# Roadmap after RH-353

RH-353 closes the normalized actual signed-completion coordinates at the
critical order `2k` and the first-lower order `2k-2`.  Together with RH-352,
the actual lower-even branch is now known at the leading natural scale:

- RH-352 controls every growing `j>=2`, `J_k=o(k)` ladder coordinate;
- RH-353 controls the exceptional `j=0,1` boundary coordinates;
- the critical/first-lower remainder pair has the phase-free affine gap
  `Z_k^0-lambda Z_k^- -> 2-lambda`.

These results identify the actual signed supply that cancels the large
deterministic/parity packets.  They do not show that any unnormalized direct
coefficient is `o(H_m)`, so the physical prefix leaf remains open.

The strongest source-backed continuation is now to remove the parity
restriction and sum the actual direct coefficient cap over a full
near-alias band containing even, odd, critical, first-lower, and upper-alias
orders.  A theorem on

    2k-L_k <= n < 4k,    L_k=o(k),

with the same normalized `x^(-k)` scale would be genuinely broader than the
lower-even completion results and would audit the remaining high-order
`E_off` channels at one common coefficient type.

An unnormalized prefix theorem would still require cancellation beyond the
separate absolute caps, since `lambda^2(qR)^2>1`.  Low orders, head
transport, and a direct annular theorem remain independent routes.  RH-288
requires the complete direct prefix and both analytic tails in one physical
determinant data type.  Gates A--E remain false/open, and no RH conclusion
follows.
