# Derivation package

At fixed `H=66,N=264`, the TPC-406 local identities are independent of the
shell scale.  For each even complete shell, the alternating CRT residues make
the two local energies

    G_0=V_-S_0,
    G_1=V_-S_1+V_+(S_1-t_1^2),

and the adjacent coefficient is `M=t_1P_-`.  The chain
`G_1>=V_-S_1`, `P_-^2<=mV_-`, `V_->=m a_min^2`, and
`S_0,S_1>=H/4` yields `z<=4/(a_min H)<=4/H` at every declared Q.

The Q ladder is a finite exact extension, with no asymptotic inference.
