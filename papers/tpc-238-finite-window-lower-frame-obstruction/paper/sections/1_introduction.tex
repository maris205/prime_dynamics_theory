\section{Introduction}

Finite-window reassembly often turns arithmetic packet estimates into an
exponential sum whose frequencies are reduced rational points.  Once the
prime variable \(q\) has been collapsed, the remaining question is geometric:
can coefficients placed at distinct primitive fractions cancel strongly on a
long interval?  Upper-frame estimates of large-sieve type are classical; see,
for example, \cite{montgomery1994ten}.  The present paper requires the reverse
direction for a separated finite family.

We give a uniform lower bound with an explicit defect.  The proof inserts a
translated triangular window below the hard interval.  Its Fourier transform
is a Fejér kernel, so the weighted Gram matrix has diagonal \(L\) and
nonnegative off-diagonal magnitudes.  Reduced fractions of height at most
\(U\) are \(U^{-2}\)-separated.  Fejér decay and a one-dimensional circular
packing argument then bound the entire off-diagonal row by
\(\pi^2U^4/(12L)\).

The paper makes three falsifiable contributions.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.0em]
\item We prove the exact finite-window inequality
\[
\Energy_I(z)\geq
\pos{L-\frac{\pi^2U^4}{12L}}\normtwo{z}^{\,2}.
\]
\item We derive the normalized form
\[
\frac{\Energy_I(z)}{N}\geq
\pos{\frac12-\frac{\pi^2U^4}{6N^2}}\normtwo{z}^{\,2},
\]
including all parity and positive-part cases.
\item We specialize the defect to V59 and obtain
\(U^4/N^2=x^{-67/100+o(1)}\), which rules out a fixed-power gain arising
only from interference between distinct reduced frequencies after
\(q\)-collapse.
\end{enumerate}

The conclusion is deliberately scoped.  It redirects the search for saving
into the collapsed coefficient itself; it does not prove arithmetic
cancellation there.  Section~\ref{sec:setup} fixes notation.
Section~\ref{sec:theorem} proves the lower frame.
Section~\ref{sec:route} records the route consequence and claim firewall.
Section~\ref{sec:audit} describes the finite audit.
