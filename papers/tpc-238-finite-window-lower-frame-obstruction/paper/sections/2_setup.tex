\section{Finite-window setup}
\label{sec:setup}

Write \(\e(t)=\exp(2\pi i t)\), and let
\(\dist{\theta}=\min_{m\in\mathbb Z}|\theta-m|\) be circular distance to the
nearest integer.  A fraction \(a/h\pmod1\) is \emph{primitive} when
\(\gcd(a,h)=1\).  We use a finite set
\[
\FU=\left\{\frac ah\pmod1:\ 1\leq h\leq U,\ \gcd(a,h)=1\right\},
\]
or any subset consisting of distinct points of \(\T\).  The theorem also
applies to finitely supported coefficient families without taking the full
set.

Let
\[
I=\{M,M+1,\ldots,M+N-1\},\qquad
L=\left\lfloor\frac{N+1}{2}\right\rfloor,
\]
and define
\[
\Energy_I(z)=
\sum_{n\in I}\left|\sum_{\alpha\in\FU}z_\alpha\e(n\alpha)\right|^2.
\]
The norm \(\normtwo{z}^{\,2}\) means
\(\sum_{\alpha\in\FU}|z_\alpha|^2\).

\begin{theorem}[finite-window lower frame]
\label{thm:lower-frame}
For every \(N\geq1\), \(U\geq1\), interval \(I\) as above, and finite
coefficient vector on distinct primitive fractions of height at most \(U\),
\[
\Energy_I(z)\geq
\pos{L-\frac{\pi^2U^4}{12L}}\normtwo{z}^{\,2}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[normalized lower frame]
\label{cor:normalized}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:lower-frame},
\[
\frac{\Energy_I(z)}{N}\geq
\pos{\frac12-\frac{\pi^2U^4}{6N^2}}\normtwo{z}^{\,2}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[V59 specialization]
\label{cor:v59}
If \(U=x^{133/400}\) and \(N\asymp x\), then
\[
\frac{U^4}{N^2}=x^{-67/100+o(1)}.
\]
The normalized lower-frame constant in
Corollary~\ref{cor:normalized} is therefore \(1/2-o(1)\).
\end{corollary}

No sharpness assertion is attached to either constant.  The role of the
explicit constants is to separate a vanishing defect from a fixed-power
saving at the V59 scale.
