# TPC-386 paper plan

## Research question

Does the finite high-bandwidth phase transferred across origins in TPC-385
survive a predeclared increase from `N=512` to `N=1024`, and is the inherited
`0.64` spectral diagnostic compatible with that count change?

## Claim-sized contributions

1. A response-blind, coordinate-disjoint count holdout with a locked parent
   reference and two bandwidth masks.
2. An exact finite certificate for 160 rows, including rational anchor
   positivity/symmetry and a reverse-order independent replay.
3. A scoped obstruction: the inherited finite spectral cap fails on every
   all-plus `N=1024` row, even when the fixed three-block band is used.
4. A separation between a broad finite transfer envelope (all four forecast
   ratios are within 20%) and any count-uniform operator assertion.

## Planned sections

1. Motivation and claim firewall.
2. Count-split proxy and two bandwidth masks.
3. Exact anchor and numerical certificate protocol.
4. Results: count ratios, cap failures, and law controls.
5. Independent replay and mutation stress.
6. Obstruction interpretation and next clue.
