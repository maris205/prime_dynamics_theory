## First factor-class enlargement blocker

The theorem still assumes `c11(r)=0` phase by phase. Enlarging the active class
requires a natural-average theorem for every fixed-period phase-weighted
shift-two Mobius correlation generated by active `c11(r)`. No locked source
provides that theorem. This route is `STOP_SCOPED`, not silently averaged.

An orthogonal within-class successor may source-lock RH-2's prime-counting
PNT and prove, for each fixed `r`, asymptotics and scale separation for
`P_r(y)`. In particular, it may test and rigorously place the independent
`P_2` scale before the `T_y^3` layer. Such a source-backed scale dictionary
would be a separate theorem edge; it would not retroactively strengthen
RH-383 or promote any operator/RH Gate. RH-383 itself uses no PNT and makes
no such translation.

## Breadth-first reopen triggers

Other genuine routes remain available only when they acquire a new
source-backed theorem edge:

1. a fixed non-Parry equilibrium state with the required mixing theorem,
   rather than an externally selected measure;
2. a deterministic fractional/tower-adapted strong-space Ulam theorem with
   a mesh-independent norm, uniform lift/projection estimate, and a common
   contour around `-1`;
3. a composite-order primitive-divisor upgrade, exact return-radius law,
   finite-entropy-data theorem, or intrinsic globalization theorem that
   supplies its stated missing bridge;
4. one of the same-clock physical Gates A--E.

The next numbered paper must have independent theorem content. Additional
finite rows, an unsupported numerical rewrite, or a heuristic one-scale
collapse is not such a trigger. A separately source-locked analytic scale
dictionary is not excluded by this warning.
