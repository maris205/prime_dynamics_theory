# Roadmap after RH-369

RH-369 closes the fixed-parameter branch-symmetric Markov/Gibbs candidate on
the RH-366 graph.  The family is useful because it separates a genuine
nonadaptive measure theorem from the offline Möbius optimizer of RH-366:

``text
fixed t in (0,1) -> mixing Markov law -> a.s. Möbius orthogonality,
                 exact covariance and variance budget.
``

The Parry point \(t=\varphi^{-1}\) is recovered exactly, while all other
interior parameters are new derived measures.  The route does not select a
canonical \(t\), transport a measure to a physical trace, or close any Gate.

Next source-lock candidates, in priority order:

1. A common strong-space projector/resolvent theorem for the RH-367 finite
   Ulam family and a declared continuum operator.
2. A theorem or scoped negative for the RH-366 distance-two capacity limit.
3. A general mixing constraint-graph classification with arithmetic-weighted
   covariance and capacity identities.
4. The composite primitive-divisor, sharp-radius, finite-entropy-data,
   intrinsic-globalization, cyclic-noise, and original same-clock branches.

Do not claim a uniform theorem in \(t\), a geometrically selected Gibbs law,
or a prime-power/von-Mangoldt trace.  Gates A--E remain false/open.
