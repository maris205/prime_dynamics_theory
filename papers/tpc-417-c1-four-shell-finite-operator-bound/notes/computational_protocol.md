# Computational protocol

The producer regenerates every prime in each complete shell and computes all
aggregate and row-energy quantities as `Fraction` values.  The independent
checker uses a separate aggregation order and recomputes the four height rows.
The star certificate records the exact Cauchy--Schwarz envelope, not an exact
spectral norm or a direct sum with prohibitively large denominators.  The
stress checker mutates 14 typed/domain/firewall fields and rejects duplicate
keys and non-finite JSON.  Normal and optimized modes are both mandatory.
