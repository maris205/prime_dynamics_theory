\title{\textbf{Signed Prefixes and the Distinguished Zero Mode}\\
\large Sharp Abel-BV Duality, an Exact Exponent Transfer, and the

\author{Liang Wang}

\begin{abstract}
TPC-111 showed that the distinguished determinant zero mode survives
every provenance-preserving coarsening and proved the sharp
signed-prefix/BV duality.  We turn that finite criterion into a
complete exponent ledger.  For a growing family, a literal prefix
saving \(X^{-\delta}\), an outer BV-envelope cost \(X^{\ell_Z}\), and
a content-remainder exponent \(\eta_{\rm cont}\) imply
\[
 \eta_Z^{\rm cert}
 =\min\{\delta-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}.
\]
Here \(\delta\ge\ell_Z\) is required; otherwise this certificate
provides no nonnegative zero-mode exponent for the TPC-112 cone.
Combined with the TPC-112 determinant compatibility cone, sufficient
thresholds for this Abel/triangle certificate are
\[
 \delta\ge\ell_Z+\frac{\lambda_D}{2},
 \qquad
 \eta_{\rm cont}\ge\frac{\lambda_D}{2}.
\]
We also give the minimum rule for a subpower family of differently
scaled fiber classes.  This is an exact transfer statement, not a
proof of any growing input.  The finite duality is imported and
restated self-contained; the typed fixed-\(h_0\) ledger is
\(\Lzero/\Lone\).  No \(\Ltwo\) cancellation theorem is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{The literal ordered zero-mode pairing}

Fix the prescribed nonzero shift \(h_0\).  At the verified
outer-reassembly interface of TPC-111---that is, conditional on its
content, masks, polarizations and native keys being retained and the
literal outer reassembly being verified---the literal zero mode is
indexed by outer affine keys \(\theta\in\Theta_X\), with canonically
ordered intervals
\[
 \mathcal I_{\theta,X}
 =\{r_{\theta,1}<\cdots<r_{\theta,m_\theta}\},
\]
and writes
\[
 Z_X=
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}c_{\theta,X}
 \sum_{i=1}^{m_\theta}
 W_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i})
 \sigma_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i})
 +E_{\rm content,X}^{(111)}
\]
\citep{WangTPC111}.  Choose a predeclared retained set
\(\cF_X\subseteq\Theta_X\).  For \(f=\theta\in\cF_X\), set
\[
 t_{f,i}=r_{\theta,i},\qquad
 \sigma_{f,i}=\sigma_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i}),\qquad
 w_{f,i}=c_{\theta,X}W_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i}).
\]
Define \(E_{\rm cont,X}\) to be the original content term plus the
exact contributions of every outer key in
\(\Theta_X\setminus\cF_X\).  Then the complete literal zero mode is
\[
 Z_X=\sum_{f\in\cF_X}Z_f+E_{\rm cont,X},\qquad
 Z_f=\sum_{i=1}^{m_f}\sigma_{f,i}w_{f,i}.
\]
Thus \(\sigma_{f,i}\) retains the literal arithmetic sign and affine
data, while \(w_{f,i}\) retains the outer coefficient and
reconstruction multiplier.  The retained/content partition and this
factor allocation are fixed before applying the estimate.  Moving a
rough arithmetic factor between the two changes the gate and must be
recorded.

Define signed prefixes
\[
 S_{f,k}=\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_{f,i},\qquad
 \Delta_f=\max_{1\le k\le m_f}|S_{f,k}|,
\]
and the endpoint-anchored variation norm
\[
 \|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}
 =
 |w_{f,m_f}|
 +\sum_{k=1}^{m_f-1}|w_{f,k}-w_{f,k+1}|.
\]

\section{Imported Abel identity and sharp duality}

The next theorem is the finite duality already proved in TPC-111.  We
include its short proof to fix the exact norm used by the exponent
ledger; it is not presented as a second new duality theorem.

\begin{lemma}[Finite Abel identity]
\label{lem:abel}
For every complex pair of sequences
\((\sigma_i)_{i=1}^m\) and \((w_i)_{i=1}^m\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_iw_i
 =
 S_mw_m+\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}S_k(w_k-w_{k+1}),}
\qquad
 S_k=\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_i.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use \(\sigma_i=S_i-S_{i-1}\), with \(S_0=0\), and telescope.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp signed-prefix/BV duality]
\label{thm:duality}
For a fixed signed sequence \(\sigma=(\sigma_i)_{i=1}^m\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_{\|w\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}\le1}
 \left|\sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_iw_i\right|
 =\max_{1\le k\le m}|S_k|.}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\Cref{lem:abel} and the triangle inequality give the upper bound.
Choose \(k_*\) maximizing \(|S_k|\).  If \(S_{k_*}\ne0\), set
\[
 \omega=\frac{\overline{S_{k_*}}}{|S_{k_*}|},
 \qquad
 w_i=\begin{cases}
 \omega,&i\le k_*,\\
 0,&i>k_*.
 \end{cases}
\]
If \(k_*<m\), the norm has one jump of size one and zero endpoint;
if \(k_*=m\), it has endpoint one and no jump.  Thus
\(\|w\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}=1\), while the pairing equals
\(\omega S_{k_*}=|S_{k_*}|\).  The zero sequence is immediate.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Fiberwise sharpness]
The theorem is sharp for each fixed fiber.  After summing fibers, the
triangle inequality
\[
 |Z_X|\le\sum_f\Delta_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}
\]
is a sufficient outer certificate, not a necessary one: additional
inter-fiber cancellation may occur.
\end{remark}

\section{The growing exponent transfer}

Let \(A_f\ge0\) be a literal comparison scale for the signed
coefficient on fiber \(f\).  The future prefix theorem and the outer
reassembly theorem must be stated separately.

\begin{definition}[Typed prefix and envelope inputs]
\label{def:inputs}
For exponents \(\delta,\ell_Z\ge0\), define:
\begin{align}
\text{\rm(Prefix)}\qquad
&\Delta_f\le
 X^{-\delta+o(1)}A_f
 \quad\text{uniformly on the retained actual outer fibers};
\label{eq:prefix-input}\\
\text{\rm(Envelope)}\qquad
&\sum_{f\in\cF_X}
 A_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}
 \le X^{\ell_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2.
\label{eq:envelope-input}
\end{align}
In addition, a complete certificate must record
\begin{equation}
 |E_{\rm cont,X}|
 \le X^{-\eta_{\rm cont}+o(1)}Q_X^2
\label{eq:content-input}
\end{equation}
for some \(\eta_{\rm cont}\ge0\).  All three statements use the same
factor allocation and quantifier range.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Prefix-to-zero-mode transfer]
\label{thm:transfer}
If \eqref{eq:prefix-input}--\eqref{eq:content-input} hold and
\(\delta\ge\ell_Z\), then
\[
 \boxed{
 |Z_X|
 \le
 X^{-\eta_Z^{\rm cert}+o(1)}Q_X^2,}
\]
where the admissible zero-mode exponent is
\[
 \boxed{
 \eta_Z^{\rm cert}
 =\min\{\delta-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}.}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{thm:duality} fiberwise and sum:
\[
 |Z_X|
 \le\sum_f\Delta_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}
 \le X^{-\delta+o(1)}
 \sum_fA_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}.
\]
Now use the envelope input and add the content remainder.  The slower
of the two powers controls the sum.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[A negative displayed exponent]
If \(\delta<\ell_Z\), the prefix branch still gives
\(X^{-(\delta-\ell_Z)+o(1)}Q_X^2\), but it does not certify a
nonnegative saving exponent.  One must not enter a negative
\(\eta_Z^{\rm cert}\) into the TPC-112 cone.
\end{remark}

\section{Compatibility with determinant energy}

TPC-112 requires
\[
 D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,
\qquad
 |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,
\qquad
 \lambda_D\le2\eta_Z
\]
on the same literal post-bin coefficient \citep{WangTPC112}.

\begin{corollary}[Exact threshold for this Abel/triangle certificate]
\label{cor:threshold}
Under the typed inputs of \cref{def:inputs}, a separately certified
determinant exponent \(\lambda_D\) is compatible whenever both
\[
 \boxed{
 \delta\ge\ell_Z+\frac{\lambda_D}{2},
 \qquad
 \eta_{\rm cont}\ge\frac{\lambda_D}{2}.}
\]
The reserve in the determinant-zero cone is
\[
 \rho_{\rm det}
 =
 2\min\{\delta-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}-\lambda_D.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Insert the admissible exponent \(\eta_Z^{\rm cert}\) from
\cref{thm:transfer} into \(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\).
\end{proof}

Combining with the phase certificate of TPC-121, where one admissible
recorded exponent is
\[
\lambda_D^{\rm cert}
=(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+,
\]
gives the explicit joint target
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 \delta&\ge
 \ell_Z+
 \frac{(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+}{2},\\
 \eta_{\rm cont}&\ge
 \frac{(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+}{2}.
 \end{aligned}}
\]
This equation is bookkeeping, not a joint arithmetic theorem
\citep{WangTPC121}.

\begin{corollary}[Uniform subpower-class ledger]
\label{cor:classes}
Let \(\{\cF_{X,j}:j\in\mathcal J_X\}\) be a predeclared partition of
the retained actual fibers.  Suppose there are a common constant
\(C\), a common sequence \(\varepsilon_X\to0\), and nonnegative
numbers \(\delta_{X,j},\ell_{X,j}\) such that
\[
 |\mathcal J_X|\le X^{\varepsilon_X},
\]
\[
 \Delta_f\le
 C X^{-\delta_{X,j}+\varepsilon_X}A_f
 \quad(f\in\cF_{X,j}),
 \qquad
 \sum_{f\in\cF_{X,j}}
 A_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}
 \le C X^{\ell_{X,j}+\varepsilon_X}Q_X^2
\]
for every \(j\in\mathcal J_X\).  Assume also the content estimate
\[
 |E_{\rm cont,X}|
 \le C X^{-\eta_{\rm cont}+\varepsilon_X}Q_X^2.
\]
For any fixed \(\eta_{\rm cls}\ge0\) satisfying
\[
 \eta_{\rm cls}
 \le
 \liminf_{X\to\infty}
 \inf_{j\in\mathcal J_X}(\delta_{X,j}-\ell_{X,j}),
\]
where the infimum of the empty set is \(+\infty\) (in that case the
retained sum is zero),
one admissible recorded exponent is
\[
 \boxed{
 \eta_Z^{\rm cert}
 =\min\{\eta_{\rm cont},\eta_{\rm cls}\}.}
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
On class \(j\), \cref{thm:duality} and the two uniform bounds give
\[
 \sum_{f\in\cF_{X,j}}
 |Z_f|
 \le
 C^2X^{-(\delta_{X,j}-\ell_{X,j})+2\varepsilon_X}Q_X^2.
\]
The liminf condition makes
\(\delta_{X,j}-\ell_{X,j}\ge\eta_{\rm cls}-o(1)\) uniformly in \(j\).
Summing at most \(X^{\varepsilon_X}\) classes and adding the content
remainder proves the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Typed losses and no double charging]
The quantity \(\delta-\ell_Z\) and the content exponent
\(\eta_{\rm cont}\) have zero-mode/soft-remainder roles;
\(\lambda_D\) has the determinant-energy role; and
\(\Lambda_{\rm phys}\) has the physical-reassembly role.  A source
identifier may not be spent in two roles unless a theorem supplies
the corresponding joint inequality and records the dependency
replacement.  In particular, determinant reserve is not physical
endpoint slack.
\end{remark}

\section{Why all prefixes are necessary}

\begin{proposition}[The final sum is insufficient]
\label{prop:final}
For every \(N\ge1\) there is a real sequence of length \(2N\) with
\[
 S_{2N}=0,\qquad \max_{k\le2N}|S_k|=N.
\]
Hence cancellation of the complete unweighted fiber sum does not
control the zero-mode pairing against varying multipliers.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Take \(N\) entries equal to \(1\), followed by \(N\) entries equal to
\(-1\).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Ordering is part of the certificate]
The multiset of signed atoms determines the final sum but not the
maximum prefix.  Therefore a prefix theorem must use the canonical
literal order, or prove a permutation-uniform estimate strong enough
to include it.  Choosing a favorable order after seeing the signs is
not a provenance-preserving argument.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For \(N\) signs \(+1\) and \(N\) signs \(-1\), grouping equal signs
gives maximum prefix \(N\), whereas alternating signs gives maximum
prefix \(1\).
\end{proof}

The signed sequence imported from TPC-107 and TPC-108 contains actual
Möbius/Liouville-type factors \citep{WangTPC107,WangTPC108}.  A bound
for independent random signs, an averaged shift, or a rearranged
sequence does not prove \eqref{eq:prefix-input}.

\section{Audit certificate and next decision}

The deterministic script
\texttt{experiments/tpc122\_prefix\_transfer\_audit.py} checks the
complex Abel identity, the BV bound, the sharp step extremizer, the
large-prefix/final-zero counterexample, the TPC-111 factor-allocation
crosswalk, the content-aware compatibility/reserve ledger, and the
uniform subpower-class minimum rule.

\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.42\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule

\midrule
Abel identity and sharp BV duality
& Exact finite theorem (\(\Lzero\)).\\

TPC-122 reduces the zero-mode side to three typed estimates and
computes the exact saving delivered by this certificate.  It does not establish
any of them.  A next paper should inspect the literal ordering and
factor allocation on one actual fiber class; if the multiplier has
polynomial BV cost, the signed prefix saving must exceed that cost
before the determinant exponent is even considered.

\paragraph{Explicit exclusions.}
Nothing here proves H3, the actual prefix estimate, a Möbius or
Liouville cancellation theorem, a parity breach, a prime-pair lower
bound, a Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, or the twin-prime conjecture.
The strict physical endpoint
\(\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400\) remains independent.
