\begin{abstract}
TPC-101 reduces the diagonal part of the positive multiplicative
resonance carrier to two inputs: the resolved principal mass and a
pointwise cap \(W_X\) for the normalized opened-atom weights.
TPC-102 records \(W_X\le X^{o(1)}\) as the first inexpensive gate
after its three-ledger audit.  We close that gate in the literal
TPC-33--TPC-100 frame.

An invertible opened atom is \(p=(\beta,z)\), with exact weight
\[
 w_p
 =
 |A_{\beta,X}|\,|\mathcal W_{\beta,X}(z)|
 \mu^2(\widetilde D_\beta(z))\mu^2(V_\beta(z)).
\]
We pull this coefficient back through the lossless TPC-93
source--child inverse and place every factor in exactly one of six
ledgers: physical rows, the opened logarithmic leg, the matched
prefix, the row-gcd projector, exact content, and bounded
local/smooth data.  The literal row formula gives pointwise
\(O(\log X)\) coefficients; the logarithmic leg is \(O(\log X)\);
the matched prefixes, projector coefficients, and every remaining
divisor expansion are bounded by a fixed power of the maximal
divisor function on \(X^{O(1)}\).  No second column or fiber
normalization occurs.  Consequently, for a fixed integer \(A\),
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 W_X:=\max_{p\ {\rm active}}w_p
 \ll
 (\log X)^A
 \left(\max_{n\le X^{A}}\tau(n)\right)^A
 =X^{o(1)}.\quad}
\]
The maximum is over the actual \(q_X\nmid u_p\) support and retains
one prescribed \(h_0\ne0\), both polarizations, all source,
content, projector, interval and mask labels, and the inherited
physical normalization.

Combining the cap with the TPC-101 transfer shows that the
width-weighted opened-atom diagonal ledger follows from the
principal ledger; no separate diagonal-energy theorem is needed.
This is a genuine \(\Lone\) closure of Gate W, not an \(\Ltwo\)
arithmetic saving.  The principal mass, cross-atom collisions,
generic signed M\"obius sector, full-block return, and strict
physical endpoint remain open.
\end{abstract}

\section{The first post-audit gate}
\label{sec:introduction}

At the critical TPC scales,
\[
 Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad
 J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad q_X\asymp Q_X.
\]
After the conductor-one and \(q_X\mid u\) branches have been
returned, TPC-102 bounds the remaining positive resonant carrier by
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{exc},X}^{\mathrm{abs}}
 \ll
 X^{o(1)}Q_X^2+
 B_X\left[
 \PX+
 \sqrt{W_X|\HX|(q_X-1)\PX}+\XX
 \right].
\label{eq:master}
\end{equation}
Here \(B_X\ll1\) is the Wiener mass of the fixed low-window filter,
\(\PX\) is the principal resonance mass, \(\XX\) is the
width-weighted cross-atom excess, and \(W_X\) is the maximum
opened-atom weight on the actual invertible support
\citep{WangTPC101,WangTPC102}.

The purpose of this paper is deliberately narrow: prove the
pointwise bound
\[
 W_X\le X^{o(1)}
\]
from the literal factor list.  This is not a cancellation problem.
It is nevertheless not a consequence of the row \(\ell^1\) bound:
an abstract vector of \(\ell^1\)-mass \(Q_X\) may place all its mass
on one coordinate.  The actual TPC row coefficient has a much
stronger pointwise formula, and that formula must survive the entire
source--child crosswalk.

\section{The exact opened atom}
\label{sec:atom}

\subsection{Native record}

Let
\[
 \beta=(\theta,c,\kappa),\qquad
 \theta=(L,\gamma,\ell,j,\sigma,v,\iota)
\]
be a soluble, nonempty nonconstant branch in the canonical
TPC-94 ledger.  Put
\[
 b_\beta=c_\beta\kappa_\beta,\qquad
 g_\beta=(b_\beta,\sigma_\beta),\qquad
 B_\beta=b_\beta/g_\beta.
\]
The resolved progression is
\[
 t_\beta(z)=\tau_\beta+B_\beta z,\qquad
 z\in I_\beta^\ast.
\]
An \(r\)-free opened atom is
\[
 p=(\beta,z),\qquad
 u_p=U_\theta(t_\beta(z)),\qquad
 m_p=M_\theta(t_\beta(z)),
\]
and it obeys the exact source identity
\begin{equation}
 m_pj_\theta+h_0=\sigma_\theta u_p.
\label{eq:source}
\end{equation}
We restrict to the remaining invertible support
\[
 1\le N_\beta:=\#I_\beta^\ast\le q_X,\qquad q_X\nmid u_p.
\]
The restrictions are part of the actual support, not an ambient
rectangular completion.

TPC-94 and TPC-100 define
\begin{align}
 A_{\beta,X}
 &=
 c_{\theta,X}\mu(\kappa_\beta)\mu(B_\beta),
\label{eq:outer}\\
 w_p=w_{\beta,z}
 &=
 |A_{\beta,X}|\,
 |\mathcal W_{\beta,X}(z)|
 \mu^2(\widetilde D_\beta(z))
 \mu^2(V_\beta(z)).
\label{eq:weight}
\end{align}
On canonical support the robustness content is one.  Equation
\eqref{eq:weight} is the exact positive atomization:
\[
 w_\beta=\sum_{z\in I_\beta^\ast}w_{\beta,z}.
\]
There is no division by \(N_\beta\), no cell-count normalization,
and no second frequency factor \citep{WangTPC94,WangTPC100}.

\begin{definition}[Literal atom cap]
\label{def:cap}
Let \(\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}\) be the preceding actual support
after the single inherited normalization.  Set
\[
 W_X=
 \begin{cases}
 \max_{p\in\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}}w_p,
   &\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}\ne\varnothing,\\
 0,&\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}=\varnothing.
 \end{cases}
\]
\end{definition}

\subsection{Why the word ``normalized'' introduces no new scalar}

The physical units in \eqref{eq:weight} are exactly those of the
TPC-93 low-window coefficient.  TPC-93 explicitly reassembles the
two polarized physical rows and states that no new fiber
normalization is introduced.  Thus ``normalized opened atom'' means
an atom of the already normalized physical coefficient, not the
probability weight \(w_p/\sum w_p\).  In particular, this paper
does not insert a factor \(N_\beta^{-1}\), \(Q_X^{-1}\), or a
columnwise renormalization.

\eqref{eq:weight}, with the following meaning.

\begin{lemma}[Physical row factors]
\label{lem:rows}
Uniformly on the actual dyadic packet,
\[
 |\mathsf R_p|\ll_{\mathscr D}(\log X)^2.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The literal TPC-25/TPC-33 row formula is
\[
 \gamma_\alpha^{(i)}
 =
 \mu(d_\alpha)(\log\ell_\alpha)
 \omega_D^{(i)}(d_\alpha)
 \psi_L^{(i)}(\ell_\alpha/L)\zeta_\alpha^{(i)}.
\]
The final three factors are fixed and bounded on the physical
packet, while \(\ell_\alpha\le X^{O(1)}\).  Hence each row
coefficient is \(O_{\mathscr D}(\log X)\)
\citep{WangTPC25,WangTPC33}.  In fact TPC-25 records directly
\(\|\gamma^{(i)}\|_\infty\ll\log(2L)\).  Both polarizations use
the same estimate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Divisor-type factors]
\label{lem:divisor}
There are fixed constants \(A_0,C_0>0\), depending only on the
dyadic data and the fixed decomposition, such that
\[
 |\mathsf U_p\mathsf H_p\mathsf V_p
   \mathsf C_p\mathsf S_p|
 \ll
 (\log X)^{A_0}
 \Delta_X^{A_0},
 \qquad
 \Delta_X:=\max_{1\le n\le X^{C_0}}\tau(n).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
All effective targets, divisors, row coordinates, and content
variables are at most \(X^{C_0}\) for one fixed \(C_0\).
The opened logarithmic coefficient is at most \(O(\log X)\).
TPC-98 records the uniform bounds
\[
 \sum_{\substack{u\mid N\\T_X<u\le U_{0,X}}}
 |\mu(u)|\log u=X^{o(1)}
\]
and
\[
 |\mathsf H_m(j)|+
 |\mathsf A_{m,T_X}(j)|+
 |\mathsf A_{m,U_{0,X}}(j)|=X^{o(1)}
\]
on the literal support \citep{WangTPC98}.  More explicitly, these
are bounded by a fixed product of powers of \(\log X\) and divisor
functions of effective integers.

For the projector,
\[
 \lambda_{G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}(v)
 =
 \sum_{\substack{d\mid v\\d\le G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}}\mu(v/d),
\]
so \(|\lambda_{G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}(v)|\le\tau(v)\).
Every M\"obius factor, squarefree indicator, and coprimality mask
has modulus at most one.  The fixed smooth and residue factors are
bounded.  The absolutely convergent separation representation has
bounded total projective mass, so each of its retained coefficient
atoms is bounded by that mass.  The number of multiplicative
factor types is fixed, giving the displayed envelope.
\end{proof}

\section{Closure of Gate W}
\label{sec:cap}

\begin{theorem}[Normalized opened-atom cap]
\label{thm:cap}
For the actual TPC-100 invertible opened-atom support, with the
literal TPC-33--TPC-94 factors and the single inherited physical
normalization,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 W_X
 \ll_{\mathscr D,h_0}
 (\log X)^A
 \left(\max_{n\le X^A}\tau(n)\right)^A
 =X^{o(1)}}
\label{eq:cap}
\end{equation}
for one fixed \(A>0\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If the support is empty there is nothing to prove.  Otherwise apply
\cref{lem:rows,lem:divisor} to the exact partition
\eqref{eq:six-factor}.  The classical divisor estimate, uniformly
for \(n\le X^A\), is
\[
 \tau(n)
 \le
 \exp\!\left(O_A\!\left(\frac{\log X}{\log\log X}\right)\right)
 =X^{o(1)}.
\]
A fixed product of this quantity and powers of \(\log X\) remains
\(X^{o(1)}\).  No count over rows, children, contents, or branches
occurs in a pointwise maximum.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Uniformity and the fixed shift]
The exponent \(o(1)\) is uniform over the fixed dyadic packet data
and the one prescribed \(h_0\).  The result is not an average over
shifts.  The condition \(q_X\nmid u_p\) merely selects the actual
invertible support and does not enlarge the maximum.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[The diagonal ledger is no longer independent]
\label{cor:diagonal}
Let
\[
 \PX=
 \sum_H\frac{2H}{q_X-1}M_H^{\mathrm{inv}}.
\]
Then the TPC-101 principal-to-diagonal transfer becomes
\[
 \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}
 \le
 X^{o(1)}
 \sqrt{|\HX|(q_X-1)\PX}.
\]
Consequently, if
\[
 \PX\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,
\]
then
\[
 \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}
 \le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Insert \cref{thm:cap} into the exact TPC-101 transfer
\[
 \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}
 \le
 \sqrt{W_X|\HX|(q_X-1)\PX}.
\]
Use \(|\HX|\le(q_X-1)/2\) and \(q_X\asymp Q_X\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What was and was not removed]
The corollary removes the need for a separate positive
opened-atom diagonal theorem.  It does not prove the principal
bound, and it does not touch the distinct-atom cross ledger
\(\XX\).  In particular, same-branch different-\(z\) atoms still
collide in that cross ledger.
\end{remark}

\section{Why an \texorpdfstring{\(\ell^1\)}{l1} row bound was insufficient}
\label{sec:obstruction}

\begin{proposition}[Sharp abstract obstruction]
\label{prop:l1-obstruction}
Knowledge only of
\[
 \sum_\alpha|\gamma_\alpha^{(i)}|\le Q_X
\]
cannot imply a subpower opened-atom cap.  Indeed, there are
nonnegative abstract row families satisfying this inequality for
which one two-row atom has weight \(Q_X^2\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Take one active row in each polarization and give both rows
coefficient \(Q_X\); set all other row coefficients to zero and
all local factors to one.  Each \(\ell^1\) bound is saturated, while
the product atom has weight \(Q_X^2\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The counterexample is not physical]
\Cref{prop:l1-obstruction} does not contradict
\cref{thm:cap}: it deliberately discards the explicit TPC-33 row
formula.  It explains why TPC-101 and TPC-102 correctly isolated
Gate W instead of inferring it from an aggregate norm.
\end{remark}

\section{Finite provenance regression}
\label{sec:certificate}

The deterministic script
\texttt{experiments/tpc103\_atom\_cap\_audit.py} performs three
kinds of exact checks:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the row-gcd projector identity
\[
 \sum_{v\mid(d,e)}
 \sum_{\substack{g\mid v\\g\le G}}\mu(v/g)
 =
 \one_{(d,e)\le G};
\]
\item the source-to-child and child-to-source round trip on both
polarizations for finite primitive samples; and
\item a schema audit that assigns every literal factor category
to exactly one of the six ledgers in \eqref{eq:six-factor}.
\end{enumerate}
The committed JSON records the counts and the claim boundary.
These are regression tests for finite identities; they are not
numerical evidence for an asymptotic M\"obius estimate.  The
asymptotic input in \cref{thm:cap} is only the standard divisor
bound.

\section{Route consequence and claim boundary}
\label{sec:boundary}

\subsection*{Proved here}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The exact opened atom has a six-ledger factor audit with no
duplicated normalization.
\item Every literal pointwise factor is logarithmic, bounded, or
divisor-function sized on \(X^{O(1)}\).
\item The actual normalized cap satisfies \(W_X=X^{o(1)}\).
\item Therefore the TPC-101 width-weighted diagonal gate follows
from the unresolved principal gate.
\end{itemize}

The finite factor partition is \(\Lzero\); its lossless attachment
to the actual growing fixed-\(h_0\) carrier and the resulting cap
are \(\Lone\) bookkeeping progress.

\subsection*{Not proved here}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item No bound for \(\PX\) or \(\XX\) is proved.
\item No positive-resonance closure, generic affine M\"obius
cancellation, determinant lower bound, zero-mode saving,
high-frequency return, or TPC-18 full-block cover is proved.
\item No fixed-power saving for the complete growing fixed-\(h_0\)
coefficient is obtained; hence no \(\Ltwo\) event occurs.
\item No Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound,
parity breakthrough, or twin-prime conclusion is proved or implied.
\end{itemize}

The strict physical endpoint remains
\[
 \Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<\frac1{400}.
\]
The present subpower cap spends no fixed exponent in that ledger.
The next gate is the resolved principal-mass disintegration; the
cross-map quotient comes only after that positive principal term is
understood, in accordance with TPC-MVP1
\citep{WangTPCMVP1}.
