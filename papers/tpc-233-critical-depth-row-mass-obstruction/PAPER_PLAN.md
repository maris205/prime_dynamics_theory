# TPC-233 paper plan

## Question

Does the critical-depth dilated clock automatically provide the fixed row-mass
comparability assumed by the TPC-232 energy transfer?

## Contribution

Construct a critical sequence of primorial-saturated clocks for which low-cutoff rows
have two atoms while high-cutoff rows have asymptotically `2L/log L` atoms.  This
refutes fixed raw comparability as a structural consequence of the clock.

## Sections

1. Motivation and precise non-claim.
2. Raw row mass and universal bounds.
3. Critical primorial construction and prime-window lemma.
4. Exact mass separation and finite reproduction.
5. Consequences for the route.

## Claim class

`PROVED_ARITHMETIC_OBSTRUCTION_L1`.  No V59 source weight, signed cancellation, `L2`,
or full Gate-B claim.
