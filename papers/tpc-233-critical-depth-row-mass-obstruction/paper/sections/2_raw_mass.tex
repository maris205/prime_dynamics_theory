\section{Raw row mass and a universal envelope}

Fix integers $Q\ge8$ and $3\le L<Q/4$, put $h=4LQ$, and let $Q<q<2Q$ be prime.
The modeled support is
\[
 S_{q,L}=\left\{mq^{-1}\pmod h:
 0<|m|\le R_q,\ (m,h)=1\right\},
 \qquad R_q=\left\lfloor\frac{Lq}{Q}\right\rfloor .
\]
Since $R_q<2L<q$, the support is internally injective.  Write
\[
 A_h(R)=\#\{1\le m\le R:(m,h)=1\},
 \qquad N_q=|S_{q,L}|=2A_h(R_q).
\]
For a common uniform atom amplitude, diagonal row mass is a common scalar times
$N_q$.  We call $N_q$ the raw row mass, without identifying it with a physical source
weight.

\begin{lemma}[Universal comparability envelope]\label{lem:envelope}
For every admissible clock,
\[
 2\le N_q\le2(2L-1),
 \qquad
 1\le \kappa_{\raw}(Q,L):=
 \frac{\max_{q\in\Pcal_Q}N_q}{\min_{q\in\Pcal_Q}N_q}\le2L-1,
\]
whenever the prime shell $\Pcal_Q$ is nonempty.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The shell inequalities give $L\le R_q\le2L-1$.  The multiplier $1$ is always
primitive, so $A_h(R_q)\ge1$, while trivially $A_h(R_q)\le R_q\le2L-1$.
Taking the largest possible numerator and smallest possible denominator proves the
claim.
\end{proof}

The lemma already shows that fixed comparability is not a formal consequence of the
cutoff range: its best structure-only upper bound grows linearly in $L$.  The next
section constructs genuine active rows whose ratio diverges nearly linearly, up to one
logarithm.
