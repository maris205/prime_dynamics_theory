# RH-374 updated roadmap

RH-374 closes the first family-level extension of RH-373: the square-clock
one-site selector optima form a strictly increasing sequence with an exact
Euler-product limit, and that supremum is a valid lower floor for the RH-366
capacity.  It does not identify the adaptive capacity limit.

The shortest remaining routes are:

1. Compare the square-clock family with other fixed-clock families under an
   equally exact selector-class optimization, without calling any one family
   the all-clock optimum.
2. Prove a genuine run-envelope or convergence theorem for the RH-371
   eight-run expression, which would address the adaptive capacity rather
   than another externally prescribed arithmetic certificate.
3. Obtain a higher-order Möbius correlation theorem for genuinely
   memory-dependent safe transducers.
4. Reopen the strong-space Ulam route only after a mesh-independent norm,
   deterministic projection estimate, and common contour around `-1` exist.

No current route supplies a canonical operator, prime-power trace, zero
identification, Hilbert--Polya construction, or any Gate A--E result.
