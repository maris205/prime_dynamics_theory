# Derivation package

For each complete shell, $f_Q(x)=x^3/[Q^2(x-1)]$ has derivative
$x^2(2x-3)/[Q^2(x-1)^2]>0$. Since a selected prime is odd and $2Q$ is even,
$p\le2Q-1$, and therefore $1<f_Q(p)<4$. Even-block endpoint variation is
less than $3$ and odd-block endpoint amplitude is less than $4$.

The global alternating sum is decomposed shell by shell. If
$\epsilon_j=(-1)^{\sum_{\ell<j}n_\ell}$ is the start sign, the alternating
block lemma gives actual sign
$\sigma_j=\epsilon_j(-1)^{n_j+1}$ and magnitude at most $b_j$. Thus
$|A|\le B_*:=\max(\sum_{\sigma_j=+1}b_j,\sum_{\sigma_j=-1}b_j)$.
Odd shells alternate actual sign and even shells are bounded by the total
number of even shells, giving $B_*<3E+4\lceil O/2\rceil\le3K+1$.

For $N=4H$, the exact CRT mask and diagonal deletion give
$M_{0r}=P_-T_r$, $M_{rs}=-AT_{r-s}$ for interior off-diagonal entries, and

$$D_0=V_-S_0,\qquad D_r=V_-S_r+V_+(S_r-T_r^2).$$

Writing $Z=D^{-1/2}MD^{-1/2}=\left[\begin{smallmatrix}0&q^T\\q&C\end{smallmatrix}\right]$,
the endpoint star satisfies $\|q\|_2\le2/(a_{\min}\sqrt H)$ and the
symmetric interior bulk satisfies $\|C\|_2\le16B_*/V_-$. Hence

$$\|Z\|_2\le2/(a_{\min}\sqrt H)+16B_*/V_-.$$
