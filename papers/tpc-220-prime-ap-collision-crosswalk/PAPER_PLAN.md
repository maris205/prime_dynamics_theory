# TPC-220 paper plan

Research question: what exact arithmetic object represents the q-transverse energy of
the TPC-218 literal rows?

Contributions:

1. an exact weighted prime-AP crosswalk obtained by multiplying the row congruence by `q`;
2. an exact multiplicative collision Gram formula with primitive numerator conditions;
3. a diagonal lemma for `q=q'` under the inherited cutoff injectivity;
4. a rational finite certificate comparing direct rows, AP sums, and collision Gram entries.

The paper is a structural bridge. It does not invoke an asymptotic theorem for primes in
progressions and does not award arithmetic `L2` credit.
