\section{The minimum viable hypothesis stack}
\label{sec:hypotheses}

Set
\[
\sigma_*=\frac1{400}.
\]
The architecture has three macro hypotheses:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{\mathbf L\ \text{literal completeness}}
\quad+\quad
\boxed{\mathbf P\ \text{literal packet saving}}
\quad+\quad
\boxed{\mathbf S\ \text{physical synthesis}}\\
\Longrightarrow\quad B_{h_0,\delta}(X)=o(X).
\end{gathered}
\]
They are resolved below into nine gates.  The resolution is
intentionally redundant enough to be falsifiable: a failed gate names
the object and the route that failed.

\subsection{Macro hypothesis
\texorpdfstring{\(\mathbf L\)}{L}: literal completeness}

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H1: canonical physical synthesis]
\label{hyp:canonical-synthesis}
There is a provenance-preserving canonical packet family
\(\{\mathcal P_b\}_{b\in\BX}\), with
\(|\BX|=X^{o(1)}\), defined from the actual fixed-\(h_0\) coefficient.
On the actual support, this family is an exhaustive disjoint
classification into exceptional resonant, remaining resonant,
generic, and returned-complement packets; no physical block lies
outside these classes.
Its keys retain every invariant in
\cref{subsec:invariants}.  The synthesis map is the inherited physical
map, not an arbitrary signed representation, and its cost is entered
once in \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}\).
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H4: high-frequency and boundary return]
\label{hyp:high}
The high-frequency reconstruction tail, ultra-long residual
complement, short and bounded fibers, slope collisions, both
polarizations, endpoint bands, and outer keys are either
assigned to a soft remainder whose \emph{complete outer sum} is
\(o(X)\) in the original normalization, or retained as packet terms
that satisfy the same raw saving \(X^{1-\sigma_*+o(1)}\) used below.
Write \(\BX^{\mathrm{ns}}\subseteq\BX\) for the indices of these
retained nonsoft packets.
No favorable sector is silently promoted to the full coefficient.
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H6: full-block physical return]
\label{hyp:full-block}
The TPC-18 block family covers the literal physical tail in one
carrier and one quantifier scope.  Either an exact certificate
\(Bc=w\) holds, or the uncovered physical remainder is estimated
separately by \(o(X)\).  Overlap, missing blocks, and normalization
are included in the synthesis operator.
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H7: prescribed-shift localization]
\label{hyp:localization}
Every frozen, aliased, frequency-averaged, or auxiliary-parameter
estimate used by the route returns, with subcritical recorded cost, to
the same prescribed nonzero \(h_0\).  An almost-all or averaged-shift
statement does not satisfy this hypothesis.
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H8: literal tail reconnection]
\label{hyp:reconnection}
After all packet estimates and reassembly, the output intertwines
exactly with the original hard packet \(B_{h_0,\delta}(X)\) in
\eqref{eq:hard-packet-anchor}.  There is no second global
normalization, omitted branch, duplicated atom, or substitution of a
surrogate Gram matrix for the native physical Gram matrix.
\end{hypothesis}

Together, H1, H4, H6, H7, and H8 constitute macro hypothesis
\(\mathbf L\).  They are structural and analytic, but they are not
declared easy.

\subsection{Macro hypothesis
\texorpdfstring{\(\mathbf P\)}{P}: packet saving}

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H2: literal positive resonance]
\label{hyp:resonance}
On the actual growing TPC-102 invertible carrier,
\begin{equation}
B_X\le X^{o(1)},\qquad
W_X\le X^{o(1)},\qquad
\PX\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,\qquad
\XX\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.
\label{eq:resonance-hypothesis}
\end{equation}
These bounds use the opened atomic weights and active widths, not an
ambient branch census.  The exact inherited normalization transports
\eqref{eq:three-ledger}, including its leading exceptional term and
the two E5 returns, to the raw packet target
\[
|\mathcal P_{b,\mathrm{res}}|
\le X^{1-\sigma_*+o(1)}.
\]
\end{hypothesis}

The last sentence is part of the hypothesis because
\eqref{eq:three-ledger} is a native \(TT^*\)-scale statement.  A
normalization crosswalk must be proved rather than inferred by
dimension counting.

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H3: restricted generic affine arithmetic]
\label{hyp:generic}
For every actual long generic affine block, retain in the same sum the
literal M\"obius weights, the \(r\)-dependent phase, the affine
character, the reconstruction multiplier, all prefixes, and the fixed
\(h_0\).  Its normalized signed \(TT^*\) correlation satisfies the
working target
\begin{equation}
\mathcal G_{b,\mathrm{gen}}^{\mathrm{signed}}
\ll
XQ_b\,X^{-1/200+o(1)},
\label{eq:generic-ttstar}
\end{equation}
and, jointly, its exact physical \(TT^*\) normalization supplies the
packet target
\begin{equation}
|\mathcal P_{b,\mathrm{gen}}|
\ll
X^{1-\sigma_*+o(1)}.
\label{eq:generic-packet}
\end{equation}
The estimate is required only for the literal coefficient family
generated by the TPC dictionary, not for arbitrary slopes, arbitrary
weights, or a uniform Chowla family.
\end{hypothesis}

No derivation of \eqref{eq:generic-packet} from
\eqref{eq:generic-ttstar} is asserted in this paper.  The two displays
are joint parts of H3.  A future proof must exhibit the exact
normalizing prefactor that turns the \(1/200\) quadratic saving into a
\(1/400\) packet saving.

Hypothesis H3 is the parity-facing arithmetic gate.  It is the
boldest assumption in the paper and may contain most of the difficulty
of the original problem.  Calling it ``spectral'' does not make it a
proved cancellation theorem.

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H5: determinant and distinguished zero mode]
\label{hyp:det-zero}
On the actual post-bin coefficient, there exist exponents
\(\lambda_D,\eta_Z\ge0\) such that
\[
D_X\ge
X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,
\qquad
|Z_X|\le
X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,
\]
and
\[
\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z.
\]
The corresponding literal transfer inequality is assumed to
introduce no further fixed-power loss beyond the exponents already
charged in this determinant ledger and in
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}\).
Ambient, random-model, pre-bin, or support-only energy is not a
substitute for \(D_X\), and a nonzero-frequency spectral gap is not a
substitute for the estimate of \(Z_X\).
\end{hypothesis}

H2, H3, and H5 constitute macro hypothesis \(\mathbf P\).  H2 is a
sufficient positive route for the resonance sector.  If it fails, a
signed filter-before-majorant replacement may be inserted, but the
replacement must still deliver the packet bound at the same
normalization.

\subsection{Macro hypothesis
\texorpdfstring{\(\mathbf S\)}{S}: strict physical endpoint}

\begin{hypothesis}[\Htag\ H9: endpoint feasibility]
\label{hyp:endpoint}
After every frame, grouping, missing-high, localization, cover,
boundary, and native-Gram cost has been charged exactly once, the
physical synthesis operator obeys
\[
\left|
\mathscr S_X\bigl(\{\mathcal P_b\}_{b\in\BX^{\mathrm{ns}}}\bigr)
\right|
\le
X^{\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}+o(1)}
\max_{b\in\BX^{\mathrm{ns}}}|\mathcal P_b|,
\]
with
\[
\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<\sigma_*=\frac1{400}.
\]
\end{hypothesis}

Subpower block multiplicity is absorbed in the \(o(1)\).  Equality
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}=1/400\) does not close the route.  The
compatibility \(\lambda_D=2\eta_Z\) in H5 is not reusable as physical
endpoint slack.

\subsection{Strength and nonminimality}

\begin{remark}[The stack may be stronger than the target]
The conjunction H1--H9 is designed to imply a scalar
Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, but the converse is neither claimed nor
expected.  A scalar asymptotic need not imply a positive bound on
every fiber, a uniform cross-map majorant, or a canonical frame
estimate.  Thus ``minimum viable'' means a compact program exposing
the interfaces currently identified in this series; it does not mean
a logically minimal or independently easier reformulation of the
twin-prime problem.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The three resonance ledgers are not all remaining
obstacles]
The quantities \(W_X,\PX,\XX\) are the remaining ledgers in one
positive resonant subcarrier.  Generic signed arithmetic, full-block
return, fixed-\(h_0\) localization, determinant/zero-mode
compatibility, high-frequency return, and the strict endpoint remain
independent.
\end{remark}
