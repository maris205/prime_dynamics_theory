\begin{abstract}
For the literal post-bin determinant coefficient
\(A_X(n)\), the distinguished frequency is
\[
 Z_X=\sum_nA_X(n).
\]
We first prove that \(Z_X\) is exactly invariant under every
provenance-preserving partition and coarsening of the native atoms:
determinant binning can change energy but cannot create zero-mode
cancellation.  We then insert the literal fixed-\(h_0\) affine
M\"obius split.  On each ordered physical fiber the remaining sign
is
\[
 \sigma(r)=\mu^2(L_1(r))\mu^2(L_2(r))
            \lambda(L_1(r))\lambda(L_2(r)).
\]
If \(S(k)=\sum_{i\le k}\sigma(r_i)\), discrete summation by parts
gives the sharp dual identity
\[
 \sup_{\|W\|_{\BV}\le1}
 \left|\sum_i\sigma(r_i)W(r_i)\right|
 =\max_k|S(k)|.
\]
Thus the exact structure-free norm controlling the weighted zero
mode is a signed-prefix discrepancy, not determinant energy,
support density, or nonzero-frequency dispersion.

For the actual outer synthesis we obtain a fully explicit sufficient
criterion involving its literal coefficients, fiber variations,
signed-prefix discrepancies, and content remainder.  The criterion
is fiberwise sharp for uniform control over the declared weight
class; after summing outer keys it is sufficient and need not be
necessary.  No
power saving is proved for the growing physical signs.  This is an
\(\Lzero/\Lone\) reduction and obstruction theorem.  It does not
establish the exponent \(\eta_Z>0\), a parity breakthrough, or a
prime-pair conclusion.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} distinguished zero mode; determinant

\section{The distinguished frequency}

Fix a prescribed \(h_0\ne0\).  Let \(\cT_X\) be the canonical native
archive and \(\nu:\cT_X\to\cN_X\) the determinant map.  For literal
coefficients \(u_t\), define
\[
 A_X(n)=\sum_{\nu(t)=n}u_t,\qquad
 Z_X=\sum_{n\in\cN_X}A_X(n),\qquad
 D_X=\sum_{n\in\cN_X}|A_X(n)|^2.
\]
This is the normalized determinant-DFT frequency \(r=0\) of
TPC-32, not an orbit or transfer-operator zero mode
\citep{WangTPC32,WangTPC81}.

\begin{theorem}[Exact coarsening invariance]
\label{thm:invariance}
Let \(\pi:\cT_X\to Y\) be any finite partition map, and form
\[
 C_\pi(y)=\sum_{\pi(t)=y}u_t.
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{y\in Y}C_\pi(y)=\sum_{t\in\cT_X}u_t=Z_X.}
\]
In particular, determinant binning, content binning, or any further
exact coarsening preserves the distinguished zero mode.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The fibers of \(\pi\) form a disjoint partition of \(\cT_X\), so the
finite sums may be interchanged.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Energy behaves differently]
The identity in \cref{thm:invariance} has no analogue for
\(\sum_y|C_\pi(y)|^2\): coarsening creates cross terms and can either
raise or lower energy.  Hence an energy census and a zero-mode
estimate are logically independent.
\end{remark}

\section{Literal affine sign decomposition}

TPC-90 resolves a primitive physical branch into affine forms
\[
 L_{1,\theta,X}(r)=d_{\theta,X}+s_{\theta,X}r,\qquad
 L_{2,\theta,X}(r)=u_{\theta,X}+a_{\theta,X}r
\]
with the same prescribed determinant
\[
 s_{\theta,X}u_{\theta,X}
 -a_{\theta,X}d_{\theta,X}=h_0.
\]
Assume, on the declared block, that content, masks, both
polarizations, and all native outer keys have been retained and that
the literal outer reassembly has been verified.  Its zero-mode
interface then has the form
\begin{equation}
 Z_X
 =
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}
 c_{\theta,X}
 \sum_{r\in\cI_{\theta,X}}
 W_{\theta,X}(r)\sigma_{\theta,X}(r)
 +E_{\mathrm{content},X},
\label{eq:outer}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 \sigma_{\theta,X}(r)
 =
 \mu^2(L_1(r))\mu^2(L_2(r))
 \lambda(L_1(r))\lambda(L_2(r))
 \in\{-1,0,1\}.
\]
This is the exact split \(\mu=\mu^2\lambda\)
\citep{WangTPC90}.  Local squarefree density determines where
\(\sigma\) vanishes, but not the signs on its active support.

\section{Sharp signed-prefix duality}

We now prove the energy-free analytic statement used by the literal
zero-mode route.

\begin{definition}[Terminal variation norm]
For an ordered finite list \(w=(w_1,\dots,w_N)\), define
\[
 \|w\|_{\BV}
 =
 |w_N|+\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}|w_k-w_{k+1}|.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact Abel duality]
\label{thm:duality}
Let \(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_N\in\C\), and put
\[
 S_k=\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_i.
\]
Then every \(w\in\C^N\) satisfies the exact identity
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^N\sigma_iw_i
 =
 S_Nw_N+
 \sum_{k=1}^{N-1}S_k(w_k-w_{k+1}),
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_{\|w\|_{\BV}\le1}
 \left|\sum_{i=1}^N\sigma_iw_i\right|
 =
 \max_{1\le k\le N}|S_k|.}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put
\[
 d_k=w_k-w_{k+1}\quad(k<N),\qquad d_N=w_N.
\]
Then \(w_i=\sum_{k=i}^Nd_k\), so
\[
 \sum_i\sigma_iw_i=\sum_kS_kd_k.
\]
Also \(\|w\|_{\BV}=\sum_k|d_k|\).  The upper bound is
\(\ell^1\)-\(\ell^\infty\) duality.  Equality is obtained by placing
all \(d\)-mass at an index where \(|S_k|\) is maximal, with the
conjugate phase of \(S_k\).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Literal fiber bound]
\label{cor:fiber}
Order one actual interval
\(\cI_{\theta,X}=\{r_1<\cdots<r_N\}\), and define
\[
 \Delta_{\theta,X}
 =
 \max_{1\le k\le N}
 \left|\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_{\theta,X}(r_i)\right|,
\qquad
 V_{\theta,X}
 =
 \|(W_{\theta,X}(r_i))_{i=1}^N\|_{\BV}.
\]
Then
\[
 \left|
 \sum_{r\in\cI_{\theta,X}}
 W_{\theta,X}(r)\sigma_{\theta,X}(r)
 \right|
 \le
 \Delta_{\theta,X}V_{\theta,X},
\]
with optimal constant for the declared variation norm.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[The ordering is part of the statement]
The order is the literal translated integer order on the physical
fiber.  Reordering signs to improve their prefixes changes the
coefficient and is not permitted.
\end{remark}

\section{A complete energy-independent go criterion}

\begin{theorem}[Outer zero-mode certificate]
\label{thm:outer}
Assume the exact literal reassembly \eqref{eq:outer}.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 |Z_X|
 \le
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}
 |c_{\theta,X}|
 \Delta_{\theta,X}V_{\theta,X}
 +|E_{\mathrm{content},X}|.}
\]
Consequently, the desired estimate
\[
 |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2
\]
follows if the right-hand side has that size.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{cor:fiber} to every term of
\eqref{eq:outer}, then use the triangle inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Sufficient, not necessary for one physical weight]
The criterion allows no cancellation between different outer keys,
so it may be stronger than the scalar target.  For uniform estimates
over all weights with prescribed variation, however,
\cref{thm:duality} shows that the prefix discrepancy is the exact
operator norm.  A future proof may instead exploit literal outer
cancellation, but it must retain the same coefficients and
provenance.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Unsigned data cannot pass the criterion]
\label{prop:unsigned}
If the Liouville product in \(\sigma_{\theta,X}\) is replaced by the
constant sign \(1\), then
\[
 \Delta_{\theta,X}
 =
 \#\{r_i:\mu^2(L_1(r_i))\mu^2(L_2(r_i))=1\}.
\]
Thus squarefree-support density, by itself, gives the full active
mass rather than cancellation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every nonzero summand is \(+1\), so the prefix sums are nonnegative
and maximal at the terminal prefix.
\end{proof}

\section{Why determinant energy does not estimate the zero mode}

For a vector \(A=(A(n))_{n\in\cN}\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives only
\[
 |Z(A)|^2\le|\cN|\,D(A).
\]
It is sharp for a constant vector.  On the same support and with the
same moduli, roots of unity can have zero sum.  This finite
nonidentifiability was isolated in TPC-82
\citep{WangTPC82}.  TPC-97 further shows that
constant-preserving projection preserves \(Z\) while reducing
energy, so it cannot manufacture a better numerator
\citep{WangTPC97}.

\begin{theorem}[Independent-gate verdict]
\label{thm:verdict}
None of the following data implies a positive exponent
\(\eta_Z\) for the actual growing coefficient:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item determinant support and multiplicities;
\item the value or a lower bound of \(D_X\);
\item unsigned squarefree density;
\item cancellation at every nonzero determinant-DFT frequency; or
\item exact provenance-preserving coarsening.
\end{enumerate}
A proof of \(\eta_Z>0\) must estimate the literal signed prefixes,
exploit a comparably strong outer cancellation, or introduce another
arithmetic theorem that controls the same scalar \(Z_X\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Items (i)--(ii) are defeated by aligned and root-of-unity vectors
with the same support and moduli.  Item (iii) is
\cref{prop:unsigned}.  A constant signal has no nonzero Fourier
frequencies but has maximal zero mode, proving (iv).  Item (v) is
\cref{thm:invariance}.
\end{proof}

TPC-111 therefore produces a sharp \emph{criterion}, not the desired
cancellation estimate.  TPC-112 may combine a future
\(\eta_Z\) with a future determinant exponent \(\lambda_D\), but it
must record the current values as ``unproved'' and keep the physical
endpoint ledger separate.

\paragraph{Explicit exclusions.}
No theorem here controls a growing two-form Liouville or M\"obius
correlation at fixed \(h_0\).  Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).
The paper proves no \(\Ltwo\) saving, parity breach,
Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime
theorem.
