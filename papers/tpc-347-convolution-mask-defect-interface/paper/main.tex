\begin{abstract}
We isolate the two mechanisms in the literal deleted--diagonal prime--shell
matrix used in the current twin--prime route.  The residue kernel is a
translation--invariant convolution on $\mathbb Z$, whereas the two endpoint
divisibility masks are coordinate projections.  We prove an exact
convolution--plus--defect factorisation, the Fourier multiplier formula for
the unmasked convolution on $\el(\mathbb Z)$, and a finite triangle envelope
which retains the mask defect.  A guarded audit on two disjoint origins,
three source counts, four shells, two kernel exponents, and four sign laws
contains 192 rows.  The ideal matrix is translation invariant in all 96
parameter groups, but the defect-to-ideal spectral ratio reaches
$0.467075645603$ and exceeds $1/4$ in 93 rows.  The result is a precise
interface and a finite obstruction to discarding the masks; it is not an
arithmetic $L^2$ estimate, a power saving, or a twin-prime theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and scope}

The recent finite route has repeatedly encountered a distinction between a
signed source calculation and the physical output operator.  Here we return
to the unresolved source-uniform $L^2$ question at the operator level.  The
question is deliberately narrow: can the literal matrix be compared with a
translation-invariant object without erasing the divisibility information?

Throughout, $Q<p\leq 2Q$ ranges over primes, $H=66$, and $s\in\{1,2\}$.
For $d\in\Z$ define
\begin{equation}
 k_p(d)=\begin{cases}
 0,&d=0,\\[2mm]
 \displaystyle p\frac{H^{2s}}{(H^2+d^2)^s}
 \left(\mathbf 1_{p\mid d}-\frac1{p-1}\right),&d\ne0.
 \end{cases} \label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}
For a sign law $e=(e_p)$ let $K_e=\sum_{Q<p\leq2Q}e_pK_p$, where
$(K_pf)(u)=\sum_d k_p(d)f(u-d)$.  The physical finite matrix on an interval
$I$ has entries
\begin{equation}
 A_I(u,t)=\sum_p e_p\,\mathbf 1_{u\ne t}\mathbf 1_{p\nmid u}
 \mathbf 1_{p\nmid t}\,p\frac{H^{2s}}{(H^2+(u-t)^2)^s}
 \left(\mathbf 1_{p\mid u-t}-\frac1{p-1}\right). \label{eq:physical}
\end{equation}
No source vector is needed for this interface audit.  In particular, the
finite table below must not be read as evidence for a twin-prime density.

\section{Exact factorisation}

Let $P_p$ be multiplication by $\mathbf 1_{p\nmid n}$ on sequences, and let
$E_I$ extend a vector on $I$ by zero while $R_I$ restricts a sequence to $I$.

\begin{proposition}[mask factorisation]
With $K_p$ defined by \eqref{eq:kernel}, the physical operator is
\begin{equation}
 A_I=\sum_p e_pR_IP_pK_pP_pE_I. \label{eq:factor}
\end{equation}
If $T_I=R_IK_eE_I$ and $D_I=A_I-T_I$, then
\begin{equation}
 D_I=\sum_p e_pR_I\bigl((P_p-I)K_pP_p+K_p(P_p-I)\bigr)E_I. \label{eq:defect}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The two projections in \eqref{eq:factor} remove exactly the entries whose
endpoints are divisible by $p$, and $k_p(0)=0$ removes the diagonal.  This
gives \eqref{eq:physical} entry by entry.  For one prime,
$(P-I)KP+K(P-I)=PKP-K$; summing and applying restriction and extension gives
\eqref{eq:defect}.  All sums over the shell are finite.
\end{proof}

The decomposition is more informative than a generic Frobenius envelope:
$T_I$ is the part to which Fourier analysis applies, while $D_I$ records
every position-sensitive mask contribution.

\section{The unmasked Fourier interface}

Since $|k_p(d)|\ll_{p,H,s}|d|^{-2s}$ for $d\ne0$, $k_e\in\ell^1(\Z)$.
Its Fourier series
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{k}_e(\theta)=\sum_{d\in\Z}k_e(d)e^{-id\theta},
 \qquad -\pi\leq\theta\leq\pi,
 \label{eq:symbol}
\end{equation}
is continuous and periodic.

\begin{theorem}[Fourier and compression bounds]
For the explicitly unmasked operator $K_e$,
\begin{equation}
 \norm{K_e}_{\ell^2(\Z)\to\ell^2(\Z)}
 =\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\theta\in[-\pi,\pi]}|\widehat{k}_e(\theta)|
 \leq\sum_{d\in\Z}|k_e(d)|. \label{eq:fourierbound}
\end{equation}
For every finite interval $I$,
\begin{equation}
 \norm{A_I}\leq\norm{T_I}+\norm{D_I}
 \leq\norm{K_e}+\norm{D_I}_F. \label{eq:triangle}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Fourier transform on $\ell^2(\Z)$ is unitary and sends convolution by
$k_e$ to multiplication by \eqref{eq:symbol}; the norm of a multiplication
operator is the essential supremum.  Young's inequality gives the second
inequality in \eqref{eq:fourierbound}.  Since $R_I$ and $E_I$ have norm one,
$\norm{T_I}\leq\norm{K_e}$.  Apply the triangle inequality to
$A_I=T_I+D_I$ and then $\norm{D_I}\leq\norm{D_I}_F$.
\end{proof}

For $R\geq1$, the tail in the Young envelope has the explicit bound
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{|d|>R}|k_e(d)|
 \leq \frac{2H^{2s}\sum_{Q<p\leq2Q}p}{(2s-1)R^{2s-1}}. \label{eq:tail}
\end{equation}
Indeed, the centered residue factor has absolute value at most one and
$\sum_{d>R}d^{-2s}\leq R^{1-2s}/(2s-1)$.  Equation \eqref{eq:tail} is a
sign-free baseline; it supplies no cancellation.

\section{Finite audit}

The protocol uses origins $40097$ and $48097$, source counts
$M\in\{256,512,1024\}$, $Q\in\{24,36,54,80\}$, $s\in\{1,2\}$, and the
four sign laws `all-plus', alternating shell index, the mod--4 character, and
the half split.  Thus
$2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot2\cdot4=192$ physical/ideal/defect rows.  The Young
finite sum uses $R=65536$ and the analytic tail in \eqref{eq:tail}.

For each row we compute the symmetric spectral norms of $A_I$, $T_I$, and
$D_I$, and the Frobenius norm of $D_I$.  The certificate also checks
\begin{equation}
 \norm{A_I}\leq\norm{K_e}+\norm{D_I}_F. \label{eq:finitecertificate}
\end{equation}
For each fixed $(M,Q,s,e)$, translating the interval changes neither the
differences nor the ideal matrix.  There are 96 such two-origin checks.

\section{Adversarial checks and claim boundary}

The independent checker rebuilds the shells and matrices in reverse prime
order, recomputes all spectral quantities, and verifies the canonical JSON
payload.  A rational six-point anchor with $Q=4$ and shell $\{5,7\}$ checks
the identity $A_I=T_I+D_I$ and symmetry without floating point.  The stress
suite attacks a sign-flipped defect, a reintroduced diagonal, random
projection contractions, and the analytic tail scale.  Normal and optimized
executions agree byte-for-byte at the reporting layer and produce empty
standard error.

\begin{remark}
The finite obstruction does not say that every possible mask estimate fails,
nor that the defect has a positive asymptotic lower bound.  It says only that
the current finite physical object is not faithfully represented by its
unmasked convolution on the tested panel.  Any future arithmetic $L^2$
argument must either control $D_I$ with its position dependence or prove
cancellation for the masked sum directly.
\end{remark}

\section{Conclusion and next question}

The main route contribution is a typed interface: Fourier analysis is
available for a precisely identified unmasked component, and the missing
piece is an explicit operator defect rather than an implicit normalization
error.  The finite audit shows that this defect is sometimes substantial,
with 93 of 192 rows above the quarter threshold.  We therefore freeze the
shortcut of dropping the masks and return to the position-aware arithmetic
$L^2$ problem.  No arithmetic advance is claimed in this release.
