# RH-378 updated roadmap

## Closed in this paper

- Universal safety for any fixed periodic finite window reduces exactly to a
  finite compatible-block test.
- The complete lag-two table class is exhausted: 13 safe tables and a
  rank-five arithmetic ledger.
- The precise arithmetic boundary of the six hard lag tables is ordinary
  shift-two Chowla cancellation.
- Two fixed causal machines realize the endpoint extrema, while a single
  deterministic all-branch/all-prefix capacity-optimal policy is impossible.
- The frozen greedy stream has exact four-state complexity, and on the
  run-at-most-eight class its stateless contiguous window complexity is 15.

## First unresolved objects

1. Prove `D2(N)=o(N)` by a genuine ordinary shift-two Chowla theorem, or
   supply a theorem that implies it.  Logarithmic cancellation and finite
   rows do not suffice.
2. Resolve convergence of the RH-371/RH-377 two-envelope capacity.  Exact
   online computation does not create the missing arithmetic limit.
3. For broader memory tables, identify a source-backed higher-order Möbius
   correlation theorem before enlarging the census.  Formal basis dimension
   alone is not a theorem edge.

Route A remains `GO` for exact finite automata and scoped classification.
Route B remains `STOP_SCOPED` at the two arithmetic limits above.  Reopen only
with a new theorem-backed bridge, not with larger finite extrapolation.
