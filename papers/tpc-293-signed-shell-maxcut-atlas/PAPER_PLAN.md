# TPC-293 paper plan

## Question

TPC-292 shows that three pairwise cancellation preferences are controlled by
triangle parity. Does the obstruction remain local, or does an entire prime
shell admit a useful global coefficient-sign assignment?

## Claim spine

1. Encode every nonzero physical Gram cross term by a signed edge.
2. Define favorable cancellation edges by
   `a_i a_j sign(G_ij)=-1` and maximize their number over vertex labels.
3. Prove the all-positive `K_m` benchmark `floor(m^2/4)` and the exact
   frustration-index complement identity.
4. Recompute the signed complete graph on the inherited 18-row literal grid,
   including the earlier triangle census as a consistency diagnostic.
5. Interpret the sole finite `+3` exception as a clue for a magnitude-weighted
   Rayleigh test, not as physical cancellation credit.

## Claim ceiling

The graph identities are exact finite combinatorics. The atlas is an
exact-rational finite certificate generated from the frozen physical outputs.
No theorem for growing shells, no source-restricted coefficient feasibility,
no arithmetic `L2` estimate, no fixed-power credit, no Gate-B closure, and no
twin-prime conclusion is claimed.
