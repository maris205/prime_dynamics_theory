\large A Route Review, One Shift No-Go Theorem, and Two Surviving Physical Frontiers}
\author{Liang Wang}

\begin{abstract}
RH-171 identified the physical packet-to-Riesz interface as the first open
operator wall inside Gate A and split it into ambient realization, validated
data, uniform margins, and shellwise transport.  RH-172--180 investigate the
ambient leaf from the actual source-memory definition rather than from an
abstract equal-rank packet.

The reset memory has an exact finite history realization:
$M_t=F_t^*F_t$, with a canonical polar packet and an exact rectangular
cocycle $F_{t+1}=T_tF_t$.  On all 130 archived snapshots these identities and
the stable polar factors are numerically sound.  However, none of the 120
consecutive updates passes a symmetric $0.25$ primal/adjoint residual gate;
the adjoint residual is always at least $0.3233$.  The simplest consecutive
reset reducing-bundle hypothesis is therefore not supported by the finite
data.

The literal square infinite-history completion is rigorously rejected as a
direct determinant operator: it contains a weighted unilateral shift, has a
spectral disk, and no positive power is compact or Schatten.  A finite cyclic
closure survives.  Its reduced determinant is exactly $\Pi_N$, two copies
give the RH-80 factor $\Pi_N^2$, and a compressed rank-one edge marker
separates forward from reverse orientation by exactly one.  The seven RH-15
cloud rows imply a formal rank-to-cycle offset in $\{3,4\}$, but only one row
overlaps the certified reset atlas.  An explicit conditional Schur theorem
then lifts every doubled cycle root to a rank-two Riesz shell if a physical
cycle embedding and uniform resolvent/coupling budgets are supplied.

We formalize two surviving architecture-relative frontiers.  The reset-
history branch is missing history-to-transfer realization, physical data,
uniform margins, and shell transport.  The finite-cycle branch is missing
cycle calibration, cycle-to-transfer realization, and the same three
physical controls.  An aggregate audit records 2,600 finite formula or shell
items with zero implementation/rank failures; this count is not a statistical
sample size and coexists with the intentional RH-174 negative result.

Neither branch closes physical R, and the cycle branch would require a typed
redesign of the reset-seeded Gate-A assembly.  Q, U, Z, T and macro Gate A
remain open; Gates B--E are untouched.  No Hilbert--Polya or Riemann-
hypothesis conclusion is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Coordinate before this batch}

RH-161 gives a typed conditional assembly for a moving relative determinant
once six obligations are met: a seed S, packet-to-Riesz lift R, cloud ledger
Q, complementary Schatten limit U, canonical normalization Z, and directed
marked limit T \citep{WangRH161}.  RH-162--171 then resolve the abstract
proof architecture of R and arrive at
\begin{equation}\label{eq:old-R}
 R=X_{\rm phys}\wedge D_{\rm phys}\wedge
 K_{\rm phys}\wedge H_{\rm phys},
\end{equation}
where X is ambient realization, D validated finite block data, K uniform
Schur/graph margins, and H shellwise common-coordinate transport
\citep{WangRH171}.

At that point all four leaves remained physically open.  X was first because
the RH-151 reset packet belongs to a source-memory Gram while the RH-80 cloud
belongs to a noisy transfer operator.  The present batch asks whether the
special form of that Gram supplies a canonical realization and, if so,
whether that realization can support a determinant.

\section{The reset-history branch}

\subsection{What is now closed}

For $S_t=A^tS_0$ and $\widehat S_t=S_t/\norm{S_t}_F$, define
\[
 F_tx=(\widehat S_tx,\sqrt\eta\widehat S_{t-1}x,
 \ldots,\eta^{t/2}\widehat S_0x).
\]
RH-172 proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gram}
 M_t=F_t^*F_t
\end{equation}
and realizes every positive packet projection $P_t$ by the canonical partial
polar isometry
\begin{equation}\label{eq:polar}
 J_t=F_tP_t(P_tM_tP_t|_{\operatorname{Ran}P_t})^{-1/2}.
\end{equation}
This is invariant under source-coordinate gauge and packet-frame gauge
\citep{WangRH172}.

RH-173 proves the exact time update
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cocycle}
 F_{t+1}=T_tF_t,
 \qquad
 T_t(y_0,\ldots,y_t)=
 (r_tAy_0,\sqrt\eta y_0,\ldots,\sqrt\eta y_t),
\end{equation}
with
\[
 T_t^*T_t=operatorname{diag}(r_t^2A^*A+\eta I,
 \eta I,\ldots,\eta I).
\]
Hence the finite source-memory-to-history leaf
\[
 X_{\rm mem\to hist}
\]
is closed exactly.  This is a genuine improvement over the RH-171 status,
where no canonical ambient realization was known.

\subsection{What the physical audit says}

The RH-174 reconstruction covers five scales, two channels, 130 packets, and
120 updates \citep{WangRH174}.  The maximum Gram and cocycle residuals are
below $10^{-15}$, and all stable SVD polar packets have isometry defect below
$3.6\times10^{-15}$.  The direct inverse-square-root formula becomes
inaccurate at selected condition numbers up to $3.61\times10^{12}$, but the
stable polar subspace remains well defined.

Temporal coherence is asymmetric.  The primal median residual is $0.0288$,
but the adjoint median is $0.7130$ and the adjoint minimum is $0.3233$.  The
transported reset distance reaches $0.99999965$, with 23 values at least
$0.90$.  Therefore the data do not support treating independently reset top
packets as a two-sided reducing bundle for the one-step history cocycle.

This is not an all-level no-go theorem.  It leaves open lagged packets,
block-time packets, separate primal/dual packets, and direct transfer-space
constructions.

\subsection{Why the literal infinite completion is closed off}

The most direct square completion of \eqref{eq:cocycle} acts on infinite
history by
\[
 \mathcal L(y_0,y_1,\ldots)
 =(By_0,qy_0,qy_1,\ldots),
 \qquad q=\sqrt\eta.
\]
RH-175 proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shift-spectrum}
 \sigma(\mathcal L)=\sigma(B)\cup\overline{D(0,q)},
 \qquad
 \sigma_{\rm e}(\mathcal L)=\partial D(0,q),
\end{equation}
and no positive power is compact or Schatten \citep{WangRH175}.  Thus
\[
 \text{literal infinite history}
 \not\longrightarrow
 \text{direct Fredholm determinant}.
\]
Relative scattering against the shift background remains possible, but it is
a different typed object.

\section{The finite-cycle branch}

\subsection{Exact determinant model}

For the length-$L$ cycle $C_L$ and zero-mean restriction $C_L^\circ$,
RH-176 proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cycle-det}
 \det(I-qC_L^\circ)=\frac{1-q^L}{1-q}=\Pi_{L-1}(q).
\end{equation}
The cycle differs from the nilpotent finite shift by one rank-one wrap edge of
operator norm one.  It converges locally/strongly to the unilateral dynamics,
not in operator norm \citep{WangRH176}.

With $L=N+1$, RH-177 defines
\begin{equation}\label{eq:double-cycle}
 K_N=\lambda^{-1}
 (C_L^\circ\oplus C_L^\circ)
\end{equation}
and proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rh80-factor}
 \det(I-wK_N)=\Pi_N(w/\lambda)^2,
\end{equation}
exactly the canonical RH-80 cloud factor \citep{WangRH177}.  The complete
power-trace ledger is also explicit.  This closes an algebraic model leaf,
not the physical cloud coefficient ledger.

\subsection{Direction is additional data}

Forward and reverse cycles have identical determinants and ordinary traces.
RH-178 compresses the edge marker $J=|e_0\rangle\langle e_1|$ to the
zero-mean space and proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:orientation}
 \Tr(J^\circ C_L^\circ)=1-L^{-1},
 \qquad
 \Tr(J^\circ(C_L^\circ)^{-1})=-L^{-1}.
\end{equation}
The orientation gap is one and is stable under operator perturbations by a
trace-duality bound \citep{WangRH178}.  Therefore the appropriate finite datum
is
\[
 (\text{cloud determinant},\text{directed marked traces}),
\]
exactly the enhanced type anticipated by RH-161.

\subsection{Clock calibration is narrow but not unique}

Let
$h_\sigma=\log(1/\sigma)/(2\log\lambda)$,
$r_\sigma=\lceil h_\sigma\rceil+2$,
$L_\sigma=N_\sigma+1$, and
$d_\sigma=h_\sigma-N_\sigma$.  RH-179 proves the exact identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:clock}
 r_\sigma-L_\sigma=\lceil d_\sigma\rceil+1.
\end{equation}
On all seven RH-15 cloud rows, the right side is three or four
\citep{WangRH179}.  Only $\sigma=0.01$ overlaps the actual RH-151 reset atlas;
there $r=7$, $N=3$, and $L=4$.  The remaining six ranks are formal clock
predictions.  Therefore the cycle length is constrained but not physically
selected.

\subsection{Conditional Riesz shells}

The doubled reduced cycle has roots
$\zeta_k=\rho e^{2\pi ik/L}$ of multiplicity two and half-spacing
$s_L=\rho\sin(\pi/L)$.  RH-180 considers a physical block candidate
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}K_L+E&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix}
\]
and circles of radius $\delta<s_L$.  If $\norm E=\varepsilon<\delta$, the
complement disks are spectrum-free with contour resolvent bound $d$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cyclic-schur}
 \frac{d\norm B\norm C}{\delta-\varepsilon}<1,
\end{equation}
then every root circle encloses a rank-two Riesz projection
\citep{WangRH180}.  This is the exact finite theorem needed after a physical
cycle embedding is found.

\section{Two surviving architecture-relative frontiers}

The old leaf $X_{\rm phys}$ is no longer a single opaque request.  There are
two candidate routes to a physical finite cloud seed.

For the reset-history route define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reset-route}
 R_{\rm hist}=
 M_H\wedge I_{HT}\wedge D\wedge K\wedge H,
\end{equation}
where $M_H$ is memory-to-history realization and $I_{HT}$ is a
history-to-transfer map.  For the finite-cycle route define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cycle-route}
 R_{\rm cyc}=
 C_A\wedge C_{\rm cal}\wedge I_{CT}
 \wedge D\wedge K\wedge H,
\end{equation}
where $C_A$ is exact cycle algebra, $C_{\rm cal}$ physical cycle calibration,
and $I_{CT}$ cycle-to-transfer realization.

The currently proved leaves are
\[
 M_H=\text{proved},
 \qquad C_A=\text{proved}.
\]
All other displayed leaves are open.

\begin{proposition}[Current minimal branch frontiers]\label{prop:frontier}
Relative to \eqref{eq:reset-route}--\eqref{eq:cycle-route}, the reset-history
branch has missing bundle
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hist-frontier}
 \boxed{\{I_{HT},D,K,H\}},
\end{equation}
and the finite-cycle branch has missing bundle
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cycle-frontier}
 \boxed{\{C_{\rm cal},I_{CT},D,K,H\}}.
\end{equation}
Neither branch is closed.  The no-go theorem for the literal infinite shift
does not remove the finite reset-history route or the finite-cycle route.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each route is a conjunction.  Delete the two proved leaves and collect the
remaining open leaves.  The RH-175 no-go object is an attempted square
completion after $M_H$; it is not identical to the finite map $I_{HT}$ and is
not a leaf of the finite-cycle route.
\end{proof}

The symbols $D,K,H$ are shared only at the architectural level.  Their actual
contours, block decompositions, and transport coordinates can differ between
branches.  A future implementation must type them branch by branch.

\section{Relation to the macro Gate-A formula}

The reset-history branch fits the existing RH-161 typed assembly: an eventual
reset seed is lifted through R and then combined with Q, U, Z, and T.  The
finite-cycle branch may bypass the reset seed entirely.  If it becomes
primary, the Gate-A theorem must be restated with a cyclic seed type; RH-180
alone does not authorize silently substituting a cycle for S.

Consequently the safe macro status remains
\begin{equation}\label{eq:macro}
 S\text{ conditional},
 \qquad R,Q,U,Z,T\text{ open},
 \qquad \text{Gate A open}.
\end{equation}
The exact cycle factor advances a model for Q, not the physical Q leaf.  The
finite edge marker advances a model for T, not the physical common-space
marked limit.  The distinction between algebraic model and physical
identification is maintained throughout.

\section{Recommended next physical experiment}

The finite-cycle route is now algebraically cleaner, but it must be derived
from data rather than reverse-engineered from the desired determinant.  The
next paper should construct a target-independent finite temporal clock from
the physical history cocycle.  A strict protocol is:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item choose candidate lengths $L=r-3$ and $L=r-4$ before inspecting transfer
eigenvalues;
\item form consecutive stable history packet frames and their reduced
cocycle matrices $B_t=V_{t+1}^*T_tV_t$;
\item build the chronological product and a polar/Procrustes wrap map from
the endpoint back to the seed, using only time-domain data;
\item measure the wrap residual, primal and adjoint defects, cycle-root phase
error, and the rank-one orientation mark;
\item compare both integer branches on all available scales without choosing
the better branch separately at each target spectral point;
\item only if one branch survives, replace floating residuals by outward
matrix balls and feed them into RH-180.
\end{enumerate}
If both candidate lengths fail badly, the finite-cycle physical branch is
rejected while the direct transfer-space branch remains.  If one survives,
the next wall is a validated cycle-to-transfer isometry and shrinking
$L^{-1}$ contour budgets.

\section{Longer-term roadmap}

Even a successful RH-180 physical instantiation would close only a version of
R.  The remaining Gate-A sequence is:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item prove that the actual finite Riesz determinant has the geometric cloud
coefficient ledger Q;
\item prove a common-space $\mathcal S_1$ or $\mathcal S_2$ complement limit
U;
\item prove schedule, gauge, and zero-free normalization canonicity Z;
\item transport the directed marked words and prove their limit T;
\item assemble the canonical intrinsic relative determinant.
\end{enumerate}
Only after that would the program enter the scattering/unitary completion and
self-adjoint questions associated with macro Gates B and C.  The
$T\log T$ counting law, von Mangoldt prime-power trace formula, and completed
zeta divisor identity belong to Gates C--E and have not been approached by
this batch.

\section{Final theorem boundary}

The batch proves an exact finite history realization, its exact cocycle, a
branch-specific infinite-shift no-go theorem, an exact finite cyclic cloud
determinant, an exact directed marker, an integer clock translation, and a
conditional rank-two cyclic Riesz theorem.  The finite physical audit is
reported with its negative two-sided result.

It does not prove a physical history-to-transfer or cycle-to-transfer map,
unique cycle calibration, outward physical block budgets, uniform all-level
Riesz shells, the actual cloud coefficient bridge, a Schatten complement
limit, determinant canonicity, directed marked convergence, macro Gate A,
any self-adjoint Hilbert--Polya operator, a $T\log T$ law, a prime-power trace
formula, equality with the completed zeta divisor, or the Riemann Hypothesis.
