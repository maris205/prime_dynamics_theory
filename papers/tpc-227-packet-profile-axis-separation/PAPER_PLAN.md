# TPC-227 paper plan

## Research question

Can the signed packet profiles used in the finite TPC-226 collision fixture be promoted
automatically to the literal V59 four-phase source compiler?

## Theorem edge

Prove a necessary-and-sufficient Gram criterion for four packet-dependent transforms to
reproduce one source-native polarized bilinear form for every pair of source vectors.

## Deliverables

1. Freeze the V59 typing: phase on `beta+i^j w`, one common `psi_+`.
2. Expand the packet-dependent quadratic compiler by the four-point discrete Fourier
   transform.
3. Prove compatibility iff every packet Gram equals the physical Gram.
4. Instantiate the theorem on the TPC-226 `Q=25` resonance block.
5. Separate global packet signs (invisible) from row-dependent signs (Gram-changing).
6. Produce exact-rational certificates, an independent checker, an adversarial checker,
   and a compiled PDF.

## Claim discipline

- The operator theorem is `PROVED_EXACT`.
- The `Q=25` mismatch is `PROVED_EXACT_FINITE_BLOCK`.
- Automatic V59 transfer of the TPC-226 profile sign is `REFUTED_SCOPED`.
- No arithmetic cancellation, exponent gain, `L2`, or twin-prime theorem is claimed.

## Exit condition

TPC-227 is complete when the theorem, finite witness, PDF, bridge checker and Route-B
firewall all agree and normal/optimized independent outputs are byte-identical.
