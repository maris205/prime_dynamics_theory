# RH-277: Fixed-rank Calkin quotient obstruction

The deterministic zero-noise Koopman limit is an isometry on the infinite
dimensional Perron/parity complement.  Finite-rank compression is invisible
in the Calkin algebra.  Therefore every fixed-rank quotient power has norm at
least `1`, and after Hardy scaling at least `0.85^{-m}>1`.

Consequently the limiting contractive-power hypothesis in RH-269 cannot hold
at zero noise for any fixed-rank selector in this natural geometry.  This
does not exclude a rank-growing selector or a block length growing with
noise.
