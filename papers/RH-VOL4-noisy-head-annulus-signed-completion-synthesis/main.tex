\begin{abstract}
This fourth volume synthesizes eighty numbered papers, RH-282--RH-361, at the
point where projection-free noisy spectral tails meet weighted prefixes,
annular endpoint criteria, first-alias boundary layers, and deterministic
counterloops.  The range contains substantial exact mathematics: canonical
finite noisy heads, regularized-determinant tail bounds, sharp clock and mass
barriers, endpoint expansions, exact local affine interfaces, corrected
trace observations, boundary-orbit decompositions, and an unconditional
deterministic terminal-lag phase diagram.  These statements live in several
different data types and cannot be merged by notation alone.

We give a machine-checked provenance theorem for all eighty labels and the
four historical duplicate-directory groups.  We then state the exact common
clock identities
\[
 p=\tau-a=q-d,\qquad d=h-s,\qquad q=p+d,\qquad h=s+d,
\]
where $p,q,h$ are actual coefficients or heads in their declared physical
types and $s$ is the deterministic graded counterloop.  A typed nonpromotion
theorem shows that the selected or normalized actual branch and the
deterministic branch do not determine an unnormalized full-trace or head
budget without an independent theorem for $d=h-s$.

The first missing physical leaf is
\[
 D_{4k}(R)=\sum_{2\le n<4k}|h_{\sigma,n}-s_{k,n}|\frac{R^n}{n}\longrightarrow0.
\]
No source in the volume proves this limit, proves a physical obstruction to
it, or supplies an equivalent complete $q/\Eoff$ theorem.  The resulting
verdict is source-relative \texttt{NOT\_TESTABLE}, not nonexistence.  Gates
A--E remain false/open.  This synthesis does not construct a Hilbert--Polya
operator, identify Riemann zeros, prove a von Mangoldt trace, prove
completed-zeta divisor equality, or prove the Riemann Hypothesis.  RH-362 is
not activated.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} transfer operators; regularized determinants;
spectral heads; annular Hardy bounds; first alias; signed completion;

\section{Series position and rules of synthesis}

The four-volume partition is functional rather than chronological rhetoric:
Volume I covers RH-1--RH-160, Volume II covers RH-161--RH-241, Volume III
covers RH-242--RH-281, and this volume covers RH-282--RH-361.  The original
numbered papers remain the atomic sources.  A review anchor records status
and dependency information, but it does not replace the proof in the source
paper it reviews.

We use five noninterchangeable labels.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.4cm,style=nextline,font=\normalfont]
\item[\textsc{proved}] an analytic theorem in the exact scope of its source;
\item[\textsc{certified}] a finite exact, stored, or outward computation;
\item[\textsc{conditional}] a valid implication with unproved physical inputs;
\item[\textsc{scoped negative}] a theorem rejecting one named information
class, model, or method, not every physical realization;
\item[\textsc{open/not testable}] a required source, map, clock, uniformity,
or observation is absent.
\end{description}

Three firewalls govern every cross-batch statement.  First, actual noisy,
synthetic spectral, deterministic combinatorial, and abstract coefficient
objects retain their types.  Second, fixed order, a selected slow diagonal,
and the physical logarithmic first-alias clock are not interchangeable.
Third, normalized or selected-window control is not an unnormalized complete
prefix theorem.

\section{Executable provenance theorem}

The range has eight review anchors:
\[
291,301,311,321,331,341,351,361.
\]
They close the consecutive ten-paper blocks beginning at RH-282.  Four
numbers, RH-302, RH-303, RH-304, and RH-306, have an additional empty legacy
directory.  The audit chooses the candidate containing a README, TeX source,
and PDF and records every alternative instead of deleting it.

\begin{theorem}[Volume IV provenance theorem]\label{thm:provenance}
The executable inventory defines a canonical nonempty source directory for
each integer $282\le n\le361$.  The resulting map contains eighty consecutive
labels, records exactly four duplicate-directory groups, and assigns every
label to exactly one of eight ten-paper phases.  Selected README, manuscript,
theorem-ledger, roadmap, and result files are bound by SHA-256.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Directory discovery uses the numerical prefix rather than a hand-written
list.  For each label the canonical candidate maximizes the required-file
score and then the finite file count; the chosen candidate is rejected unless
all three publication anchors are present.  The expected integer list and
the union of the declared phase intervals are both checked against
$\{282,\ldots,361\}$.  Hash replay is performed independently by the archive
verifier.  This proves presence and identity, not the mathematical claims in
the source files.
\end{proof}

\section{The common coefficient dictionary}

On the source-locked Hardy clock
\[
 k=\frac{\log(1/\sigma)}{2\log\lambda}+O(1),\qquad 2\le n<4k,
\]
let $p_{\sigma,n}$ denote the actual direct coefficient, $q_{\sigma,n}$ the
actual full-trace coefficient, $h_{\sigma,n}$ the actual modulus-complete
Hardy head, and $s_{k,n}$ the deterministic graded counterloop.  Write
$d_{\sigma,k,n}=h_{\sigma,n}-s_{k,n}$.  In the typed source formulas,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dictionary}
 p=\tau-a=q-d,\qquad d=h-s,qquad q=p+d,qquad h=s+d.
\end{equation}

Equation~\eqref{eq:dictionary} is algebra, not a spectral identification.
In particular, it does not say that the roots or moments defining $s$ occur
inside the noisy operator, that the rank of the deterministic model is a
physical rank, or that the off-cloud coefficient $q$ is controlled by $p$
without a bound for $d$.

The natural unnormalized defect budget is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:defect}
 D_{4k}(R)=\sum_{2\le n<4k}
 |h_{\sigma,n}-s_{k,n}|\frac{R^n}{n}.
\end{equation}
It is distinct from the normalized upper-band error, from coordinatewise
relative matching, and from any maximum over a selected window.

\section{RH-282--RH-291: noisy heads and determinant tails}

This phase reopens the operator route without requiring a global moving
Riesz projector.  RH-282 constructs an actual modulus-complete normal
spectral tail in the data type required by an earlier conditional tail
criterion.  RH-283 gives the sharp logarithmic block clock for the declared
mass-and-cap class.  RH-284 defines the canonical minimal head relative to a
threshold; the word ``canonical'' is relative to that threshold and does not
mean a canonical Hilbert--Polya spectrum.

RH-285 proves uniform fixed-derivative bounds for the complementary
$\det_2$ tail.  RH-287 obtains a synchronized growing actual-noisy prefix on
a selected, nonquantitative diagonal.  RH-288 gives the exact prefix--tail
gluing criterion and separates two error types.  The direct complement
prefix remains open.  RH-289 constructs hidden-shell moment
nonidentifiability, and RH-290 records why two typed ledgers cannot be united
coordinatewise.

The durable gain is a complete analytic tail and a real, finite actual head.
The durable limitation is rate: no result places the complete direct prefix
on the logarithmic first-alias clock.  A slow diagonal is an existence
statement, not an admissible replacement for the required clock.

\section{RH-292--RH-301: weighted prefixes and clocks}

The weighted norm magnifies high orders, so seemingly strong coordinatewise
information may be insufficient.  RH-292 shows how analytic tail absorption
shortens a bridge clock.  RH-293 obtains a sharp coefficient-error exponent
for its information class.  RH-294 proves a weighted full-trace bridge along
a rate-free selected clock, while RH-295 gives a scoped counterexample: the
unweighted maximum can vanish on the correct window even as the weighted
prefix diverges.

RH-296 proves that the existing orbit-localization and analytic tail clock
ranges are disjoint for the named method.  RH-297 counts the one or two exact
counterloop aliases crossed by the natural clock.  RH-298 gives a square-root
parity barrier for separate absolute majorants, leaving grouped signed
cancellation open.  RH-299 proves a zero-padded root-$\ell^1$ transport
criterion and its sharp rate, but actual matching is absent.  RH-300 gives
strict outer-circle $H^\infty/H^2$ direct-prefix criteria and an endpoint
counterexample.

These are genuine theorems about rates, clocks, and information classes.
They do not supply the actual outer-circle norm required to activate the
criteria.  Finite coefficient rows cannot replace that missing uniform
analytic input.

\section{RH-302--RH-311: annular mass and Hardy barriers}

RH-302 reduces an annular tail problem to a moving head.  RH-303 proves that
fixed-order head-moment transport is necessary.  RH-304 computes the mass
exponent demanded by minimal-clock relative matching, while RH-305 gives an
interior annular mass-rate lower barrier.  RH-306 is sharp for the declared
coefficient-envelope class; sharpness in that class is not a theorem about
the actual noisy spectrum.

RH-307 treats shrinking-annulus thresholds, RH-308 identifies the optimal
Hardy conversion order, and RH-309 separates endpoint $H^2$ membership from

localization clocks and records a typed matching necessity.  The RH-311
review shows that the strongest rows in the two branches each miss a
different leaf; their scores cannot be joined into a complete row.

The phase therefore isolates the exact annular price of a physical bridge.
It does not prove endpoint convergence or weighted cross-branch glue.

\section{RH-312--RH-321: endpoint expansions and synthetic sharpness}

RH-312 separates a universal endpoint logarithm from an analytic remainder.
RH-313 resolves parity-orthogonal endpoint channels, and RH-314 proves the
optimal polynomial endpoint rate $N^{-1/2}$ in its declared setting.
RH-315 constructs exact root-of-unity moment packets.  RH-316 realizes exact

The word ``spectral'' in this block describes an explicit finite or growing

of the physical folded Gaussian operator.  Consequently the sharp synthetic
laws are valuable extremizers and countermodels, but they cannot promote an
actual endpoint theorem.

\section{RH-322--RH-331: local first-alias interfaces}

The first-alias block begins with actual boundary geometry.  RH-322 proves a
folded-row half-line profile and phase stability.  RH-323 retains orientation
in an affine Gaussian chain and proves an exact retained-coordinate
$L^1$ isometry.  RH-324 replaces the first tangent leg by the physical row
and finds a sharp $O(\sigma)$ curvature remainder.

RH-325 separates Markov path composition from operator trace composition.
For path laws, transported row errors compose by contraction.  For traces, a
separately bounded observation and controlled prefix/suffix products are
necessary; a dimension-free Markov-to-trace implication is false.  Its
stability window is
\[
 \gamma<1-\frac{\log(1.4)}{\log\lambda}
 =0.3503698834605293\ldots,
\]
provided every local operator error is $O(\sigma)$ and the required weights
are actual upper bounds.

RH-326 gives the Hardy-normalized parity and first-alias packet identity.
RH-327--RH-330 develop localized trace ledgers, matching equations, exchange
models, and a grouped signed transfer criterion.  Their negative examples
are scoped to the named information classes or frozen models.  RH-331 ends
at an exact transfer criterion whose actual replacement hypotheses are open.

\section{RH-332--RH-341: actual observation and replacement}

RH-332 supplies the second sharp physical affine-leg remainder, but no
single global row theorem follows.  RH-333 proves escape of the canonical raw
full-line forward affine retained path on the first-alias clock.  It
explicitly does not refute a cyclic bridge, Doob transform, compact folded
reference, or nonlinear closing profile.

RH-334 repairs the physical observation: multiplication marks the cyclic
basepoint of the backward folded operator, and the signed/folded localized
traces agree for powers at least two.  A forward probability law is not
thereby identified with that trace.  RH-335 localizes the parity projector as
a signed measure and proves nonuniqueness of cellwise extensions; projector
mass need not be a probability.

RH-336--RH-340 refine boundary atoms, signed allocations, and actual/model
replacement formulas.  The RH-341 review records a decisive distinction.
Separate absolute orbit, diffuse, and head majorants encounter a divergent
two-atom burden, while grouped signed cancellation remains unknown.  Thus the
separate-majorant route is stopped, but the physical aggregate is not
refuted.

\section{RH-342--RH-351: boundary atoms and lower completion}

RH-342 freezes the common Hardy head and deterministic counterloop data
types.  Subsequent papers decompose critical and lower-sideband boundary
orbits, track parity phases, and prove exact algebraic identities.  RH-345
and RH-347 expose scalar balance underdetermination; RH-349--RH-350 prove

RH-351 then separates a deterministic $P/S$ ledger from the unknown actual

Odd orders, upper aliases, the complete $\Eoff$ aggregate, and the RH-241

\section{RH-352--RH-361: actual tails and deterministic counterloops}

The final phase contains two real advances of different types.  RH-352 proves

quantity.  RH-353 obtains a phase-free two-coordinate gap for an actual
critical/first-lower normalized quantity.  RH-354 proves an actual

RH-355--RH-360 then solve a deterministic counterloop problem: the complete
strict-upper burden, mesoscopic crossover, linear-depth profile, geometric

exponential-tilt phase diagram.  Every unconditional statement here is for

$D_{4k}(R)\to0$.

RH-361 closes the batch with an exact finite coefficient-fiber theorem and a
ten-layer audit.  It proves that current data do not determine a uniform
$q$ or $h$ budget in the abstract coefficient class.  It does not construct
a physical pair with arbitrary defect.

\section{Typed nonpromotion theorem}

The synthesis can now state the cross-batch inference exactly.

\begin{theorem}[Typed nonpromotion theorem]\label{thm:nonpromotion}
Fix the common clock and orders $2\le n<4k$.  The proved Volume IV inputs
consisting of
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item selected or normalized actual statements for $p$ and related signed
quantities, and
\item unconditional deterministic statements for $s$
\end{enumerate}
do not, by the identities~\eqref{eq:dictionary} alone, imply an unnormalized
weighted bound for the complete actual $q$ or $h$.  Such an implication
requires an independent same-clock estimate for $d=h-s$ on the complete
order range.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both target identities contain the unpaid term:
$q=p+d$ and $h=s+d$.  Neither a selected/normalized estimate for $p$ nor a
deterministic estimate for $s$ controls the weighted norm of $d$.  Moreover,
normalization cannot be removed without its scale, and a moving upper window
does not include the omitted low orders.  Hence the claimed full budgets do
not follow from the stated premises.  This is a theorem about implication
and types; it makes no assertion about which defects are physically
realizable.
\end{proof}

For completeness, on a fixed finite set $I$ and positive weights, hold signed
arrays $p,s$ fixed and let $e$ range over all signed arrays.  Define
\[
 d[e]=e,\qquad q[e]=p+e,\qquad h[e]=s+e.
\]
Along $e=tv$ with $v\ne0$, the weighted norms of $q[e]$ and $h[e]$ are
unbounded as $|t|\to\infty$.  Choosing $e=-p$ or $e=-s$ makes the respective
norm zero.  This verifies information-class nonidentifiability, but the two
zero choices need not coincide and arbitrary $e$ is not declared physical.

\section{Frontier theorem and reopening tests}

\begin{theorem}[Source-relative frontier theorem]\label{thm:frontier}
Relative to the canonical RH-282--RH-361 sources, no proved result supplies
\eqref{eq:defect}, a genuine physical obstruction to it, a complete actual
$q/\Eoff$ theorem on the same clock, or the RH-241 moving noisy all-order
envelope with its coefficient bridge.  Therefore the source-relative verdict
is \texttt{NOT\_TESTABLE}, and RH-362 is not activated by this synthesis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each ten-layer review records its first missing typed leaf.  RH-291 and
RH-301 stop at a rate-free or inactive prefix criterion; RH-311 lacks
cross-branch glue; RH-321 is sharp only for a synthetic class; RH-331 and
RH-341 lack all-leg actual trace replacement; RH-351 lacks the actual growing
signed remainder; and RH-361 explicitly records~\eqref{eq:defect} as an
assumption of every actual-head inheritance statement.  The executable audit
binds these reviews and their source ledgers.  Absence of a source is not a
physical impossibility theorem, so the conclusion is \texttt{NOT\_TESTABLE}
rather than a global negative.
\end{proof}

One new direction survives the scoped raw-forward obstruction.  For
full-line affine Gaussian rows whose composition has slope $A$, shift $B$,
and variance $V$, the cyclic diagonal integral is
\[
 \int_{\mathbb R}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi V}}
 \exp\!\left(-\frac{((1-A)x-B)^2}{2V}\right)dx
 =\frac1{|1-A|},\qquad A\ne1.
\]
Raw forward variance growth therefore does not by itself obstruct a cyclic
diagonal functional.  However the full-line kernel is not Hilbert--Schmidt on
$L^2(\mathbb R)$, and compact truncation and inverse row normalizers act in
opposite directions.  A physical reopening must first define the complete
signed/folded compact reference in the RH-334 observation type and test
\[
 C_{\sigma,k}=1+o\bigl((\beta R)^{-2k}\bigr).
\]
Only after this correction-factor test may all-leg curvature Duhamel and
observation-weight estimates be attempted.

\section{Reproduction protocol and limitations}

The executable protocol has four layers:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
\item discover and canonicalize the eighty numerical labels;
\item bind selected source files and the eight review anchors by SHA-256;
\item test the range, aliases, phase cover, typed identities, route coordinate,
Gate values, and forbidden claims;
\item build the PDF, archive local publication artifacts, and independently
replay local and external hashes.
\end{enumerate}

This protocol detects omission, alias confusion, source drift, and explicit
claim promotion.  It does not re-prove eighty papers, certify an asymptotic
from finite data, or decide whether a currently undefined physical bridge
exists.  Numerical tables in the sources retain exactly their original
status.

Volume IV proves Theorem~\ref{thm:provenance}, the typed logical statement in
Theorem~\ref{thm:nonpromotion}, and the source-relative audit conclusion in
Theorem~\ref{thm:frontier}.  It preserves all stronger mathematical claims in
their atomic sources and does not silently strengthen them.

Gates A--E remain false/open.  The volume does not construct a Hilbert--Polya
operator, identify Riemann zeros, prove a von Mangoldt trace, prove equality
with the completed-zeta divisor, or prove the Riemann Hypothesis.  The active
coordinate remains
\[
\boxed{\texttt{actual\_same\_clock\_unnormalized\_head\_transport\_open}}.
