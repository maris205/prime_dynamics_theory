# TPC-384 proof package

## Finite proposition

For the predeclared affine panel, the producer constructs the complete
`3 origins × 3 Q × 4 laws × 4 bandwidths × 2 normalizations = 288` row panel
from one common raw component geometry per origin and Q. The certificate
contains all rows, all 96 origin-spread cells, the failure census, and the
exact q=8 anchor.

## Verification

The independent checker does not import the producer. It sieves the prime
shell, accumulates in reverse shell order, rebuilds all bandwidth masks and
normalizations, and compares every spectral value and phase statistic. The
adversarial checker applies 25 schema, protocol, row, phase, and claim-firewall
mutations. The local Bridge-B locks the source, certificate, proof materials,
PDF, and documentation, then repeats producer, independent, and stress checks
in normal and optimized modes.

## What is proved and what is not

The protocol, exact anchor, finite row identity, and finite numerical replay
are the only claims paid by this package. `TPC384_BANDWIDTH_MONOTONICITY` is
OPEN: the observed all-plus means increase over this four-point panel, but no
monotonicity theorem is asserted. Neither normalization is declared
source-valid, and no arithmetic (L^2), power saving, Route-B reassembly, or
twin-prime conclusion follows.
