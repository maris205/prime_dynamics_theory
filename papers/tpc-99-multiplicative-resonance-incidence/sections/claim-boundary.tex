\section{Claim boundary}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\subsection*{Unconditional results}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The resonance condition is exactly a multiplicative product
  window \(r\omega\in E_H\).
\item The resonance incidence graph is biregular and its complete
  singular spectrum is given by incomplete multiplicative character
  sums.
\item All odd characters are exact null modes; the principal and
  nonprincipal Hilbert--Schmidt masses are explicit.
\item The principal/nonprincipal bilinear identity and
  P\'olya--Vinogradov \(L^2\) bound are rigorous.
\item The positive literal carrier and the two sufficient gates
  retain exact widths, multiplier magnitudes and support, both
  polarizations, and one global normalization.  The phase of the
  reconstruction multiplier is deliberately lost only in the
  stated majorant.
\item Conductor \(q_X\) alone cannot yield a pointwise or aggregate
  density saving without multiplier dispersion.
\end{itemize}

The finite-field results are \(\Lzero\).  The native census and
sufficient-gate crosswalk are \(\Lone\) interfaces.

\subsection*{Not proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Neither \eqref{eq:principal-gate} nor
  \eqref{eq:dispersion-gate} is proved for the growing literal
  packet.
\item The positive incidence carrier may be larger than the signed
  resonant contribution; no equivalence is asserted.
\item No generic affine M\"obius cancellation, determinant-energy
  lower bound, TPC-18 cover, fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood
  asymptotic, parity breakthrough, prime-pair lower bound, or
  twin-prime conclusion is proved.
\item Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).
\end{itemize}

Accordingly this paper sharpens the remaining door but does not
claim an \(\Ltwo\) arithmetic advance.
