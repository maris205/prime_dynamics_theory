\begin{abstract}
After the conductor-one sector is returned at the endpoint scale,
the unresolved TPC-94 exceptional branch has algebraic conductor
equal to the strong no-wrap prime \(q_X\) but insufficient
archimedean variation on its affine interval.  We isolate the exact
finite-field geometry of this resonance.

For an odd prime \(q\), put \(G=\mathbb F_q^\times\) and
\[
 E_H=\{\pm1,\ldots,\pm H\}\subset G,
 \qquad 1\le H\le(q-1)/2.
\]
The resonance carrier is the multiplicative incidence operator
\[
 (K_Hb)(\omega)
 =\sum_{r\in G}b(r)\one_{E_H}(r\omega).
\]
We diagonalize it exactly by multiplicative characters.  Its
singular values are
\[
 \left|\widehat{\one_{E_H}}(\chi)\right|
 =
 \left|
 (1+\overline{\chi(-1)})
 \sum_{n\le H}\overline{\chi(n)}
 \right|.
\]
Thus every odd character is an exact null mode.  The principal
singular value is \(2H\), and
\[
 \sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}
 |\widehat{\one_{E_H}}(\chi)|^2
 =2H(q-1-2H).
\]
For nonprincipal even characters the P\'olya--Vinogradov inequality
gives \(O(\sqrt q\log q)\).

Consequently, for arbitrary weights \(a,b\) on \(G\), the incidence
bilinear form has the exact principal/nonprincipal decomposition
\[
 \sum_{\omega,r}a(\omega)b(r)\one_{E_H}(r\omega)
 =
 \frac{2H}{q-1}\Big(\sum_\omega a(\omega)\Big)
 \Big(\sum_r b(r)\Big)
 +\mathcal R_H(a,b),
\]
with
\[
 |\mathcal R_H(a,b)|
 \ll\sqrt q\log q\,
 \|a^\circ\|_2\|b^\circ\|_2.
\]
For a frequency-free literal branch \(\beta=(\theta,c,\kappa)\)
with resolved \(z\)-fiber length \(1\le N_\beta\le q_X\) and
nonzero phase multiplier \(\omega_\beta\), resonance is exactly
\[
 r\omega_\beta\in E_{H_{q_X}(N_\beta)},\qquad
 H_q(N)=\min\{(q-1)/2,\lfloor q/N\rfloor\}.
\]
Branches with \(N_\beta>q_X\) have no resonant nonzero frequency.
This converts the resonant-sector census into a family of
multiplicative spectral diagnostics and gives an explicit sufficient
native-dispersion gate.  A sharp delta-mass example proves that
conductor \(q_X\) alone gives no saving.  The paper proves exact
\(\Lzero\) incidence theory and an \(\Lone\) literal certificate
interface, but no new signed M\"obius estimate or \(\Ltwo\) bound for
the growing fixed-shift packet.
\end{abstract}
