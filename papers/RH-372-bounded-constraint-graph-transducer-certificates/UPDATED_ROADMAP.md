# Roadmap after RH-372

RH-372 proves a bounded-resource theorem for arithmetic certificates on
finite constraint graphs.  It gives exact open-prefix max-plus capacity, a
finite universal safety test for clocked finite-memory transducers, and an
unconditional correlation formula whenever the observed label is a one-site
factor of `(phase, mu(n))`.  Two frozen graph instances and a new q=3 switch
are audited.

The route remains deliberately bounded.  Exhausting fixed `(q,m)` tables is
not a classification of all mixing subshifts, and labels depending on memory
would require higher-order Mobius results absent from the repository.  The
RH-366 distance-two capacity limit, intrinsic operator identification, and
all Gates A--E remain open.

Next candidates are a genuine higher-order arithmetic theorem for
memory-dependent transducers, the RH-366 capacity envelope, and the
fractional/tower-adapted Ulam projector route.  A larger finite table or a
reparameterization alone is not a new numbered edge.
