# TPC-330 paper plan

## Research question

Was the TPC-329 all-plus sign reversal an accident of the single affine map
`pi(i)=(5i+17) mod M`, or is it a stable finite response across a small,
predeclared family of source-multiset- and `L2`-preserving coordinate
bijections?

## Minimal advance

TPC-330 does not enlarge the arithmetic panel.  It freezes the TPC-329
32-row object and changes only the adversarial control design:

1. add two independently chosen odd-affine maps;
2. retain the TPC-329 affine map for direct replication;
3. include identity and reversal as geometry-sensitive controls;
4. record the full five-control/four-law response spectrum and all pairwise
   comparisons.

This produces one new scientific result: all three affine controls agree on a
positive all-plus sign in `32/32` rows, while identity and reversal agree with
the actual `31/1` census.

## Frozen protocol

- Origins: `28001`, `36001`.
- Scales: `4096`, `8192`; source counts `2048`, `4096`.
- Shell anchors: `24`, `36`, `54`, `80`; exponents `1`, `2`;
  `H=66`.
- Source: the finite V59 residual hash-locked through TPC-329 and TPC-267.
- Ratio guard: `5e-8`; arithmetic cutoff: `50000`.
- Controls:
  `identity`, `(3i+11) mod M`, `(5i+17) mod M`,
  `(7i+29) mod M`, and `M-1-i`.

Every control is fixed before result generation.  All are bijections on both
source cardinalities and therefore preserve the exact coordinate multiset and
Euclidean norm.

## Claim-bearing outputs

1. Exact finite proofs of the Gram split and five bijection/norm identities.
2. A canonical certificate with 32 source rows, five placement controls,
   640 law/control observations, and 10 pairwise summaries.
3. An independently implemented reverse-shell replay of every stored metric.
4. A mutation stress checker and exact rational anchor.
5. A scoped obstruction to both a source-norm-only explanation and the claim
   that the TPC-329 effect was unique to one affine map.

## Evaluation and stop rules

The release is accepted only when producer, independent checker, stress suite,
optimized variants, PDF audit, and local Bridge-B normal/optimized comparison
all pass with empty stderr.  Finite response agreement cannot be promoted to a
source-uniform theorem, fixed-power credit, Route-B Gate B, or a twin-prime
conclusion.  Session-named evaluator files are absent, so no official Route
pass is claimed.

## Decision rule for TPC-331

The observed affine/reversal split selects a position-aware decomposition as
the next minimal problem.  TPC-331 should define an exact finite
control-average plus centered response decomposition and test whether the
source-aligned deviation has a stable, independently replayable structure.
It should not add more arbitrary permutations merely to enlarge the table.
