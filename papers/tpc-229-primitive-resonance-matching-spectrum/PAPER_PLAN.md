# TPC-229 paper plan

## Question

What is the exact global structure of the primitive `3--7` resonance graph carrying the
TPC-228 source collision scalar?

## Theorem edge

Prove that the graph is a matching for every scale, diagonalize every two-coordinate
edge block, and derive a sharp necessary-and-sufficient local saving criterion.

## Deliverables

1. Prove disjoint low/high endpoint intervals.
2. Prove degree at most one and global direct-sum decomposition.
3. Compute the edge spectrum and symmetric/antisymmetric basis.
4. Prove sharp AP ratio range and source bilinear block bound.
5. Replay the exact graph over `Q=8..4096` and adversarial fixtures.
6. Preserve the arithmetic and physical crosswalk firewalls.

## Exit condition

The all-scale theorem, exact certificate, independent checker, PDF and bridge checker
must agree without promoting antisymmetric dominance to an arithmetic fact.
