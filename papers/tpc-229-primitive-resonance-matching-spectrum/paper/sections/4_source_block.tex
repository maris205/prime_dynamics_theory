\section{Sharp source-bilinear block bound}

TPC-228 gives on one edge
\[
 B_e=\langle\beta_p,w_r\rangle+\langle\beta_r,w_p\rangle.
\]
By the elementary Hilbert inequality $2|\langle x,y\rangle|\le
\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2$,
\begin{equation}
 |B_e|\le\frac12\left(
 \|\beta_p\|^2+\|\beta_r\|^2+\|w_p\|^2+\|w_r\|^2
 \right).
 \label{eq:bilinear-bound}
\end{equation}
The constant is sharp: choose $w_r=\beta_p$ and $w_p=\beta_r$.

Matching means that summing \eqref{eq:bilinear-bound} introduces no graph-degree loss.
This is stronger than a general Schur envelope, but it remains an absolute bound.  A
negative arithmetic contribution requires signed information about the exchanged source
pairs.
