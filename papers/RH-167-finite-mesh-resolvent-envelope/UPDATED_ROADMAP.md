# Roadmap after RH-167

The continuous-contour quantifier has been reduced to finite data:

```text
sample inverse bounds + geometric covering radii -> continuous a,d.
```

The remaining validation gap is now precise.  Physical matrices, packet
frames, and sample inverses are approximate.  RH-168 should transport a
nominal sample inverse through a certified operator ball and combine that
radius with mesh displacement in one Banach-lemma denominator.
