\section{The literal arithmetic gate and endpoint ledger}
\label{sec:physical-gate}

\subsection{What the export reduces}

At the critical scales, choose
\[
 R_X=X^{\varrho+o(1)}<q_X/2,\qquad
 \varrho<\frac{267}{400},
\]
and fixed \(K\ge2\).  For a desired zero-mode rate
\[
 |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,
\]
the inherited high-frequency tail has a fixed-power reserve when
\begin{equation}
 (K-\tfrac12)\varrho
 >
 \frac{133}{400}+\eta_Z.
\label{eq:gate-tail-condition}
\end{equation}
The often quoted second-order choice
\[
 K=2,\qquad
 R_X=X^{133/600+\varepsilon}
\]
belongs to the zero-loss corner \(\eta_Z=0\) for fixed
\[
 0<\varepsilon<\frac{107}{240}.
\]
For the same window and positive \(\eta_Z\), one additionally needs
\(\eta_Z<3\varepsilon/2\).

\begin{theorem}[Exact sufficient sector certificate]
\label{thm:sector-certificate}
Assume \eqref{eq:gate-tail-condition}.  If a future theorem proves
\begin{equation}
 \left|
 \Low_{\Short,X}
 +\Low_{\Res,X}
 +\Low_{\Gen,X}
 \right|
 \le
 X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,
\label{eq:gate-coherent-sector-target}
\end{equation}
then
\[
 |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2.
\]
A stronger sufficient condition is
\begin{equation}
 |\Low_{\Short,X}|
 +|\Low_{\Res,X}|
 +|\Low_{\Gen,X}|
 \le
 X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2.
\label{eq:gate-separate-sector-target}
\end{equation}
Neither estimate is proved here.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use \eqref{eq:sector-sum} in
\eqref{eq:row-tail-return}.  Under
\eqref{eq:gate-tail-condition}, the tail is smaller than the target
by a fixed power.  The second displayed hypothesis implies the first
by the triangle inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The coherent certificate is weaker]
Equation \eqref{eq:gate-coherent-sector-target} permits cancellation
between the three returned sectors.  Requiring three separate bounds
may lose a fixed power.  A proof may use either route, but must state
which outer cancellations survive.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Sector-specific tasks}

\paragraph{Short fibers.}
By \cref{prop:resolved-occupancy}, large values of
\(\sigma_\theta v_\theta B_\xi\) automatically enter the short
sector.  These fibers have no asymptotic averaging parameter.  They
must be returned by an exact enumeration, an outer-energy estimate,
or a physical counting theorem.  Calling them negligible because
each is short does not bound their number or outer coefficient mass.

\paragraph{Long resonant fibers.}
Exact resonances satisfy
\eqref{eq:exact-resonance}; near resonances satisfy
\(N_\xi\delta_\xi\le1\).  The additive determinant phase provides no
uniform power saving on this sector.  A viable estimate must instead
use the two-sign arithmetic, a structural outer cancellation, or a
proved small multiplier/provenance mass.

\paragraph{Long generic fibers.}
Here the additive phase makes more than one resolvable turn, and the
reduced M\"obius pair has the fixed determinant \(h_0\).  This is the
natural sector for a large-sieve, bilinear, \(TT^\ast\), or
phase-sensitive affine-correlation theorem.  Such a theorem still
must be uniform in the growing slopes, the content-factor masks,
prefixes, interval origins, and complete outer ledger.

\paragraph{Local squarefree split.}
\Cref{cor:tpc90-after-extraction} applies TPC-90 without changing the
determinant.  It classifies local square-power support but leaves the
weighted Liouville product.  The extracted \(\mu(B_\xi)\) may
participate in outer cancellation, but no such cancellation is
proved by the factorization itself.

\subsection{A provenance success certificate}

The following definition records the minimum data that a later
arithmetic estimate must return.

\begin{definition}[Callable low-window theorem]
\label{def:callable-low-window}
A future estimate is callable for the literal TPC-86 gate only if it
returns:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the same filter multiplier
\(\mathfrak m_{K,X}(r)\) and every \(r\in\Win_X(R_X)\);
\item both polarizations and the complete constructed source--child
ledger \(\Theta_X\), including its inverse provenance and projector
multiplicity;
\item every \(c,\kappa,B,\mu(B)\), content mask, and exact phase;
\item the short, resonant, and generic sectors, either separately or
through their complete coherent sum;
\item the same fixed nonzero \(h_0\), interval origins, prefixes,
weights, and one global post-aggregation normalization; and
\item a quantitative outer rate compatible with
\eqref{eq:gate-tail-condition} and the physical endpoint ledger.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Downgrade conditions]
\label{prop:low-window-downgrade}
An estimate is not the literal low-window certificate if it:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item treats only one generic frequency or one fixed affine column;
\item deletes exact or near resonances;
\item assumes every fiber length tends to infinity;
\item uses the unreduced determinant-\(Bh_0\) pair but labels it
determinant \(h_0\);
\item extracts \(B\) but omits \(\mu(B)\) or
\(\one_{(B,V)=1}\);
\item sums an exceptional-set cardinality without its reconstruction
multiplier and outer coefficients; or
\item leaves an unreturned average over shifts, scales, origins, or
native keys.
\end{enumerate}
Such a result may remain valid at a restricted, frozen, averaged, or
negative-transfer level.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each item changes or omits a term in the exact master formula
\eqref{eq:sector-master-formula} or in the fixed-\(h_0\) reduction
\eqref{eq:fixed-h-reduced-inner}.  It therefore estimates a different
coefficient unless a separate return theorem is supplied.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Independent ledgers}

If future work proves
\[
 D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2
\]
and the zero-mode bound at rate \(\eta_Z\), the determinant
compatibility remains
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z.}
\label{eq:gate-determinant-ledger}
\end{equation}
Separately, every fixed-power loss introduced by the sector
partition, coefficient amplification, short-fiber return, masks,
outer reassembly, and downstream physical comparison, excluding
losses already recorded in
\(\lambda_D-2\eta_Z\), must satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\Lambda_{\rm phys}<\frac1{400}.}
\label{eq:gate-physical-ledger}
\end{equation}
Equality in \eqref{eq:gate-determinant-ledger} has no determinant
reserve; equality in \eqref{eq:gate-physical-ledger} stops the
physical route.  The same cost is not entered in both ledgers.
