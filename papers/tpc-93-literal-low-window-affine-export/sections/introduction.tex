\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

TPC-86 reduces the distinguished zero-mode problem to a coherent
low-frequency window for the literal normalized-determinant
coefficient \citep{WangTPC86}.  At the critical row,
\[
 Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad
 J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad
 N_{0,X}=J_XQ_X^2,
\]
and an order-\(K\) zero-arc filter gives
\begin{equation}
 |Z_X|
 \ll_K
 \left|
 \sum_{0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R_X}
 \mathfrak m_{K,X}(r)\widehat A_X(r)
 \right|
 +
 X^{o(1)}N_{0,X}R_X^{1/2-K}.
\label{eq:intro-low-window}
\end{equation}
The minimal arithmetic input is the signed multiplier-weighted sum,
not a maximum over generic frequencies.  TPC-92 therefore lists a
literal export of that sum as its first next-stage deliverable
\citep{WangTPC92}.

The word ``export'' carries more content than inserting an additive
character in an abstract affine sum.  The physical coefficient has:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item two polarized cutoff legs;
\item an exact common-target content \(G\le C_X=\lfloor J_X\rfloor\);
\item a normalized determinant
\((m_\alpha-m_\gamma)/G\);
\item a joint row-pair mask;
\item an opposite row fixed outside each affine column;
\item row-gcd divisor layers and fixed-period factors;
\item long and bounded-length affine fibers; and
\item one outer reassembly under the original normalization.
\end{itemize}
Every one of these data affects either the phase or the coefficient.

This paper performs that export exactly.  It also identifies a
content-factor extraction that is invisible at determinant frequency
zero.  Once exact content inversion imposes
\[
 c\kappa\mid \sigma U(t),
\]
solving the divisibility condition restricts the affine parameter to
one progression \(t=\tau+Bz\).  The unreduced reparametrized pair has
determinant \(Bh_0\), but the same divisibility also gives
\[
 U(\tau+Bz)=B\,V(z).
\]
Extracting this literal content factor restores determinant \(h_0\):
\[
\begin{array}{ccl}
\bigl(D(\tau+Bz),U(\tau+Bz)\bigr)
&:&\det=Bh_0,\\[1mm]
\bigl(D(\tau+Bz),V(z)\bigr)
&:&\det=h_0.
\end{array}
\]
On the nonzero M\"obius support, the extraction contributes the
literal factor \(\mu(B)\) and the mask \(\one_{(B,V)=1}\).  Dropping
either one is an invalid hybrid; retaining both gives a genuine
fixed-\(h_0\) affine family.

\subsection{Main results}

The exact chain has four steps.

\paragraph{Row-native window.}
We first keep the physical rows and prove that the complete
low-window functional is obtained by replacing the determinant
character with one finite kernel
\[
 \Ker_{K,R,X}(y)
 =
 \sum_{0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R}
 \mathfrak m_{K,X}(r)e_{q_X}(-ry).
\]
This identity already returns every low frequency with the correct
multiplier and phase.

\paragraph{Decorated affine columns.}
The TPC-33 reflection and row-gcd projection are pointwise identities
\citep{WangTPC33}.  We prove that they remain valid after multiplication
by the small-content determinant decoration.  This produces an exact
two-sign affine formula for both polarizations, with the opposite row
and every separation key retained in the outer provenance tuple.  The
source-to-child construction, its inverse, and the
projector-weighted child multiplicity are all displayed explicitly;
losslessness is therefore a conclusion rather than an interface
assumption.

\paragraph{Content-progression reduction.}
We expand exact content by the TPC-32 common-divisor projector
\citep{WangTPC32}.  The resulting congruence is solved explicitly and
the two-stage determinant law
\[
 Bh_0\longrightarrow h_0
\]
is proved after exact extraction of the forced factor \(B\) from one
affine value.  This is an algebraic theorem, not a heuristic
normalization.

\paragraph{Complete sector partition.}
Every resolved term is assigned to exactly one of three sets:
short, long resonant, or long generic.  Resonance is measured by the
actual phase increment on the resolved progression.  The partition
is an equality.  In particular, neither exact resonances nor
near-resonant long fibers are hidden inside an exceptional
``generic'' remainder.

\subsection{Proof levels}

We use the established labels:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3em,style=nextline]
\item[\(\Lzero\)]
finite Fourier identities, exact projector expansions, affine
reparametrization, determinant laws, and set partitions;
\item[\(\Lone\)]
an exact attachment to the same fixed-\(h_0\) TPC-32 coefficient,
with its full native key list and global normalization;
\item[\(\Ltwo\)]
a new growing-\(X\) cancellation estimate for the resulting literal
window or one of its returned sectors.
\end{description}
The new theorems are \(\Lzero\), and the export is \(\Lone\).
No \(\Ltwo\) estimate is asserted.
