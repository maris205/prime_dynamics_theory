\section{Claim boundary and conclusion}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\begin{theorem}[Audited Deliverable 1A conclusion]
\label{thm:audited-conclusion}
For the literal high-row TPC-32 coefficient at one prescribed
nonzero \(h_0\), the complete TPC-86 multiplier-weighted
low-frequency window admits:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the exact row-native formula
\eqref{eq:row-native-export};
\item the constructed, lossless two-polarization affine formula
\eqref{eq:decorated-affine-export};
\item the exact-content expansion
\eqref{eq:content-expanded-window};
\item the one-progression resolution
\eqref{eq:fully-resolved-inner-sum};
\item the exact content-factor extraction
\eqref{eq:fixed-h-reduced-inner}, whose reduced affine pair has
determinant \(h_0\); and
\item the disjoint short/resonant/generic decomposition
\eqref{eq:sector-sum}.
\end{enumerate}
No native key, low frequency, exact-content term, resonance, bounded
fiber, or polarization is deleted in these identities.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Items (i)--(vi) are
\cref{thm:row-native-export,thm:decorated-affine-export,thm:content-expanded-window,cor:fully-resolved-window,thm:fixed-h-reduced-export,thm:complete-sector-decomposition},
respectively.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What is new.}
The paper proves three exact advances.
First, the full coherent TPC-86 window is now written in the native
affine variables rather than represented by a schematic twisted
column.  Second, exact content resolution is reconciled with the
fixed determinant: the unreduced progression has determinant
\(Bh_0\), but the forced factor \(B\) in one M\"obius argument can be
extracted exactly, restoring determinant \(h_0\) together with the
literal sign and coprimality mask.  Third, the resulting physical
family is partitioned into explicit short, resonant, and generic
sectors with no exceptional remainder.

\paragraph{Proof levels.}
The Fourier reindexing, phase-compatible content inversion,
progression solution, M\"obius extraction, determinant identities,
and sector partition are finite \(\Lzero\) theorems.  Their
attachment to the already aggregated TPC-32 coefficient through the
actual TPC-33 column decomposition is an \(\Lone\) theorem.

\paragraph{Finite regression certificate.}
The standard-library script
\path{experiments/verify_literal_export.py} separately exercises
both source polarizations, the explicit source--child inverse,
projector and sign reassembly, phase-compatible content inversion,
progression resolution, determinant reduction, and M\"obius
extraction on deterministic finite instances.  This guards the
literal bookkeeping; it is not evidence for an \(\Ltwo\) estimate.

\paragraph{What is not proved.}
This paper proves no nontrivial upper bound for any of
\[
 \Low_{\Short,X},\qquad
 \Low_{\Res,X},\qquad
 \Low_{\Gen,X},
\]
no new bound for \(Z_X\), no post-bin mass or determinant-energy lower
bound, and no growing fixed-\(h_0\) Liouville/M\"obius correlation
estimate.  It proves no \(\Ltwo\) saving.

\paragraph{The surviving arithmetic fork.}
The next positive step may:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item control short fibers by their complete outer mass or an exact
physical return;
\item bound the multiplier/provenance mass of resonant fibers or use
the fixed-\(h_0\) sign correlation there;
\item prove a phase-sensitive estimate for the long generic reduced
pair; or
\item estimate the complete signed sum of all three sectors without
separate absolute bounds.
\end{enumerate}
Any one-sector theorem is progress at its native level only after the
other two sectors are returned.

\paragraph{Stop rules.}
A generic-frequency theorem does not pass if it leaves resonant
multiplier mass uncontrolled.  A long-fiber theorem does not pass if
the aggregate bounded-fiber contribution is unreturned.  A
determinant-\(h_0\) theorem cannot be applied to the unreduced
determinant-\(Bh_0\) pair.  After extracting \(B\), the factors
\(\mu(B)\) and \(\one_{(B,V)=1}\) are mandatory.  Any unavoidable
fixed-power return loss reaching \(1/400\) stops the physical branch.

\paragraph{Independent requirements.}
The zero-mode and determinant-energy exponents still require
\[
 \lambda_D\le2\eta_Z.
\]
The separate physical ledger still requires
\[
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400.
\]
TPC-18 full-block reassembly and primitive determinant dispersion
remain independent downstream gates; the affine export proves
neither one.

\paragraph{Final boundary.}
Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).  The paper does not prove a
fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, breach the sieve parity
barrier, give a prime-pair lower bound, or prove the twin-prime
conjecture.  Its contribution is a complete literal representation
and a corrected fixed-determinant content reduction on which the next
arithmetic estimate can be stated without hidden sectors.
