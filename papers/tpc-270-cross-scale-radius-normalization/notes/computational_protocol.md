# TPC-270 computational protocol

The producer imports the released TPC-269 producer to access its exact finite
interval engine and recomputes the residual radius on the declared base and
profile rows. It applies only positive rational cube and ratio operations to
form `Xi` and the derived scale ratios.

The independent checker is standalone: it implements its own prime sieve,
factorization, Mobius source, shifted-prime comparison, two-kernel operator,
projection, radius product, and normalization. It does not import the producer.

The stress audit checks all registry sizes, the cutoff schedule, ratio
classifications and thresholds, profile-control band, normalization identity,
and the finite-to-asymptotic firewall. Normal and optimized runs must be
byte-identical.
