\section{Transport of the TPC-242 selected mode}\label{sec:transport}

TPC-242 fixes a convention that cannot be suppressed in a complex argument.
For phase energies $E_j=\norm{X+\ii^jY}^2$ and Fourier weights $\ii^{kj}$,
the selected mode is $F_1=\ip{Y}{X}$ under the conjugate-linear-first inner
product~\cite{WangTPC242}.

Set
\[
X=N^{-1/2}Tz,\qquad Y=N^{-1/2}Tw.
\]
Then
\[
F_1=\ip{Y}{X}=N^{-1}\ip{Tw}{Tz}.
\]
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:main} with the ordered coefficient pair $(w,z)$ rather
than $(z,w)$.  The result is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:selected-transfer}
\abs{F_1-\ip{w}{z}}
\leq\eps\norm{w}_2\norm{z}_2.
\end{equation}
The target $\ip{w}{z}$ is generally the complex conjugate of
$\ip{z}{w}$.  Reversing the target would therefore change the theorem, not
merely its notation.

Equation~\eqref{eq:selected-transfer} is a signed bilinear interface.  It says
that a coefficient-space phase and sign survive the hard-window map up to an
explicit norm-weighted error.  It does not show that $F_1$ is small, large,
nonzero, or of a prescribed sign.  More importantly, the repository does not
yet identify the two literal V59 top-prime lanes with $z$ and $w$ in this one
common synthesis map.  Such an attachment theorem and a coefficient norm
bound are separate arithmetic requirements.
