\section{Setup and source lock}\label{sec:setup}

Let $\cF\subset\Torus$ be finite.  Circular distance is denoted by
$\dist{\theta}$.  The set is $\delta$-separated when
$\dist{\alpha-\beta}\geq\delta$ for every distinct
$\alpha,\beta\in\cF$, where $0<\delta\leq1/2$.  Let
$\cI=\{M,\ldots,M+N-1\}$ with $N\geq1$, and define
\[
  Tz(n)=\sum_{\alpha\in\cF}z_\alpha\ee^{2\pi\ii n\alpha}.
\]
All complex inner products are conjugate-linear in the first slot.  Thus
\[
  \ip{Tz}{Tw}
  =\sum_{\alpha,\beta\in\cF}
  \overline{z_\alpha}w_\beta
  \sum_{n\in\cI}\ee^{2\pi\ii n(\beta-\alpha)}.
\]
The order $\beta-\alpha$ fixes the Gram orientation used throughout.

Put
\BeginAccSupp{method=escape,ActualText={K equals floor of one over two delta; H K equals the sum from j equals one to K of one over j; R delta equals delta inverse times H K; epsilon equals R delta over N.}}
\[
K=\left\lfloor\frac1{2\delta}\right\rfloor,
\qquad
\Harm_K=\sum_{j=1}^{K}\frac1j,
\qquad
\Rdel=\delta^{-1}\Harm_K,
\qquad
\eps=\frac{\Rdel}{N}.
\]
\EndAccSupp{}
The range of $\delta$ implies $K\geq1$.  We retain the convention
$\Harm_0=0$ for completeness.
