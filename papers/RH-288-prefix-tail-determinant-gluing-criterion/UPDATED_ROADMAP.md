# Roadmap after RH-288

The remaining executable leaf is a direct weighted estimate from the noisy
modulus complement to the deterministic anchor, making `P_sigma(R)` vanish
on the target disk.  A sufficient typed route must prove both a weighted
total-noisy-trace versus counterloop-plus-anchor estimate and a weighted
modulus-head versus finite-radius-counterloop estimate.  Fixed-order,
unweighted-prefix, or head-only transport is insufficient.
