# Roadmap after RH-299

An actual noisy-head theorem may have unequal ranks; zero padding is legal
because it leaves all positive moments unchanged.  On the minimal bridge
clock, this disk-bounded root-Lipschitz route is guaranteed by a padded
root-l1 cost little-o of sigma raised to 0.6729348509145321.  The radial
family makes that threshold sharp for the stated information class; a
stronger aggregate norm may avoid the termwise bound.

Finite floating root errors do not supply this asymptotic rate.
