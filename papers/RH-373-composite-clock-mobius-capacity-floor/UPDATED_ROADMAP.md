# RH-373 updated roadmap

RH-373 upgrades the unconditional RH-366 capacity floor from `4/pi^2` to
`97/(24*pi^2)` using a composite arithmetic clock.  The proof uses only an
explicit independent phase set, squarefree densities in arithmetic
progressions, and fixed-frequency Möbius cancellation.

The shortest remaining routes are:

1. Determine whether a family of composite clocks has a provable limiting
   selector floor, without confusing a supremum of fixed certificates with
   the adaptive capacity limit.
2. Prove a genuine Möbius run-envelope theorem for the RH-371 eight-run
   expression, or a precise scoped negative for its ordinary convergence.
3. Obtain a higher-order correlation theorem for memory-dependent safe
   transducers.
4. Reopen the strong-space Ulam route only after an explicit mesh-independent
   norm, projection estimate, and contour around `-1` are supplied.

No current route supplies a canonical operator, prime-power trace, zero
identification, or any Gate A--E result.
