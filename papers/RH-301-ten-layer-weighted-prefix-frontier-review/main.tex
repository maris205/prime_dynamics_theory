\begin{abstract}
RH-292--RH-301 audit the last analytic determinant leaf after the
projection-free spectral tail.  Tail absorption shortens the missing bridge
from slope four to the sharp mass-and-cap slope
$1/\log(10/7)$.  We derive the exact uniform coefficient-rate threshold,
upgrade coefficientwise convergence to a weighted bridge on an unspecified
slow clock, and prove that unweighted agreement even on the exact clock is
insufficient.  The existing Gaussian orbit-tube clock is strictly too short.
At the natural endpoint-resolution clock, the shortened bridge crosses one
counterloop alias and the slope-four cut crosses two.  The square-root parity
term blocks separate absolute majorants.  Zero-padded root transport gives a
sharp conditional head rate, while annular $H^\infty$ or $H^2$ convergence
would directly close the coefficient norm without resolving roots.  None of
these actual quantitative inputs is presently verified.  Both typed ledgers
remain four-of-five and Gates A--E remain open.
\end{abstract}

\section{The shortened bridge}
Let $R=7/5$, $q=1/2$, and
\[
 m_\sigma=\lceil4L_\sigma\rceil,\qquad
 h_\sigma=\lceil a_*L_\sigma\rceil,\qquad
 a_*=\frac1{\log(10/7)},\qquad L_\sigma=\log(1/\sigma).
\]
RH-292 proves
\[
 P_\sigma(m_\sigma)\le
 P_\sigma(h_\sigma)+S_\sigma(h_\sigma)+T_\sigma(h_\sigma),
\]
where the last two terms tend to zero and
$S_\sigma(h_\sigma)=O(L_\sigma^{-1})$.  Thus the shortest bridge clock
certified by the current sharp mass-and-cap envelope has slope
$a_*=2.803673252\ldots$, not four.  RH-293 shows that a uniform coefficient
error $O(\sigma^\beta)$ has the sharp information-class threshold
\[
 \beta_*=a_*\log R=0.9433582098747317\ldots,
\]
with logarithmic decay at equality.

RH-294 strengthens the fixed-order noisy/counterloop limits to a weighted
full-trace constituent on some selected $h_\sigma\to\infty$.  It gives no
lower bound on that clock.  RH-295 proves why a rate is indispensable: one
escaping coefficient spike can have a vanishing maximum on the prescribed
window and a divergent weighted budget.

\section{Two admissible quantitative interfaces}
For finite multisets padded by zeros, RH-299 proves
\[
 |\sum x_j^n-\sum y_j^n|\le nB^{n-1}d_1^0(X,Y).
\]
At $B=\beta$ and the minimal clock, the disk-bounded Lipschitz route is
guaranteed by a root cost decaying faster than
$\sigma^{0.6729348509\ldots}$.  The radial family makes this
information-class threshold sharp.  No such noisy modulus-head matching is
known, and this route would supply only the head constituent.

RH-300 gives the direct aggregate alternative.  If
\[
 g_\sigma(z)=\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{(\tau_{\sigma,n}-a_n)z^n}{n}
\]
is holomorphic on a neighborhood of $|z|\le\rho$ for $\rho>R$ and has a
controlled annular norm, then
\[
 P_\sigma^\infty(R)\le
 \|g_\sigma\|_{H^\infty(\rho)}
 \frac{(R/\rho)^2}{1-R/\rho},
\]
or
\[
 P_\sigma^\infty(R)\le
 \|g_\sigma\|_{H^2(\rho)}
 \frac{(R/\rho)^2}{\sqrt{1-(R/\rho)^2}}.
\]
The certified target radius leaves the strict annulus
$1.4<\rho<1.426787483864073\ldots$.  An endpoint counterexample shows why
the $H^2$ radius gap matters.
