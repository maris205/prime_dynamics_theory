# Roadmap after RH-301

The preferred reopening input is a direct aggregate theorem for

    g_sigma(z) = sum_(n>=2) (tau_(sigma,n)-a_n) z^n/n

on one certified annulus 1.4<rho<1.4267874838640739.  Vanishing H-infinity
or H2 norm there directly supplies the weighted prefix.

The typed two-constituent alternative must prove, on the minimal clock
ceil(log(1/sigma)/log(10/7)):

1. a parity-renormalized, alias-inclusive weighted full-trace bridge; and
2. actual modulus-head transport with a quantified root/Fourier/contour rate.

Pre-alias fixed moments, finite cloud fits, more tail theorems, and separate
absolute parity majorants are not reopening inputs.  Gates A--E remain open.
