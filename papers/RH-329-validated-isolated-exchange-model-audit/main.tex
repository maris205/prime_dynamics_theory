\begin{abstract}
The joint first-alias certificate requires a boundary observation, a fixed
reference, a shell scale and contrast, every Duhamel weight, and a far
remainder from one coherent data source.  We freeze a graded
finite-dimensional source defined from exact rational data and audit it
without fitting any field after the demand is known.  Two-state exchange
blocks give
$\Tr K_c^m=1+c^m$; diagonal alias blocks and scalar parity blocks realize the
other packets exactly.  The boundary, observation error, Duhamel defects,
and far remainder vanish in this model.  Nevertheless its frozen contrast
$4/5$ fails the joint equation.  We prove
\[
 \frac{\cP_k}{\cA_k}\longrightarrow C_*C_M\in(0,1),\qquad
 \frac{e_k}{\cA_k}\longrightarrow-(1-C_*C_M)<0,
 \qquad \frac{e_k}{kR^{-2k}}\longrightarrow-\infty.
\]
The required even power remains eventually reachable, but its contrast
radius tends to one.  Thus zero best-case reachability does not imply
matching for this pre-frozen isolated model.  The family is graded rather
than one all-order operator, and it is not identified with the actual noisy
transfer operator.  No full-trace replacement, full-trace divergence, or
Riemann-hypothesis conclusion follows.
\end{abstract}

\section{Frozen data and the audit question}

All constants in this paper are model definitions, and the audit domain is
the set of integers $k\ge2$.  Put
\begin{align}
 r_H&=\frac{17}{20},& R&=\frac75,
 &\Lambda&=\frac{2098216888035403}{1250000000000000},
 \label{eq:radii}\\
 C_M&=\frac{9731714526004839}{5000000000000000},
 &C_*&=\frac{26314633984227}{250000000000000},
 &C_b&=\frac{1152012873}{2500000000}.
 \label{eq:constants}
\end{align}
The terminating decimal strings from which these fractions arise are
treated as exact rationals.  They are not interval enclosures of physical
constants.  We also freeze
\begin{equation}
 c_{\rm iso}=\frac45,
 \qquad c_0=\frac35,
 \qquad c_b=\frac12,
 \qquad \eta=0,
 \qquad \sigma_k=\Lambda^{-2k}.
 \label{eq:frozen-contrasts}
\end{equation}
The clearance metadata are $C_b$ and the inherited orientation
$(+,-,+)$.  Because the boundary blocks below are identical, these metadata
do not enter a fitted correction.

Define
\begin{equation}
 \beta^2=\frac{1}{r_H^2\Lambda},
 \qquad
 a_k=\frac{2k-2}{C_M}+2,
 \qquad
 \cA_k=a_k\beta^{2k},
 \qquad
 H_k=kR^{-2k}.
 \label{eq:scales}
\end{equation}
The RH-328 fixed-reference equation asks whether
\begin{equation}
 e_k=\cB_k+\cS_k+\cR_k+\cP_k-\cA_k=o(H_k)
 \label{eq:joint-ledger}
\end{equation}
for data obtained before inspecting the demand.  Our answer is negative for
the model defined below.

\begin{remark}[Graded scope]
For each $k$ we define a finite-dimensional collection of blocks.  Their
dimensions and some roots depend on $k$.  We do not claim that one operator
has all these power traces simultaneously.  This distinction is part of the
theorem statement, not a technical omission.
\end{remark}

\section{Exact block realization}

For $0\le c\le1$ let
\begin{equation}
 K_c=\frac12
 \begin{pmatrix}1+c&1-c\\1-c&1+c\end{pmatrix}.
 \label{eq:exchange}
\end{equation}
The symmetric and antisymmetric unit vectors are eigenvectors with
eigenvalues $1$ and $c$.

The positive contrast range is a deliberate model restriction.  We do not
claim to identify the sign in the larger even-power class $|c|\le1$.

\begin{lemma}[Exchange trace law]
For every integer $m\ge1$,
\begin{equation}
 \Tr K_c^m=1+c^m.
 \label{eq:exchange-trace}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Diagonalize $K_c$ in its symmetric--antisymmetric basis and take the trace.
\end{proof}

Let $\beta_k>0$ be the unique root satisfying
\begin{equation}
 \beta_k^{2k}=\frac{\beta^{2k}}{C_M}
 \label{eq:beta-k}
\end{equation}
and define the $2k$-dimensional alias block
\begin{equation}
 C_k=\operatorname{diag}(\beta_k I_{2k-2},\beta I_2).
 \label{eq:alias-block}
\end{equation}
For the parity channel put
\begin{equation}
 \delta_k=C_*\Lambda^{-k},\qquad
 J_k^{\rm ref}=[r_H^{-1}],\qquad
 J_k^{\rm iso}=[r_H^{-1}(1-\delta_k)].
 \label{eq:parity-blocks}
\end{equation}
Finally set $s_k=\cA_k^{1/(2k)}$ and define
\begin{align}
 D_k^{\rm iso}&=D_k^{\rm ref}=s_kK_{c_b},
 \label{eq:boundary-blocks}\\
 Q_k^{\rm iso}&=s_kK_{c_{\rm iso}},
 &Q_k^{\rm ref}&=s_kK_{c_0}.
 \label{eq:shell-blocks}
\end{align}

\begin{proposition}[Operator-coherent packet identities]
The blocks above give
\begin{align}
 \Tr C_k^{2k}
 &=\left(\frac{2k-2}{C_M}+2\right)\beta^{2k}=\cA_k,
 \label{eq:alias-identity}\\
 \cP_k
 &:=\Tr\{(J_k^{\rm ref})^{2k}-(J_k^{\rm iso})^{2k}\}
 =r_H^{-2k}\{1-(1-\delta_k)^{2k}\},
 \label{eq:parity-identity}\\
 \cB_k
 &:=\Tr\{(D_k^{\rm iso})^{2k}-(D_k^{\rm ref})^{2k}\}=0,
 \label{eq:boundary-identity}\\
 \cS_k
 &:=\Tr\{(Q_k^{\rm iso})^{2k}-(Q_k^{\rm ref})^{2k}\}
 =\cA_k(c_{\rm iso}^{2k}-c_0^{2k}).
 \label{eq:shell-identity}
\end{align}
There is no omitted block, so $\cR_k=0$, and the matrix observation is the
definition of the shell, so $\cE_k^{\rm obs}=0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:beta-k} and the multiplicities in
\eqref{eq:alias-block} give \eqref{eq:alias-identity}.  The scalar power
difference gives \eqref{eq:parity-identity}.  Identical boundary blocks give
\eqref{eq:boundary-identity}.  Apply \eqref{eq:exchange-trace} to the two
scaled shell blocks; the common eigenvalue-one contribution cancels and
$s_k^{2k}=\cA_k$, proving \eqref{eq:shell-identity}.
\end{proof}

Every scale and contrast in this proposition is frozen by
\eqref{eq:radii}--\eqref{eq:frozen-contrasts}.  In particular, we do not
solve the matching equation for $c_{\rm iso}$ and then insert the result into
the model.

\section{Exact matching equation and asymptotic no-go}

In this model the signed demand and required even power are
\begin{align}
 D_k&=\cA_k-\cP_k-\cB_k=\cA_k-\cP_k,
 \label{eq:demand}\\
 y_k&=c_0^{2k}+\frac{D_k}{\cA_k}
 =c_0^{2k}+1-\frac{\cP_k}{\cA_k}.
 \label{eq:required-power}
\end{align}
The best-case RH-328 reachability screen is zero precisely when
$y_k\in[0,1]$.  The actual fixed-contrast mismatch is different:
\begin{equation}
 \frac{e_k}{\cA_k}
 =c_{\rm iso}^{2k}-y_k
 =c_{\rm iso}^{2k}-c_0^{2k}
   +\frac{\cP_k}{\cA_k}-1.
 \label{eq:normalized-residual}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Small parity gap]
If $x_k=C_*\Lambda^{-k}$, then
\begin{equation}
 \frac{1-(1-x_k)^{2k}}{2kx_k}\longrightarrow1.
 \label{eq:binomial-limit}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Here $x_k\to0$ and $kx_k\to0$.  The inequalities
$1-(1-x)^n\le nx$ and
$1-(1-x)^n\ge nx-\binom n2x^2$ for $0\le x\le1$ squeeze the ratio between
$1-(2k-1)x_k/2$ and $1$ for all sufficiently large $k$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Validated isolated fixed-contrast failure]
\label{thm:no-go}
For the frozen graded family,
\begin{align}
 \frac{\cP_k}{\cA_k}&\longrightarrow\gamma:=C_*C_M,
 &0&<\gamma<1,
 \label{eq:parity-alias-limit}\\
 y_k&\longrightarrow1-\gamma\in(0,1),
 \label{eq:y-limit}\\
 \frac{e_k}{\cA_k}&\longrightarrow-(1-\gamma)<0,
 \label{eq:e-alias-limit}\\
 \frac{\cA_k}{H_k}&\longrightarrow+\infty,
 &\frac{e_k}{H_k}&\longrightarrow-\infty.
 \label{eq:target-divergence}
\end{align}
Thus the reachability screen is eventually exactly zero, while the frozen
model fails the target matching condition.  If
$r_k=y_k^{1/(2k)}$ is the required contrast radius, then
\begin{equation}
 r_k\longrightarrow1,
 \qquad r_k-c_{\rm iso}\longrightarrow\frac15.
 \label{eq:radius-failure}
\end{equation}
Moreover, the RH-328 precision scales are exactly
\begin{equation}
 \frac{H_k}{\cA_k}
 =\frac{k}{a_k}(\beta R)^{-2k},
 \qquad
 \frac{H_k}{k\cA_k}
 =\frac{1}{a_k}(\beta R)^{-2k}.
 \label{eq:precision-scales}
\end{equation}
The power mismatch in \eqref{eq:normalized-residual} is not
$o(H_k/\cA_k)$, and the radius gap in \eqref{eq:radius-failure} is not
$o(H_k/(k\cA_k))$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Because $\beta^{2k}=r_H^{-2k}\Lambda^{-k}$, the preceding lemma yields
\[
 \frac{\cP_k}{\cA_k}
 =\frac{2kC_*}{a_k}\{1+o(1)\}
 \longrightarrow C_*C_M,
 \]
since $a_k/k\to2/C_M$.  Exact integer comparison gives
\begin{equation}
 \gamma
 =\frac{256086505790802487295251674453}
        {1250000000000000000000000000000}\in(0,1).
 \label{eq:exact-gamma}
\end{equation}
Both fixed contrasts are strictly subunit, so
\eqref{eq:required-power} and \eqref{eq:normalized-residual} prove
\eqref{eq:y-limit} and \eqref{eq:e-alias-limit}.  In particular,
$y_k\in(0,1)$ for all sufficiently large $k$.

Next,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\cA_k}{H_k}
 =\frac{a_k}{k}(\beta R)^{2k},
 \qquad
 (\beta R)^2
 =\frac{980000000000000000}{606384680642231467}>1.
 \label{eq:growth-certificate}
\end{equation}
The first factor tends to $2/C_M>0$, proving alias-to-target divergence.
Multiplying it by the negative nonzero limit in
\eqref{eq:e-alias-limit} proves $e_k/H_k\to-\infty$.
Finally $\log y_k$ converges to the finite number $\log(1-\gamma)$, so
$\log r_k=(\log y_k)/(2k)\to0$.  Hence $r_k\to1$ and
\eqref{eq:radius-failure} follows.  Inverting
\eqref{eq:growth-certificate} gives the first identity in
\eqref{eq:precision-scales}; division by $k$ gives the second.  Both right
sides tend to zero exponentially, whereas
\eqref{eq:normalized-residual} has the nonzero limit
$-(1-\gamma)$ and the radius gap tends to $1/5$.  This proves both precision
failures.
\end{proof}

This theorem is stronger than a finite failed fit: its sign and target-scale
failure hold eventually by exact asymptotics.  It is weaker than an actual
noisy-trace theorem because every block belongs only to the isolated model.

\section{Duhamel and omitted-remainder audit}

The product comparison is deliberately recorded leg by leg even though the
model representation is exact.  In each shell channel
$c\in\{c_{\rm iso},c_0\}$, compare the $2k$ factors
\begin{equation}
 A_{j,k}^{(c)}=G_{j,k}^{(c)}=Q_{c,k},
 \qquad 1\le j\le2k.
 \label{eq:identical-legs}
\end{equation}
For the spectral matrix norm, $\lVert K_c\rVert=1$ and hence
$\lVert Q_{c,k}\rVert=s_k$.  The trace observation on two-dimensional
matrices obeys $|\Tr X|\le2\lVert X\rVert$.  Therefore every telescoping
weight is
\begin{equation}
 W_{j,k}^{(c)}=2s_k^{2k-1}
 =2\cA_k^{(2k-1)/(2k)},
 \label{eq:duhamel-weight}
\end{equation}
while every leg defect is
$\delta_{j,k}^{(c)}=\lVert A_{j,k}^{(c)}-G_{j,k}^{(c)}\rVert=0$.

\begin{proposition}[Complete isolated Duhamel ledger]
Across the noisy and reference shell channels there are exactly $4k$
prefix/suffix weights.  None is discarded, and
\begin{equation}
 \cU_k
 =\sum_{c\in\{c_{\rm iso},c_0\}}
   \sum_{j=1}^{2k}W_{j,k}^{(c)}\delta_{j,k}^{(c)}=0.
 \label{eq:zero-majorant}
\end{equation}
Together with $\cE_k^{\rm obs}=\cR_k=0$, the best- and worst-case
uncertainty residuals both equal $|e_k|$.
\end{proposition}

The zero in \eqref{eq:zero-majorant} certifies internal implementation of the
isolated block identity.  It does not bound the defect between an actual
noisy critical leg and this model.  Likewise $\cR_k=0$ says that the model
has no omitted block; it is not an estimate for the physical far region.

\section{Boundary and RH-330 handoff}

The validated conclusion is precisely this: the pre-frozen graded isolated
exchange family fails the RH-328 joint matching equation, even though its
best-case contrast reachability screen eventually passes.  The conclusion
does not identify the family with the actual noisy operator, establish a
physical shell scale or contrast, control actual moving-order Duhamel
defects, prove an actual far-remainder little-$o$ estimate, replace the full
trace, or prove actual full-trace divergence.

RH-330 must state a transfer criterion retaining boundary, shell, parity,
alias, observation, and far-remainder terms on one clock.  It must quantify
the replacement of every isolated field and preserve signed cancellation at
scale $o(H_k)$.  Passing or failing a different isolated model cannot bypass
that transfer step.

All five program gates remain false/open: no canonical intrinsic dynamical
spectral determinant, oriented unitary completion, self-adjoint generator
with an intrinsic $T\log T$ law, von Mangoldt prime-power trace, or equality
with the completed-zeta divisor is proved.  In particular, no
Hilbert--Polya operator is constructed, no Riemann zero is identified, and
the Riemann Hypothesis is not proved.
