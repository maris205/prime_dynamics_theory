\begin{abstract}
TPC-337 found that centered variation dominates the response of four masked
source components under five coordinate controls.  We enlarge that orbit to
nine controls while holding the source and the all-plus operator fixed.  The
centered fraction remains high in every one of six windows (87.72--89.73
percent), and the trace-normalized covariance spectrum changes by only
0.0264--0.0441 in $L^1$.  A selected signed interaction is not stable:
twin--zero covariance is negative for the five-control ensemble and positive
for the nine-control ensemble in all six rows.  Thus the finite energy
structure is more reproducible than any signed covariance law.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and nested ensembles}

Write the parent-locked source as
\[
 \beta=\sum_{C\in\{T,B,P,Z\}}\beta_C,
 \qquad \beta(t)=\Lambda(t+2)-b^{(2)}(t),
\]
on the six intervals $I_{o,N}=\{o,\ldots,o+N/2-1\}$ with
$o\in\{42001,44001\}$ and $N\in\{2048,4096,8192\}$.  The labels denote
twin prime, non-twin prime shift, prime-power shift, and zero-support.

The fixed operator is the all-plus deleted-diagonal shell matrix
\[
 A=\sum_{54<p\le108}B_{p,54,1},\qquad H=66.
\]
The five-control ensemble is identity, the affine maps $(3,11)$, $(5,17)$,
$(7,29)$, and reversal.  The nine-control ensemble adds $(9,1)$, $(11,13)$,
$(13,17)$, and $(17,19)$ modulo the source count.  All multipliers are odd,
so these are bijections on the powers-of-two source counts.

For an ensemble $J$, define
\[
 y_{C,j}=AP_j\beta_C,\quad
 \bar y_{C,J}=\frac1{|J|}\sum_{j\in J}y_{C,j},\quad
 z_{C,j,J}=y_{C,j}-\bar y_{C,J}.
\]

\section{Finite identity and spectrum}

The centered vectors sum to zero.  Therefore, for either nested ensemble,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:split}
 \frac1{|J|}\sum_{j\in J}\|y_{C,j}\|_2^2
 =\|\bar y_{C,J}\|_2^2+
 \frac1{|J|}\sum_{j\in J}\|z_{C,j,J}\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
The class covariance Gram matrix is
\[
 K_J(C,D)=\frac1{|J|}\sum_{j\in J}
 \langle z_{C,j,J},z_{D,j,J}\rangle.
\]
For any $a\in\mathbb R^4$,
\[
 a^TK_Ja=\frac1{|J|}\sum_{j\in J}
 \left\|\sum_Ca_Cz_{C,j,J}\right\|_2^2\ge0.
\]
This proves positive semidefiniteness but says nothing about the sign of an
individual off-diagonal entry.  Since changing $J$ changes the centering,
there is no nested sign monotonicity to assume.

We compare the eigenvalues of $K_J$ after division by its trace.  This removes
the overall response scale for a descriptive finite spectral comparison; it
does not create a limiting object.

\section{Audit and exact anchor}

The producer computes all nine outputs once per class and derives both nested
ledgers.  An independent checker rebuilds the source by trial factorisation
and accumulates the prime shell in reverse order.  Mutation stress tests the
control count, energy dominance, sign reversal, anchor, and firewall.  A
rational two-vector anchor verifies the mean/centered identity exactly:
for orbit vectors $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$, the average, coherent, and centered
energies are $1$, $1/2$, and $1/2$.

The normalized covariance-spectrum $L^1$ distance between the two ensembles is
0.0264396--0.0440592.  The corresponding unnormalized covariance matrices
change by 0.217894--0.237903 in relative Frobenius norm.  These two facts can
coexist: the spectral shape is fairly close after normalization, while a
particular off-diagonal entry crosses zero.

The sign reversal is not a numerical borderline phenomenon.  The smallest
absolute twin--zero entry in each ensemble is separated from zero by the
declared guard, and the independent replay reproduces both six-row censuses.
It nevertheless remains a finite statement about two explicitly chosen
ensembles.

\section{Interpretation and firewall}

The positive result is a nested validation of the robust part of TPC-337:
position-sensitive covariance carries most of the output response even after
four new affine controls are added.  The obstruction is that signed class
interaction is not canonical.  A proof strategy that needs a fixed sign for
the twin--zero term cannot be justified by the five-control panel.

The identities are \texttt{PROVED\_EXACT\_FINITE\_DECLARED\_MODEL}; the
nested replay, energy census, and finite spectral distances are
\texttt{NUMERICALLY\_CERTIFIED\_FINITE}.  The sign reversal is
\texttt{REFUTED\_SCOPED} for ensemble invariance.  There is no arithmetic
advance, fixed-power credit, official evaluator pass, or twin-prime result:
\[
 \texttt{ARITHMETIC\_ADVANCE=NO},\qquad
 \texttt{FIXED\_POWER\_CREDIT=0},\qquad
 \texttt{FULL\_GATE\_B=OPEN}.
\]
The official Session evaluator files are absent; local Bridge-B is fail-closed.

\section{Next question}

The next route should avoid relying on signed covariance.  We therefore test a
sign-free mask-aware operator envelope and compare it with the observed gains.
