\title{\textbf{A Literal Fixed-Shift Generic-Affine Packet}\\
\large Exact \(TT^*\), Signed-Prefix Crosswalks, and a
Growability Audit}
\author{Liang Wang}
\date{July 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
TPC-108 isolated the generic-affine arithmetic object, while
TPC-123--125 specify the archive, physical reassembly, and
prescribed-shift interfaces needed to call it without replacing the
literal coefficient.  We give a self-contained invocation theorem for
one complete fixed-\(h_0\) packet.  For every archived block
\[
 T_b=\sum_{z\in I_b}A_b(z)\e(-\alpha_bz),
 \qquad s_bu_b-a_bd_b=h_0,
\]
we prove the exact lag identity
\[
 |T_b|^2
 =\sum_r\e(-\alpha_br)
   \sum_{\substack{z,z+r\in I_b}}
   A_b(z+r)\overline{A_b(z)}
\]
and the exact canonical-order Abel identity.  For the complete block
family \(S_X=\sum_b\gamma_bT_b\), the normalized literal ratios obey
\[
 \frac{|S_X|}{\sum_b|\gamma_b|M_b}
 \le
 \left(\sum_bq_b\frac{|T_b|^2}{M_b^2}\right)^{1/2},
 \quad
 q_b=\frac{|\gamma_b|M_b}{\sum_c|\gamma_c|M_c}.
\]
This identifies a precise growing theorem that would close the H3
subgate after its separately audited outer loss.  The present paper
proves the finite identities and their typed invocation only.  It
does not prove the required uniform bound, a fixed-shift
\(\Ltwo\) saving, a parity breakthrough, or a twin-prime result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why another block statement is needed}

The hard packet of TPC-15 is an exact scalar remainder whose
\(o(X)\) estimate is equivalent, within that decomposition, to the
corresponding weighted fixed-shift prime-pair asymptotic
\citep{WangTPC15}.  TPC-93 exports its low window into affine
coordinates while preserving content, projector, polarization, and
the extracted M\"obius factors \citep{WangTPC93}.  TPC-107 insists
that the signed filter be applied before positive majorization
\citep{WangTPC107}; TPC-108 then writes the exact \(TT^*\) kernel and
explains why an unrestricted replacement would reach a
Chowla-strength problem \citep{WangTPC108,Tao2016LogChowla}.

Those identities are not callable merely because a sequence with the
same support has been constructed.  TPC-119 found that the audited
snapshot lacked a complete native reconnection archive
\citep{WangTPC119}.  TPC-123--125 therefore separate three interfaces:
path conservation, leaf-to-physical cover, and direct
prescribed-\(h_0\) intertwining
\citep{WangTPC123,WangTPC124,WangTPC125}.  This paper starts
\emph{after} those interfaces.  Its purpose is to state exactly what
the extracted arithmetic block is, what finite equalities may be
checked, and what genuinely growing assertion is still missing.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
An exact finite equality is \(\Lzero\).  Its verified attachment to
the literal fixed-\(h_0\) packet is \(\Lone\).  Only a uniform
coefficient-specific estimate on the complete growing family can be
\(\Ltwo\).  A finite computation, a frozen form, an averaged shift,
or a surrogate sign model does not change this typing.

\section{The typed literal block}

Fix one prescribed nonzero integer \(h_0\).  For each scale \(X\), let
\(\mathcal B_X\) be the \emph{complete} set of long generic blocks
selected by the verified archive and physical-cover maps.  A block
record is
\[
 b=(I_b,d_b,s_b,u_b,a_b,\alpha_b,\gamma_b,\mathfrak m_b,
    \mathfrak p_b,\mathfrak n_b),
\]
where:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item \(I_b\subset\mathbb Z\) is a finite ordered interval;
\item
  \(D_b(z)=d_b+s_bz\), \(V_b(z)=u_b+a_bz\), and
  \[
    s_bu_b-a_bd_b=h_0;
    \label{eq:determinant}
  \]
\item \(\alpha_b\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z\) is the literal frequency,
  and \(\gamma_b\in\C\) is the literal outer multiplier;
\item \(\mathfrak m_b,\mathfrak p_b,\mathfrak n_b\) contain,
  respectively, all masks, the path/provenance key, and the unique
  inherited physical normalization.
\end{enumerate}
The coefficient is not abbreviated to its two visible M\"obius
factors.  We write
\begin{equation}
 A_b(z)
 =
 \mu(D_b(z))\mu(V_b(z))\,
 \omega_b(z)\,
 \mathbf 1_{\mathcal M_b}(z),
 \label{eq:literal-coefficient}
\end{equation}
where \(\omega_b\) is the full complex reconstruction weight and
\(\mathbf 1_{\mathcal M_b}\) is the product of the actual content,
coprimality, prefix, interval, projector, and polarization masks.
In particular, the extracted factor
\(\mu(B)\mathbf 1_{(B,V_b(z))=1}\) from TPC-93 is part of
\eqref{eq:literal-coefficient}; it may not be deleted.

\begin{definition}[Archive extraction]
Let \(c_X\) be the native coefficient vector, \(M_X\) the
path-expanded archive matrix of TPC-123, and \(E_b\) the row selector
and exact reconstruction map for block \(b\).  The block is
\emph{literally extracted} if
\begin{equation}
 (A_b(z))_{z\in I_b}=E_bM_Xc_X
 \label{eq:archive-extraction}
\end{equation}
with equality of values and of the metadata listed above.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
Equation \eqref{eq:archive-extraction} is stronger than agreement of
one scalar block sum.  Null pairs can preserve a scalar sum while
changing the lag kernel, a prefix, or a determinant fiber.  It is
also stronger than equality after absolute values, which erases the
signed channel isolated in TPC-107.
\end{remark}

Define
\[
 \e(t)=e^{2\pi it},\qquad
 T_b=\sum_{z\in I_b}A_b(z)\e(-\alpha_bz),\qquad
 M_b=\sum_{z\in I_b}|A_b(z)|.
\]
Zero-mass blocks are retained in the archive but omitted from ratios.

\section{Exact \texorpdfstring{\(TT^*\)}{TT-star} and prefix identities}

\begin{theorem}[Literal lag expansion]
\label{thm:ttstar}
For every finite literal block,
\begin{equation}
 |T_b|^2
 =
 \sum_{r\in I_b-I_b}\e(-\alpha_br)C_b(r),
 \qquad
 C_b(r)=
 \sum_{\substack{z\in I_b\\z+r\in I_b}}
 A_b(z+r)\overline{A_b(z)}.
 \label{eq:ttstar}
\end{equation}
Moreover \(C_b(-r)=\overline{C_b(r)}\), so the right side is real and
nonnegative even though its individual lag terms need not be.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the square gives
\[
 |T_b|^2
 =
 \sum_{x,z\in I_b}
 A_b(x)\overline{A_b(z)}
 \e(-\alpha_b(x-z)).
\]
Set \(r=x-z\), hence \(x=z+r\), and collect all admissible pairs.
The symmetry follows by conjugating and replacing \(r\) by \(-r\).
Nonnegativity follows from the left side, not from termwise
positivity.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The four-M\"obius interior]
Substituting \eqref{eq:literal-coefficient} into \(C_b(r)\) exposes
\[
 \mu(D_b(z+r))\mu(V_b(z+r))
 \mu(D_b(z))\mu(V_b(z))
\]
times the literal masks and weights.  Both shifted affine pairs still
have determinant \(h_0\).  Replacing this expression by independent
random signs, or dropping the mask intersection at \(z,z+r\), proves
a statement about another kernel.
\end{remark}

The ordered form supplies a second exact diagnostic.  Write
\(I_b=\{z_1<\cdots<z_m\}\), and suppose one declared factorization is
\[
 A_b(z_i)\e(-\alpha_bz_i)=\sigma_iw_i.
\]
The choice of \(\sigma_i\) and \(w_i\), and the order \(z_i\), are
part of the archive.  Put \(P_k=\sum_{i\le k}\sigma_i\).

\begin{theorem}[Canonical-order Abel crosswalk]
\label{thm:abel}
For every such factorization,
\begin{equation}
 T_b=P_mw_m+\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}P_k(w_k-w_{k+1}).
 \label{eq:abel}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\[
 |T_b|
 \le
 \max_{1\le k\le m}|P_k|
 \left(
 |w_m|+\sum_{k<m}|w_k-w_{k+1}|
 \right).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since \(\sigma_i=P_i-P_{i-1}\), with \(P_0=0\),
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^m(P_i-P_{i-1})w_i
 =P_mw_m+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}P_i(w_i-w_{i+1}).
\]
The inequality is the triangle inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Theorems \ref{thm:ttstar} and \ref{thm:abel} are two exact views of
the same literal block, not two interchangeable hypotheses.
\(TT^*\) asks for a weighted aggregate of four-point lags.  Abel
asks for every signed prefix under one canonical order.  A bound in
one language must be transferred with all literal weights before it
can be used in the other.
\end{remark}

\section{Complete-family normalization}

The physical generic contribution has the exact form
\begin{equation}
 S_X=\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_X}\gamma_bT_b.
 \label{eq:family}
\end{equation}
Set
\[
 \mathcal A_X=\sum_b|\gamma_b|M_b,\qquad
 q_b=\frac{|\gamma_b|M_b}{\mathcal A_X}
\]
when \(\mathcal A_X>0\).  Then \((q_b)\) is a probability vector.

\begin{theorem}[Literal family invocation]
\label{thm:family}
For the complete archived block family,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{|S_X|}{\mathcal A_X}
 \le
 \sum_{b:M_b>0}q_b\frac{|T_b|}{M_b}
 \le
 \left(
 \sum_{b:M_b>0}
 q_b\frac{|T_b|^2}{M_b^2}
 \right)^{1/2}.
 \label{eq:family-bound}
\end{equation}
Both inequalities have sharp finite examples.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first inequality follows from
\[
 |S_X|\le\sum_b|\gamma_b||T_b|
 =\mathcal A_X\sum_bq_b|T_b|/M_b.
\]
The second is Cauchy--Schwarz in the probability measure \(q_b\).
Equality in both occurs when all nonzero \(\gamma_bT_b\) have a
common phase and all ratios \(|T_b|/M_b\) are equal.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Typed termwise sufficient condition]
\label{cor:h3}
Assume, on the complete actual growing family and in one physical
normalization, that
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{b:M_b>0}
 q_b\frac{|T_b|^2}{M_b^2}
 \le X^{-2\eta+o(1)}.
 \label{eq:growing-input}
\end{equation}
If the separately proved outer reconstruction and norm-conversion
cost is \(X^{\lambda_{\rm out}+o(1)}\), then the net relative saving
delivered by \eqref{eq:family-bound} is
\[
 X^{-(\eta-\lambda_{\rm out})+o(1)}.
\]
In the deliberately termwise TPC-108 ledger, this certificate reaches
the stronger displayed \emph{linear generic-window} exponent when
\begin{equation}
 \eta-\lambda_{\rm out}\ge\frac1{200}.
 \label{eq:h3-threshold}
\end{equation}
This is a conservative sufficient condition for that linear window,
not the exact quadratic-to-amplitude threshold of MVP1.  Calling the
MVP1 packet saving \(1/400\) still requires the separately verified
physical \(TT^*\) crosswalk and prefactor.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Take the square root in \eqref{eq:growing-input}, apply
\eqref{eq:family-bound}, and multiply by the separately certified
outer cost.  The last condition is the stronger termwise
linear-window threshold used in TPC-108; it is not derived from a
finite block and is not claimed necessary for every coherent H3
proof.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{No hidden averaging.}
The quantifier in \eqref{eq:growing-input} is over the actual
\(X\)-indexed family.  It cannot be replaced by any of the following:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2em]
\item a fixed finite list of blocks;
\item an average over shifts when \(h_0\) is prescribed;
\item an average over unrelated phases or moduli;
\item a density-one subfamily without charging its complement;
\item a family selected after inspecting favorable phase
  cancellation.
\end{enumerate}
The archive rule, the block family, and any good/bad selection must be
predeclared.

\section{What finite audits can and cannot decide}

\begin{proposition}[Finite saturation]
\label{prop:saturation}
For every finite support \(I\), every nonnegative magnitudes
\((m_z)_{z\in I}\), and every phase \(\alpha\), there are coefficients
with \(|A(z)|=m_z\) such that
\[
 \left|\sum_{z\in I}A(z)\e(-\alpha z)\right|
 =\sum_{z\in I}m_z.
\]
There are also coefficients with the same magnitudes whose sum is
zero whenever the polygon inequality
\(\max m_z\le\frac12\sum m_z\) holds.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For saturation take \(A(z)=m_z\e(\alpha z+\theta)\).
For cancellation, the polygon inequality is the classical necessary
and sufficient condition that vectors of lengths \(m_z\) close to a
polygon; rotate those vectors back by \(\e(\alpha z)\).
\end{proof}

Thus support, occupancy, and magnitudes alone do not determine the
signed ratio.  Conversely, one unusually small finite ratio does not
prove \eqref{eq:growing-input}.  A finite experiment is valuable for
checking the literal extraction and choosing a candidate analytic
lemma, but not for certifying an asymptotic exponent.

\begin{definition}[Four verdicts for this interface]
The block audit uses:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.3cm,style=nextline,font=\normalfont]
\item[\(\GOI\)]
The complete finite archive-to-block extraction, metadata,
normalization, \(TT^*\), and prefix identities pass.  This is at most
\(\Lone\).
\item[\(\GOH\)]
In addition, a theorem proves \eqref{eq:growing-input} on the complete
actual growing family and the separate cost satisfies
\eqref{eq:h3-threshold}.  Only this can close the declared H3
subgate.
\item[\(\STOP\)]
An exact growing counterfamily inside the quantifiers of a declared
candidate lemma violates it.  This stops that lemma, not all signed
routes.
\item[\(\NT\)]
The archive extraction, complete block census, metadata, physical
normalization, or growing theorem is absent.
\end{description}
\end{definition}

\section{Executable certificate and status}

The standard-library script
\texttt{experiments/tpc126\_literal\_packet\_audit.py} checks on exact
integer metadata and ordinary double-precision finite data:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the determinant identity and the presence of a declared sample
  metadata subset, not completeness of the literal archive metadata;
\item the complex lag identity and its Hermitian symmetry;
\item the canonical Abel identity and BV bound;
\item both family inequalities and a saturation example;
\item the logical separation between \(\GOI\) and \(\GOH\).
\end{enumerate}
It writes a deterministic JSON certificate.  It does not sample
primes, estimate a growing exponent, or promote a finite regression
to arithmetic evidence.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The literal arithmetic target is now an executable object with an
exact normalization and a sharp family-level invocation rule.  This
is forward movement at the interface: a future theorem has one
explicit left-hand side, one complete set of quantifiers, and one
loss threshold.  The hard step remains the growing signed estimate
itself.  Nothing here proves M\"obius cancellation for the actual
family, breaches the parity barrier, gives a prime-pair lower bound,
establishes a Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, or proves the twin-prime
conjecture.
