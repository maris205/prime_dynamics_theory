# Roadmap after RH-351

The RH-342--RH-351 batch ends at

    actual_growing_lower_even_signed_remainder_open.

The deterministic side is not the current blocker.  RH-263 proves the
all-order deterministic numerator anchor, RH-267 proves its certified
all-order envelope, RH-268 proves the sharp radius, and RH-350 proves
growing-window uniform laws for the selected lower-even demand and parity
arrays.

RH-351 proves that those arrays do not determine the selected direct
residual without a theorem for the actual signed remainder.  The two formal
completions

    Y=S-P  and  Y=0

give respectively zero and exponentially divergent selected subprefixes.
They are abstract coefficient ledgers, not physical noisy operators.

The narrowest positive reopening input is an actual moving-order theorem for

    Y_(k,j)=T_(k,m_(k,j))^rest-d_(sigma,k,2m_(k,j))

on a window `J_k->infinity`, `J_k=o(k)`.  In particular:

1. proving RH-350's aggregate small-`Y` hypothesis would yield a genuine
   physical selected-subprefix obstruction;
2. proving weighted closeness of `Y` to `S-P` would close only this selected
   lower-even window and would still leave the rest of the strict prefix;
3. an actual same-clock theorem for the critical and first-lower signed
   complements is still separately required;
4. actual head/counterloop transport, odd orders, upper-alias orders, and the
   full signed `E_off` aggregate remain open;
5. a direct annular theorem for the physical modulus complement may bypass
   the coefficientwise route.

RH-241's moving noisy all-order trace envelope and coefficient bridge must
remain separate from the later deterministic anchor and envelope.  A finite
table, a scalar completion, or the RH-350 deterministic uniformity theorem
cannot close that moving noisy obligation.

RH-288 may be activated only when its direct prefix and both analytic tails
hold in one physical determinant data type.  Gate A still additionally
requires a canonical intrinsic physical determinant, and Gates B--E remain
independent.  No current result constructs a Hilbert--Polya operator,
identifies Riemann zeros, proves a von Mangoldt trace, proves completed-zeta
divisor equality, or proves RH.
