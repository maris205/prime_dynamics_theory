\section{Coefficient case table}

For completeness, equation $a q_2+b q_1=4LQ$ and the inequalities
$2L<a+b<4L$ leave only a short finite table.  At $L=2$, the pair $(3,3)$ is
incompatible with its two cutoff lower bounds.  At $L=3$, primitivity removes $3$ and
the remaining pair $(5,5)$ is too large.  At $L=4$, the candidates with sum between
eight and sixteen are $(3,7)$, $(5,5)$, $(5,7)$, and $(7,7)$.  Cutoff lower bounds
remove the last two; primitive divisibility removes $(5,5)$.  Hence $(3,7)$ and its
exchange exhaust the primitive collision table.

The balanced sign profile can be chosen smoothly.  Start with any smooth odd function
that equals $-1$ on $[-1,-1/8]$, equals $1$ on $[1/8,1]$, and interpolate smoothly
inside $[-1/8,1/8]$.  Multiplication by an even compactly supported cutoff equal to one
on $[-1,1]$ preserves every sampled row value and makes the profile compactly
supported.
