\section{Exact advances and permanently removed shortcuts}
\label{sec:exact-advances}

\subsection{Magnitude branch: mass, energy, and coherence}

Let
\[
 S_X=\#\supp A_X,\qquad
 L_X=\|A_X\|_\infty,\qquad
 M_X=\|A_X\|_1,\qquad
 D_X=\|A_X\|_2^2,
\]
and \(N_{0,X}=J_XQ_X^2\).  TPC-83 combines the universal inequalities
\[
 \frac{M_X^2}{S_X}\le D_X\le L_XM_X
\]
with the verified literal envelopes
\[
 S_X\ll Q_X,\qquad
 L_X\ll X^{o(1)}\frac{N_{0,X}}{Q_X}.
\]
It follows exactly that subpower natural mass survival is equivalent
to subpower natural energy survival.  This closes the choice of
variable, not its lower bound \citep{WangTPC83}.

TPC-84 opens the post-bin coefficient into native determinant fibers.
For fiber moduli \(r_i\), the sharp free-phase resultant interval is
\[
 \left[(2r_{\max}-\sum_i r_i)_+,\ \sum_i r_i\right].
\]
It also proves
\[
 D_X=E_{{\rm trip},X}+C_{{\rm eq},X},
\]
where the physical triplet diagonal is at most on the loss-one scale.
Thus an eventual energy lower bound with fixed loss
\(\lambda_D<1\) must be carried asymptotically by positive
equal-determinant off-diagonal coherence.  This is a necessary
mechanism, not a proved lower bound \citep{WangTPC84}.

\begin{proposition}[Audited magnitude output]
\label{prop:magnitude-output}
Under the literal support and pointwise envelopes above, natural
post-bin mass survival is equivalent to natural determinant-energy
survival.  Moreover, if a future theorem proves
\[
 D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2
 \qquad\text{with fixed }\lambda_D<1,
\]
then
\[
 C_{{\rm eq},X}=(1-o(1))D_X>0.
\]
Neither conclusion supplies the mass or energy lower bound itself.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first statement is the two-sided TPC-83 exponent transfer.  For
the second, TPC-84 proves that the physical triplet diagonal is at
most on the loss-one scale, so it is \(o(D_X)\) under the displayed
fixed-loss hypothesis.  Insert this in
\(D_X=E_{{\rm trip},X}+C_{{\rm eq},X}\).
\end{proof}

A continuation may seek dominant-native-term survival on enough
actual fibers, or a positive structured same-determinant pairing
with controlled remainder.  These are research strategies, not an
exhaustive theorem-level disjunction.  Raw mass precedes fiber
summation and cannot lower-bound the post-bin norm.

\subsection{Zero-mode branch: Fourier, Tauberian, and surrogate
interfaces}

TPC-85 proves that
\[
 A_X(0)=0
\quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad
 Z_X=\widehat A_X(0)=0.
\]
Under no-wrap padding,
\[
 A_X=A_X^\circ+\frac{Z_X}{q_X}\one,\qquad
 D_X=\|A_X^\circ\|_2^2+\frac{|Z_X|^2}{q_X},
\]
and
\[
 Z_X=-\sum_{r\ne0}\widehat A_X(r).
\]
Conditional expectation preserves \(Z_X\) and changes the energy
normalization; content inversion returns the full shell in its
\(t=1\) term.  These identities eliminate three normalization
shortcuts but retain coherent Fourier reassembly as a valid branch
\citep{WangTPC85}.

TPC-86 sharpens that branch.  A positive-density structural zero arc
supports a fixed-order filter with bounded Wiener norm.  For the
literal packet,
\[
 |Z_X|
 \ll_k
 \max_{0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R}
 |\widehat A_X(r)|
 +
 X^{o(1)}N_{0,X}R^{1/2-k}.
\]
Write
\[
 R_X=X^{\varrho+o(1)}<q_X/2,\qquad
 \varrho<\frac{267}{400}.
\]
At the critical row, a target
\[
 |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2
\]
has an admissible filter tail whenever
\[
 (k-\tfrac12)\varrho>\frac{133}{400}+\eta_Z.
\]
The often quoted choice
\[
 k=2,\qquad R_X=X^{133/600+\eps}
\]
is the zero-loss corner \(\eta_Z=0\), with sufficiently small fixed
\[
 0<\eps<\frac{267}{400}-\frac{133}{600}
 =\frac{107}{240}.
\]
More generally that same window requires
\(\eta_Z<3\eps/2\).  The missing input is therefore
a coherent literal low-frequency estimate at a window matched to
the desired rate, not control of all \(q_X\) frequencies
\citep{WangTPC86}.

TPC-87 identifies the exact cost of importing logarithmic
cancellation.  For
\[
 S_N=\sum_{n\le N}a_n,\qquad
 L_N=\sum_{n\le N}\frac{a_n}{n},
\]
one has
\[
 \frac{S_N}{N}
 =
 \underbrace{L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m}_{\mathcal T_N(L)}.
\]
Thus ordinary cancellation is exactly the vanishing of the signed
backward Ces\`aro defect.  Normalized logarithmic cancellation alone
does not imply it.  Terminal-half control or vanishing dyadic
logarithmic variation can bound the defect for one sequence, but a
physical application must also survive the weighted outer sum.  If
\[
 Z_X=\sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}
 c_{\theta,X}S_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})
 +E_{\mathrm{content},X},
\]
the relevant quantitative certificate is
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}|c_{\theta,X}|N_{\theta,X}
 \left|\mathcal T_{N_{\theta,X}}(L_{\theta,X})\right|
 +|E_{\mathrm{content},X}|
 \le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2.
\label{eq:tauberian-aggregate-target}
\end{equation}
The Abel identity itself is \(\Lzero\); the growing estimate
\eqref{eq:tauberian-aggregate-target} is not proved
\citep{WangTPC87}.

TPC-88 and TPC-89 turn the same target into magnitude--phase and
projective geometry.  For \(A_X\ne0\),
\[
 \Flat_X
 =
 \frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}
 =
 \underbrace{\left(\frac{|Z_X|}{M_X}\right)^2}_{\canc_X^2}
 \underbrace{\frac{M_X^2}{D_X}}_{S_{\mathrm{eff},X}}.
\]
Under natural post-bin mass survival,
\[
 S_{\mathrm{eff},X}=X^{o(1)}Q_X,
\]
and at the critical endpoint
\[
 \Flat_X\le X^{1/400+o(1)}
 \iff
 \canc_X\le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X}
 \iff
 |Z_X|\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.
\]
If a same-frame surrogate \(b_X\) is used, its projective angle
transfers with the sharp law
\[
 |\rho(A_X)-\rho(b_X)|
 \le
 \frac{\|A_X-b_X\|_2}{\|b_X\|_2}.
\]
Uniform transfer of the critical angle therefore requires relative
accuracy \(O(J_X^{-1})\) in order; \(o(1)\) is insufficient
\citep{WangTPC88,WangTPC89}.

\begin{theorem}[Audited zero-mode fork]
\label{thm:zero-mode-fork}
In the natural-mass corner, any positive zero-mode continuation must
establish at least one of the following on the same literal frame:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the TPC-86 coherent low-frequency bound with
  \((k-\tfrac12)\varrho>133/400+\eta_Z\);
\item the TPC-87 signed Tauberian defects on every required affine
  fiber and prefix, with the aggregate rate
  \eqref{eq:tauberian-aggregate-target};
\item a same-frame TPC-89 surrogate with both angle and relative
  error \(X^{o(1)}/J_X\); or
\item a direct bound of equivalent strength for \(Z_X\).
\end{enumerate}
The audited results prove the equivalence or sufficiency of these
certificates where stated, but prove none for the growing physical
coefficient.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Items (i)--(iii) are the literal sufficient interfaces proved in
TPC-86, TPC-87, and TPC-89.  TPC-88 converts their common target to
the displayed \(Z_X\) scale under natural mass.  Each source paper
explicitly leaves its growing arithmetic hypothesis unproved.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Primitive affine branch}

For individually primitive forms
\[
 L_1(r)=d+sr,\qquad L_2(r)=u+ar,\qquad su-ad=h_0\ne0,
\]
TPC-90 proves
\[
 \gcd(a,s)\mid h_0,\qquad
 \gcd(L_1(r),L_2(r))\mid h_0.
\]
If \(\nu_{p,k}\) counts residues modulo \(p^k\) on which
\(p^k\mid L_1(r)\) or \(p^k\mid L_2(r)\), then
\[
 \nu_{p,k}
 =
 \one_{p\nmid s}+\one_{p\nmid a}
 -
 \one_{p\nmid as}\one_{p^k\mid h_0}.
\]
Consequently the simultaneous squarefree local product is positive
and uniformly bounded below by
\[
 \prod_p\left(1-\frac2{p^2}\right)>0.
\]
On positive intervals the exact sign split is
\[
 \mu(L_1)\mu(L_2)
 =
 \mu^2(L_1)\mu^2(L_2)
 \lambda(L_1)\lambda(L_2).
\]
All square-power local obstructions are now explicit; the unresolved
quantity is the weighted Liouville product on the actual growing
physical family \citep{WangTPC90}.

\subsection{Full-block reassembly branch}

TPC-91 places the zero-residual strengthening of the TPC-18
all-block hypothesis on the literal atom space.  With exact candidate
columns \(B_X\), target \(w_X\), and the coefficient rule
\(\mathscr C_X\) actually authorized upstream, this certificate is
\[
 B_Xc_X=w_X,\qquad c_X\in\mathscr C_X.
\]
For unrestricted synthesis the exact defect is
\[
 \eps_{\cov}
 =
 \|(I-B_XB_X^\dagger)w_X\|_2.
\]
For nonnegative synthesis, Farkas' alternative gives an exact dual
obstruction.  For a general coefficient rule, exact feasibility is
the membership \(w_X\in B_X\mathscr C_X\); zero distance is equivalent
only when the attainable image is closed.  Bounded overlap, even
overlap one with every atom covered, need not give an
\(o(1)\)-relative coefficient cover.

The original TPC-18 hypothesis is weaker: it may allow
\[
 w_X=B_Xc_X+r_X,\qquad \mathcal T_X(r_X)=o(X)
\]
in its physical scale, where \(\mathcal T_X\) denotes the original
literal physical reassembly functional, with the prescribed
coefficients and every
quantitative loss retained.  Thus exact coverage is the clean
zero-remainder sufficient route.  Its failure does not kill TPC-18
reassembly unless the physical residual route is also ruled out
\citep{WangTPC18,WangTPC91}.
