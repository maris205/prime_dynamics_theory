\section{Audited conclusion}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\begin{theorem}[Ten-paper audit conclusion]
\label{thm:ten-paper-conclusion}
TPC-83 through TPC-91 establish the following exact chain of
reductions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item natural determinant energy is equivalent to natural literal
  post-bin mass;
\item any determinant-energy lower bound with fixed loss
  \(\lambda_D<1\) must use asymptotically positive equal-determinant
  off-diagonal coherence;
\item the distinguished zero mode is not removed by centering,
  conditional expectation, or content inversion;
\item its coherent Fourier route reduces to a mesoscopic literal
  low-frequency window satisfying a rate-dependent filter condition
  (with \(R_X=X^{133/600+\eps}<q_X/2\) for fixed
  \(0<\eps<107/240\) in the second-order zero-loss corner);
\item logarithmic-to-ordinary transfer is measured by an exact signed
  Ces\`aro defect;
\item in the natural-mass corner the flatness target is exactly
  relative cancellation \(X^{o(1)}/J_X\);
\item a surrogate transfers that target in the uniform worst case
  only with \(O(J_X^{-1})\) relative accuracy in order, corresponding
  to \(X^{o(1)}/J_X\) in the physical convention;
\item primitive affine local squarefree obstructions are completely
  separated from the remaining Liouville sign correlation; and
\item the zero-residual strengthening of full-block reassembly is an
  exact allowed synthesis equation with sharp dual obstruction
  certificates, while the original TPC-18 hypothesis still permits a
  separately controlled physical residual.
\end{enumerate}
These are \(\Lzero\) closures and \(\Lone\) interfaces.  No paper in
the block proves a new \(\Ltwo\) growing fixed-\(h_0\) arithmetic
estimate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Items (i)--(ix) are respectively the audited main results of
TPC-83--TPC-91 summarized and cited in
\cref{sec:dependency-graph,sec:exact-advances}.  Each source states
its remaining growing arithmetic hypothesis explicitly.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Positive progress.}
The program now knows the correct post-bin mass variable, the
necessary coherence mechanism, the exact zero-mode target, a shorter
Fourier window, the exact Tauberian defect, the sharp relative
cancellation and surrogate scales, the complete primitive affine
local factor, and a falsifiable zero-residual cover equation with a
separate physical-residual alternative.  These are not merely
editorial refinements: each closes a required interface, strictly
reduces a literal target, or removes an invalid route by exact
counterexample.

\paragraph{Unresolved arithmetic.}
No lower bound for the actual \(M_X\) or \(D_X\), upper bound for
\(Z_X\), growing affine M\"obius/Liouville cancellation theorem,
literal same-frame surrogate, physical full-block reassembly, or TPC-18
primitive determinant-dispersion theorem has been proved.  The
completion conjunction in \cref{def:completion-conjunction} remains
open.

\paragraph{Independent ledgers.}
The recorded determinant bounds must satisfy
\[
 \lambda_D\le2\eta_Z.
\]
Separately, all other physical fixed-power losses must satisfy
\[
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400.
\]
Equality or excess stops the latter ledger.  The same gain or loss is
never entered in both.

\paragraph{Independent TPC-18 gates.}
Full-block reassembly and primitive determinant dispersion are
different hypotheses.  An exact cover is a sufficient zero-residual
reassembly certificate but does not estimate its columns; column
estimates prove neither exact coverage nor a physical residual bound.
A nonzero residual remains admissible only with a separate theorem for
its physical image.

\paragraph{Literal invariants.}
Every continuation retains one fixed nonzero \(h_0\), actual support,
native keys, masks, prefixes, short fibers, polarizations, exceptional
sectors, and one global post-aggregation normalization.  A coarse-key,
averaged-shift, fixed-form, or almost-all result remains at that scope
until a literal transfer is proved.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This paper is an \(\Lzero/\Lone\) audit and roadmap.  It does not
specialize \(h_0\) to \(2\), prove a fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood
asymptotic, breach the sieve parity barrier, give a prime-pair lower
bound, prove the twin-prime conjecture, or make any Riemann-hypothesis
claim.

Run priorities one and two first: a literal zero-mode certificate and
a post-bin mass/coherence theorem.  Use TPC-90 as the arithmetic
engine only after a specific physical block has been exported, and
use TPC-91 to certify the zero-residual route or kill the proposed
zero-residual family, while retaining the controlled-residual
alternative.  Stop
any assembled route at physical loss \(1/400\), but retain every exact
negative certificate as a publishable boundary result.
