\subsection{Directed dependency graph}

The arrows below mean ``uses or sharpens an interface from,'' not
``logically implies the final arithmetic theorem'':
\[
\begin{array}{rclcl}
\text{TPC-83} &\longrightarrow& \text{TPC-84},
&\qquad&
\text{TPC-83}\longrightarrow\text{TPC-88}
\longrightarrow\text{TPC-89},
\\[2mm]
\text{TPC-85} &\longrightarrow& \text{TPC-86}
\longrightarrow\text{TPC-87},
&&
\text{TPC-85},\text{TPC-87}\longrightarrow\text{TPC-90},
\\[2mm]
\text{TPC-18} &\longrightarrow& \text{TPC-91},
&&
\text{TPC-83--91}\longrightarrow\text{TPC-92}.
\end{array}
\]
TPC-84 is the coherence continuation of the mass branch.
TPC-86 and TPC-87 sharpen the Fourier and logarithmic alternatives
opened by TPC-85.  TPC-88 and TPC-89 express the final flatness target
as relative phase cancellation and then as a sharp surrogate-angle
requirement.  TPC-90 opens the affine correlation from TPC-85 after
the Tauberian warning in TPC-87.  TPC-91 is a cross-cutting audit of
the separate TPC-18 reassembly gate.

\subsection{Exact scorecard}

\begin{theorem}[Dependency audit]
\label{thm:dependency-audit}
The outputs summarized in \cref{tab:paper-scorecard} close exact identities,
sharp stability thresholds, and falsifiable interfaces.  Taken
together, they do not prove any of the remaining-gate entries in the
same row or in another row.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
TPC-83 and TPC-88 explicitly assume rather than prove mass or energy
survival \citep{WangTPC83,WangTPC88}.  TPC-84 proves only a
conditional necessity for positive coherence
\citep{WangTPC84}.  TPC-85--TPC-87 give identities, reductions, and
countermodels but no literal M\"obius bound
\citep{WangTPC85,WangTPC86,WangTPC87}.  TPC-89 supplies a sharp
transfer conditional on a surrogate that it does not construct
\citep{WangTPC89}.  TPC-90 proves local squarefree support while
leaving the Liouville product unresolved \citep{WangTPC90}.  TPC-91
defines and diagnoses the stronger zero-residual certificate and its
residual alternative, but neither instantiates the full physical
system, controls its physical remainder, nor proves determinant dispersion
\citep{WangTPC91}.  Therefore no remaining-gate entry is supplied by
the audited theorem statements.
\end{proof}
