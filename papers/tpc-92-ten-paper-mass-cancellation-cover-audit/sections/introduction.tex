\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Long proof programs accumulate two different kinds of progress.
Some steps establish an arithmetic estimate on the literal physical
coefficient.  Others remove ambiguity: they identify the correct
normalization, prove an exact transfer theorem, or exhibit a
counterexample to an invalid shortcut.  Both are useful, but they
must not be reported at the same proof level.

TPC-83 through TPC-91 were designed as a controlled exploration of
the fixed-shift determinant route after the completion-to-flatness
audit in TPC-82 \citep{WangTPC82}.  They investigate three quantities
and two reassembly interfaces:
\[
 M_X=\sum_n|A_X(n)|,\qquad
 D_X=\sum_n|A_X(n)|^2,\qquad
 Z_X=\sum_nA_X(n),
\]
together with the TPC-18 all-block reassembly and primitive determinant
dispersion \citep{WangTPC18}.  Here \(A_X\) is always the already
aggregated literal determinant coefficient on its actual support.
At the audited critical row we use
\[
 Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad
 J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad
 N_{0,X}=J_XQ_X^2.
\]
The padded Fourier modulus satisfies \(q_X\asymp Q_X\), and
\[
 d_q(r,0)=\min_{m\in\mathbb Z}|r+mq|
\]
denotes cyclic distance.  If \(v\ne0\) is a coefficient on the same
literal frame \(\Omega\), with \(S=\#\Omega\), put
\[
 Z(v)=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}v_\omega,\qquad
 D(v)=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}|v_\omega|^2,\qquad
 \rho(v)=\frac{|Z(v)|}{\sqrt{S D(v)}}.
\]
Every surrogate comparison below uses this same \(\Omega\); zero
padding or changing the frame changes \(\rho\).

The nine papers do not supply nine interchangeable estimates.
Instead, they form four branches:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item post-bin mass, determinant energy, and equal-determinant
  coherence;
\item distinguished zero-mode cancellation through Fourier,
  Tauberian, or surrogate interfaces;
\item primitive affine local support versus the remaining sign
  correlation; and
\item full-block reassembly of the upstream hard packet, with exact
  coverage as its zero-residual sufficient certificate.
\end{enumerate}
These branches must merge only after their normalizations and losses
have been matched literally.

\subsection{Purpose of the present audit}

This paper has four tasks.  First, it records the precise dependency
graph for TPC-83--TPC-91 from their actual theorem statements, rather
than from suggestive titles.  Second, it classifies every advance as
\(\Lzero\), \(\Lone\), or \(\Ltwo\).  Third, it identifies which
routes have been sharpened, which shortcuts have been killed, and
which arithmetic gates remain independent.  Fourth, it turns those
gates into a prioritized roadmap with explicit stop/go criteria.

The result is not a proof by accumulation.  Exact identities do not
become a growing fixed-shift correlation estimate merely because
many of them are now available.  Conversely, a negative transfer
result can be durable progress when it prevents an invalid route from
being retried.

\subsection{Headline conclusion}

The nine-paper block has made the route substantially more
falsifiable:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the correct mass variable is post-bin, not raw;
\item any determinant-energy lower bound with fixed loss below one
  requires equal-determinant coherence;
\item the zero mode is a coherent low-frequency or affine-sign
  problem, not a centering convention;
\item logarithmic-to-ordinary transfer requires a signed Tauberian
  defect with a rate that survives outer reassembly;
\item a surrogate must have uniform worst-case accuracy at the full
  \(J^{-1}\) scale (or \(X^{o(1)}/J_X\) in the physical convention);
\item local squarefree density does not supply sign cancellation; and
\item bounded overlap does not supply an exact full-block certificate,
  while a nonzero coefficient residual still needs a physical
  remainder theorem.
\end{itemize}
No paper in the block proves the remaining growing arithmetic bound.
The honest aggregate status is therefore strong \(\Lzero/\Lone\)
progress with no new \(\Ltwo\) theorem.
