\begin{abstract}
TPC-83 through TPC-91 form nine consecutive audits of the
fixed-shift matched-shell route; the present synthesis is the tenth
paper in that block.  We verify their exact dependency structure and
separate what has been closed from what remains arithmetic.

On the magnitude side, TPC-83 proves the sharp equivalence between
natural post-bin \(\ell^1\) mass and natural determinant-energy
survival, while TPC-84 shows conditionally that any
determinant-energy lower bound with fixed loss \(\lambda_D<1\) must
be carried asymptotically by positive equal-determinant off-diagonal
coherence.  On the phase side,
TPC-85 rules out three false normalization shortcuts.  TPC-86 gives
an exact coherent Fourier reconstruction and, in the zero-loss
corner \(\eta_Z=0\) with a second-order filter, reduces the critical
zero-mode input to a low-frequency window
\(R=X^{133/600+\varepsilon}\) for sufficiently small fixed
\(0<\varepsilon<107/240\); the general window depends on the
desired \(\eta_Z\) and must remain below \(q_X/2\).  TPC-87
identifies the precise signed
Ces\`aro defect needed to convert logarithmic cancellation to
ordinary cancellation.  TPC-88 factors determinant flatness as
\[
 \Flat=\canc^2\Seff
\]
and identifies the critical relative cancellation scale
\(\canc\le X^{o(1)}/J\) in the natural-mass regime.  TPC-89 proves
that a surrogate transfers this scale uniformly only at relative
\(\ell^2\) accuracy \(O(J^{-1})\).  TPC-90 completely classifies the
primitive affine squarefree local support and isolates the remaining
weighted Liouville sign correlation.  TPC-91 models the stronger
zero-residual full-block certificate as the constrained equation
\(Bc=w\), proves that bounded overlap does not imply it, and supplies
projection and Farkas obstruction certificates.  The original
TPC-18 hypothesis also permits a separately controlled physical
remainder, so exact coverage is a sufficient strengthening rather
than the only admissible reassembly.

These are genuine \(\mathrm{L0}\) closures and \(\mathrm{L1}\)
interfaces, but none is a new \(\mathrm{L2}\) estimate for the
literal growing fixed-\(h_0\) coefficient.  The actual post-bin
mass/energy lower bound and zero-mode upper bound remain open and
their recorded exponents must satisfy
\(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\).  TPC-18 full-block reassembly
(by zero residual or a proved physical remainder), primitive
determinant dispersion, and the strict physical loss
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400\) remain independent.  Equality
\(\lambda_D=2\eta_Z\) is admissible but has no determinant reserve;
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}\ge1/400\) stops the physical ledger.
Losses are not counted twice.

We finish with a prioritized roadmap and explicit stop/go tests for
four branches: low-frequency/log-Tauberian/surrogate zero-mode
control, determinant mass and coherence, primitive affine sign
arithmetic, and full-block reassembly certification.  The audited outcome
is forward but incomplete: no fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood
asymptotic, parity breach, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime
conclusion is claimed.
\end{abstract}
