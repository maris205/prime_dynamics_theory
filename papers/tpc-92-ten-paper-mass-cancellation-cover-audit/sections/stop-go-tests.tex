\section{Explicit branch stop/go tests}
\label{sec:stop-go-tests}

A stop test kills only the stated branch or certificate unless an
end-to-end impossibility theorem is proved.  Numerical failure is a
diagnostic, not a stop certificate.

\subsection{Branch I: zero-mode control}

\paragraph{Fourier go test.}
Fix the TPC-86 filter of order \(k\ge2\), its literal padding, and
\[
 R_X=X^{\varrho+o(1)}<q_X/2,\qquad
 \varrho<\frac{267}{400},\qquad
 (k-\tfrac12)\varrho>\frac{133}{400}+\eta_Z.
\]
For the zero-loss target \(\eta_Z=0\), the second-order choice
\(R_X=X^{133/600+\eps}\) is admissible for fixed
\(0<\eps<107/240\); for positive \(\eta_Z\) the window must be
enlarged according to the displayed condition while remaining below
\(q_X/2\).
The branch advances if the multiplier-weighted low-frequency term and
returned tail jointly imply
\[
 |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2
\]
for an \(\eta_Z\) compatible with
\eqref{eq:determinant-compatibility}.  Every exceptional frequency
must be charged by reconstruction-multiplier mass, not cardinality.

\paragraph{Fourier stop test.}
The particular filter certificate stops if a rigorous literal lower
bound on its exceptional or resonant contribution makes the target
impossible, if the zero arc loses positive density without a
replacement estimate, or if its unavoidable physical loss makes
\(\Lambda_{\rm phys}\ge1/400\).  A mean-square theorem or an
all-but-exceptional theorem that does not return the weighted
exceptional set is merely insufficient input, not a global
impossibility result.

\paragraph{Tauberian go test.}
For every physical affine sequence and every prefix used by outer
reassembly, prove a quantitative signed defect, terminal-half, or
variation bound such that the complete returned estimate satisfies
\[
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}|c_{\theta,X}|N_{\theta,X}
 \left|
 L_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})
 -\frac1{N_{\theta,X}}\sum_{m<N_{\theta,X}}
 L_{\theta,X}(m)
 \right|
 +|E_{\mathrm{content},X}|
 \le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,
\]
or the corresponding quantitative target for the literal block to
which the certificate is attached.  A per-fiber \(o(1)\) statement
without this outer rate does not pass.  The reciprocal weight
introduced on translated fibers must be transferred exactly.

\paragraph{Tauberian stop test.}
A theorem controlling only normalized logarithmic means, only fixed
forms, or almost all scales/origins fails this certificate until its
exceptional quantifiers are removed.  A proof that no available
uniformity can survive the literal growing coefficients kills that
transfer route, not M\"obius-specific alternatives.

\paragraph{Surrogate go test.}
Construct a same-frame \(b_X\) and prove
\[
 \rho(b_X)\le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X},
\qquad
 \frac{\|A_X-b_X\|_2}{\|b_X\|_2}
 \le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X}.
\]
The support map must be exact because zero padding changes \(\rho\).

\paragraph{Surrogate stop test.}
The chosen surrogate class is killed by a rigorous lower bound showing
that every allowed \(b_X\) has relative error
\(\gg X^c/J_X\) for some fixed \(c>0\), or by an exact
nonidentifiability counterexample.  Merely obtaining \(o(1)\) error
does not pass the gate.

\subsection{Branch II: determinant mass and coherence}

\paragraph{Mass go test.}
Prove
\[
 M_X\ge X^{-o(1)}N_{0,X}
\]
for the literal post-bin coefficient, or prove the equivalent natural
energy lower bound directly.  A more general fixed-loss result is
still usable, but must enter the full
\((\mu,\lambda_D,\eta_Z)\) profile rather than the natural corner.

\paragraph{Census go test.}
Execute the native-key TPC-84 decomposition with exact determinant
keys, then prove either a dominant-term lower bound on enough fibers
or a positive structured pairing whose unpaired remainder is smaller
uniformly in \(X\).  Finite computation can certify finite identities;
the growing lower bound requires a theorem.

\paragraph{Mass stop tests.}
The phase-blind dominant-term strategy stops if its exact lower
envelope is too small.  The natural-mass branch stops if one proves on
an unbounded sequence of scales the literal upper bound
\[
 M_X\le X^{-c+o(1)}N_{0,X}
\]
for some fixed \(c>0\).  Neither event rules out a nonnatural
loss-profile route.  A raw majorant or unexecuted census proves no
stop.

\subsection{Branch III: primitive affine sign arithmetic}

\paragraph{Go test.}
Prove cancellation for
\[
 \sum_{\substack{r\\
 L_{1,\theta,X}(r)L_{2,\theta,X}(r)\ne0}}
 W_{\theta,X}(r)
 \mu^2(|L_{1,\theta,X}(r)|)
 \mu^2(|L_{2,\theta,X}(r)|)
 \lambda(|L_{1,\theta,X}(r)|)
 \lambda(|L_{2,\theta,X}(r)|)
\]
uniformly over the literal growing coefficients, prescribed
nonzero determinant \(h_0\), origins, masks, relevant prefixes,
short-fiber treatment, polarizations, and outer keys.  The aggregate
bound must fit either the zero-mode target or the TPC-18 block target
to which it is attached.

\paragraph{Weaker go tests.}
A fixed-power saving on one literal residual block, a certified range
of square-divisor moduli, or a smoothed/frozen one-slot theorem is
publishable progress at its native level if it has an exact route back
toward the physical coefficient.  It is not reported as full
fixed-\(h_0\) cancellation.

\paragraph{Stop tests.}
Further local Euler-product calculations do not address the remaining
sign factor.  The present implementation stops if a rigorous transfer
lower bound shows that returning its excluded sectors necessarily
forces \(\Lambda_{\rm phys}\ge1/400\).  An averaged-shift or fixed-form
theorem that cannot select the prescribed growing family is an input
mismatch, not proof that every affine route fails.

\subsection{Branch IV: full-block reassembly}

\paragraph{Exact-cover go test.}
Export the complete physical target and candidate blocks to one
native row list.  Solve the coefficient rule actually derived
upstream; for the original unit cover the test is
\[
 B_X\one=w_X,
\]
not merely existence of an arbitrary signed solution.  Verify the
coefficient-weighted block error under the original normalization.

\paragraph{Exact-cover stop test.}
For the proposed block family, any verified nonzero orthogonal
residual or applicable Farkas vector is a rigorous stop certificate
for that zero-residual candidate family.  It is not a stop for the
original TPC-18 asymptotic reassembly.  A coarse determinant-key
solution does not pass.

\paragraph{Physical-residual go test.}
If only \(w_X=B_Xc_X+r_X\) is available with the upstream-prescribed
coefficients, prove the required physical estimate for
\(\mathcal T_X(r_X)\), retain its rate in
\(\Lambda_{\rm phys}\), and verify the original global normalization.
Small \(\|r_X\|_2\) alone is not this theorem.

\paragraph{Determinant-dispersion go test.}
After an admissible exact or residual reassembly, prove the TPC-18
primitive generic estimate uniformly on every returned block with the
required strict exponent margin.  Reassembly and dispersion are
checked separately.

\paragraph{Global stop test.}
Even if every local branch passes, equality or excess in
\(\Lambda_{\rm phys}\) stops the assembled route.  Failure of
\(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\) stops only the certificate formed from those
recorded \(D_X,Z_X\) bounds unless a sharper estimate is impossible.
