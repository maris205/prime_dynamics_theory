# Roadmap after RH-341

The RH-332--RH-341 batch ends at

    synchronized_actual_first_alias_signed_completion_open.

The deterministic target side is no longer the blocker: RH-263 supplies the
all-order numerator anchor, RH-267 supplies the certified all-order envelope,
and RH-268 supplies its sharp radius law.  The remaining obstruction is a
moving physical signed bridge on one common Hardy clock.

At `u=4k`, the next positive route must prove all three aggregate budgets

    D_(4k) -> 0,
    E_off,(4k) -> 0,
    q_(sigma,k,2k) = o(H_k),

while respecting the mandatory two-order compensation equations

    C_k^0-d_(sigma,k,2k) = D_k^orb + o(H_k),
    C_k^--d_(sigma,k,2k-2) = D_(k-1)^orb + o(H_(k-1)).

The narrowest admissible RH-342 route is therefore a physical moving-order
signed estimate for the combined complements and head defect on the same
clock and coefficient type.  It must either:

1. prove both orbit compensations and control the rest of `E_off` together
   with the head budget; or
2. prove that at least one compensation fails by a nonzero target-normalized
   amount in the actual corrected observation data type.

A separate absolute majorant cannot reopen the route.  Neither can a finite
fit, RH-329's rejected clock, a nonphysical isospectral family, an abstract
ledger completion, or a forward probability model without a cyclic trace
identification.

RH-288 may be invoked only after its direct prefix leaf and both tail leaves
are present in the same determinant data type.  Even then, Gate A requires a
canonical intrinsic physical determinant; Gates B--E remain independent.

No current source constructs a Hilbert--Polya operator, identifies a Riemann
zero, proves a von Mangoldt prime-power trace, proves completed-zeta divisor
equality, or proves the Riemann Hypothesis.
