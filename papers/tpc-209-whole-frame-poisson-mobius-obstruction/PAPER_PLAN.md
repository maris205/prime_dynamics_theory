# Paper plan

## Title

Whole-Frame Poisson Reindexing and the Möbius-Dilation Obstruction

## Main question

Does applying Poisson to the complete TPC-208 additive edge frame produce one
shared dual variable that can be sent directly to a source-valid Kloosterman
estimate?

## Main claims

1. Fixed-divisor Poisson reindexing is exact and shares one dual integer across
   the complete edge frame.
2. The full divisor sum is a vector-valued packet with divisor-dependent
   multiplicative permutations.
3. Multiplicative Fourier gives an exact shared-character profile normal form
   and a Gauss crosswalk back to V59.
4. A sharp alignment theorem rules out frame-only scalar collapse and any
   automatic power saving from the complete graph.

## Claim ceiling

`PROVED_STRUCTURAL_L1 / STOP_SCOPED_FRAME_ONLY_SAVING`.

The paper does not claim a prime-only BDH estimate, a Kloosterman attachment,
strict `1/400`, arithmetic `L2`, fixed-atom credit, or a twin-prime theorem.

## Validation

- exact producer/checker certificate;
- independent checker and optimized-mode checker;
- finite dilation and dual-index mutation checks;
- Gaussian Poisson sanity experiment;
- LaTeX compilation, text extraction, font and page QA.

## Follow-up

The next legitimate question is a profile-aware nonprincipal-character bound
for the actual Möbius/Poisson dual packets, with the V59 diagonal and prime
shell still present.  This is an open TPC-210 candidate, not an assumption in
TPC-209.
