\section{Audited conclusion and next gate}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\begin{theorem}[Audited determinant-fiber conclusion]
\label{thm:audited-conclusion}
For every finite native determinant packet, let
\[
 d_n=(2r_{\max,n}-R_n)_+.
\]
Then
\[
 D\ge\sum_nd_n^2,
\]
and this is the sharp lower endpoint over all phase assignments with
the same fiber moduli.  The corresponding free-phase coherence range
is
\[
 \left[
 \sum_nd_n^2-E_{\rm trip},\
 \sum_nR_n^2-E_{\rm trip}
 \right].
\]

For the canonical parent TPC-32 packet at one prescribed nonzero
\(h_0\), the composite key
\[
 (\nu(t),x_t,y_t)
\]
is injective.  Distinct equal-determinant parents share at most one
complete target in any slot.  Their complete shared-target
correlation has the exact compressed formula
\eqref{eq:compressed-Ctar}, and
\[
 |C_{\tar,X}|
 \le
 X^{-1+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2.
\]
Conditional on any future lower bound
\[
 D_X\ge
 X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2
 \quad\text{with fixed }\lambda_D<1,
\]
one has
\[
 |C_{\tar,X}|=o(D_X),
\]
and the remaining non-target-sharing coherence carries
\((1-o(1))D_X\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine
\cref{thm:sharp-energy-interval,cor:sharp-coherence-interval,thm:composite-uniqueness,lem:one-shared-target,thm:compressed-target-correlation,thm:target-loss-one,cor:target-negligible}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact progress.}
The paper adds one positive and one negative certificate.  The
positive certificate is the exact modulus-only determinant-energy
floor \(D_{\dom}\), with a sharp dominant-share exponent transfer.
The negative certificate is a loss-one theorem for every
same-determinant complete-target collision, including left--right
cross-sharing.  It converts the TPC-84 shared-target candidate from an
open possible main term into a rigorously lower-order class on every
future below-one energy branch.

\paragraph{Canonical duplicate rule.}
If an exact physical duplicate includes both ordered complete targets,
then after canonical parent aggregation its off-diagonal class is
empty.  Repeated computational children must not be counted as native
coherence.  Equal numerical coefficient values on different targets
are a different relation and receive no conclusion from the duplicate
statement.

\paragraph{What is not proved.}
No lower bound is proved for
\[
 R_X,\qquad M_X,\qquad D_{\dom,X},\qquad D_X.
\]
No upper bound is proved for the distinguished zero mode.  No
positive main term is obtained from a shared-divisor class with
distinct targets, a determinant-preserving symmetry, or the unpaired
remainder.  Hence the paper contains no new \(\Ltwo\) growing
fixed-shift arithmetic estimate.

\paragraph{Level ledger.}
\Cref{thm:sharp-energy-interval,cor:sharp-coherence-interval} are
\(\Lzero\) finite geometry.
\Cref{thm:composite-uniqueness,thm:compressed-target-correlation,thm:target-loss-one}
are \(\Lone\) results on the literal fixed-shift
carrier.  The conditional statement \(|C_{\tar}|=o(D)\) is asserted
only under the future lower bound with \(\lambda_D<1\).

\paragraph{Independent endpoint ledgers.}
Any future determinant lower and zero-mode upper bounds still require
\[
 \lambda_D\le2\eta_Z.
\]
All other physical fixed-power losses separately require
\[
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400.
\]
The same loss is never counted in both, and equality in the physical
ledger is a stop.

\paragraph{Arithmetic claim boundary.}
Nothing is specialized from a general prescribed \(h_0\ne0\) to
\(h_0=2\).  The paper proves no unweighted Chowla estimate, no breach
of the sieve parity barrier, no fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood
asymptotic, no prime-pair lower bound, and no twin-prime conclusion.

\paragraph{Next gate.}
Run the exact \(D_{\dom}\) and compressed \(C_{\tar}\) diagnostics on
the literal archive.  Analytically, the next positive theorem must
either:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item prove a raw-mass lower bound together with a macroscopic
  dominant-fiber share;
\item find a high-capacity determinant-preserving pairing that does
  not share complete targets and control its signed remainder; or
\item prove the determinant-energy lower bound directly by a
  different arithmetic mechanism.
\end{enumerate}
Shared-target coherence is no longer one of those doors.
