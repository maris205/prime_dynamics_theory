# Roadmap after RH-168

At a fixed scale, the Riesz rank/graph problem now has a complete finite
certificate schema:

```text
validated nominal inverse defects
+ contour covering radii
+ operator/frame balls
+ two directed residuals
-> exact continuous Schur certificate.
```

The remaining problem is not another fixed-scale inequality.  The spaces and
cloud ranks change with scale.  RH-169 should place fixed-rank Riesz packets
in common coordinates and derive a summable resolvent-transport criterion.
