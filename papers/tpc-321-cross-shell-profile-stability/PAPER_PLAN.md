# TPC-321 paper plan

## Research question

Does the trace-normalized ordered spectrum of the literal centered
prime-shell Gram (G_{X,Q,s}) remain stable as the prime shell changes from
(Q) to its next anchor, after the scale normalization studied in TPC-320?

## Minimal claim

On the declared finite panel, every adjacent-(Q) pair has ordered-profile
total variation (>0.03) and cumulative-profile distance (>0.02), with
outward numerical intervals.  The same panel contains forward majorization,
reverse majorization, and mixed cumulative-sign patterns.

## Evidence ladder

1. **PROVED:** for a PSD Gram with positive trace, the ordered vector
   (p_j=lambda_j/operatorname{tr}(G)) is invariant under (Gmapsto cG),
   (c>0), and all three profile diagnostics have the stated definitions.
2. **NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED:** 24 literal finite matrices, three spectral
   paths, 18 adjacent-shell comparisons, outward distance intervals, and the
   (3/2/13) majorization census.
3. **ADVERSARIAL:** reverse shell accumulation, an independent einsum replay,
   an independent eigensolver path, canonical certificate replay, and
   deterministic metric stress tests.
4. **OPEN:** uniform-in-(X,Q) profile control, signed prime-shell
   reassembly, any arithmetic saving, fixed-power credit, and a twin-prime
   conclusion.

## Planned paper sections

1. Scope and relation to TPC-320.
2. Literal operator and normalized rank profile.
3. Profile distances and majorization firewall.
4. Finite panel and numerical certificate.
5. Independent and adversarial checks.
6. What the obstruction does and does not imply.
