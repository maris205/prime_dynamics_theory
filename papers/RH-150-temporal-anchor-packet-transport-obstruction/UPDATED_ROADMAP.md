# Roadmap after RH-150

RH-150 changes the RH-149 plan before outward assembly can begin.  The ten
RH-142 balls cannot be inserted into RH-96 because their temporal and rank
types differ.  Correct time-zero clock-rank packets do certify, but the
separated cross-direction-Ritz radius algebra loses every finite chain by the
third update.

## Revised primary route

1. **RH-151: joint graph/Ritz output-packet enclosure.** Bound the next
   packet directly from the input packet and Gram ball, eliminating the
   intermediate demand to identify every weak selected left-singular
   direction.  Compare residual, graph-transform, and block Riccati forms.
2. **RH-152: branch-aware direct interval audit.** Apply the successful joint
   enclosure to all ten source-aligned clock-rank chains.  Archive the first
   genuine branch wall if one remains.
3. **RH-153: transported outward assembly.** Only after a complete packet
   chain exists, rebuild the RH-138 Gram/tail recurrence on the same interval
   objects.
4. **RH-154: finite source-to-support composition.** Re-enter RH-148 on the
   delayed clean suffix and measure the largest common support tube.

## Fallback redesigns

- Start a new recursive construction at the postblock horizon so that the
  RH-142 temporal anchor is native rather than transferred backward.
- Raise or adapt the packet rank before thresholding if the joint output gap
  still collapses.
- Replace hard selected-direction coordinates by a contour or graph
  projector that is invariant under rotations inside weak direction clusters.

## Claim boundary

The current result proves that the archived norm-ball interfaces do not
compose.  It does not prove failure of the exact recursive chain, close
`E-update`, establish Stage A, construct a Hilbert--Polya operator, identify
zeta zeros, or prove the Riemann Hypothesis.
