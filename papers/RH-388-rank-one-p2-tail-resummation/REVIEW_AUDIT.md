# RH-388 peer-review audit

## Review question

Does retaining the exact rank-one prime tail permit a uniform `P_2`-scale
factorial replacement of every higher rank, and do bounded consecutive
prime gaps show that smoothing rank one fails for the declared `J/I`
surrogates?

## Major-claim review

1. Rank split: strict Stieltjes transfer begins at `r=2`; `P_1` remains
   exact in every `Psi^[K]` coordinate.
2. Higher-rank source: boundary plus integral terms and the exact
   `R(z)` ledger yield `60 epsilon_x/(x^3L)`.
3. Higher-rank power kernel: the exact derivative and `t^-6` integral
   yield `13/(x^5L)`.
4. Factorial remainder: a finite geometric identity gives the exact
   sign, denominator `1+av`, and `K!` bound simultaneously for every
   `r>=2`.
5. Moving window: alternating positivity places `Psi^[K]` in the cube,
   and the universal `b_(K+1)/b_K` recurrence controls every integer
   `1<=K<=floor(3L)`.
6. Endpoint: the `l_infinity/l_1` estimate multiplies
   `60,13,28/3` by `126`, giving `7560,1638,1176` with `pi^2` retained.
7. Uniform scale: `P_2~1/(3x^3L)`, `epsilon_x->0`, and
   `b_K<=1/(3L)` give the maximum-over-window limit.
8. Bounded gaps: Maynard's integer-valued liminf supplies infinitely
   many consecutive gaps at most 600.  Exact succession gives scalar
   jump one and vector direction `(1,...,7)`.
9. Sharp endpoint transfer: the exact dot product is `2X_infinity`;
   the Hessian bound makes both Taylor remainders `o(x^-2)`, producing
   sharp limsup constants `1/2` and `X_infinity`.

## Novelty and scope review

The positive result is not a direct finite-partition consequence: it
separates rank one, resums every higher rank, and controls a growing
integer truncation window uniformly.  The negative result uses actual
bounded consecutive prime gaps and the nonlinear endpoint direction.
It applies only to the frozen `P/J/I` hierarchy.

The manuscript claims no convergent factorial series, enlarged `K`
window, universal surrogate impossibility, `P_3` or cubic precision,
complex channel, growing clock, active `c11`, `K_N`, operator, trace,
zero identification, or RH.  Gates A--E are false.
