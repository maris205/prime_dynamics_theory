\begin{abstract}
TPC-405 established an inverse-height bound for one locally normalized
adjacent entry of a selected-prime CRT proxy, but its certificate used only
small selected prefixes of the prime shell.  We close that finite selection
gap: for each of five kernel heights, we select every one of the 872 primes
in $8192<p\leq16384$, impose the explicit alternating CRT residues, and
reconstruct an origin above $10^6$.  The same exact argument gives
$0\leq z\leq t_1/(a_{\min}\sqrt{S_0S_1})\leq4/(a_{\min}H)\leq4/H$.
An exact rational certificate and an independent literal masked-energy
replay verify all five complete-shell rows.  The normalized observations
range from $0.0217719$ at $H=16$ to $0.0013396$ at $H=256$; these decimals
are finite observations, not an asymptotic theorem.  The result remains a
bound for one synthetic proxy entry, not a complete operator, arithmetic, or
twin-prime theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and complete-shell model}
Let $H,N,Q$ be integers with $H\geq1$, $N\geq H+2$, and $Q>N$.  Assume the
complete shell $(Q,2Q]$ has even cardinality $2m$, and let
$p_0<\cdots<p_{2m-1}$ be every prime in that shell.  Impose
\[
 o\equiv0\pmod {p_i}\ (i\ \mathrm{even}),\qquad
 o\equiv-N\pmod {p_i}\ (i\ \mathrm{odd}).
\]
For the certificate, $Q=8192$, the shell has 872 primes, and hence
$m=436$.  A CRT representative $o$ is chosen above $10^6$; since
$p_i>Q>N$, the residues determine the hits in the half-open window
$I_o=\{o,\ldots,o+N-1\}$.

\[
 t_d=\frac{H^2}{H^2+d^2},\quad
 S_0=\sum_{d=1}^{N-1}t_d^2,\quad
 S_1=\sum_{d=1}^{N-2}t_d^2+t_1^2,
\]
and $a_i=p_i^3/[Q^2(p_i-1)]$.  Write
\[
 P_- =\sum_{i\ \mathrm{odd}}a_i,\qquad
 V_- =\sum_{i\ \mathrm{odd}}a_i^2,\qquad
 V_+ =\sum_{i\ \mathrm{even}}a_i^2.
\]
The selected-prime local proxy inherited from TPC-404 has
\[
 G_0=V_-S_0,\qquad G_1=V_-S_1+V_+(S_1-t_1^2),\qquad M=t_1P_-.
\]
The word ``complete'' refers to the finite declared shell, not to the full
operator or to an arithmetic source.

\section{Complete-shell adjacent-entry theorem}
\textbf{Theorem.} Put $a_{\min}=\min_i a_i$ and
$z=M/\sqrt{G_0G_1}$.  Under the hypotheses above,
\[
 0\leq z\leq\frac{t_1}{a_{\min}\sqrt{S_0S_1}}
 \leq\frac{4}{a_{\min}H}\leq\frac4H. \tag{1}
\]

\textbf{Proof.}
All amplitudes and kernel terms are positive.  Also
$S_1-t_1^2=\sum_{d=1}^{N-2}t_d^2\geq0$, so $G_1\geq V_-S_1$.
Cauchy--Schwarz gives $P_-^2\leq mV_-$, while the $m$ odd-indexed terms
give $V_-\geq ma_{\min}^2$.  Consequently,
\[
 z^2\leq\frac{t_1^2mV_-}{(V_-S_0)(V_-S_1)}
 =\frac{t_1^2m}{V_-S_0S_1}
 \leq\frac{t_1^2}{a_{\min}^2S_0S_1}. \tag{2}
\]
For $1\leq d\leq H$, $t_d\geq1/2$.  The condition $N\geq H+2$
places these terms in both finite sums, so $S_0,S_1\geq H/4$.
Finally $a_i=(p_i/Q)^2p_i/(p_i-1)>1$ because $p_i>Q$.  Taking square
roots in (2) proves (1). \hfill$\square$

\section{Exact complete-shell certificate}
The certificate fixes $Q=8192$, uses the complete shell of 872 primes, and
sets $N=4H$ for $H\in\{16,32,66,128,256\}$.  Each row stores exact
rational kernel sums, amplitudes, CRT residue and period, local energies,
and both sides of (1).  The independent checker rebuilds the sieve and CRT
origin and then replays both masked row energies literally.  Thus the
complete-shell claim is not inferred from a small-prime prefix.

\section{Route boundary}
TPC-406 advances the finite local proxy question from a selected prefix to
the complete declared shell.  It still concerns one adjacent entry and one
explicit alternating CRT profile.  It does not bound all entries, the
complete normalized matrix or operator, arbitrary origins, unselected
source primes, the physical $h_0$, or the arithmetic sign law.  Accordingly
it pays no arithmetic $L^2$ estimate, no fixed-power $1/400$ credit, and no
Route-A, Route-B, or twin-prime conclusion.
