# Roadmap after RH-311

Two routes remain mathematically legitimate:

1. Prove actual endpoint `H2(rho_star)` convergence of the complement
   mismatch, at a rate compatible with the `1/sqrt(log(1/sigma))` lower
   barrier.  An interior annular theorem is also sufficient, but its power
   rate cannot exceed `kappa(rho)`.
2. Prove a joint moving-order boundary-layer trace law at the first natural
   alias, including parity and shell cancellation, with error
   `o(k R^(-2k))`.

Do not spend the next batch on more tail estimates, pre-alias fixed moments,
finite root fits, or separate absolute majorants.  Those leaves are already
audited and do not activate the weighted cross-branch glue.  Gates A--E
remain open.
