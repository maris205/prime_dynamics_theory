
The ratio (ho_e) compares a coherent, unnormalised signed operator with
the direct-sum Hilbert--Schmidt energy.  The actual projected fraction is
(phi_e=ho_e/m) and is always at most one.  Thus a ratio above one is not a
violation of projection contraction; it is a statement about the unnormalised
coherent sum.

No finite ratio is translated into a power of (X).  In particular, the
all-plus and alternating counts are not a claim about Möbius or von Mangoldt
signs.
