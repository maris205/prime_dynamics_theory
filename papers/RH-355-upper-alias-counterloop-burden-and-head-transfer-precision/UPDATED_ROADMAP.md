# Roadmap after RH-355

RH-355 quantifies the full strict upper-alias burden carried by the graded
counterloop.  After the RH-354 direct tail has become exponentially small at
the normalized near-alias scale, the corresponding counterloop head budget
is not small: its normalized `k`th-root is `x>1`.  Any same-clock actual-head
transport theorem must therefore match an exponentially large aggregate.

The original unnormalized RH-288/RH-340 leaf is much stronger than aggregate
normalized matching.  If `D_(4k)(R)->0`, it forces uniform relative precision
`o(kx^(-k))` throughout the even upper band and `o(kx^(-2k))` at the terminal
coordinate.  This paper derives those obligations but does not prove the
leaf.

The complete-shell counterexample closes one tempting shortcut: normalized
upper-defect convergence alone cannot be promoted to uniform coordinatewise
matching or unnormalized determinant gluing.  A future positive route must
therefore supply at least one of:

1. the original unnormalized same-clock `D_(4k)(R)->0` theorem for the actual
   modulus-complete head;
2. another actual theorem strong enough to control the bottom upper-band
   coordinates while preserving the RH-340 data type;
3. a direct full-trace theorem for `q` or `E_off` that bypasses head transport
   without conflating it with RH-354's `p=tau-a` result.

Root matching still needs an actual rank law, common cap, and sufficient
rate.  The finite shell obstruction is information-class only and does not
show actual nonmatching.  RH-241's moving noisy envelope and coefficient
bridge remain open, RH-288 remains inactive, Gates A--E remain false/open,
and no conclusion about the Riemann Hypothesis follows.
