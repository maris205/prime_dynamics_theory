# TPC-281 paper plan

## Question

What exactly follows if a literal arithmetic source operator supplies a typed
`L2` estimate, and which part of the Gate-B output can packet geometry alone
identify?

## Claim-driven contributions

1. Prove the typed interface from an operator bound
   `||A_X||_(2->2)<=K X^(-sigma)` and the TPC-280 two-term gain budget to an
   output-energy bound.
2. Prove the collapsed exponent form and the scalar-readout contraction,
   making the conditional nature of the arithmetic input explicit.
3. Give an exact rank-one orthogonal-functional obstruction: equal packet
   geometry and equal operator norm can produce full or zero attachment.
4. Certify four exact rational packet witnesses, four interface budgets, and
   the twelve-row TPC-280 finite transfer independently.

## Non-claims

This paper does not prove the literal arithmetic `L2` estimate, identify the
actual TPC source attachment, pay a fixed power, close full Gate B, or prove a
twin-prime theorem.  The finite transfer is inherited coordinate bookkeeping.
