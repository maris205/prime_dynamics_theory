# TPC-272 paper plan

## Research question

Can the signed residual scalar and its correlation margin be placed in one
endpoint budget, and does a locked finite phase sign provide a lower bound for
that margin?

## Frozen object

The paper consumes only the released TPC-271 certificate.  Its physical
operator, cutoff registry, profile interface, projection, and normalization
are unchanged.  The new coordinates are

```text
m = |C_perp|/R,     R^2 = W_perp G_perp,
m^6 = Xi_C/Xi,      amplification = Xi/Xi_C.
```

## Claim ceiling

1. Prove the conditional implication
   `|C_perp| <= A x^(E0-sigma_c+epsilon)` and
   `m >= b x^(-eta-epsilon)` => endpoint saving `sigma_c-eta`.
2. Prove the exact two-dimensional converse: a phase sign alone does not
   force any positive lower bound on `m`.
3. Numerically certify the rational `m^6` intervals and the `96->192`
   margin-collapse diagnostic inherited from TPC-271.

No source-level margin estimate, fixed-power credit, arithmetic `L2` bound,
full Gate B closure, or twin-prime conclusion is permitted.

## Planned deliverables

The result JSON is generated by the producer and replayed by an independent
checker.  A hostile stress script mutates phase, threshold, conditional
status, and firewall fields.  The paper records the exact proof and its
finite limitations; the bridge checker freezes the TPC-271 parent release.
