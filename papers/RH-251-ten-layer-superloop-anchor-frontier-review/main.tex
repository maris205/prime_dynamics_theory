\begin{abstract}
RH-242--RH-251 connect cloud-extracted traces to noisy periodic loops, define
the deterministic numerator anchor, and identify the exact frontier of the
current cloud class.  Fixed-noise superloop and orthogonal quotient identities
are exact, and a block-power theorem gives the correct conditional route to
an all-order envelope.  Conversely, the frozen candidate window fails the
anchor test even after convex relaxation, while unbounded weights produce
non-spectral multiplicity explosion.  No complete finite-head/analytic-tail
certificate exists.  We classify the ten layers, preserve the genuine local
progress, and keep Gates A--E open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact progress that survives}

\paragraph{Periodic superloops.}
RH-242 proves for every fixed noisy operator and selected algebraic multiset
that
\[
 \tau_n=\Tr A_\sigma^n-\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_\sigma}s^n
 =\operatorname{Str}((A_\sigma\oplus S_\sigma)^n),\qquad n\ge2,
\]
and realizes the physical term as a cyclic Gaussian loop integral
\citep{WangRH242}.  The identity is exact; the continuum selector is still an
assumption.

\paragraph{Independent coefficient anchor.}
RH-243 separates the all-order size problem from the numerator identity.  If
$P_n$ is the deterministic flat trace, then
\[
 a_n=r_H^{-n}\left[P_n-1-(-1)^n
 +2\mathbf{1}_{\{2\mid n\}}\lambda^{-n/2}\right],
\]
and the symmetric two-step coefficient is $b_k=a_{2k}$
\citep{WangRH243}.  This defines the target but does not put it in the cloud.

\paragraph{Cancellation-preserving quotient.}
RH-245 replaces the ill-conditioned Riesz estimate by an orthogonal quotient.
For the invariant generalized root space $E$, $Q=I-\Pi_E$, and
$C=QAQ|_{E^\perp}$,
\[
 \tau_n=\Tr C^n.
\]
The projection has norm one; $E^\perp$ need not reduce $A$
\citep{WangRH245}.  RH-246 then proves that one uniformly contractive power
$C^m$ gives the geometric trace envelope and an explicit logarithmic tail
\citep{WangRH246}.  These theorems survive even though their uniform
hypotheses are not yet certified.

\paragraph{Determinant interface.}
RH-250 proves that, on $|z|\le R$, the logarithmic determinant error is bounded
by the anchored finite-head error plus the quotient tail plus the target tail
\citep{WangRH250}.  This is the exact interface to a future RH-240 application;
the present three certificates are incomplete.

\section{Obstructions and their scope}

The zero-target and anchored problems cannot be conflated.  The anchor norm
through order 12 is $0.4945054357$, whereas all archived tolerances are at
most $0.04$; the two equal-radius balls are disjoint.  Exhausting the frozen
prefix class gives 0/32 passes \citep{WangRH244}.

RH-248 removes the radial-order excuse.  It places every complete shell in a
box $0\le w_j\le1$ and dualizes the anchored weighted $\ell^1$ distance.  The
box itself has distance $0.146498$--$0.424018$, at least 10.17 times the local
tolerance, so every prefix, interval, and binary subset fails
\citep{WangRH248}.  This is a frozen-window statement.

Allowing $w_j\ge0$ without an upper bound does not produce a credible rescue.
Six endpoints remain outside the cone; the other 26 need minimum maximum
weights between 40.584 and $5.8018\times10^{10}$ \citep{WangRH249}.  These are
arbitrary real repetitions, not algebraic spectral multiplicities.

The long-loop estimate also has a strict firewall.  Any majorant that takes
absolute values of physical and atomic sectors before cancellation contains
$|p_\sigma|^n=r_H^{-n}$, so its root rate is superunit.  All 352 archived
order cases confirm rates 1.2532--3.5568 \citep{WangRH247}.  A viable proof
must work after signed/complex grouping.

\section{Next-batch reopening triggers}

The same frozen shell window should not be reweighted again.  A new batch is
justified by at least one genuinely new input:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a larger resolved spectral window, followed immediately by an anchored
zonotope/cone audit rather than a zero-target fit;
\item a signed or complex selector derived from invariant quotient structure,
not arbitrary real shell weights;
\item a noise-uniform theorem for one quotient block power, including the
finest discretizations and a continuum bridge; or
\item an analytic all-order tail estimate for the deterministic numerator
target.
\end{enumerate}
Until then, the honest endpoint is a scoped route stop with exact
fixed-noise progress preserved.
