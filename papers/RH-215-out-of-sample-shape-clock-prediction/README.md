# RH-215: out-of-sample shape-clock prediction

Affine-log, logistic-log, and power-gap laws are fitted on seven fine scales
and scored on two held-out scales.  Power-gap wins both holdouts and both
physical channels, with two-point RMS errors about `0.0099--0.0100`; the other
models overpredict more strongly.  The win is finite and does not identify an
asymptotic law or prove `u -> 1`.
