# RH-391 peer-review audit

## Review question

Does smoothing a prime-tail rank remain detectably wrong when that same
rank moves through the full linear scale at both endpoints of repeated
bounded consecutive-prime gaps; and what is the exact two-scale pair
profile?

## Major-claim review

1. Maynard's consecutive-gap liminf supplies infinitely many integer gaps
   at most 600, and finite pigeonhole extraction fixes one repeated value
   `h_*`.
2. The theorem uses one exact rank at both endpoints, with `r->infinity`
   and `r<=C*x`; the optional lambda limit is not silently conjoined with
   the general theorem.
3. The strict successor contributes exactly `(q^2-1)^(-r)`.
4. The I and J smooth interval terms vanish uniformly under the declared
   linear bound.
5. The identities for `a`, `rho`, `rho/a`, the finite-slope limit, and the
   coarse exponent `1200*C` are exact.
6. The gamma formula follows from the frozen endpoint map.  Directed
   rational interval comparisons give the uniform lower
   `kappa_gamma*7^r/r` for exact `r>=7`.
7. Higher-rank P and I tails, the complete J/I bridge, and the common head
   are paid by explicit integer/integral chains with constants `4/2/4/14`.
8. The endpoint Taylor lift uses the seven-channel Hessian bound 224 and
   square coefficient 112; normalized cross and square errors vanish.
9. The scalar and endpoint edge jumps share the same factor `a+o(1)` only
   after their declared normalizations.
10. The pair inequality retains `rho` and therefore yields the exact
    `a_0/(1+a_0)` profile, not an unsupported single-endpoint constant.
11. The coarse and sublinear pair constants are `exp(-1200*C)/2` and
    `1/2`.
12. The ratio to `P_(r+1)` diverges by a one-sided integer upper bound; no
    linear-rank prime-tail asymptotic is used.

## Novelty and scope review

RH-388 established the rank-one prototype, while RH-390 proved
fixed-rank necessity at all thresholds.  RH-391's new edge is the
same-rank pair obstruction uniformly through `r=O(x)`, with the exact
finite-slope profile and an elementary next-rank separation that does not
invoke `P_r~K_r`.

The paper claims no arbitrary single-vertex rank schedule, different
endpoint ranks, arbitrary-surrogate obstruction, `r/x->infinity`, complex
channel, active `c11`, ordinary Cesaro or growing clock, `K_N`, RH-389,
TPC-137, Tao active-log dependence, operator, trace, zero identification,
or RH.  Gates A--E remain false.

## Decision

Accept.  Independent theorem review and independent source/citation/PDF
review each report zero blockers and zero minors.  The executable archive
decision is recorded in `REPLAY_AUDIT.md`.
