\section{Exact result}
\begin{theorem}
If P=max\_\allowbreak{}k |sum\_\allowbreak{}{j<=k} a\_\allowbreak{}j| and B=max\_\allowbreak{}{r<s}|sum\_\allowbreak{}{r<j<=s}a\_\allowbreak{}j|, then P<=B<=2P.  The factor two is sharp for general complex sequences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write S\_\allowbreak{}k=sum\_\allowbreak{}{j<=k}a\_\allowbreak{}j and S\_\allowbreak{}0=0.  Every prefix is the block S\_\allowbreak{}k-S\_\allowbreak{}0, hence P<=B.  Every block is S\_\allowbreak{}s-S\_\allowbreak{}r, so |S\_\allowbreak{}s-S\_\allowbreak{}r|<=|S\_\allowbreak{}s|+|S\_\allowbreak{}r|<=2P.  Prefix values 1 and -1 show that the factor two is sharp for unrestricted complex sequences.
\end{proof}
