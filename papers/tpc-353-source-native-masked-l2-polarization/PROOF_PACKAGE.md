## Proposition 1: finite operator polarization

Let `A` be any real matrix with finite dimensions and let `beta=L-b`.  Then

```text
||A beta||_2^2 = ||A L||_2^2 + ||A b||_2^2 - 2 <A L,A b>.
```

**Proof.**  Since `A beta=A L-A b`, expand the finite Euclidean square and use
symmetry of the real inner product.  No limiting operation is involved. `∎`

## Proposition 2: normalized Cauchy envelope

If `E_L=||A L||_2^2`, `E_b=||A b||_2^2`, and `E_L+E_b>0`, then

```text
(sqrt(E_L)-sqrt(E_b))^2/(E_L+E_b)
 <= ||A beta||_2^2/(E_L+E_b)
 <= (sqrt(E_L)+sqrt(E_b))^2/(E_L+E_b).
```

**Proof.** Apply Cauchy--Schwarz to `<A L,A b>` in Proposition 1 and divide
by the positive component sum. `∎`
