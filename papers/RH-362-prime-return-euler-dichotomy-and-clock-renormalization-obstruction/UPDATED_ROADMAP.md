# Roadmap after RH-362

RH-362 opens a post-four-volume arithmetic-dynamical zeta branch without
altering the physical frontier inherited from RH-361.

The durable foundation remains:

```text
RH-1--RH-160    foundational symbolic/determinant architecture
RH-161--RH-241  physical Riesz and moving trace-envelope frontier
RH-242--RH-281  deterministic numerator/anchor/counterloop closure
RH-282--RH-361  noisy heads, aliases, and signed-completion separation
```

The original first physical blocker is still

```text
D_(4k)(R)
  = sum_(2<=n<4k) |h_(sigma,n)-s_(k,n)| R^n/n
  -> 0.
```

RH-362 instead uses trigger 5.  It constructs an exact all-prime object from
one marked modular cycle per prime and proves its natural analytic domain.
The result narrows the zeta route in two directions:

1. For nonperiodic `P`, natural cycle weight `p^(-s)` retains the modular
   return length and gives a zero-free product on `Re(s)>0`, with weights
   `r_p log p` rather than von Mangoldt weights.
2. Dividing the local clock by `r_p` forces the Riemann Euler factor, but the
   output becomes independent of the return lengths and the global block
   operator loses every finite Schatten class.

Thus the shortest genuine continuations are now:

1. **Arithmetic distribution route.** Prove an average-over-primes theorem,
   Chebotarev-type finite-period law, primitive-divisor theorem, or height
   estimate that constrains the ranks `r_p(P)` beyond finite intervals.
2. **Canonical-global-dynamics route.** Supply a source-backed compact or
   trace-ideal global object whose local factors arise intrinsically rather
   than from a marked-cycle bouquet.  Dependence on `P`, `p`, and `s` must be
   typed explicitly.
3. **Weighted Hénon survivor route.** Close the currently conditional relation
   between the explicit local hyperbolic survivor's weighted periodic-orbit
   determinant and a specified continuous transfer operator.
4. **Original physical route.** Return to same-clock unnormalised head
   transport, typed `q/E_off`, the complete direct prefix, or the RH-241
   moving noisy envelope/coefficient bridge.

Do not continue by changing every prime's time unit so that the desired Euler
factor appears by definition.  Do not call the pointed bouquet a Hasse--Weil
zeta, the full `H_p` zeta, or the physical RH determinant.  Gates A--E remain
false/open, and no Hilbert--Polya, Riemann-zero, completed-divisor, or RH
conclusion follows.
