\section{Claim boundary and next gate}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\subsection{Exact results}

The following are unconditional finite \(\Lzero\) statements:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item the anchored Abel identity
  \eqref{eq:anchored-defect-identity};
\item the decomposition
  \(\cT_{r,N}=\cT_{0,N}+(r/N)L_N\);
\item the regime criteria and the prefix-uniform short-fiber theorem;
\item the sharp counterexamples in \cref{sec:counterexamples}; and
\item the aggregate identity
  \eqref{eq:aggregate-signed}, conditional only on being supplied the
  exact finite reassembly \eqref{eq:literal-reassembly}.
\end{enumerate}

Verifying that the \(\theta\)-family in
\eqref{eq:literal-reassembly} is the complete raw hard packet of the
TPC-93 low-frequency export, with no missing native sector, is an
\(\Lone\) provenance certificate.  It does not become \(\Ltwo\)
merely because the identity is attached.

\subsection{Unproved arithmetic}

This paper does not prove:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item a quantitative anchored-defect estimate for the actual growing
  affine M\"obius fibers;
\item uniformity of a fixed-form logarithmic theorem in the growing
  slopes, intercepts, origins, masks, or prefixes;
\item selection of the prescribed \(h_0\) from an averaged-shift
  theorem;
\item control of the complete multiplier-weighted low-frequency
  window;
\item a lower bound for \(M_X\) or \(D_X\);
\item TPC-18 full-block reassembly or primitive determinant
  dispersion;
\item a fixed-shift Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, parity
  breakthrough, prime-pair lower bound, or the twin-prime
  conjecture.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Audited conclusion}

\begin{theorem}[Anchored transfer conclusion]
\label{thm:conclusion}
For every translated physical fiber, ordinary cancellation is
equivalent to cancellation of the anchored defect
\[
 \cT_{r,N}(L)
 =
 \left(1+\frac rN\right)L_N
 -\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m.
\]
For a complete native packet the exact target is the signed aggregate
\eqref{eq:aggregate-signed}; the absolute expression
\eqref{eq:aggregate-target} is a sufficient quantitative
certificate.  Neither an anchor-free defect, a terminal logarithmic
bound without prefix control, nor per-fiber \(o(1)\) without weighted
outer mass is a valid substitute.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The fiber statement is \cref{thm:anchored-identity}.  Summing it with
the literal coefficients gives \cref{thm:aggregate-identity}.  The
three forbidden substitutions are ruled out respectively by
\cref{prop:no-anchor-free,rem:prefix-quantifier,ex:outer-mass}.
\end{proof}
