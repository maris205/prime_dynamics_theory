\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Logarithmic correlation theorems naturally weight an arithmetic
variable \(m\) by \(1/m\).  A physical affine fiber, however, is often
written after translation as
\[
 m=r+n,\qquad 1\le n\le N.
\]
The inherited weight is \(1/(r+n)\), not \(1/n\).  Replacing it by
\(1/n\) changes both the primitive and its Tauberian inverse.

TPC-87 isolated the exact signed Ces\`aro defect for the unshifted
primitive and warned that a translated reciprocal weight must be
transferred rather than relabelled \citep{WangTPC87}.  The present
paper computes that transfer exactly.  The new term is elementary,
but its scale is decisive when the physical origin is comparable to
or larger than the fiber length.

The distinction is also required when comparing the literal packet
with logarithmically averaged Chowla and Elliott theorems.  Tao's
two-point theorem has fixed affine coefficients and a logarithmic
window \citep{Tao2016LogChowla}.  Averaged-shift and almost-all-scale
results have different quantifiers
\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015,TaoTeravainen2019}.  None may be
attached to a polynomially growing, prescribed fixed-\(h_0\) family
without checking coefficients, origins, prefixes, masks, and the
outer sum.

\subsection{Main contributions}

We prove:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item the exact anchored Abel identity and its necessary-and-sufficient
  signed defect;
\item the exact necessary-and-sufficient terminal condition and its
  consequences in the regimes \(r=o(N)\), \(r\asymp N\), and
  \(r\gg N\);
\item exact counterexamples showing that unnormalized logarithmic
  smallness and per-fiber qualitative cancellation do not survive
  translation or growing outer mass;
\item a prefix-uniform positive criterion for genuinely short
  translated fibers;
\item the signed and absolute aggregate certificates for the complete
  literal affine packet; and
\item a separate determinant and physical-loss ledger that prevents
  a Tauberian normalization change from being counted as arithmetic
  cancellation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What changes in the TPC route}

The correct fiber-level datum is
\[
 \cT_{r,N}(L)
 =
 \left(1+\frac rN\right)L_N
 -\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m,
\]
not the unshifted defect alone.  This refines the Tauberian branch of
the TPC-92 roadmap \citep{WangTPC92}.  It does not estimate the datum
for the actual M\"obius coefficient.  That remains an arithmetic
problem after the literal low-frequency export of TPC-93
\citep{WangTPC93}.
