\begin{abstract}
We integrate the frozen TPC-153--160 structural and arithmetic outputs
against the TPC-151/152 route anchors.  On the structural side, the
canonical cut-occurrence shadow is an L1 result, but the
current-schema-only recovery route has only a scoped stop certificate.
The selected augmented route is not testable because a theorem-backed
production occurrence-provenance crosswalk is absent.  We encode H1 as
an explicit map/scalar disjunction.  The map clause contains nine
separate defects and an independent occurrence registry; the scalar
clause contains both a complete frontier estimate and a growing-scale
eligible-tail disposition.

On the arithmetic side, the strongest imported advance is the TPC-159
actual periodic-core prefix theorem outside a sparse dyadic shadow,
classified as L1 actual-prefix almost-endpoint.  TPC-157, TPC-158, and
TPC-160 give positive weight, phase, and Abel-return interfaces, but
the production weight, phase, normalization, and atomic endpoint
registries remain unavailable.  A typed proof DAG records evidence,
scope, carrier, normalization, and artifact readiness, computes
clause-wise minimal missing antichains and parent-ready open
frontiers, and enforces a transitive arithmetic firewall at H9.  A
third-generation endpoint ledger gives each of six listed charges a
unique owner; it does not declare the unknown physical registry
complete.
The resulting verdict is \(\mathsf{NOT\_TESTABLE}\): there is no
positive fixed-\(X\)-power L2 result, \(1/400\) endpoint, prime-pair
lower bound, or twin-prime theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Integration statement}

The earlier integration snapshot located the first unsupported object
at a frontier occurrence lift \cite{WangTPC151,WangTPC152}.  The next
structural papers sharpened that pointer: the archive determines a
canonical shadow, but not theorem-backed downstream occurrence
provenance \cite{WangTPC153,WangTPC154,WangTPC155,WangTPC156}.  In
parallel, the determinant-two M\"obius corridor acquired weight,
phase, prefix, and Abel interfaces
\cite{WangTPC157,WangTPC158,WangTPC159,WangTPC160}.  The purpose of the
present paper is to assemble these two lines without allowing an
interface theorem to fill a missing physical artifact.

\begin{theorem}[Source-locked occurrence-to-return integration]
\label{thm:integration}
For the frozen TPC-151--160 source snapshot, the following statements
hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item The current-schema-only canonical-lift route is stopped in its
  declared formal-schema cell, while the theorem-augmented map route
  is not stopped.
  \item On the selected map route the unique minimal
  \(\NT\)-ancestor is
  \[
    \code{H1.theorem\_backed\_occurrence\_provenance\_crosswalk}.
  \]
  On the scalar route the minimal antichain consists of the complete
  FUM \(o(X)\) theorem and the theorem-backed ETO disposition.
  \item The H9 physical-registry node has no direct or transitive
  arithmetic ancestor.
  \item The strongest arithmetic import is the TPC-159 theorem,
  classified as L1 actual-prefix almost-endpoint.  The production
  weight, phase, endpoint, and normalization registries are all
  \(\NT\).
  \item The arithmetic fixed-\(X\) exponent is zero and the physical
  loss is unknown.  Consequently the \(1/400\) endpoint is unpaid.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the selected synthesis verdict is
\(\mathsf{NOT\_TESTABLE}\), not \(\mathsf{GO}\),
\(\mathsf{STOP}\), or architecture infeasibility.
\end{theorem}

The theorem is an integration statement: its mathematical inputs are
the cited source results, and its machine component verifies their
exact declared fields and the consistency of the composed contract.
A source hash is an integrity lock, never theorem evidence.

\section{Frozen structural inputs}

\subsection{The shadow and the scoped obstruction}

Let \(\mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{ns}}\) denote the archived nonsoft cut
paths.  It is the declared disjoint union
\[
  \mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{ns}}
  =\mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{ETO}}\sqcup
   \mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{FUM}},
\]
where ETO denotes \emph{eligible-tail open} and FUM denotes
\emph{frontier unmapped}.  TPC-153 constructs one sourced unit shadow
row for each archived nonsoft path.  The resulting identity injection
is row-separated and conservative, but it contains no actual
occurrence identifier, branch count, canonical parent, later stage,
physical group, or active-support certificate \cite{WangTPC153}.

The finite production census is
\[
  \#\mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{ETO}}=0,\qquad
  \#\mathcal C_X^{\mathrm{FUM}}=2988.
\]
The first equality is a finite archive fact, not an asymptotic ETO
disposition theorem.

TPC-154 exhibits two distinct \emph{formal} conservative completions
of every shadow row---one child of weight \(1\), or two children of
weight \(1/2\)---that forget to the same shadow
\cite{WangTPC154}.  This proves nonuniqueness in the maximal current
schema class.  It does not assert that both completions occur on the
actual arithmetic carrier.  Hence
\[
 \code{current-schema-only lift}=\STOP_{\rm scoped},
 \qquad
 \code{augmented actual lift}=\NT.
\]

TPC-155 supplies a sound finite verifier for a proposed occurrence
bundle, including quotient, grouping, selector, cover, reconnection,
and registry exports \cite{WangTPC155}.  Its positive fixture is
synthetic L0 evidence.  No production bundle has been supplied.

\subsection{The exact H1 disjunction}

Write \(C_{\mathrm{prov}}\) for the theorem-backed cut-to-occurrence
provenance crosswalk and let
\[
 \boldsymbol D=
 (D_L,D_{QD},D_{QZ},D_G,D_P,D_{DZ},D_{GP},D_{\rm cover},D_{\rm rec}).
\]
The occurrence registry \(R_{\rm occ}\) is deliberately not renamed
\(D_{\rm occ}\) or absorbed into \(\boldsymbol D\).  It is a separate
positive totality object.

\begin{definition}[Typed H1 contract]
\label{def:h1}
The two sufficient clauses are
\begin{align}
 \cM_{\mathrm{H1}}
  &=
  C_{\mathrm{prov}}
  \ \wedge\
  \bigwedge_{\Delta\in\boldsymbol D}(\Delta=0)
  \ \wedge\ R_{\rm occ},                                      \label{eq:map}\\
 \cS_{\mathrm{H1}}
  &=
  \bigl[S_{\mathrm{FUM}}(X)=o(X)
     \text{ on the complete original carrier}\bigr]
  \ \wedge\ E_{\mathrm{ETO}},                                 \label{eq:scalar}\\
 \mathrm{H1}
  &=\cM_{\mathrm{H1}}\ \vee\ \cS_{\mathrm{H1}}.                \label{eq:h1}
\end{align}
Here \(E_{\mathrm{ETO}}\) is a growing-scale theorem-backed
totalization or complete softness statement for every ETO path.
\end{definition}

This formulation prevents three compressions: a shadow is not an
actual lift; nine map defects are not a single unnamed ``four-map
check''; and a frontier scalar estimate does not dispose of ETO.

\begin{proposition}[Clause-wise minimal missing antichains]
\label{prop:antichain}
In the frozen snapshot,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \min\NT(\cM_{\mathrm{H1}})
  &=\{C_{\mathrm{prov}}\},\\
 \min\NT(\cS_{\mathrm{H1}})
  &=\{S_{\mathrm{FUM}}(X)=o(X),\,E_{\mathrm{ETO}}\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
All nine defect evaluations and the occurrence registry are blocked
downstream of \(C_{\mathrm{prov}}\); they are not promoted to zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The crosswalk is the first unavailable artifact on every selected
map branch.  The lift, quotient, grouping, selector, cover,
reconnection, and registry nodes therefore have readiness
\(\mathsf{BLOCKED\_BY\_PARENT}\).  Removing descendants of an earlier
unavailable ancestor leaves the singleton map antichain.  The two
scalar conjuncts are independent missing leaves, so both remain.
\end{proof}

\section{Typed DAG and readiness semantics}

Each proof node is stored as the tuple
\[
\begin{split}
(&\mathrm{id},\mathrm{type},\mathrm{status},\mathrm{role},
  \mathrm{level},\mathrm{parents},\mathrm{clauses},\\[-2mm]
 &\mathrm{evidence},\mathrm{scope},\mathrm{carrier},
  \mathrm{normalization},\mathrm{artifact},\mathrm{readiness}).
\end{split}
\]
Allowed dependency types are evidence, target, all, any-clause, and
scoped-stop.  Allowed readiness states distinguish
\(\mathsf{READY}\), \(\mathsf{MISSING}\),
\(\mathsf{BLOCKED\_BY\_PARENT}\), and
\(\mathsf{NOT\_APPLICABLE}\).  Thus the statement ``not proved'' is
not silently identified with ``testable on the current data.''

The manifest contains 53 typed nodes and 12 exact imports.  Every
import and node references a registered scope, carrier,
normalization, and artifact.  For an all-node the status is derived
from every parent; for an any-clause node it is derived clause by
clause.  The graph is checked for cycles before any verdict is
computed.

\begin{definition}[Parent-ready open frontier]
An open target is parent-ready if its required artifact is ready and
all of its declared parents are proved.
\end{definition}

Exactly two targets satisfy this definition:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item a direct additive-twist theorem on the existing actual
  determinant-two core; and
  \item a pointwise theorem at endpoints inside the dyadic bad set.
\end{enumerate}
They are genuine arithmetic research frontiers, but neither supplies
a production occurrence crosswalk or physical registry.

\subsection{The H9 transitive firewall}

Let \(\operatorname{Anc}(v)\) be all direct and transitive ancestors
of a DAG node.  The integration enforces
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Anc}(\mathrm{H9})
 \cap
 \{\text{arithmetic-core, arithmetic-target,
 arithmetic-negative roles}\}
 =\varnothing.                                                 \label{eq:firewall}
\end{equation}
H9 may depend on structural occurrence, cover, and reconnection
records and on literal physical registries.  It may not inherit
TPC-157--160 as evidence for those registries.  The frozen H9
ancestor set passes \eqref{eq:firewall}.  This is stronger than
checking only the immediate parents.

\section{The actual-core arithmetic corridor}

We now record exactly what the arithmetic line contributes.  Let
\[
 q=as,\qquad t(z)=ad+qz,\qquad
 c(z)=\Mob(d+sz)\Mob(u+az),
\]
with \(su-ad=2\) and the small-polylogarithmic modulus conditions from
TPC-149.  The quantitative affine-correlation input ultimately comes
from Tao--Ter\"av\"ainen \cite{TaoTeravainen2026}; the actual periodic
M\"obius corridor was isolated in TPC-149 \cite{WangTPC149}.
Throughout this section,
\[
  \cL:=\log X,\qquad e(x):=\exp(2\pi i x).
\]

\subsection{Weight and phase interfaces}

For \(I_N=\{z:N<t(z)\le 2N\}\), TPC-157 proves outside the same
exceptional set that
\begin{equation}
 \frac qN\left|\sum_{z\in I_N}c(z)w(z)\right|
 \ll
 \inf_{\substack{\rho\ {\rm period}\ R\\qR\le \cL^{\eta_0}}}
 \left[
   \|\rho\|_\infty\cL^{-\kappa_0}
   +\frac qN\sum_{z\in I_N}|w(z)-\rho(z)|
 \right].                                                     \label{eq:weight}
\end{equation}
This is a positive L1 actual-core weight \emph{interface}.  A literal
physical theorem additionally needs a source-locked production
\(w\) and a decaying approximation cost.

TPC-158 applies \eqref{eq:weight} on a major-arc cell.  If
\[
 qR\le\cL^{\eta_0},
 \qquad
 L\left|\alpha-\frac{k}{R}\right|\le\cL^{-A},
\]
then
\begin{equation}
 \frac qN\left|\sum_{z\in I_N}c(z)e(-\alpha z)\right|
 \ll \cL^{-\kappa_0}+\cL^{-A}.                                \label{eq:phase}
\end{equation}
For uniformly separated minor-arc cells, TPC-158 proves only that the
small-period approximation route fails.  It neither proves that the
M\"obius correlation is large nor stops a direct additive-twist
theorem.

\subsection{Almost-endpoint prefixes}

Let \(\cE_X^\star\) be the source exceptional-scale set and put
\[
 J=\left\lceil A\log_2\log X\right\rceil,
 \qquad
 \cS_{X,J}=\bigcup_{1\le j\le J}2^j\cE_X^\star.
\]
TPC-159 performs exact dyadic telescoping.  For
\[
 2^J\sqrt X\le T\le X,\qquad T\notin\cS_{X,J},
\]
it obtains
\begin{equation}
 \frac qT\left|
   \sum_{\substack{z\\0<t(z)\le T}}c(z)\rho(z)
 \right|
 \ll
 \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left(\cL^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+\frac qT\right).                 \label{eq:prefix}
\end{equation}
There is no factor \(J\) in the correlation term because the dyadic
block masses form a geometric sum.  Meanwhile
\[
 \frac1{\log X}\int_{\cS_{X,J}}\frac{dT}{T}
 \ll J\cL^{-\kappa_0}
 =\cL^{-\kappa_0+o(1)}.
\]
This moves the actual periodic core from source-native dyadic blocks
to cumulative prefixes outside a sparse shadow.  It is therefore the
strongest arithmetic export in the present integration.

It is not an all-endpoint statement.  A predetermined atomic endpoint
can lie in a continuously sparse set.

\subsection{Abel return and its exact boundary}

For ordered points \(t_1<\cdots<t_m\le T\), put
\[
 \sigma_i=c(z_i)\rho(z_i),\qquad
 A_i=\sum_{j\le i}\sigma_j,\qquad
 d_i=
 \begin{cases}
   w_i-w_{i+1},&i<m,\\
   w_m,&i=m.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then the exact identity
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_iw_i=\sum_{i=1}^mA_id_i
\]
and \eqref{eq:prefix} yield the TPC-160 interface
\begin{equation}
 \frac qT\left|\sum_i\sigma_iw_i\right|
 \ll \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left[
   \eps_X V_{\rm good}(w)
   +\left(1+\frac qT\right)V_{\rm bad}(w)
 \right],                                                     \label{eq:abel}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 \eps_X=\cL^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+\frac qT.
\]
For a prefix cutoff the Abel derivative is one atom at the selected
endpoint.  Continuous exceptional density cannot certify that this
atom avoids the bad set.  Hence TPC-160 is a positive weighted
almost-endpoint interface, but not a production physical return.

\section{Route decision and next exact objects}

The route table now has five cells.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item \emph{Current-schema-only lift:} scoped stop.
  \item \emph{Occurrence-augmented map:} selected,
  \(\NT\), first missing \(C_{\mathrm{prov}}\).
  \item \emph{Scalar plus ETO:} open but \(\NT\), with two independent
  missing leaves.
  \item \emph{Direct additive twist on the actual core:} parent-ready
  \(\OPEN\).
  \item \emph{Pointwise bad-endpoint theorem on the actual core:}
  parent-ready \(\OPEN\).
\end{enumerate}

The first two arithmetic open targets can improve the core even if
the structural crosswalk remains unavailable.  Conversely, a future
crosswalk can complete H1 without solving the additive-twist problem.
This separation is intentional: it allows progress on either line
without relabeling one as progress on the other.

For the selected full synthesis, the minimal unavailable artifact set
is
\[
\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
C_{\mathrm{prov}},\
\text{literal weight registry},\
\text{phase-cell registry},\\
\text{atomic endpoint registry},\
\text{physical normalization registry}
\end{array}
\right\}.
\]
The two parent-ready arithmetic targets are recorded separately as
open dependencies, not as missing production artifacts.

The standard-library audit uses
\(\code{CANONICAL\_UTF8\_LF\_V2}\) to lock 24 scripts and outputs.
It validates:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2.1em]
  \item exact frozen source statuses and claim boundaries;
  \item strict JSON schemas with no undeclared object fields;
  \item the acyclic 53-node ALL/ANY-clause DAG;
  \item the nine defects and independent occurrence registry;
  \item clause-wise minimal \(\NT\) antichains;
  \item the two parent-ready \(\OPEN\) frontiers;
  \item the transitive H9 arithmetic firewall; and
  \item unique ownership of the six listed charges in Endpoint Ledger V3.
\end{itemize}
Mutation regressions reject shadow-to-lift promotion, defect omission,
registry compression, an arithmetic H9 ancestor, duplicate or omitted
members of the six-charge contract, log-to-\(X\)-power conversion, and
unsupported L2 or \(1/400\) claims.

The status \(\mathsf{PASS}\) means that this integration certificate
is internally consistent with its source-locked inputs.  It does not
mean that a production occurrence lift exists, that a generic phase
is controlled, that every endpoint is good, or that the full physical
H3 packet has a saving.  In particular, this paper proves no positive
fixed-\(X\)-power L2 estimate, no \(B_{h_0,\delta}(X)=o(X)\) theorem,
no prime-pair lower bound, and no twin-prime theorem.
