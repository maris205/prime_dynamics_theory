## Abstract

We integrate the frozen TPC-153–160 structural and arithmetic outputs against the TPC-151/152 route anchors. On the structural side, the canonical cut-occurrence shadow is an L1 result, but the current-schema-only recovery route has only a scoped stop certificate. The selected augmented route is not testable because a theorem-backed production occurrence-provenance crosswalk is absent. We encode H1 as an explicit map/scalar disjunction. The map clause contains nine separate defects and an independent occurrence registry; the scalar clause contains both a complete frontier estimate and a growing-scale eligible-tail disposition.

On the arithmetic side, the strongest imported advance is the TPC-159 actual periodic-core prefix theorem outside a sparse dyadic shadow, classified as L1 actual-prefix almost-endpoint. TPC-157, TPC-158, and TPC-160 give positive weight, phase, and Abel-return interfaces, but the production weight, phase, normalization, and atomic endpoint registries remain unavailable. A typed proof DAG records evidence, scope, carrier, normalization, and artifact readiness, computes clause-wise minimal missing antichains and parent-ready open frontiers, and enforces a transitive arithmetic firewall at H9. A third-generation endpoint ledger gives each of six listed charges a unique owner; it does not declare the unknown physical registry complete. The resulting verdict is $\mathsf{NOT\_TESTABLE}$: there is no positive fixed-$X$-power L2 result, $1/400$ endpoint, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem.

## The exact H1 disjunction

Write $C_{\mathrm{prov}}$ for the theorem-backed cut-to-occurrence provenance crosswalk and let $$\boldsymbol D=
 (D_L,D_{QD},D_{QZ},D_G,D_P,D_{DZ},D_{GP},D_{\rm cover},D_{\rm rec}).$$ The occurrence registry $R_{\rm occ}$ is deliberately not renamed $D_{\rm occ}$ or absorbed into $\boldsymbol D$. It is a separate positive totality object.

> **Definition: Typed H1 contract** <span id="def:h1" label="def:h1">\[def:h1\]</span> The two sufficient clauses are $$\begin{aligned}
>  \mathcal M_{\mathrm{H1}}
>   &=
>   C_{\mathrm{prov}}
>   \ \wedge\
>   \bigwedge_{\Delta\in\boldsymbol D}(\Delta=0)
>   \ \wedge\ R_{\rm occ},                                      \label{eq:map}\\
>  \mathcal S_{\mathrm{H1}}
>   &=
>   \bigl[S_{\mathrm{FUM}}(X)=o(X)
>      \text{ on the complete original carrier}\bigr]
>   \ \wedge\ E_{\mathrm{ETO}},                                 \label{eq:scalar}\\
>  \mathrm{H1}
>   &=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{H1}}\ \vee\ \mathcal S_{\mathrm{H1}}.                \label{eq:h1}\end{aligned}$$ Here $E_{\mathrm{ETO}}$ is a growing-scale theorem-backed totalization or complete softness statement for every ETO path.

This formulation prevents three compressions: a shadow is not an actual lift; nine map defects are not a single unnamed “four-map check”; and a frontier scalar estimate does not dispose of ETO.

> **Proposition: Clause-wise minimal missing antichains** <span id="prop:antichain" label="prop:antichain">\[prop:antichain\]</span> In the frozen snapshot, $$\begin{aligned}
>  \min\mathsf{NT}(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{H1}})
>   &=\{C_{\mathrm{prov}}\},\\
>  \min\mathsf{NT}(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{H1}})
>   &=\{S_{\mathrm{FUM}}(X)=o(X),\,E_{\mathrm{ETO}}\}.
> \end{aligned}$$ All nine defect evaluations and the occurrence registry are blocked downstream of $C_{\mathrm{prov}}$; they are not promoted to zero.

> **Proof** The crosswalk is the first unavailable artifact on every selected map branch. The lift, quotient, grouping, selector, cover, reconnection, and registry nodes therefore have readiness $\mathsf{BLOCKED\_BY\_PARENT}$. Removing descendants of an earlier unavailable ancestor leaves the singleton map antichain. The two scalar conjuncts are independent missing leaves, so both remain.

# The actual-core arithmetic corridor

We now record exactly what the arithmetic line contributes. Let $$q=as,\qquad t(z)=ad+qz,\qquad
 c(z)=\mu(d+sz)\mu(u+az),$$ with $su-ad=2$ and the small-polylogarithmic modulus conditions from TPC-149. The quantitative affine-correlation input ultimately comes from Tao–Teräväinen `\cite{TaoTeravainen2026}`; the actual periodic Möbius corridor was isolated in TPC-149 `\cite{WangTPC149}`. ## Weight and phase interfaces

For $I_N=\{z:N<t(z)\le 2N\}$, TPC-157 proves outside the same exceptional set that $$\frac qN\left|\sum_{z\in I_N}c(z)w(z)\right|
 \ll
 \inf_{\substack{\rho\ {\rm period}\ R\\qR\le \mathcal L^{\eta_0}}}
 \left[
   \|\rho\|_\infty\mathcal L^{-\kappa_0}
   +\frac qN\sum_{z\in I_N}|w(z)-\rho(z)|
 \right].                                                     \label{eq:weight}$$ This is a positive L1 actual-core weight *interface*. A literal physical theorem additionally needs a source-locked production $w$ and a decaying approximation cost.

TPC-158 applies [\[eq:weight\]](../main.tex#L317){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:weight"} on a major-arc cell. If $$qR\le\mathcal L^{\eta_0},
 \qquad
 L\left|\alpha-\frac{k}{R}\right|\le\mathcal L^{-A},$$ then $$\frac qN\left|\sum_{z\in I_N}c(z)e(-\alpha z)\right|
 \ll \mathcal L^{-\kappa_0}+\mathcal L^{-A}.                                \label{eq:phase}$$ For uniformly separated minor-arc cells, TPC-158 proves only that the small-period approximation route fails. It neither proves that the Möbius correlation is large nor stops a direct additive-twist theorem.

## Almost-endpoint prefixes

Let $\mathcal E_X^\star$ be the source exceptional-scale set and put $$J=\left\lceil A\log_2\log X\right\rceil,
 \qquad
 \mathcal S_{X,J}=\bigcup_{1\le j\le J}2^j\mathcal E_X^\star.$$ TPC-159 performs exact dyadic telescoping. For $$2^J\sqrt X\le T\le X,\qquad T\notin\mathcal S_{X,J},$$ it obtains $$\frac qT\left|
   \sum_{\substack{z\\0<t(z)\le T}}c(z)\rho(z)
 \right|
 \ll
 \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left(\mathcal L^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+\frac qT\right).                 \label{eq:prefix}$$ There is no factor $J$ in the correlation term because the dyadic block masses form a geometric sum. Meanwhile $$\frac1{\log X}\int_{\mathcal S_{X,J}}\frac{dT}{T}
 \ll J\mathcal L^{-\kappa_0}
 =\mathcal L^{-\kappa_0+o(1)}.$$ This moves the actual periodic core from source-native dyadic blocks to cumulative prefixes outside a sparse shadow. It is therefore the strongest arithmetic export in the present integration.

It is not an all-endpoint statement. A predetermined atomic endpoint can lie in a continuously sparse set.

## Abel return and its exact boundary

For ordered points $t_1<\cdots<t_m\le T$, put $$\sigma_i=c(z_i)\rho(z_i),\qquad
 A_i=\sum_{j\le i}\sigma_j,\qquad
 d_i=
 \begin{cases}
   w_i-w_{i+1},&i<m,\\
   w_m,&i=m.
 \end{cases}$$ Then the exact identity $$\sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_iw_i=\sum_{i=1}^mA_id_i$$ and [\[eq:prefix\]](../main.tex#L358){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:prefix"} yield the TPC-160 interface $$\frac qT\left|\sum_i\sigma_iw_i\right|
 \ll \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left[
   \varepsilon_X V_{\rm good}(w)
   +\left(1+\frac qT\right)V_{\rm bad}(w)
 \right],                                                     \label{eq:abel}$$ where $$\varepsilon_X=\mathcal L^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+\frac qT.$$ For a prefix cutoff the Abel derivative is one atom at the selected endpoint. Continuous exceptional density cannot certify that this atom avoids the bad set. Hence TPC-160 is a positive weighted almost-endpoint interface, but not a production physical return.
