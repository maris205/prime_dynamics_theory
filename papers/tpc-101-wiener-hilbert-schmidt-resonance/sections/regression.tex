\section{Exact finite regression}
\label{sec:regression}

The script
\texttt{experiments/tpc101\_certificate.py} enumerates odd primes,
every admissible width, complete multiplicative-character tables,
and deterministic complex test weights.  It verifies:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the direct incidence remainder against
  \eqref{eq:exact-remainder};
\item the exact fixed-filter norm inequality and an explicitly
  constructed Fourier extremizer;
\item the Wiener-to-Hilbert--Schmidt bound;
\item equality for a one-frequency atom;
\item the full-window zero remainder and the resolved-length bound;
  and
\item the principal-to-diagonal transfer on deterministic finite
  opened-cell ledgers.
\end{enumerate}
Roots of unity are evaluated in floating arithmetic; all set,
cardinality, endpoint, and ledger data are deterministic.  The
reported roundoff tolerance is numerical verification of finite
identities, not evidence for an asymptotic census bound.
