\section{Claim boundary}
\label{sec:claim-boundary}

\subsection*{Proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The exact fixed-filter functional norm
  \(\mathfrak S_H(b)\) is unconditional finite-group algebra.
\item Bounded Wiener mass transfers the complete nonprincipal
  Hilbert--Schmidt ledger to the sharp factor \(g_q(H)\).
\item A frequency atom proves sharpness using only \(\|b\|_1\).
\item Full resonance width is purely principal, and resolved length
  \(N\ge3\) gives the factor \(O(\sqrt{q/N})\).
\item The corrected frequency-free TPC-99 positive carrier obeys
  the width-sensitive principal/centered return
  \eqref{eq:literal-width-bound}.
\item After the separate \(q_X\mid u\) return, the explicitly stated
  opened-atom cap and principal gate imply the width-weighted
  diagonal gate for the remaining invertible census.  The fine
  cells are singleton, so this is an atomic-cap transfer and not a
  new intra-cell dispersion theorem.
\end{itemize}

The finite-group statements are \(\Lzero\).  The proof-carrying
literal census crosswalk and the conditional atom-ledger reduction
are \(\Lone\).

\subsection*{Not proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The growing literal principal gate
  \eqref{eq:new-principal-gate} is not proved.
\item The maximum normalized opened-atom bound
  \(W_X\le X^{o(1)}\) is isolated, not asserted.
\item The width-weighted cross-cell gate
  \eqref{eq:weighted-cross-gate} is not proved.
\item No positive carrier is identified with the original signed
  low window.
\item No new affine M\"obius cancellation theorem, determinant
  lower bound, TPC-18 cover, parity breakthrough, fixed-shift
  Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or
  twin-prime conclusion is proved or implied.
\item Nothing is specialized to \(h_0=2\).
\end{itemize}

The paper therefore removes a genuine worst-eigenvalue loss and
narrows the next literal obligations, but it does not claim
\(\Ltwo\) arithmetic progress.
