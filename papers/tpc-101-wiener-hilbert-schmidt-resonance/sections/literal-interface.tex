\section{Return to the frequency-free literal census}
\label{sec:literal-interface}

We use the corrected TPC-99 crosswalk.  Its base key is
\[
 \beta=(\theta,c,\kappa),
\]
not the resolved key \((\theta,c,\kappa,r)\) containing a frequency.
For each soluble nonconstant branch, put
\[
 w_{\beta,X}
 =
 |A_{\beta,X}|\mathcal A_{\beta,X},
\]
where \(A_{\beta,X}\) is the exact outer coefficient and
\(\mathcal A_{\beta,X}\) is the uniform absolute mass of the
resolved \(z\)-fiber.  Thus \(w_{\beta,X}\) is independent of \(r\)
and includes one copy of every source, content, projector, mask, and
global-normalization factor.

For
\[
 1\le N_\beta\le q_X,\qquad
 H_\beta=H_{q_X}(N_\beta),\qquad
 \omega_\beta
 =
 \varepsilon_\theta\frac{\Omega_\beta}{c}
 \pmod{q_X},
\]
TPC-99 defines
\begin{equation}
 a_H(\omega)
 =
 \sum_{\substack{\beta:H_\beta=H\\
                  \omega_\beta=\omega}}
 w_{\beta,X}.
\label{eq:literal-census}
\end{equation}
The branch length \(N_\beta\) is the resolved \(z\)-fiber
cardinality; \(j\) is already retained in \(\theta\).  Branches with
\(N_\beta>q_X\) have no resonant nonzero frequency.

The fixed positive frequency weight is
\[
 b_X(r)
 =
 |\mathfrak m_{K,X}(r)|
 \one_{\{0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R_X\}},
\qquad
 B_X=\|b_X\|_1\ll_K1.
\]
The phase of \(\mathfrak m_{K,X}\) is lost only because this is the
positive carrier, not the original signed low window.

\begin{theorem}[Width-sensitive literal carrier bound]
\label{thm:literal-width-bound}
Let \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{res},X}^{\mathrm{abs}}\) be the corrected
TPC-99 positive nonconstant resonance carrier.  Then
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{res},X}^{\mathrm{abs}}
 \le{}&
 B_X
 \sum_H\frac{2H}{q_X-1}\|a_H\|_1
\notag\\
 &+
 B_X\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\|a_H^\circ\|_2.
\label{eq:literal-width-bound}
\end{align}
In particular, it is \(O(X^{o(1)}Q_X^2)\) if
\begin{align}
 \sum_H\frac{H}{q_X}\|a_H\|_1
 &\ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,
\label{eq:new-principal-gate}\\
 \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\|a_H^\circ\|_2
 &\ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.
\label{eq:new-centered-gate}
\end{align}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
TPC-99 gives the exact positive-census identity
\[
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{res},X}^{\mathrm{abs}}
 =
 \sum_H\cI_H(a_H,b_X).
\]
Apply \eqref{eq:principal-remainder} and then
\cref{thm:wiener-hs} to every width.  The first gate is sufficient
for the displayed principal term because \(q_X-1\asymp q_X\) and
\(B_X\ll_K1\); the second controls the remaining sum.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Strict weakening of the old centered gate]
\label{cor:old-implies-new}
The TPC-99 condition
\[
 \sum_H\|a_H^\circ\|_2
 \ll
 \frac{X^{o(1)}Q_X^2}{\sqrt{q_X}\log q_X}
\]
implies \eqref{eq:new-centered-gate}.  The converse is false even at
the level of abstract nonnegative censuses.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Use \(g_{q_X}(H)\le\sqrt{q_X}\); the logarithm is a soft factor.  For
the converse, take a nonnegative point-mass census at one width
\(H=O(1)\), with centered norm of order \(Q_X^2\).  The new left
side is \(O(Q_X^2)\),
whereas the old target requires the norm to be
\(O(Q_X^{3/2+o(1)})\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[No positive carrier identity with the signed sum]
The crosswalk covers every frequency-free branch once and preserves
its exact magnitude and support.  It still uses
\(|A_{\beta,X}S_{\beta,X}(r)\mathfrak m_{K,X}(r)|\), so
\eqref{eq:literal-width-bound} is an upper bound, not an equality
with the signed affine M\"obius contribution.
\end{remark}
