\begin{abstract}
TPC-99 diagonalizes the multiplicative resonance carrier but bounds
its nonprincipal part by the largest incomplete-character
eigenvalue, costing \(\sqrt q\log q\) at every resonance width.
That estimate treats the low-window reconstruction weight as an
arbitrary \(L^2\) vector.  In the literal problem this weight is
fixed and has bounded Wiener mass.  We exploit that asymmetry.

For \(G=\mathbb F_q^\times\),
\(E_H=\{\pm1,\ldots,\pm H\}\), and
\[
 \cI_H(a,b)
 =
 \sum_{\omega,r\in G}
 a(\omega)b(r)\one_{E_H}(r\omega),
\]
write \(\mathcal R_H(a,b)\) for the nonprincipal part.  We prove
that the exact norm of this functional in the centered \(a\)
variable is
\[
 \mathfrak S_H(b)^2
 =
 \frac1{q-1}
 \sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}
 |\widehat{\one_{E_H}}(\chi)|^2
 |\widehat b(\overline\chi)|^2.
\]
Consequently
\[
 |\mathcal R_H(a,b)|
 \le
 \|b\|_1
 \sqrt{\frac{2H(q-1-2H)}{q-1}}\,
 \|a^\circ\|_2.
\]
The factor is exact using only \(\|b\|_1\): a point mass \(b\)
attains it.  It vanishes at \(H=(q-1)/2\), and for the literal width
\(H_q(N)\) it is at most \(\sqrt{2q/N}\) whenever \(N\ge3\).

Applied to the corrected frequency-free TPC-99 census and its fixed
filter \(b_X=|\mathfrak m_{K,X}|\), this replaces the uniform
\(Q_X^{3/2}\) centered-energy target by the weaker
width-sensitive gate
\[
 \sum_H
 \sqrt{\frac{2H(q_X-1-2H)}{q_X-1}}\,
 \|a_H^\circ\|_2
 \ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.
\]
After TPC-100 separately returns its \(q_X\mid u\) subcarrier, its
exact singleton-cell decomposition produces separate
width-weighted atomic-diagonal and cross-cell gates.  The singleton
fact itself supplies no dispersion.  We also prove an
abstract principal-to-diagonal transfer under a stated maximum-atom
bound.  The finite-group theorem is \(\Lzero\), and
the literal crosswalk is \(\Lone\).  The principal mass, literal
atom cap, and cross-cell estimate are not proved here; no
\(\Ltwo\) fixed-shift cancellation or prime-pair conclusion is
claimed.
\end{abstract}
