\section{Finite-window theorem}

The remaining analytic input is the additive large sieve in its standard separated-
frequency form \citep{MontgomeryVaughan1973}.  If the frequencies are separated
modulo one by $\delta$, then on any consecutive interval $I$ of $N$ integers,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{n\in I}\left|\sum_\ell z_\ell\ee^{2\pi i n\alpha_\ell}\right|^2
 \le (N-1+\delta^{-1})\sum_\ell|z_\ell|^2.           \label{eq:large-sieve}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Primitive spacing]\label{lem:spacing}
Distinct primitive fractions $a/h$ and $a'/h'$ with $h,h'\le U$ have circular
distance at least $U^{-2}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Their difference, or its complement modulo one, is a nonzero rational whose
denominator divides $hh'$.  Its numerator is a nonzero integer, so the distance is
at least $(hh')^{-1}\ge U^{-2}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Collision-compressed finite-window reassembly]
\label{thm:finite-window}
For every consecutive interval $I$ of $N$ integers,
\begin{align}
 \sum_{n\in I}\sum_{j=1}^J|K_j(n)|^2
 &\le (N-1+U^2)\Rstar
 \sum_{h,a,j,q}|C_hB_{h,q}^{(j)}(a)|^2,              \label{eq:exact-chain}\\
 &\ll (N+U^2)JM^2\Rstar\frac{Q^2}{H}(\log x)^5.
                                                                    \label{eq:source-chain}
\end{align}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply \eqref{eq:large-sieve} for each packet $j$ to the coefficients
\[
 z_{h,a}^{(j)}=C_h\sum_{q\in\Qx}B_{h,q}^{(j)}(a).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:spacing} permits $\delta^{-1}\le U^2$.  Summing in $j$ and applying
Lemma~\ref{lem:collision} proves \eqref{eq:exact-chain}.  Lemma~\ref{lem:direct}
then gives \eqref{eq:source-chain}.
\end{proof}

The order of the proof is load-bearing.  Collapsing $q$ by the unconditional bound
$|\sum_qz_q|^2\le P\sum_q|z_q|^2$ would restore the TPC-218 exponent.  Here physical
occupancy replaces $P$ before the finite-window Gram is diagonalized.
