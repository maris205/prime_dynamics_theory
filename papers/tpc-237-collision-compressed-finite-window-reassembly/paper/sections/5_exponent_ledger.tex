\section{V59 exponent ledger and method boundary}

Take $I=I_x=(x/2,x]\cap\ZZ$ and $N=|I_x|$.  The exact exponent identities are
\begin{align}
 \frac{Q^2}{H}&=x^{1/96}, &
 \frac{UQ}{H}&=x^{23/2400},\label{eq:factor-exponents}\\
 \left(\frac{Q^2}{H}\right)^2&=x^{1/48}, &
 \frac{UQ}{H}\frac{Q^2}{H}&=x^{1/50},\label{eq:product-exponents}\\
 \frac{U^2}{N}&=x^{-67/200+o(1)}.                     \label{eq:window-exponent}
\end{align}
Dividing \eqref{eq:source-chain} by $N$ therefore yields
\begin{equation}
 \frac1N\sum_{n\in I_x}\sum_j|K_j(n)|^2
 \ll JM^2\bigl(x^{1/48}+x^{1/50}\bigr)(\log x)^5
 \ll JM^2x^{1/48}(\log x)^5.                         \label{eq:main-result}
\end{equation}
The leading unnormalized exponent is $1+1/48=49/48$.

sequentially composes a collision Bessel inequality, an absolute $C_h$ harmonic
majorant, and the additive large sieve.  Valid upper bounds need not be simultaneously
saturated by one physical source.  Conversely, no step uses the signs in $C_h$.

\[
 \text{unsigned packet trace}
 \ne \text{signed four-packet Gate-B scalar}.

twin-prime conclusion follows from \eqref{eq:main-result}.
