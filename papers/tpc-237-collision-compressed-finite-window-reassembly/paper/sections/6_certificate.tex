\]

\[
 8830^{32}\le101^{63}<8831^{32},\qquad

\[
 C_{82}^{\mathrm{rat}}=\frac{\mu(82)}{82}=\frac1{82}.
\]

the asymptotic theorem.

normalized energy $5/1681$.
