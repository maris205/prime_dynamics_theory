# TPC-312 paper plan

## Research question

Does a genuinely new source interval and new prime-shell spine reproduce the
finite sign-separation geometry observed in the earlier Bridge-B diagnostics,
without importing the earlier target labels?

## Claims to establish

1. Define the literal finite source and deleted-diagonal prime components.
2. Prove the Gram/PSD identity and the global-sign reduction.
3. Prove that the Gray traversal enumerates every sign class exactly once.
4. Recompute eight new rows over exact rationals and certify modular full rank.
5. Record the exact finite Q-spine orderings and their strict separation.

## Deliberate non-claims

The project does not claim an external physical holdout, a canonical weight law,
uniformity in a growing parameter, an arithmetic `L2` estimate, fixed-power
credit, Route-B passage, or a twin-prime theorem.

## Planned artifacts

The producer writes a canonical JSON certificate.  An independent checker
rebuilds the physical outputs and sign extrema without importing the producer.
A small rational stress suite checks Gray coverage and the finite schema.  The
paper reports exact statements separately from decimal presentation.
