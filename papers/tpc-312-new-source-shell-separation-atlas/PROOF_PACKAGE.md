# TPC-312 proof package

## Proposition 1: Gram positivity

For any real vector `c`,

\[
 c^T Gc=\sum_{u\in I}\left(\sum_{p\in S_Q}c_pg_p(u)\right)^2\ge0.
\]

Thus each physical Gram matrix is positive semidefinite.  All `g_p(u)` are
rational because the source coefficients and kernels are rational.

## Proposition 2: global-sign reduction

`E(-c)=(-c)^TG(-c)=c^TGc=E(c)`.  Every sign vector has exactly one
representative with its first coordinate `+1`; hence `2^(|S_Q|-1)` states are
enough for the finite extremum search.

## Proposition 3: Gray coverage

The reflected Gray map `g(k)=k xor (k>>1)` is a bijection on the integers
`0,...,2^m-1`, and consecutive values differ in exactly one bit.  The
producer's incremental update changes one sign at a time and visits every
global-sign class once.  The independent stress suite compares its visited
energy multiset with direct enumeration on a four-dimensional rational Gram
fixture.

## Proposition 4: modular rank certificate

After clearing rational denominators, a nonzero determinant modulo the declared
prime `1000000007` implies a nonzero rational determinant, provided the cleared
denominator is invertible modulo that prime.  The checker verifies these
conditions and obtains full rank for all eight rows.  This is a finite rank
certificate, not an asymptotic statement.

## Proposition 5: finite separation and ordering

The producer and independent checker compare exact `Fraction` values.  They
verify `r^-<1<r^+` in every row, the two strict Q-spine chains, and the four
exponent-two strengthening inequalities.  These are direct finite arithmetic
facts recorded in the certificate and do not imply a limit law.
