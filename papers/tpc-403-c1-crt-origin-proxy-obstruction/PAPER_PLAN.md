# Paper plan

## Question

Can the signed diagonal-deletion profile remain large when its unmasked scalar
sum is small, under the exact TPC-401 production-domain proxy?

## Contribution

An exact CRT lemma constructs arbitrarily large admissible origins whose unit
mask profile separates positive and negative alternating shell primes.  A
finite certificate verifies the construction for four increasing values of
`m`.

## Claim ceiling

The theorem is exact for the declared proxy and explicit synthetic signs.
It makes no claim about the arithmetic source sign, normalized operator norms,
uniform growing estimates, Route-B, or twin primes.

`results/`, and `notes/`, including `paper/paper.pdf`.
