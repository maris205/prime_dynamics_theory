\begin{abstract}
We audit a declared seven-source theorem corpus against the literal
fixed-atom determinant-two two-M\"obius target selected by MVP9.  Two
theorems act directly on the arithmetic coefficient: the
Ter\"av\"ainen--Walker logarithmically averaged affine twist at every fixed
atom, and the Tao--Ter\"av\"ainen almost-scale correlation theorem after
periodic reassembly at a rational atom.  Neither preserves the full
conjunction of natural normalization, deterministic all-prefix/all-scale
quantifiers, fixed-\(X\) power, and the actual-support axis.  Thus the
declared corpus has two direct candidates and zero eligible mechanisms.

The audit also finds a prior formula-level gap.  The written direct
transform uses the terminal block \(N<t(z)\le2N\), while the bad-endpoint
contract uses the cumulative prefix \(0<t(z)\le T\).  The source-locked
direct contract does not specify a cumulative replacement, so the claimed
specialization \(N=T\) is not formula-certified.  The named atom, packet
schedule, uniform constant, exponent, ranges and physical loss ledger are
also absent.  The result is a fail-closed L1 scoped exhaustion, not an L2
estimate or a global nonexistence theorem.  The batch therefore stops at
TPC-193 for user confirmation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Gate scope and literal coefficient}

Put \(\e(x)=\exp(2\pi i x)\).  TPC-167 writes the actual arithmetic core as
\citep{WangTPC167}
\begin{align}
 &a,s\ge1,\quad d,u\in\mathbb Z,\quad (a,s)=1,\quad
 as\ {\rm odd},\quad su-ad=2,                                      \label{eq:data}\\
 &q=as,\qquad t(z)=ad+qz,\qquad
 c_z=\mu(d+sz)\mu(u+az).                                           \label{eq:core}
\end{align}
The determinant identity also gives
\[
 t(z)=a(d+sz),\qquad t(z)+2=s(u+az),
\]
so \eqref{eq:core} is the literal fixed-\(h_0=2\) two-M\"obius
coefficient, not a majorant or synthetic surrogate.

The MVP9 eligibility signature is \citep{WangTPC192}
\begin{equation}
\begin{gathered}
(\mathsf{ACTUAL\_FIXED\_H0\_PACKET},
 \mathsf{NAMED\_FIXED\_ATOM},\\
 \mathsf{DETERMINISTIC\_ALL\_PREFIX},
 \mathsf{DETERMINISTIC\_ALL\_SCALE},\\
 \mathsf{FIXED\_X\_POWER\_FIXED\_ATOM},
 \mathsf{ACTUAL\_ACTIVE\_SUPPORT}).
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
An eligible mechanism must be theorem-backed, act directly on
\eqref{eq:core}, preserve all six coordinates, and state its constants,
ranges, normalization and losses.  Phase \(L^2\), almost-every phase,
size-only control and renamed forms of the three stopped method cells are
excluded before promotion.

\section{The formula-completeness gate}

\subsection{Two source-locked summation domains}

The only explicit direct transform in the locked chain is
\begin{equation}
 I_N^{\rm blk}=\{z:N<t(z)\le2N\},\qquad
 F_N^{\rm blk}(\alpha)
 =\frac qN\sum_{z\in I_N^{\rm blk}}c_z\e(-\alpha z).
\label{eq:block}
\end{equation}
The TPC-149 arithmetic theorem uses the same terminal block
\citep{WangTPC149}.  In contrast, TPC-159 writes the exact cumulative
domain and \(q/T\) normalization for \(c_z\rho(z)\), while TPC-184 freezes
the cumulative actual-core object as the prescribed-atom bad-endpoint
contract.  Combining that domain with the TPC-167 additive-character
convention, the audited comparison target is
\begin{equation}
 B_T(\alpha)
 =\frac qT\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathbb Z\\0<t(z)\le T}}
 c_z\e(-\alpha z),
\label{eq:cumulative}
\end{equation}
uniformly over prescribed prefix endpoints
\citep{WangTPC159,WangTPC167,WangTPC184}.

TPC-183 states that a direct estimate specializes to the bad-endpoint
estimate by setting \(N=T\), while TPC-189 describes the direct target in
words as ``all prefix'' \citep{WangTPC183,WangTPC189}.  Neither paper writes
a direct summation domain or a prefix index set.  If ``direct'' retains
\eqref{eq:block}, setting \(N=T\) leaves \(T<t(z)\le2T\) and does not
produce \eqref{eq:cumulative}.  If it instead means a new cumulative
direct prefix, that new formula and its admissible range are absent.

\begin{proposition}[Formula-level fail-closed boundary]
\label{prop:formula}
The source-locked record does not uniquely instantiate the direct target's
summation domain.  Consequently the TPC-183 implication
\[
 \text{direct fixed atom}\Longrightarrow\text{bad-endpoint fixed atom}
\]
is not formula-certified by the locked formulas.  This audit records the
implication as unresolved, not false.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Equations \eqref{eq:block} and \eqref{eq:cumulative} have different
domains.  The substitution \(N=T\) does not identify them.  No locked
direct-target formula supplies a third definition that resolves the
difference.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Missing physical fields}

TPC-180 finds zero value-bearing named-phase records and zero production
packet-coordinate rows: the atom identifier, phase value modulo one,
source locator and packet-schedule locator are null \citep{WangTPC180}.
TPC-189 also does not freeze the admissible \(X,N,q\) ranges, a positive
pointwise exponent \(\sigma\), the uniform constant \(C\), or a complete
physical loss ledger.  Thus the desired skeleton
\[
 |F_N(\alpha_\star)|\le C X^{-\sigma}
\]
cannot yet be parsed into a unique literal theorem call.  Fixed \(h_0=2\)
remains a data fact only.

\section{The two direct theorem candidates}

\subsection{Every fixed atom under logarithmic averaging}

Ter\"av\"ainen--Walker Lemma 4.2(1) states that, for fixed nonparallel
positive affine forms, real-valued bounded multiplicative functions with
the first nonpretentious, and every \(\gamma\in\mathbb R\),
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{X\to\infty}\frac1{\log X}
 \sum_{n\le X}
 \frac{f_1(a_1n+h_1)f_2(a_2n+h_2)\e(\gamma n)}n=0.
\label{eq:tw}
\end{equation}
Take \(f_1=f_2=\mu\).  Choose a fixed \(z_0\) for which both translated
intercepts are positive, set \(z=n+z_0\), and use
\[
 (a_1,h_1)=(s,d+sz_0),\qquad
 (a_2,h_2)=(a,u+az_0),\qquad
 \gamma=-\alpha_\star.
\]
Then the nonparallel determinant is
\[
 s(u+az_0)-a(d+sz_0)=su-ad=2,
\]
so \eqref{eq:tw} acts directly on \eqref{eq:core} at every fixed prescribed
atom.  The translation changes the phase only by the unit scalar
\(\e(-\alpha_\star z_0)\).

This is the strongest direct candidate in the corpus.  Its native
normalization is nevertheless reciprocal/logarithmic, its data are fixed,
and its conclusion is qualitative \(o(1)\).  TPC-129 proves that terminal
reciprocal cancellation has no formal implication to an ordinary
all-prefix sum without extra path information \citep{WangTPC129}.  Hence
\eqref{eq:tw} supplies neither natural \(q/N\) control nor a uniform
fixed-\(X\) power.

\subsection{A rational atom outside exceptional scales}

Tao--Ter\"av\"ainen Theorem 3.1, after the quotient lift and periodic
reassembly of TPC-149, gives absolute \(\eta_0,\kappa_0>0\) and
\(\mathcal E_X^\star\subset[\sqrt X,X]\) with
\[
 \frac1{\log X}\int_{\mathcal E_X^\star}\frac{dt}{t}
 \ll(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}
\]
such that
\begin{equation}
 \frac qN\left|
 \sum_{N<t(z)\le2N}c_z\rho(z)\right|
 \ll\|\rho\|_\infty(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}
\label{eq:tt}
\end{equation}
whenever \(qR\le(\log X)^{\eta_0}\),
\(N\in[\sqrt X,X]\setminus\mathcal E_X^\star\), and \(\rho\) has
period \(R\).  For a rational atom \(\alpha_\star=r/R\), put
\(\rho(z)=\e(-rz/R)\).  This preserves the literal coefficient and the
natural \(q/N\) normalization \citep{WangTPC149,WangTPC158}.

The production record, however, contains no source-backed rational atom or
denominator.  Even if it did, \eqref{eq:tt} controls one dyadic block only,
deletes exceptional scales, and saves a power of \(\log X\), whose
fixed-\(X\) exponent is zero.  TPC-159 and TPC-170 leave respectively a
dyadic bad shadow or an almost-every-phase, prescribed-schedule
quantifier; neither repairs all required axes
\citep{WangTPC159,WangTPC170}.

\section{Scoped exhaustion theorem}

\begin{theorem}[TPC-193 candidate-mechanism gate]
\label{thm:gate}
In the declared seven-source corpus:
\[
 \#\{\text{primary records}\}=7,\qquad
 \#\{\text{direct candidates}\}=2,\qquad
 \#\{\text{eligible candidates}\}=0.
\]
The target contract is \(\NT\) at the formula level, with first missing
field
\[
 \mathsf{DIRECT\_TARGET\_SUMMATION\_DOMAIN\_AND\_PREFIX\_INDEX}.
\]
Independently, each direct candidate fails required analytic axes even
under either possible domain interpretation.  No theorem-backed genuine
sublemma is available to determine TPC-194.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Proposition \ref{prop:formula} proves target-formula incompleteness.  For
\eqref{eq:tw}, the first native mismatch is reciprocal/logarithmic rather
than natural endpoint normalization; fixed data, all-prefix uniformity,
all-scale uniformity, a fixed-\(X\) rate and actual support also fail.  For
\eqref{eq:tt}, the atom has no rational source record and the theorem has
an exceptional-scale set, a terminal-block domain and only logarithmic
saving.  Thus neither candidate satisfies the eligibility conjunction.
The other five primary sources fail the direct two-factor prefilter.  The
machine registry reviews all seven records and has no unmapped or
unreviewed cell.
\end{proof}
