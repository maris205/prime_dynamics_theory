## Abstract

We audit a declared seven-source theorem corpus against the literal fixed-atom determinant-two two-Möbius target selected by MVP9. Two theorems act directly on the arithmetic coefficient: the Teräväinen–Walker logarithmically averaged affine twist at every fixed atom, and the Tao–Teräväinen almost-scale correlation theorem after periodic reassembly at a rational atom. Neither preserves the full conjunction of natural normalization, deterministic all-prefix/all-scale quantifiers, fixed-$X$ power, and the actual-support axis. Thus the declared corpus has two direct candidates and zero eligible mechanisms.

The audit also finds a prior formula-level gap. The written direct transform uses the terminal block $N<t(z)\le2N$, while the bad-endpoint contract uses the cumulative prefix $0<t(z)\le T$. The source-locked direct contract does not specify a cumulative replacement, so the claimed specialization $N=T$ is not formula-certified. The named atom, packet schedule, uniform constant, exponent, ranges and physical loss ledger are also absent. The result is a fail-closed L1 scoped exhaustion, not an L2 estimate or a global nonexistence theorem. The batch therefore stops at TPC-193 for user confirmation.

# The two direct theorem candidates

## Every fixed atom under logarithmic averaging

Teräväinen–Walker Lemma 4.2(1) states that, for fixed nonparallel positive affine forms, real-valued bounded multiplicative functions with the first nonpretentious, and every $\gamma\in\mathbb R$, $$\lim_{X\to\infty}\frac1{\log X}
 \sum_{n\le X}
 \frac{f_1(a_1n+h_1)f_2(a_2n+h_2)\mathrm e(\gamma n)}n=0.
\label{eq:tw}$$ Take $f_1=f_2=\mu$. Choose a fixed $z_0$ for which both translated intercepts are positive, set $z=n+z_0$, and use $$(a_1,h_1)=(s,d+sz_0),\qquad
 (a_2,h_2)=(a,u+az_0),\qquad
 \gamma=-\alpha_\star.$$ Then the nonparallel determinant is $$s(u+az_0)-a(d+sz_0)=su-ad=2,$$ so [\[eq:tw\]](../main.tex#L212){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:tw"} acts directly on [\[eq:core\]](../main.tex#L63){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:core"} at every fixed prescribed atom. The translation changes the phase only by the unit scalar $\mathrm e(-\alpha_\star z_0)$.

This is the strongest direct candidate in the corpus. Its native normalization is nevertheless reciprocal/logarithmic, its data are fixed, and its conclusion is qualitative $o(1)$. TPC-129 proves that terminal reciprocal cancellation has no formal implication to an ordinary all-prefix sum without extra path information `\citep{WangTPC129}`. Hence [\[eq:tw\]](../main.tex#L212){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:tw"} supplies neither natural $q/N$ control nor a uniform fixed-$X$ power.

## A rational atom outside exceptional scales

Tao–Teräväinen Theorem 3.1, after the quotient lift and periodic reassembly of TPC-149, gives absolute $\eta_0,\kappa_0>0$ and $\mathcal E_X^\star\subset[\sqrt X,X]$ with $$\frac1{\log X}\int_{\mathcal E_X^\star}\frac{dt}{t}
 \ll(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}$$ such that $$\frac qN\left|
 \sum_{N<t(z)\le2N}c_z\rho(z)\right|
 \ll\|\rho\|_\infty(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}
\label{eq:tt}$$ whenever $qR\le(\log X)^{\eta_0}$, $N\in[\sqrt X,X]\setminus\mathcal E_X^\star$, and $\rho$ has period $R$. For a rational atom $\alpha_\star=r/R$, put $\rho(z)=\mathrm e(-rz/R)$. This preserves the literal coefficient and the natural $q/N$ normalization `\citep{WangTPC149,WangTPC158}`.

The production record, however, contains no source-backed rational atom or denominator. Even if it did, [\[eq:tt\]](../main.tex#L251){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:tt"} controls one dyadic block only, deletes exceptional scales, and saves a power of $\log X$, whose fixed-$X$ exponent is zero. TPC-159 and TPC-170 leave respectively a dyadic bad shadow or an almost-every-phase, prescribed-schedule quantifier; neither repairs all required axes `\citep{WangTPC159,WangTPC170}`.
