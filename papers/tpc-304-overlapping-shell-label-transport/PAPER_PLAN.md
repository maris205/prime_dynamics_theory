# TPC-304 paper plan

## Question

TPC-303 found finite native-budget descents on a moving prime-shell spine.
Determine whether the source-first weighted sign labels themselves undergo a
localized transport fracture on overlapping shells.

## Minimal contribution

1. Define a global-sign-gauge-invariant overlap correlation and disagreement.
2. Prove the correlation/disagreement identity exactly for binary labels.
3. Reconstruct six adjacent label transports from the locked TPC-302 source
   rows and crosswalk them with the locked TPC-303 budget census.
4. Record the finite localization without claiming causality or asymptotics.

## Acceptance criteria

- all six overlap rows reproduced independently;
- exact fractions and mismatch primes stored in a canonical certificate;
- the unique low-correlation transition and budget-descent concentration agree;
- normal/optimized producer, independent checker, stress, and bridge outputs
  are byte-identical with empty stderr;
- a compiled PDF is stored as `paper/paper.pdf`.
