\title{Equal Rank and Equal Spectral Mass Do Not Determine the\\
First-Alias Weighted Prefix}
\author{Bin Wang}

\begin{abstract}
We construct two finite normal spectral models on the physical first-alias
clock that agree in a stringent package of coarse invariants compatible with
the noisy Hardy-head ceilings but have opposite strict-prefix behavior.  Let
$\mathcal U_L(r)$ be the complete $L$th-root shell of radius $r$, let
$\mathcal Y_k$ be the RH-342 counterloop, and set
\[
 a=\frac34,\qquad b=\frac45,\qquad
 c=\sqrt{\frac{481}{800}}.
\]
The multisets
\[
 \mathcal X_k^{\inv}=\mathcal Y_k\sqcup\mathcal U_{4k}(c),
 \qquad
 \mathcal X_k^{\vis}=\mathcal Y_k\sqcup
 \mathcal U_{2k}(a)\sqcup\mathcal U_{2k}(b)
\]
both have rank $6k-2$ and squared spectral mass
$(2k-2)\beta_k^2+481k/200$.  They are conjugation closed, eventually
simple, normally realizable, contained in the same Hardy annulus, and have
the same maximum modulus $\beta_k$.  Both agree with $\mathcal Y_k$ at every
order $2\le n<2k$, hence at every fixed order eventually, but they split at
the first alias $n=2k$.

For the RH-299 budget
\[
 D_m(X,Y)=\sum_{2\le n<m}\frac{|p_n(X)-p_n(Y)|R^n}{n},
 \qquad R=\frac75,
\]
the strict endpoint is decisive:
\[
 D_{4k}(\mathcal X_k^{\inv},\mathcal Y_k)=0,
 \qquad
 D_{4k}(\mathcal X_k^{\vis},\mathcal Y_k)
 =\left(\frac{21}{20}\right)^{2k}
  +\left(\frac{28}{25}\right)^{2k}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
The genus-one quotient factors are respectively $1-(cz)^{4k}$ and
$[1-(az)^{2k}][1-(bz)^{2k}]$.  Thus equal rank, squared mass, cap, maximum
modulus, normal simplicity, and all pre-alias or eventual fixed-order data do
not determine the moving strict-prefix budget.  This is an exact finite
spectral information-class theorem, not an actual noisy-operator theorem.
No physical head transport, determinant gluing, Gate progress, or
Riemann-hypothesis claim follows.
\end{abstract}

\section{Source-locked counterloop and complete shells}

Fix the common Hardy constants and physical clock
\begin{equation}\label{eq:constants-clock}
 q=\frac12,\qquad r_H=\frac{17}{20},\qquad R=\frac75,
 \qquad
 k=\frac{\log(1/\sigma)}{2\log\lambda}+O(1),
 \qquad u=4k.
\end{equation}
RH-342 locks the graded counterloop in this normalization as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Y}
 \cY_k=\{\beta_k e^{ij\pi/k},\beta_k e^{-ij\pi/k}:
                    1\le j\le k-1\},
 \qquad \#\cY_k=2k-2,
\end{equation}
with exact moments
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Y-moments}
 p_n(\cY_k)=\beta_k^n
 \bigl(2k\one_{2k\mid n}-1-(-1)^n\bigr).
\end{equation}
Here and below $p_n(X)=\sum_{x\in X}x^n$.  The radius obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:beta-limit}
 \beta_k\longrightarrow
 \beta=(r_H\sqrt\lambda)^{-1}=0.9080523604\ldots
\end{equation}
by RH-272, and $\beta_k<1/r_H$ eventually
\citep{WangCounterloop2026,WangRankLock2026}.  The strict prefix
$2\le n<4k$ contains $2k$ and excludes $4k$.

For $L\ge2$ and $r>0$, define the complete root shell
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-shell}
 \cU_L(r)=\{re^{2\pi ij/L}:0\le j<L\}.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Complete-shell power ledger]
\label{prop:shell-ledger}
For every integer $n\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shell-ledger}
 p_n(\cU_L(r))=Lr^n\one_{L\mid n}.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
After factoring out $r^n$, the remaining sum is the finite geometric
progression
\[
 \sum_{j=0}^{L-1}e^{2\pi ijn/L},
\]
which equals $L$ when $L\mid n$ and zero otherwise.  This is the complete
shell identity used in RH-289 \citep{WangShellNonidentifiability2026}.
\end{proof}

Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:radii}
 a=\frac34,\qquad b=\frac45,\qquad
 c=\sqrt{\frac{481}{800}},
 \qquad c^2=\frac{a^2+b^2}{2},
\end{equation}
and define two model spectra
\begin{equation}\label{eq:candidates}
 \cX_k^{\inv}=\cY_k\sqcup\cU_{4k}(c),
 \qquad
 \cX_k^{\vis}=\cY_k\sqcup\cU_{2k}(a)
                         \sqcup\cU_{2k}(b).
\end{equation}
The superscripts describe visibility before the strict endpoint.  Neither
multiset is named or interpreted as the actual spectral head of
$K_\sigma/r_H$.

\section{Equal rank, mass, cap, and normal realizability}

We use \emph{squared spectral mass} for the multiset quantity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:squared-mass}
 M_2(X)=\sum_{x\in X}|x|^2.
\end{equation}
For a normal diagonal realization this also equals its squared
Hilbert--Schmidt norm; no such operator-language identification is used for
an actual noisy operator.

\begin{theorem}[Exact equal-invariant package]
\label{thm:equal-invariants}
For every $k\ge2$,
\begin{align}
 \#\cX_k^{\inv}=\#\cX_k^{\vis}&=6k-2,
 \label{eq:equal-rank}\\
 M_2(\cX_k^{\inv})=M_2(\cX_k^{\vis})
 &=(2k-2)\beta_k^2+\frac{481k}{200}.
 \label{eq:equal-mass}
\end{align}
Both multisets are closed under complex conjugation and are spectra of finite
normal diagonal matrices.  For all sufficiently large $k$, they are simple,
every root lies in
\begin{equation}\label{eq:common-annulus}
 q<|z|<1/r_H,
\end{equation}
and both have maximum root modulus exactly $\beta_k$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The added ranks are $4k$ on both sides, so \eqref{eq:equal-rank} follows
from $\#\cY_k=2k-2$.  The invisible shell adds squared mass
\[
 4kc^2=4k\frac{481}{800}=\frac{481k}{200}.
\]
The visible shells add
\[
 2ka^2+2kb^2
 =2k\left(\frac9{16}+\frac{16}{25}\right)
 =2k\frac{481}{400}=\frac{481k}{200},
\]
which proves \eqref{eq:equal-mass}.

Every complete shell and $\cY_k$ is conjugation closed, hence so are their
disjoint unions.  Listing the roots on the diagonal gives finite normal
realizations.  The exact squared comparisons
\begin{equation}\label{eq:radius-squares}
 a^2=\frac{450}{800}<c^2=\frac{481}{800}
 <b^2=\frac{512}{800}
\end{equation}
give $a<c<b$.  Since $q<a$ and the limit in \eqref{eq:beta-limit} is larger
than $b=0.8$, eventually
\begin{equation}\label{eq:radius-order}
 q<a<c<b<\beta_k<1/r_H.
\end{equation}
Each individual root shell is simple.  Their radii are then pairwise
distinct, while the roots in $\cY_k$ are the distinct nonreal $2k$th roots
on radius $\beta_k$.  Thus both unions are simple.  Equation
\eqref{eq:radius-order} also proves \eqref{eq:common-annulus} and shows that
the common maximum modulus is $\beta_k$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Compatibility with only the coarse physical ceilings]
\label{cor:coarse-compatible}
On the clock \eqref{eq:constants-clock}, the rank and squared spectral mass
of both candidates satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coarse-scale}
 O(k)=O(\log(1/\sigma))=o(1/\sigma).
\end{equation}
Consequently they do not violate the RH-282 ceilings
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rh282-ceilings}
 r_\sigma\le4/\sigma,
 \qquad M_\sigma\le1/\sigma,
\end{equation}
but those inequalities do not physically realize either candidate.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The rank formula is linear in $k$.  Since $\beta_k$ converges, the mass in
\eqref{eq:equal-mass} is also $O(k)$.  Equation
\eqref{eq:constants-clock} and the elementary relation
$\log(1/\sigma)=o(1/\sigma)$ give \eqref{eq:coarse-scale}.  RH-282 proves
\eqref{eq:rh282-ceilings} for the actual noisy spectral data
\citep{WangSpectralTail2026}; satisfying upper ceilings is only a consistency
check, not an identification theorem.
\end{proof}

\section{Pre-alias agreement and the first split}

\begin{theorem}[Exact moving-order moment dichotomy]
\label{thm:moment-dichotomy}
For every $n\ge1$,
\begin{align}
 p_n(\cX_k^{\inv})-p_n(\cY_k)
 &=4kc^n\one_{4k\mid n},
 \label{eq:inv-difference}\\
 p_n(\cX_k^{\vis})-p_n(\cY_k)
 &=2k(a^n+b^n)\one_{2k\mid n}.
 \label{eq:vis-difference}
\end{align}
Hence both candidates agree exactly with $\cY_k$ for every
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pre-alias}
 2\le n<2k.
\end{equation}
For every fixed $n\ge2$, this agreement therefore holds for all sufficiently
large $k$.  The candidates do \emph{not} both agree with $\cY_k$ throughout
$2\le n<4k$: at $n=2k$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:first-split}
 p_{2k}(\cX_k^{\inv})-p_{2k}(\cY_k)=0,
 \qquad
 p_{2k}(\cX_k^{\vis})-p_{2k}(\cY_k)
 =2k(a^{2k}+b^{2k})>0.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:shell-ledger} to each shell in
\eqref{eq:candidates}.  No positive integer $n<2k$ is divisible by either
$2k$ or $4k$, proving \eqref{eq:pre-alias}.  For fixed $n$, eventually
$n<2k$.  Finally, $2k$ is not divisible by $4k$ but is divisible by $2k$,
which gives \eqref{eq:first-split}.
\end{proof}

The distinction in Theorem~\ref{thm:moment-dichotomy} is essential.  The
invisible candidate agrees with $\cY_k$ at every order below $4k$, while the
visible candidate first differs at $2k$.  Thus the theorem does not claim
that the two candidates carry identical data on the whole strict prefix.

\section{Exact strict-prefix budgets}

Following RH-299, define for finite multisets $X,Y$ and $m\ge3$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:D-definition}
 D_m(X,Y)=\sum_{n=2}^{m-1}
 \frac{|p_n(X)-p_n(Y)|R^n}{n},
 \qquad R=\frac75.
\end{equation}
The denominator $n$ is part of the determinant-logarithm normalization and
must not be dropped \citep{WangRootTransport2026}.

\begin{theorem}[Zero versus divergent first-alias budget]
\label{thm:budget-dichotomy}
For every $k\ge2$,
\begin{align}
 D_{4k}(\cX_k^{\inv},\cY_k)&=0,
 \label{eq:inv-budget}\\
 D_{4k}(\cX_k^{\vis},\cY_k)
 &=\left(\frac{21}{20}\right)^{2k}
  +\left(\frac{28}{25}\right)^{2k}.
 \label{eq:vis-budget}
\end{align}
In particular, the second budget tends to infinity as $k\to\infty$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
There is no multiple of $4k$ in $2\le n<4k$, so
\eqref{eq:inv-difference} makes every summand in \eqref{eq:D-definition}
zero, proving \eqref{eq:inv-budget}.  The only multiple of $2k$ in the same
strict prefix is $n=2k$.  Therefore \eqref{eq:vis-difference} gives exactly
\begin{align*}
 D_{4k}(\cX_k^{\vis},\cY_k)
 &=\frac{2k(a^{2k}+b^{2k})R^{2k}}{2k}\\
 &=(aR)^{2k}+(bR)^{2k}\\
 &=\left(\frac{21}{20}\right)^{2k}
  +\left(\frac{28}{25}\right)^{2k}.
\end{align*}
This retains the exact cancellation between the shell multiplicity $2k$ and
the logarithmic denominator $n=2k$.  Both bases are larger than one, proving
divergence.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the endpoint must remain strict]
The first nonzero moment of $\cU_{4k}(c)$ occurs at $n=4k$.  Thus replacing
$n<4k$ by $n\le4k$ destroys \eqref{eq:inv-budget}.  The paper proves a
strict-prefix theorem only.
\end{remark}

\section{Exact genus-one quotient factors}

For a finite multiset $X$, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:genus-one}
 E_1(w)=(1-w)e^w,
 \qquad \Phi_X(z)=\prod_{x\in X}E_1(xz).
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Candidate quotient factors]
\label{prop:quotient-factors}
The shell quotients are the exact entire factors
\begin{align}
 \frac{\Phi_{\cX_k^{\inv}}(z)}{\Phi_{\cY_k}(z)}
 &=1-(cz)^{4k},
 \label{eq:inv-factor}\\
 \frac{\Phi_{\cX_k^{\vis}}(z)}{\Phi_{\cY_k}(z)}
 &=[1-(az)^{2k}][1-(bz)^{2k}].
 \label{eq:vis-factor}
\end{align}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For every complete shell with $L\ge2$, its first power sum is zero, so all
linear exponential terms in \eqref{eq:genus-one} cancel.  The elementary
root factorization then gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shell-factor}
 \prod_{u\in\cU_L(r)}E_1(uz)=1-(rz)^L.
\end{equation}
Since each candidate is the disjoint union of $\cY_k$ and its displayed
shells, equations \eqref{eq:inv-factor} and \eqref{eq:vis-factor} follow.
\end{proof}

These are finite analytic factor identities.  They do not identify either
quotient with a factor in one physical noisy determinant.

\section{Sharp information-class underdetermination}

\begin{corollary}[Equal coarse data do not determine the moving budget]
\label{cor:underdetermination}
Within the class of finite complex spectral multisets, the following data,
even when imposed simultaneously, do not determine the moving strict-prefix
quantity $D_{4k}(X,\cY_k)$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item rank $6k-2$ and squared spectral mass
$(2k-2)\beta_k^2+481k/200$;
\item the common cap $1/r_H$ and common maximum modulus $\beta_k$;
\item simple, conjugation-closed finite normal realizability;
\item equality with $\cY_k$ at every pre-alias order $2\le n<2k$; and
\item equality with $\cY_k$ at every fixed order eventually.
\end{enumerate}
Indeed, the same data allow the exact value zero and a value tending to
infinity.  A future actual rank cap $r_\sigma\le2k-2$ would exclude both
examples.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:equal-invariants} supplies items 1--3 for both candidates,
and Theorem~\ref{thm:moment-dichotomy} supplies items 4--5.  The two values
of the moving budget are given by Theorem~\ref{thm:budget-dichotomy}.  Both
candidates have rank $6k-2>2k-2$, proving the final exclusion statement.
\end{proof}

Corollary~\ref{cor:underdetermination} is sharp in its declared information
class: it preserves not only rank and mass but also the maximum radius and
all pre-alias data.  It does not refute a future transport theorem that uses
actual operator structure, a sharper rank cap, root matching, moving-order
moments, contour information, or an annular norm.

RH-303 proves that actual annular convergence would force fixed-order head
transport \citep{WangFixedOrderNecessity2026}.  Our examples show only that,
in a finite normal information class, eventual fixed-order transport is not
by itself sufficient for the moving $4k$ budget.  No model-to-actual
substitution is made.

\section{Protocol, verdict, and claim firewall}

The executable artifact evaluates the exact shell indicators, common ranks,
the rational squared-mass identity, the first split, both strict-prefix
budgets, and the radius ordering.  Its finite rows at $k=3,5,9,17$ reproduce
closed identities only.  They are not numerical fits, noisy eigenvalue
observations, or asymptotic evidence.

On the physical clock, the examples are compatible with only the coarse
RH-282 rank and mass ceilings.  Such compatibility cannot identify them with
the actual noisy head.  Accordingly the exact route verdict is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item equal rank, equal squared mass, common cap, and fixed-order data are
\texttt{STOP\_SCOPED} as an inference to the moving prefix without a sharper
actual rank law or moving-order physical input;
\item actual alias-inclusive head transport remains
\texttt{NOT\_TESTABLE}/open; and
\item the RH-288 determinant-gluing leaf remains inactive.
\end{enumerate}

In particular, this paper proves neither divergence nor vanishing of the
actual $D_{4k}$, no physical rank mismatch, no equivalence of the physical
$p$ and $q$ prefixes, and no failure of all future transport mechanisms.  It
does not activate RH-288, identify the model spectra with $K_\sigma$, or
prove determinant gluing.  Gates A--E remain false/open as reviewed in
RH-341 \citep{WangActualFrontier2026}.  No Hilbert--Polya operator is
constructed, no Riemann zero is identified, no von Mangoldt trace formula or
completed-zeta divisor equality is proved, and the Riemann Hypothesis is not
proved.
