# TPC-387 paper plan

## Research question

Can the count increase observed in TPC-386 be represented by a slope learned
from an intermediate count, and does that calibration transfer to new origins?

## Claim-sized contributions

1. A response-blind `512 -> 768 -> 1024` count ladder with the first two
   levels confined to calibration origins.
2. A calibration-only log-count slope and a predeclared endpoint prediction.
3. A 256-row certificate, exact rational anchor, reverse-order replay, and
   25-mutation firewall.
4. A finite panel-wide result: all 32 extrapolation cells pass the 3% cap,
   while the inherited spectral cap remains a separate open/obstructed issue.

## Non-claims

The slope is an empirical finite model. It is not a uniform-in-count law,
source-valid normalization theorem, arithmetic `L2` estimate, Route-A/Route-B
official verdict, or twin-prime result.
