\begin{abstract}
TPC-398 reported a finite endpoint microgrid on one affine family: the
interior probes through $\lambda=31/32$ were origin-stable, whereas the
endpoint $\lambda=1$ was not.  TPC-399 tests the smallest natural continuation,
namely a second coordinate-disjoint family with the same response-blind
protocol.  Its 96-row panel compares each current same-law cohort mean with a
hash-locked all-origin mean from TPC-398.  Every one of the 16 law/normalization
cells passes both the three-percent calibration and holdout transfer caps, and
all 16 within-family transfers pass.  At the same time, the endpoint $\lambda=1$
fails the one-percent origin-spread rule in all four normalizations, with
maximum spreads between $0.0622195$ and $0.0625497$.  The result is a finite
cross-family replication together with a finite obstruction to inferring origin
uniformity from mean transfer.  It is not an arithmetic, asymptotic,
Route-A/Route-B, or twin-prime theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and claim boundary}

The preceding TPC-398 audit used a response-blind grid of four finite matrix
probes and found a diagnostic split: origin stability persisted through
$31/32$, but the endpoint lost stability.  The present paper asks whether this
split is reproducible on a fresh family, while keeping the parent comparison
frozen before reading the new response.

All objects below are finite proxy objects.  In particular, a fractional
coefficient in a matrix interpolation is not asserted to be an arithmetic
character, a prime sign law, or a source weight.  The claim firewall is
\[
 \texttt{ARITHMETIC\_ADVANCE=NO},\qquad
 \texttt{FIXED\_POWER\_CREDIT=0},\qquad
 \texttt{FULL\_GATE\_B=OPEN}.
\]
The official Session evaluator files are absent from this checkout, so the
local proof, independent checker, and Bridge-B artifacts provide finite
consistency evidence only.

\section{Finite construction}

For a prime $p\in(Q,2Q]$, set $Q=8192$, $H=66$, exponent one, and $\beta=2$.
The component used in the certificate is
\begin{align*}
 K_p(u,v)={}&p(p/Q)^2\frac{H^2}{H^2+(u-v)^2}
 \left(\mathbf 1_{p\mid u-v}-\frac{1}{p-1}\right)\\
 &\qquad\cdot\mathbf 1_{u\ne v}\mathbf 1_{p\nmid u}\mathbf 1_{p\nmid v}.
\end{align*}
Let
\[
 G(u)=\sum_{p\in(Q,2Q]}\sum_v K_p(u,v)^2.
\]
If the prime shell is indexed increasingly, define
\[
 M_+=\sum_p K_p,\qquad
 M_{\rm alt}=\sum_p(-1)^{\operatorname{index}(p)}K_p.
\]
The four probes are the exact finite combinations
\[
 M_\lambda=(1-\lambda)M_+ + \lambda M_{\rm alt},qquad
 \lambda\in\left\{\frac78,\frac{15}{16},\frac{31}{32},1\right\}.
\]
This identity is linear algebra and is checked separately with exact rational
arithmetic at a small anchor; it is not a claim about an underlying
arithmetic interpolation.

The fresh candidate family is
\[
 a_j=7200001+401j,\quad 0\leq j<41,
\]
with selected indices $(0,8,16,24,32,40)$.  The origins are
\[
 7200001,\ 7203209,\ 7206417,\ 7209625,\ 7212833,\ 7216041.
\]
The first three are calibration origins and the last three are holdout
origins.  Each window has $N=1024$ points, split into eight blocks of length
128.  The fixed $c=3$ band retains block pairs whose block-index difference is
at most three.

\section{Diagnostics and frozen parent interface}

We record four normalizations: local diagonal, pooled calibration scalar,
current-origin scalar, and first-calibration frozen scalar.  If
$S_\lambda(o)$ denotes the resulting masked spectral diagnostic, its declared
origin spread is
\[
 R_\lambda=\frac{\max_o S_\lambda(o)-\min_o S_\lambda(o)}
 {\operatorname{mean}_o S_\lambda(o)}.
\]
The origin cap is $R_\lambda\leq0.01$.  Finite spectral and Schur caps are
0.64 and 0.83, respectively.

TPC-398 is imported only through its hash-locked code and canonical
certificate.  For each law and normalization, let
$B^{(398)}_\lambda$ be its recorded all-origin mean.  The current cohort
errors are defined directly by
\[
 E_{\rm cal}=\frac{\operatorname{mean}_{o\in\mathcal C}S_\lambda(o)}
 {B^{(398)}_\lambda}-1,\qquad
 E_{\rm hold}=\frac{\operatorname{mean}_{o\in\mathcal H}S_\lambda(o)}
 {B^{(398)}_\lambda}-1.
\]
The cap for both is 0.03.  No segment interpolation or response-dependent fit
is performed.  The within-family transfer error is
\[
 E_{\rm within}=\frac{\operatorname{mean}_{o\in\mathcal H}S_\lambda(o)}
 {\operatorname{mean}_{o\in\mathcal C}S_\lambda(o)}-1,
\]
also tested at cap 0.03.

The parent locks are

\section{Interpretation and route ledger}

The strongest positive result is a response-blind second-family replication:
the 16 frozen same-law TPC-398 means transfer to the new family at the
declared three-percent scale in both cohort roles.  The strongest obstruction
is that this transfer does not remove endpoint origin instability: at
$\lambda=1$, the maximum spread remains 6.22--6.25 percent.  Thus mean
transfer and origin uniformity are empirically separate finite diagnostics.

The reusable structure is a direct hash-locked same-law interface, exact finite
matrix interpolation, coordinate-disjoint calibration/holdout families,
reverse-order replay, and separate origin and transfer gates.  The next
response-blind clue is
\[
 \texttt{ROUND2\_CLUE=TEST\_C1\_ENDPOINT\_MICROGRID\_THIRD\_FAMILY\_REPLICATION}.
\]

The theorem ledger is intentionally conservative:
