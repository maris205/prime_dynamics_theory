\section{The exact physical-depth crosswalk}

Freeze the V59 scales
\[
 H=x^{21/32},\qquad Q=x^{1/3},\qquad U=x^{133/400},
\]
and let $Q<q\le 2Q$ be prime.  For a physical denominator $h$, the residue row is
\begin{equation}
 B_{h,q}(a)=
 \sum_{0<|m|\le\lfloor hq/H\rfloor}
 \psi\!\left(\frac{Hm}{hq}\right)
 \mathbf 1_{m q^{-1}\equiv a\pmod h}.                 \label{eq:physical-row}
\end{equation}
The full source additionally contains
\begin{equation}
 C_h=\sum_{\substack{d\in\mathcal D_x\\h\mid d}}
 \frac{\mu(d)\log d}{d},\qquad
 K_q(n)=\sum_{h,a}C_hB_{h,q}(a)\ee(na/h).             \label{eq:full-source}
\end{equation}
where $\ee(t)=e^{2\pi it}$.  In particular, deleting $C_h$ or collapsing the $h$-sum
is not an innocent change of coordinates.

\begin{theorem}[Physical-depth reparametrization]\label{thm:crosswalk}
Define
\[
 \lambda_h=\frac{hQ}{H}.
\]
Then (1) is exactly
\begin{equation}
 B_{h,q}(a)=
 \sum_{0<|m|\le\lfloor\lambda_hq/Q\rfloor}
 \psi\!\left(\frac{mQ}{\lambda_hq}\right)
 \mathbf 1_{m q^{-1}\equiv a\pmod h},                \label{eq:depth-row}
\end{equation}
and its modulus satisfies $h=(H/Q)\lambda_h$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The cutoff identity is
$hq/H=(hQ/H)(q/Q)=\lambda_hq/Q$.  Likewise
$Hm/(hq)=mQ/(\lambda_hq)$.  Neither substitution changes the residue condition or
the modulus.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Depth range and denominator grid]\label{cor:range}
If a row is active for some $q\le2Q$ and $h\le U$, then
\[
 \frac12\le\lambda_h\le\frac{UQ}{H}=x^{23/2400}.
\]
Consecutive integer denominators are separated by $Q/H$ in depth, so a unit depth
interval contains $H/Q+O(1)=x^{31/96+o(1)}$ available denominator-grid points.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Activity implies $hq/H\ge1$, hence $h\ge H/(2Q)$.  The upper bound follows from
$h\le U$.  Finally, $\lambda_{h+1}-\lambda_h=Q/H$.
\end{proof}

The final count is a geometric grid count only.  It does not assert that every
corresponding coefficient $C_h$ in (2) is nonzero.
