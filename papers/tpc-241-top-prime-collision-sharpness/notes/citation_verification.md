# Citation verification

The manuscript cites Montgomery--Vaughan for standard PNT/partial-summation
background and the two immediately upstream repository papers for the exact
source coefficient/direct floor and finite-window lower frame.  No citation is
used to assert the new post-collapse Cauchy step, which is proved directly.

The bibliography deliberately avoids claiming that a published source proves
the signed Gate-B theorem.  The four-packet and strict `1/400` gates remain
explicitly open.
