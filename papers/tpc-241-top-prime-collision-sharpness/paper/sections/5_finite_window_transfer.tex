\section{Finite-window transfer without deleting cross terms}\label{sec:window}

The coefficient lower bound concerns the full primitive-frequency expansion
underlying \eqref{eq:full_kernel}.  We now transfer it to the literal physical
window.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-window liminf]\label{thm:window}
For every fixed profile satisfying \eqref{eq:profile},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window_liminf}
 \liminf_{x\to\infty}\frac{\log x}{x^{1/48}}
 \meanIx |K_\psi(n)|^2
 \geq \frac{10773\log 2}{3200}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The finite-window lower frame of \cite{WangTPC238}, applied to the complete
coefficient vector indexed by distinct primitive fractions $a/h$, gives
\begin{align}
 \meanIx |K_\psi(n)|^2
 &\geq
 \left[\frac12-\frac{\pi^2U^4}{6N^2}\right]_+
 \sum_{h\leq U}\ \sum_{\substack{a\, (\mathrm{mod}\,h)\\(a,h)=1}}
 \left|C_h\sum_{q\in\Qx}B_{h,q}^\psi(a)\right|^2.
 \label{eq:full_lower_frame}
\end{align}
Every term in the coefficient norm on the right is nonnegative.  We may now,
and only now, restrict it to prime denominators $U/2<p\leq U$.  The restricted
sum is exactly $\Etop$.  Thus
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window_top_restriction}
 \meanIx |K_\psi(n)|^2
 \geq
 \left[\frac12-\frac{\pi^2U^4}{6N^2}\right]_+\Etop.
\end{equation}
By \eqref{eq:scale_arithmetic}, the bracket is $1/2-o(1)$.  Combining
\eqref{eq:window_top_restriction} with Theorem~\ref{thm:coefficient} proves
\eqref{eq:window_liminf}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the order is necessary]
The quadratic form on the left of \eqref{eq:full_lower_frame} contains
cross-frequency terms that need not be nonnegative.  Deleting them before
applying the lower frame would be invalid.  Our restriction occurs only after
the complete quadratic form has been bounded below by a diagonal coefficient
norm.
\end{remark}
