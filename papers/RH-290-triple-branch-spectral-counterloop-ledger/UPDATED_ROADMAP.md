# Roadmap after RH-290

The remaining common leaf is no longer a vague tail problem.  The noisy
spectral branch has its tail, and the graded branch has its coefficient
bridge.  The only admissible merge is a weighted aggregate identification of
the noisy modulus complement with the deterministic anchor.  Equivalently,
one may prove on the RH-282 logarithmic clock both a weighted total-trace
bridge to counterloop plus anchor and weighted head-to-counterloop transport.
Until the direct bridge or both constituent estimates exist, the ledgers must
remain separate.
