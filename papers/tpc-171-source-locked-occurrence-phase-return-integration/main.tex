\begin{abstract}
TPC-163--170 open two more precise layers of the fixed-shift
program.  The frozen structural corpus contains \(2988\) production
cut paths but no theorem-backed cut-to-actual-occurrence edge.  Its
\(866\) native triples identify neither rows nor occurrences: \(2976\)
rows lie in collision classes, with excess \(2122\).  The unique
smallest archive key
\((\ell,k,d,j_L,j_K)\) separates the frozen rows, but has addressing
semantics only.  A finite typed gluing theorem constructs a global
formal row family from supplied compatible local families.  It does
not supply those families, actual active support, or a canonical
minimal representation.  The former monolithic crosswalk gap
therefore splits into three independent not-testable roots.

The arithmetic branch proves Parseval, finite separated-registry,
maximal all-prefix and Borel--Cantelli packet theorems on the actual
determinant-two core.  On an explicit packet schedule, every
\(\delta<1/4\) is available for Lebesgue-almost every fixed phase.
This is genuine \(\Lone\) phase-metric progress, including every
prefix in the prescribed terminal shells.  It is not control at a
named physical phase.  An uncontrolled atomic registry may be
concentrated on the schedule-dependent null set.

We integrate these results through a six-axis quantifier signature,
separate complete architecture routes from arithmetic method
subroutes, enforce an arithmetic-ancestor firewall on the physical
registry, and introduce Endpoint Ledger V4.  The selected map route
has a seven-node minimal not-testable antichain.  Its first reporting
representative is the source-backed local occurrence-edge family.
No positive program-\(\Ltwo\), strict \(1/400\), prime-pair lower
bound, or twin-prime theorem is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Frozen structural state}

Let \(\mathcal C\) denote the frozen nonsoft cut archive.  TPC-163
performs a source-locator census over the declared TPC-153, TPC-156,
TPC-161 and TPC-162 corpus.  It finds
\[
 |\mathcal C|=2988,\qquad
 \#\{\text{native triples}\}=866,
\tag{1}\label{eq:census}
\]
and zero theorem-backed production edges in each required
actual-occurrence class.  This last statement is a census of one
frozen corpus.  It is not a theorem that actual occurrence edges do
not exist.

The native triple is strongly noninjective:
\[
 2976\ \text{rows lie in collision classes},\qquad
 2988-866=2122.
\tag{2}\label{eq:collision}
\]
TPC-164 exhausts all nonempty subsets of the eight archived fields
\[
 \ell,\ k,\ d,\ j_L,\ j_K,\ D_0,\ \mathrm{reason},\ \mathrm{type}.
\]
The unique smallest injective key is
\[
 K_{\rm arc}=(\ell,k,d,j_L,j_K).
\tag{3}\label{eq:key}
\]
It gives a stable address for a row of this archive.  It does not
give an occurrence identifier, a physical parent, active support, or
a canonical representation of an actual carrier.

\begin{theorem}[Formal local-to-global gluing, imported from TPC-165]
\label{thm:gluing}
Suppose a finite cover of \(\mathcal C\) is supplied with nonempty
finite typed local row families.  Suppose further that the local
families carry exact rational column sums equal to one and that their
overlap bijections preserve all typed payloads and satisfy identity,
inverse and triple-overlap composition.  Then the quotient by the
overlap relation is a global formal row family, unique up to the
unique typed isomorphism commuting with the local maps.  Exact column
conservation descends to the quotient.
\end{theorem}

The theorem is a finite descent statement.  The production corpus
does not currently supply a local family or overlap cocycle.  Even
when formal totality is supplied, two more logically independent
predicates remain:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf A&=\text{every retained row has nonzero physical coefficient},\\
\mathsf M&=\text{the actual carrier has a proved canonical or
minimal representation}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}\label{eq:AM}
\]
Neither follows from \cref{thm:gluing}, exact column conservation, or
the archive address \eqref{eq:key}.

The source-backed local-edge target therefore carries the weaker
support signature
\[
\mathsf{SOURCE\_BACKED\_LOCAL\_SUPPORT}.
\]
It is not assigned actual-active-support semantics.  Only the later
actual lift, which consumes the local-edge family together with both
certificates in \eqref{eq:AM}, has the support target
\[
\mathsf{ACTUAL\_ACTIVE\_SUPPORT}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Refined crosswalk roots]
\label{prop:roots}
The selected occurrence-augmented map route has the independent
minimal roots
\[
\begin{gathered}
\mathsf{H1.source\_backed\_local\_occurrence\_edge\_family},\\
\mathsf{H1.actual\_active\_support\_certificate},\\
\mathsf{H1.canonical\_minimal\_representation\_certificate}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{5}\label{eq:three-roots}
\]
All three have status \(\NT\).  The first is the selected reporting
representative; this selection does not erase the other two.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first predicate would instantiate the local families and their
source locators in \cref{thm:gluing}.  The second concerns nonzero
physical coefficients, and the third concerns representation
minimality.  The frozen source census, formal gluing theorem and
archive address prove none of these implications.  TPC-166 records
them as parent-free production roots.  Consequently none is an
ancestor of another, proving minimality of the antichain.
\end{proof}

\section{The phase-metric arithmetic chain}

For one determinant-two core cell write
\[
 c_z=\mu(d+sz)\mu(u+az),\qquad su-ad=2,
\]
and on an interval \(I_N\) define
\[
 S_N(\alpha)=\frac qN\sum_{z\in I_N}c_z e(-\alpha z),
 \qquad e(x)=e^{2\pi i x}.
\tag{6}\label{eq:twist}
\]
TPC-167 observes the exact Parseval identity
\[
 \int_0^1|S_N(\alpha)|^2\,d\alpha
 =\frac{q^2}{N^2}\sum_{z\in I_N}|c_z|^2.
\tag{7}\label{eq:parseval}
\]
It applies at every scale and uses no M\"obius cancellation.  If
\(q\le(\log X)^{\eta_0}\) and \(N\ge\sqrt X\), its phase-\(L^2\)
envelope is \(X^{-1/4}\) up to a polylogarithmic factor.  The norm is
analytic \(L^2\) in phase, not program-\(\Ltwo\).

TPC-168 proves the separated sampling inequality.  For
\(\delta\)-separated \(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_M\) and
\(P(\alpha)=\sum_{n<L}b_ne(n\alpha)\),
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^M|P(\alpha_j)|^2
 \le \bigl(\delta^{-1}+4\pi(L-1)\bigr)
       \sum_{n<L}|b_n|^2.
\tag{8}\label{eq:large-sieve}
\]
This controls all but a sparse fraction of a sufficiently separated
registry.  It does not identify a distinguished element of that
registry.  In particular, density-one control cannot be evaluated at
a named physical phase until the selector is supplied and justified.

TPC-169 replaces one terminal sum by every prefix in a fixed
terminal shell.  If
\[
 M_T(\alpha)
 =\max_{U\in[\theta T,T]}
   \left|\frac qT\sum_{z\le U}c_z\rho(z)e(-\alpha z)\right|,
\]
then dyadic orthogonality gives
\[
 \int_0^1M_T(\alpha)^2\,d\alpha
 \ll D_L^2\|\rho\|_\infty^2
       \left(\frac qT+\frac{q^2}{T^2}\right),
\tag{9}\label{eq:maximal}
\]
where \(D_L\ll1+\log L\).  Thus one phase-exceptional set controls
every prefix in the declared shell.  The normalization loss from
\(U\ge\theta T\) is explicit.

Finally, for a prescribed packet list \(\mathcal P_n\), TPC-170
defines packet energies \(V_{n,p}\) from the right side of
\eqref{eq:maximal}.  If
\[
 \sum_n\lambda_n^{-2}\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_n}V_{n,p}<\infty,
\tag{10}\label{eq:BC}
\]
then Borel--Cantelli implies that for Lebesgue-almost every fixed
\(\alpha\), all declared packets and all their prefixes are
eventually at most \(\lambda_n\).  With \(X_n=2^n\), polylogarithmically
many packets and moduli, \(T_{n,p}\ge\sqrt{X_n}\), one may take
\[
 \lambda_n=X_n^{-\delta}\qquad(\delta<1/4).
\tag{11}\label{eq:metric-power}
\]
Exact Abel summation also gives
\[
 \frac qT\left|\sum_jb_je(-\alpha z_j)w_j\right|
 \le M_T(\alpha)V(w).
\tag{12}\label{eq:abel}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Precise arithmetic advance]
\label{thm:metric}
Equations \eqref{eq:parseval}--\eqref{eq:abel} prove an
\(\Lone\) actual-core phase-metric packet corridor, maximal over all
prefixes in the prescribed shells.  They do not prove a bound at a
named fixed phase, at a scale-dependent phase selector, or on an
unregistered physical packet.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The positive conclusion is exactly \eqref{eq:BC}.  Its null set
depends on the full prescribed packet schedule.  An arbitrary atomic
registry can concentrate all its mass on that null set.  A
constant-coefficient example at phase zero shows that no
coefficient-blind metric theorem implies a named-atom theorem.
This is a nonimplication for the uncontrolled atomic promotion
method, not a lower bound for the literal M\"obius correlation.
\end{proof}

\section{Architecture routes and arithmetic methods}

The manifest has two disjoint route families.  Complete architecture
routes are
\[
\mathcal R_{\rm arch}
=\{\mathsf{archive},\mathsf{map+},\mathsf{scalar+ETO}\}.
\tag{15}\label{eq:arch}
\]
The archive-only cell retains its scoped stop.  The selected
occurrence-augmented map route and the scalar-plus-ETO route remain
\(\NT\).  No theorem says that \(\mathcal R_{\rm arch}\) is the
complete universe of possible architectures.

Arithmetic method subroutes include periodic major arcs, a direct
fixed-atom twist, a source-backed metric-to-atom bridge, the
uncontrolled atomic promotion and a pointwise bad-endpoint theorem.
They may prove children of a complete architecture.  They cannot be
selected as replacement architectures and cannot justify an
architecture-level reroute.

\begin{proposition}[H9 arithmetic firewall]
\label{prop:h9}
The production occurrence, literal-weight, phase, endpoint and
normalization registries have no direct or transitive arithmetic
ancestor in the integration DAG.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The H9 physical registry has as parents only the occurrence registry
and the four independent physical registries.  The occurrence
registry descends only from the three structural roots in
\eqref{eq:three-roots}.  Therefore its ancestor closure contains no
node with arithmetic-core, arithmetic-target or
arithmetic-negative role.  Arithmetic nodes consume H9 records; they
cannot manufacture them.
\end{proof}

This direction matters for the phase-metric corridor.  TPC-170 does
not produce a physical phase registry.  A future source-backed
metric-to-fixed-atom theorem would consume both the TPC-170 theorem
and a separately sourced phase record.

The four H9 leaves are data records, not decay theorems.  Their decay
coordinate is \(\mathsf{NONE}\).  The literal-weight and normalization
leaves carry their respective literal-data carrier axes; the phase
leaf carries named-fixed-atom data; and the endpoint leaf carries
deterministic-endpoint data.  Only a later arithmetic estimate that
consumes these records may carry a fixed-\(X\) decay coordinate.

\section{Typed frontiers}

Let \(\mathcal A\) be the active ancestor closure of the selected map
root.  As before, minimal \(\NT\) nodes suppress their unavailable
descendants.  The manifest recomputes the antichain from the DAG
rather than trusting a submitted list.

\begin{theorem}[Current selected-root antichain]
\label{thm:antichain}
The selected map synthesis has minimal not-testable antichain
\[
\begin{split}
\mathcal B_{\rm NT}=\{&
\mathsf{H1.source\_backed\_local\_occurrence\_edge\_family},\\
&\mathsf{H1.actual\_active\_support\_certificate},\\
&\mathsf{H1.canonical\_minimal\_representation\_certificate},\\
&\mathsf{H9.literal\_weight\_registry},
\mathsf{H9.phase\_cell\_registry},\\
&\mathsf{H9.endpoint\_registry},
\mathsf{H9.normalization\_registry}\}.
\end{split}
\tag{16}\label{eq:blockers}
\]
The canonical first reporting representative is the local
occurrence-edge family.  Separately, the parent-ready open frontier is
\[
\mathcal F_{\rm open}
=\{\mathsf{O161.direct\_additive\_twist\_fixed\_atom},
\mathsf{O161.bad\_endpoint\_pointwise\_fixed\_atom}\}.
\tag{17}\label{eq:open}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The three H1 roots are independent by \cref{prop:roots}.  The four H9

\eqref{eq:blockers}.  The two nodes in \eqref{eq:open} are status
\(\OPEN\) with no unavailable active parent.  Their status and type
are therefore disjoint from the \(\NT\) antichain.
\end{proof}

\section{Endpoint Ledger V4}

Endpoint Ledger V4 preserves the six literal questions from V3:
literal physical coefficients, fixed physical \(h_0\), physical
atomic normalization, canonical/minimal representation, actual
active support and strict \(1/400\) slack.  Only fixed \(h_0=2\) is
currently proved in the relevant actual core.

V4 adds a registry gate and records the phase mode.  The proved mode
is
\[
 \mathsf{LEBESGUE\_AE\_FIXED\_PHASE};
\]
the required physical mode is
\[
 \mathsf{NAMED\_FIXED\_ATOM}.
\tag{18}\label{eq:phase-gap}
\]
The metric exponent in \eqref{eq:metric-power} is entered in a
separate nonpromotable charge.  Hence the named-fixed-atom exponent
remains
\[
 \sigma_{\rm atom}=0,\qquad
 \sigma_{\rm required}=\frac1{400}.
\tag{19}\label{eq:ledger-power}
\]
Unknown registry costs are not zero, and no charge may be owned
twice.  The full synthesis has no defined net slack.

\begin{theorem}[TPC-171 integration verdict]
\label{thm:verdict}
For the source-locked TPC-163--170 state,
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \operatorname{Verdict}_{163:170}=\NT,\\
 \mathsf{FirstMissing}
 =\mathsf{H1.source\_backed\_local\_}\\[-2pt]
 \mathsf{occurrence\_edge\_family}.
 \end{gathered}
 }
\tag{20}\label{eq:verdict}
\]
The full seven-node blocker antichain is retained.  The arithmetic
advance is
\(\Lone\) actual-core phase-metric all-prefix packet control; the
program-positive \(\Ltwo\) flag remains false.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The source hashes validate integrity but are not theorem evidence.
The selected architecture has no exact stop and its route universe is
not proved complete.  Its active DAG has the nonempty antichain
\eqref{eq:blockers}, so its predicates are not yet testable on the
actual carrier.  Independently, \cref{thm:metric} has the phase
quantifier on the left of the forbidden promotion
\eqref{eq:forbidden}, while the literal phase, support,
normalization and endpoint registries remain unavailable.  Thus
neither a named-atom packet saving nor strict endpoint slack can be
entered in the physical ledger.  The declared selection order chooses
the source-backed local edge family without collapsing the antichain.
\end{proof}

H9; an arithmetic method inserted into the architecture universe;
unproved route-universe completeness; collapsed or merged frontiers;
metric power charged as named-atom power; and a \(1/400\) pass with
unknown literal fields.

The next structural object is one theorem-backed local edge family
with exact source locators and compatible overlaps.  The two parallel
certificates in \eqref{eq:three-roots} remain independently necessary.
The arithmetic choice is either a source-backed bridge placing the
production phase outside the schedule null set, or a genuinely
pointwise fixed-atom theorem.  Neither outcome is assumed here.

\section{Conclusion}

This batch advances the arithmetic theorem from almost endpoints to
all prefixes along a prescribed phase-metric packet corridor, and it
resolves the structural monolith into three exact roots.  It also
locates the next hard quantifier change: phase-metric fixed powers do
not pay a named physical atom.  The path remains open, but the current
state is still \(\NT\), with no positive program-\(\Ltwo\),
\(1/400\), prime-pair or twin-prime conclusion.
