\begin{abstract}
The exact-rational comparator of RH-329 freezes the exponential clock
$\sigma_k=\widehat\Lambda^{-2k}$, where
$\widehat\Lambda=2098216888035403/1250000000000000$.  We prove that this
clock is not the physical fixed-phase clock.  The physical expansion rate is
the unique positive root of $p(x)=x^3+4x^2-16$, and exact rational arithmetic
gives $p(\widehat\Lambda)>0$; hence $\widehat\Lambda>\lambda$ and the physical
phase tends to $-\infty$.  Directly from the uniform parity remainder and the
physical multiplier law, we derive an off-phase trichotomy valid for every
clock $\sigma_k=\Lambda_c^{-2k}$ with $\Lambda_c>1$.  On the RH-329 clock the
physical alias packet dominates both the physical parity packet and the two
hatted model packets exponentially.  The resulting scalar parity--alias
comparator defect divided by the physical alias packet tends to $-1$, and
divided by the target $H_k=kR^{-2k}$ tends to $-\infty$.  This rejects RH-329
only as a physical bounded-phase parity--alias comparator.  It is not an
actual full-trace divergence theorem and does not activate the fixed-phase
transfer theorem of RH-330.  Replacing the clock by the exact physical
$\lambda$ leaves target-scale replacement not testable: the archived
relative $o(1)$ laws do not supply the exponentially sharper remainder
$o((\beta R)^{-2k})$.  No determinant gate or Riemann-hypothesis conclusion
is obtained.
\end{abstract}

\section{Physical and hatted clocks}

Let $u_c\in(1,2)$ be the physical parameter and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lambda-from-u}
 \lambda=2u_c(u_c-1)>1.
\end{equation}
The algebraic identities of the gauge-fixed physical observation source give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-polynomial}
 p(\lambda)=0,
 \qquad p(x)=x^3+4x^2-16
\end{equation}
\citep{WangObservation2026}.  The RH-329 model instead defines the exact
rational constant
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Lambda-hat}
 \wh\Lambda=\frac{2098216888035403}{1250000000000000}
\end{equation}
and freezes $\sigma_k=\wh\Lambda^{-2k}$ for integers $k\ge2$
\citep{WangIsolatedAudit2026}.  The hat is part of the model type.  It is not
an interval enclosure for the physical root.

For an arbitrary clock base $\Lambda_c>1$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:clock-and-phase}
 \sigma_k=\Lambda_c^{-2k},
 \qquad
 \eta_k=k-\frac{\log(1/\sigma_k)}{2\log\lambda}
 =k\left(1-\frac{\log\Lambda_c}{\log\lambda}\right).
\end{equation}
Only $\Lambda_c=\lambda$ has bounded phase.  This observation must be proved
exactly for a proposed algebraic comparator; closeness of printed decimals is
irrelevant.

\begin{theorem}[Exact RH-329 clock mismatch]\label{thm:clock-mismatch}
The polynomial $p$ has one positive root $\lambda\in(1,2)$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exact-residual}
 p(\wh\Lambda)=
 \frac{5765081705833725291502719395827}
 {1953125000000000000000000000000000000000000000}>0.
\end{equation}
Consequently $\wh\Lambda>\lambda$, and on the RH-329 clock
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eta-minus-infinity}
 \eta_k=k\left(1-\frac{\log\wh\Lambda}{\log\lambda}\right)
 \longrightarrow-\infty.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We have $p(1)=-11$, $p(2)=8$, and
\begin{equation*}
 p'(x)=3x^2+8x=x(3x+8)>0\qquad(x>0).
\end{equation*}
Thus $p$ has a unique positive root in $(1,2)$.  Direct rational evaluation
at \eqref{eq:Lambda-hat} gives \eqref{eq:exact-residual}.  Since
$1<\wh\Lambda<2$ and $p$ is strictly increasing there,
$\wh\Lambda>\lambda$.  The coefficient of $k$ in
\eqref{eq:eta-minus-infinity} is therefore strictly negative.
\end{proof}

High-precision substitution gives the diagnostic values
\begin{align}
 \lambda&=1.6785735104283222651037051293\ldots,\nonumber\\
 \wh\Lambda-\lambda&=1.3489629487069342\ldots\times10^{-16},\nonumber\\
 1-\frac{\log\wh\Lambda}{\log\lambda}
 &=-1.5515885691166900\ldots\times10^{-16}.
 \label{eq:diagnostics}
\end{align}
These decimals reproduce the size of the drift; only
\eqref{eq:exact-residual} certifies its sign.

\section{Off-phase parity--alias trichotomy}

Retain the physical constants and packet laws of RH-326--RH-328
\citep{WangFirstAlias2026,WangMatching2026}.  Namely,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:constants}
 r_H=\frac{17}{20},\qquad R=\frac75,\qquad
 \beta=\frac1{r_H\sqrt\lambda},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-laws}
 \delta_\sigma=C_*\sqrt\sigma+o(\sqrt\sigma),
 \qquad
 \beta_k=\beta\exp\left[-\frac{\log C_M}{2k}+o(k^{-1})\right],
 \qquad C_*,C_M>0.
\end{equation}
At even order $2k$, the positive parity and alias packets are
\begin{align}
 \cP_k^{\rm route}
 &=r_H^{-2k}\{1-(1-\delta_{\sigma_k})^{2k}\},
 \label{eq:route-parity}\\
 \cA_k^{\rm route}
 &=(2k-2)\beta_k^{2k}+2\beta^{2k}.
 \label{eq:route-alias}
\end{align}
Their signs in the five-slot coefficient are $+\cP-\cA$.  The target scale
is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:target}
 \Hk=kR^{-2k}.
\end{equation}

The bounded-phase ratio theorem of RH-326 cannot be cited when
$|\eta_k|\to\infty$.  The next result instead starts from its uniform
binomial inequality, valid for every $k$ and $0\le\delta<1$.

\begin{theorem}[Off-phase clock trichotomy]\label{thm:trichotomy}
For every fixed $\Lambda_c>1$ and the clock
$\sigma_k=\Lambda_c^{-2k}$,
\begin{align}
 \cP_k^{\rm route}
 &=2kC_*r_H^{-2k}\Lambda_c^{-k}\{1+o(1)\},
 \label{eq:P-asymptotic}\\
 \cA_k^{\rm route}
 &=\frac{2k}{C_M}r_H^{-2k}\lambda^{-k}\{1+o(1)\},
 \label{eq:A-asymptotic}\\
 \frac{\cP_k^{\rm route}}{\cA_k^{\rm route}}
 &=C_*C_M\left(\frac{\lambda}{\Lambda_c}\right)^k
 \{1+o(1)\}.
 \label{eq:ratio-trichotomy}
\end{align}
Hence the alias packet dominates exponentially if $\Lambda_c>\lambda$,
a finite nonzero leading balance is possible if $\Lambda_c=\lambda$, and the
parity packet dominates exponentially if $1<\Lambda_c<\lambda$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On the stated clock,
\begin{equation*}
 \delta_{\sigma_k}=C_*\Lambda_c^{-k}\{1+o(1)\},
 \qquad k\delta_{\sigma_k}\longrightarrow0.
\end{equation*}
The uniform parity remainder from RH-326 is
\begin{equation*}
 \left|1-(1-\delta)^{2k}-2k\delta\right|
 \le k(2k-1)\delta^2.
\end{equation*}
After division by $2k\delta$, its right side is
$O(k\delta)=o(1)$, proving \eqref{eq:P-asymptotic}.  The multiplier law gives
\begin{equation*}
 \beta_k^{2k}=\frac{\beta^{2k}}{C_M}\{1+o(1)\}.
\end{equation*}
Since $\beta^{2k}=r_H^{-2k}\lambda^{-k}$, the $2\beta^{2k}$ endpoint term is
lower by a factor $O(k^{-1})$, and \eqref{eq:A-asymptotic} follows.  Division
proves \eqref{eq:ratio-trichotomy}; the three cases follow from the strict
ordering of positive clock bases.
\end{proof}

\section{Failure of the hatted fixed-phase comparator}

The RH-329 comparator defines the exact model constants
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hatted-constants}
 \wh C_M=\frac{9731714526004839}{5000000000000000},
 \qquad
 \wh C_* =\frac{26314633984227}{250000000000000}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hatted-packets}
 \wh\beta^2=\frac1{r_H^2\wh\Lambda},
 \quad
 \wh\cA_k=\left(\frac{2k-2}{\wh C_M}+2\right)\wh\beta^{2k},
 \quad
 \wh\cP_k=r_H^{-2k}
 \left\{1-(1-\wh C_*\wh\Lambda^{-k})^{2k}\right\}.
\end{equation}
Again, these are model definitions, not physical interval data.  Compare
only the parity--alias scalar, with the RH-330 actual-minus-model sign
convention:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:D-def}
 D_k=(\cP_k^{\rm route}-\wh\cP_k)
 -(\cA_k^{\rm route}-\wh\cA_k),
 \qquad \sigma_k=\wh\Lambda^{-2k}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[RH-329 parity--alias comparator obstruction]
\label{thm:comparator-obstruction}
On the RH-329 clock,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:D-over-A}
 \frac{D_k}{\cA_k^{\rm route}}\longrightarrow-1.
\end{equation}
Moreover,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A-and-D-over-H}
 \frac{\cA_k^{\rm route}}{\Hk}\longrightarrow+\infty,
 \qquad
 \frac{D_k}{\Hk}\longrightarrow-\infty.
\end{equation}
Thus RH-329 is not a physical bounded-phase parity--alias comparator.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By \cref{thm:trichotomy} and $\wh\Lambda>\lambda$,
\begin{equation*}
 \cP_k^{\rm route}
 =O\!\left(k r_H^{-2k}\wh\Lambda^{-k}\right)
 =o(\cA_k^{\rm route}).
\end{equation*}
The same uniform binomial upper bound and the exact formulas
\eqref{eq:hatted-packets} give
\begin{equation*}
 \wh\cP_k+\wh\cA_k
 =O\!\left(k r_H^{-2k}\wh\Lambda^{-k}\right)
 =o(\cA_k^{\rm route}).
\end{equation*}
Expanding \eqref{eq:D-def} therefore leaves
$D_k=-\cA_k^{\rm route}\{1+o(1)\}$, proving
\eqref{eq:D-over-A}.

Next, \eqref{eq:A-asymptotic} and \eqref{eq:target} give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A-over-H-proof}
 \frac{\cA_k^{\rm route}}{\Hk}
 =\frac{2}{C_M}\left(\frac{R^2}{r_H^2\lambda}\right)^k
 \{1+o(1)\}
 =\frac{2}{C_M}(\beta R)^{2k}\{1+o(1)\}.
\end{equation}
The exact certificate $\beta R>1$ is established in RH-336
\citep{WangProjectorMass2026}, so the first limit in
\eqref{eq:A-and-D-over-H} follows.  Multiplying it by
\eqref{eq:D-over-A} proves the second.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Strict scope of the defect]
$D_k$ contains only the parity and alias replacement defects.  It is not the
complete signed replacement aggregate
$\Delta\mathcal B+\Delta\mathcal S+\Delta\mathcal R+
\Delta\mathcal P-\Delta\mathcal A$, not the actual five-slot coefficient,
and not a full-trace residual.  The boundary, shell, and far slots could
still contribute at the same or larger scale.  In addition, the sharp
transfer theorem of RH-330 assumes one common bounded-phase clock
\citep{WangTransfer2026}; \cref{thm:clock-mismatch} proves that hypothesis
false for the RH-329 clock.  We therefore do not use that theorem to promote
\eqref{eq:A-and-D-over-H}.
\end{remark}

\section{Correct-clock target-resolution barrier}

It is natural to replace $\wh\Lambda$ by the exact physical $\lambda$.  On
that corrected clock, \cref{thm:trichotomy} removes the exponential
parity--alias mismatch, but it does not prove target-scale replacement.

\begin{proposition}[Available asymptotics do not test the target remainder]
\label{prop:not-testable}
On the correct clock $\sigma_k=\lambda^{-2k}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:H-over-A}
 \frac{\Hk}{\cA_k^{\rm route}}
 =\frac{C_M}{2}(\beta R)^{-2k}\{1+o(1)\}.
\end{equation}
Consequently a relative parity--alias replacement estimate sufficient for an
$o(\Hk)$ scalar defect must be
\begin{equation}\label{eq:required-precision}
 o\!\left(\frac{\Hk}{\cA_k^{\rm route}}\right)
 =o\!\left((\beta R)^{-2k}\right).
\end{equation}
The archived laws \eqref{eq:physical-laws} provide only relative $o(1)$
remainders.  They neither imply nor refute \eqref{eq:required-precision}; the
correct-clock physical replacement is therefore \texttt{NOT\_TESTABLE} from
the present inputs.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Equation \eqref{eq:H-over-A} is the reciprocal of
\eqref{eq:A-over-H-proof}.  Since $\beta R>1$, the right side tends to zero
exponentially.  A bare relative $o(1)$ remainder has no specified rate and
does not imply an exponentially weighted little-oh estimate.  No lower
bound in the archived source proves that such an estimate is impossible,
so only the stated insufficiency verdict is justified.
\end{proof}

This barrier is the same target-resolution issue isolated for the physical
shell in RH-327 and the matching equation in RH-328
\citep{WangShellBudget2026,WangMatching2026}.  It must not be replaced by a
finite fit of $C_*$, $C_M$, or $\lambda$.

\section{Reproduction protocol and claim boundary}

The executable artifact evaluates the polynomial residual, hatted constants,
finite model packets, and binomial enclosures with exact rational arithmetic.
It separately prints high-precision clock diagnostics.  The finite rows
$k\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ reproduce exact formulas only; they do not certify any
moving-order physical asymptotic.  The tests lock the exact root ordering,
model types, packet bounds, sign convention, target barrier, and the complete
claim firewall.

The maximum conclusion is scoped: the RH-329 hatted clock is off the physical
bounded-phase route, and its scalar parity--alias comparison has the limits
in \cref{thm:comparator-obstruction}.  No result here estimates the physical
boundary/shell aggregate or signed far remainder, proves off-alias
convergence, transports the noisy head, glues a determinant, or proves actual
full-trace divergence.  No Hilbert--Polya operator is constructed, no
Riemann zero or von Mangoldt trace is identified, no completed-zeta divisor
equality is proved, and no implication for the Riemann hypothesis is
asserted.  Gates A--E remain false/open.
