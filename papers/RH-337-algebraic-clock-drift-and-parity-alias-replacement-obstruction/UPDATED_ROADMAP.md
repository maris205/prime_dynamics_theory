# Roadmap after RH-337

RH-337 removes the RH-329 exact-rational family from the physical
bounded-phase replacement route.  Its model clock is strictly faster than
the physical clock:

    Lambda_hat > lambda,
    eta_k -> -infinity.

On that off-phase clock, the physical alias packet dominates both the
physical parity packet and the two hatted model packets.  The resulting
parity--alias scalar defect is asymptotic to minus the physical alias packet.
This is not a full-trace theorem because boundary, shell, far, and off-alias
terms remain outside D_k.

The target-scale parity--alias route can reopen only with one of the
following repository-backed inputs:

1. rigorous remainder estimates for delta_sigma and the boundary-cycle
   multiplier on the exact physical clock, with relative error
   o((beta R)^(-2k));
2. a physical interval/certificate for an alternative common bounded-phase
   comparator together with the same target-scale remainder;
3. a genuine signed physical obstruction in the corrected RH-334 five-slot
   observation type.

Ordinary relative o(1), rounded agreement of constants, finite fitted rows,
or the RH-329 graded model do not reopen this route.

The next independent admissible coordinate is the RH-338 signed far slot.
It must estimate the already aggregated corrected RH-334 quantity

    Delta_R,k

on the exact physical clock.  Separate absolute bounds for microscopic
pieces are sufficient but not required; conversely, a finite orbit table or
an unidentified model tail is not evidence.  A negative paper requires a
real nonvanishing physical periodic-orbit family or another exact physical
obstruction.  If neither exists in the repository, the coordinate remains
NOT_TESTABLE and no far-remainder theorem may be advertised.

No result closes the off-alias background, noisy-head/counterloop budget,
determinant gluing, or Gates A--E.
