# TPC-210 Paper Plan

## Question

Do Schwartz regularity, fixed-divisor Poisson reindexing, and literal Mobius
signs alone impose enough cross-divisor structure on the TPC-209 dual profiles
to yield a nonprincipal-character saving?

## Claim ceiling

The paper will prove finite profile interpolation and an exact Mobius-weighted
aligned family. It will not claim that the constructed independent packets
are the full physical twin-prime coefficient family.

## Theorem sequence

1. Define the residue profile map `F -> B_q`.
2. Prove surjectivity onto `C^(F_q^*)` using isolated compactly supported
   Fourier bumps.
3. Apply the construction independently for a finite divisor family.
4. Insert `c_D=mu(D)` and `B_D=mu(D)U_D^*z` to obtain exact alignment.
5. Express every profile-aware energy as a cross-divisor positive-semidefinite
   Gram quadratic form and identify the missing theorem.

## Experiments

The certificate replays the construction at five small prime moduli. The
independent checker does not import the producer. A separate sanity script
checks the support-isolation geometry and exact zero interpolation error.

## Route decision

uses only the admissible profile class without a physical cross-divisor
