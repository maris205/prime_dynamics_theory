# Roadmap after RH-297

A head/counterloop or full-trace/counterloop theorem tied to the natural
endpoint rank cannot stop at pre-alias moments.  It must either:

1. match the exact alias impulses inside the moving prefix;
2. renormalize the counterloop factor before absolute summation; or
3. use a direct aggregate norm in which the cancellations are retained.

The RH-16 rank is not asserted to equal the noisy spectral-head cardinality.
