# RH-153: Congruence-Covariant Reset Transport

RH-153 separates two ways of transporting reset data.  If the reduced Gram
and tail are carried together through the same inverse-overlap congruence,
Loewner dominance and the generalized tail ratio are exactly invariant.  The
normalized Gram base loses at most one overlap condition factor.

All 120 frozen transitions retain a positive correlated transported-base
lower; the minimum is `2.2304e-7`.  By contrast, decoupling packet, Gram, and
overlap balls before the inverse congruence certifies positivity on only
68/120 transitions.  The 52 failures are information losses, not failures of
the correlated reset pair.
