# Roadmap after RH-153

The large inverse-overlap factors do not damage a Gram--tail pair when both
members are transported by the same congruence: the relative tail bound is
exactly invariant.  They do damage an audit that first separates frame,
matrix, and inverse-map balls; that route loses 52 of 120 positivity gates.

1. **RH-154: delayed reset suffix.** Locate the finite overlap and base
   bottlenecks, prove finite-prefix invariance for congruence-covariant reset
   pairs, and identify the strongest common suffix.
2. **RH-155: native reset tail assembly.** Construct recent Gram and memory
   tail on the reset packet itself so that the paired Loewner relation is
   available before transport.
3. **RH-156: reset support cocycle.** Combine the paired tail ratio with the
   correlated transported-base lower.

Independent inverse-congruence radii are retained as a diagnostic only.  No
all-level reset law, Stage A theorem, Hilbert--Polya operator, zero
identification, or Riemann Hypothesis result is asserted.
