# Roadmap after the corpus synthesis

The corpus is now easier to read without changing its mathematical status.
The next work item is not another reparameterisation of the closed
deterministic terminal-lag sequence.  The synthesis leaves one narrow active
frontier:

```text
actual_same_clock_unnormalized_head_transport_open
```

An admissible numbered continuation requires at least one of:

1. a theorem proving the physical, same-clock, unnormalised
   `D_(4k)(R) -> 0` budget;
2. a genuine physical obstruction to that budget;
3. a typed actual full-trace `q` or complete `E_off` aggregate theorem on the
   same clock;
4. a complete unnormalised direct-prefix theorem, including the orders below
   the RH-354 moving cut; or
5. the RH-241 moving noisy all-order envelope together with a valid
   coefficient bridge.

Finite tables, synthetic spectra, abstract coefficient fibres, conditional
criteria, alternate names for `q`, and normalisation changes do not reopen a
numbered route.  New ideas should therefore be screened by a short source
lock and adversarial audit before any writer is started.

If a future theorem edge appears, it should be written as a new numbered
paper and then linked back to this synthesis.  If no edge appears, this
document remains the stable corpus map while research pauses or changes to a
new, explicitly declared model.
