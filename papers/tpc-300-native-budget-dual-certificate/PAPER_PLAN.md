# TPC-300 paper plan

## Title

Native budget dual certificates: exact ridge/KKT reciprocity and a rational
supporting-hyperplane atlas.

## Research question

Can the finite native-profile budget frontier from TPC-299 be certified by a
target-space dual object that is independent of the primal optimizer, and can
the parameter convention between ridge regularization and the KKT multiplier
be made unambiguous?

## Claim-bearing contributions

1. Prove the weak dual formula for every positive ridge parameter.
2. Prove strong duality and equality at the active frontier, with mu=1/rho.
3. Compile the frozen TPC-299 ridge intervals to exact rational rho values
   and exact rational dual lower bounds.
4. Rebuild all physical matrices source-first and independently replay 72
   certificates on the inherited finite grid.

## Deliberate boundary

The result is finite and restricted to the literal profile family.  It does
not supply a growing-shell budget theorem, arithmetic L2, fixed-power credit,
Gate B, or a twin-prime conclusion.
