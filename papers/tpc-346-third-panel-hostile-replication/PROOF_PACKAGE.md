# TPC-346 proof package

## Proposition 1 — finite projection identity

For a finite matrix N, let P be the orthogonal projector onto its column
space. Every finite target Y satisfies

```text
||Y||_2^2 = ||PY||_2^2 + ||(I-P)Y||_2^2.
```

The terms are orthogonal because P is self-adjoint and idempotent. The
producer and independent checker test the numerical decomposition gap for
each panel, shared model, adaptive model, pairwise transfer, and fresh LOO.

## Proposition 2 — nested panel model

Let N_1,N_2,N_3 be finite panel matrices. The shared span is contained in
the block-adaptive span because

```text
N_shared c = N_adapt(c,c,c).
```

The projection theorem then gives the non-increasing residual norm claimed in
the derivation package. This identity is exact finite linear algebra.

## Proposition 3 — finite hostile certificate

Under the hash-locked TPC-340 source/operator and the declared three-panel
protocol, the certificate contains nine rows and 324 raw records. The fresh
panel pooled residual retentions are 0.3159173453264 (raw) and
0.3294074740697 (equal-row). The three-panel adaptive retentions are
0.2999630725662 and 0.3222362713305, respectively. All 18 fresh
control-LOO retentions and all leave-one-panel-out prediction retentions
exceed 0.30.

These are numerically certified finite observations. The word “hostile”
describes the predeclared holdout protocol, not a universal impossibility
theorem.

## Explicit non-implications

Nothing here proves a source-uniform arithmetic L2 estimate, a uniform
masked operator bound, a fixed-power saving, payment of the strict 1/400
endpoint, a canonical nuisance law, an official Route-A/Route-B pass, or the
twin-prime conjecture.
