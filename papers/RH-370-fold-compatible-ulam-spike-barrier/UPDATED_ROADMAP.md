# Roadmap after RH-370

RH-370 closes only the fold-compatible finite quotient and the natural
deterministic BV projection barrier.  It does not close the general RH-367
Ulam route.

The next admissible candidates are:

1. the RH-366 distance-two capacity: exact eight-site run reduction and a
   rigorous pair-ledger obstruction (RH-371 candidate);
2. a new fractional/tower-adapted strong space with a uniform deterministic
   projector and a contour around `-1`;
3. a theorem for non-mirror, band-aligned or phase-shifted partitions;
4. the remaining nonadaptive-measure, primitive-divisor, radius, and original
   physical routes.

The first item is independent of the Ulam quotient and should be evaluated
before any reparameterization of this paper.  A finite phase scan, a fitted
noise exponent, or repeating the quotient algebra is not a new trigger.
