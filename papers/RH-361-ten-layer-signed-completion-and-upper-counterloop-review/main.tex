\begin{abstract}
We audit RH-352--RH-360 and prove a typed separation theorem for the batch.
RH-352--RH-354 are actual results for the direct coefficient
$p=\tau-a$ and actual signed completion remainders, but their closure
statements remain selected and normalized.  RH-355--RH-360 are unconditional
results for the deterministic graded counterloop and its normalized budgets;
every actual-head inheritance statement in those papers assumes the same
unproved, unnormalized, same-clock hypothesis
\[
 D_{4k}(R)=\sum_{2\le n<4k}
 \frac{|h_{\sigma,n}-s_{k,n}|R^n}{n}\longrightarrow0.
\]
The exact source identities are
\[
 p_n=q_n-d_n,\qquad d_n=h_n-s_n.
\]
We isolate their coefficient fiber: for fixed $p$ and $s$, every signed
array $e$ gives $d=e$, $q=p+e$, and $h=s+e$.  Thus $p$ and $s$ alone do not
determine $q$, $h$, or their weighted budgets.  This is an algebraic
information-class theorem, not a construction of noisy operators.  It
forbids promotion of RH-354's normalized $p$ tail to the full-trace
$q/E_{\rm off}$ ledger without defect transport, and forbids interpreting
the exponentially large deterministic $s$ budgets as actual spectral
submultisets.  The deterministic terminal-lag route is closed at its
declared budget type through the transform phase diagram:
RH-358 gives geometric localization, RH-359 gives logarithmic inverse
accuracy, and RH-360 gives the three-regime exponential-transform phase
diagram.  The nine upstream result files contain $45/45$ false Gate values
and $129/129$ false forbidden-claim values.  RH-241 and RH-288 remain
open/inactive, Gates A--E remain false, and no Hilbert--Polya, zero, divisor,
or RH conclusion follows.
\end{abstract}

\section{The two exact coefficient types}

The batch uses one bounded-phase physical clock and the Hardy normalization
fixed upstream.  The actual modulus-complement trace and deterministic
numerator anchor define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p-tau-a}
 p_{\sigma,k,n}=\tau_{\sigma,n}-a_n.
\end{equation}
RH-340 identifies the same coefficient with the difference between a Hardy
full-trace coefficient and the head/counterloop defect
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p-q-d}
 \boxed{p_{\sigma,k,n}=q_{\sigma,k,n}-d_{\sigma,k,n}},
 \qquad
 \boxed{d_{\sigma,k,n}=h_{\sigma,n}-s_{k,n}}.
\end{equation}
Here $h_{\sigma,n}$ is the moment of the actual modulus-complete Hardy head,
while $s_{k,n}$ is the moment of the deterministic graded counterloop
\citep{WangSynchronization2026,WangUpperBurden2026}.  Thus, suppressing the
common clock,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fiber-identities}
 q=p+d,\qquad h=s+d.
\end{equation}
Equations \eqref{eq:p-q-d}--\eqref{eq:fiber-identities} are identities of
coefficient arrays.  They do not assert a spectral inclusion between the
counterloop and the actual head.

The deterministic counterloop itself is the finite multiset
\begin{equation}\label{eq:counterloop}
 \cY_k=\{\beta_ke^{ij\pi/k},\beta_ke^{-ij\pi/k}:1\le j\le k-1\},
\end{equation}
whose exact moments are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:s-ledger}
 s_{k,n}=\beta_k^n\bigl(2k\one_{2k\mid n}-1-(-1)^n\bigr).
\end{equation}
It is a source-locked auxiliary graded shell.  The actual head
$\cH_\sigma$ is a different object.  Even the conditional moment transport
derived later in the batch is not a theorem that \eqref{eq:counterloop} is a
submultiset of $\cH_\sigma$.

\section{The actual selected and normalized branch}

Fix
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-scales}
 H_m=mR^{-2m},\qquad x=(\beta R)^2>1,
 \qquad m_{k,j}=k-j,\quad 2\le j\le J_k,
\end{equation}
where $J_k\to\infty$ and $J_k=o(k)$.  RH-352 uses the exact actual
coefficient identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p-YPS}
 p_{k,j}=Y_{k,j}+\cP_{k,j}-S_{k,j}
\end{equation}
and the all-order source caps for $\tau$ and $a$ to prove
\begin{equation}\label{eq:RH352-cap}
 \limsup_{k\to\infty}
 \left(\sup_{2\le j\le J_k}
 \frac{|p_{k,j}|}{2H_{m_{k,j}}x^{m_{k,j}}}\right)^{1/k}
 \le\max\left\{\frac{r_H^2\lambda^3}{4},\frac1\lambda\right\}<1.
\end{equation}
Solving \eqref{eq:p-YPS} then makes the actual $Y$ array track
$S-\cP$ uniformly at the same local natural scale.  This is a genuine
moving-order actual theorem.  Removing the factor $x^{m}$ is not allowed:
the separate noisy bound then has certified root
\begin{equation}\label{eq:raw-barrier}
 \lambda^2(q_0R)^2>\frac{9604}{7225}>1,
 \qquad q_0=\frac12.
\end{equation}
The inequality is a failure of that absolute-majorant method, not a lower
bound for the signed difference $p$.

RH-353 treats the two boundary coordinates excluded from the RH-352 ladder.
For the actual normalized signed remainders
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Z}
 Z_k^0=\frac{C_MY_k^0}{2H_kx^k},\qquad
 Z_k^-=\frac{C_MY_k^-}{2H_{k-1}x^{k-1}},
\end{equation}
it proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:boundary-gap}
 Z_k^0-\lambda Z_k^-\longrightarrow2-\lambda,
 \qquad
 \liminf\max\{|Z_k^0|,|Z_k^-|\}
 \ge\frac{2-\lambda}{1+\lambda}>\frac19.
\end{equation}
The resulting selected unnormalized $Y$ supply cancels the leading packets;
it does not imply divergence of either direct coefficient.

Finally RH-354 removes parity and order restrictions above a moving cut.  If
$N_k=2k-L_k$ and $L_k=o(k)$, its actual bottom-normalized direct tail
\begin{equation}\label{eq:W}
 \mathfrak W_k=x^{-N_k/2}\sum_{n\ge N_k}|p_{\sigma,k,n}|R^n
\end{equation}
has a subunit root ceiling.  The corresponding alias-normalized logarithmic
tail
\begin{equation}\label{eq:direct-log-tail}
 x^{-k}\sum_{n\ge N_k}\frac{|p_{\sigma,k,n}|R^n}{n}
\end{equation}
also decays.  Equations \eqref{eq:W}--\eqref{eq:direct-log-tail} are the
broadest actual direct theorem in the batch, but their object is still $p$,
not the full-trace coefficient $q$ in \eqref{eq:p-q-d}.

\section{The deterministic upper-counterloop branch}

Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:x-y}
 y_k=(\beta_kR)^2
 =x\exp\left[-\frac{\log C_M}{k}+o(k^{-1})\right].
\end{equation}
For $1\le L\le k-1$, the exact first-alias and post-alias budgets are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A-B}
 A_k=(1-k^{-1})y_k^k,\qquad
 B_k(L)=\sum_{j=1}^{L}\frac{y_k^{k+j}}{k+j}.
\end{equation}
RH-355--RH-357 determine \eqref{eq:A-B} on every depth scale.  In particular,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:complete-band}
 B_k(L)=
 \frac{x^{k+L+1}(1-x^{-L})}
 {C_M^{1+L/k}(k+L)(x-1)}(1+o(1))
\end{equation}
uniformly over the complete strict upper band.  At $L=k-1$, this gives the
RH-355 burden
\begin{equation}\label{eq:upper-burden}
 B_k(k-1)\sim\frac{x^{2k}}{2C_M^2k(x-1)}.
\end{equation}
These are exact asymptotics for the deterministic moments
\eqref{eq:s-ledger}.  Their exponential size does not identify an actual
head moment or actual spectral atom.

RH-358 reverses the upper band.  With $C_k=B_k(k-1)$ and terminal-lag
probability
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pi}
 \pi_k(r)=\frac{y_k^{2k-1-r}/(2k-1-r)}{C_k},
 \qquad0\le r\le k-2,
\end{equation}
it proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:geometric}
 \left\|\pi_k-(1-x^{-1})x^{-r}\right\|_{\ell^1}\longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
RH-359 inverts the terminal tail $E_k(q)$: for
$q_k=\lfloor a\log_xk+c\rfloor$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:log-phase}
 k^aE_k(q_k)=x^{\{a\log_xk+c\}-c}(1+o(1)),
\end{equation}
and the minimal admissible window differs from $a\log_xk+c$ by a sequence
with complete limit set $[0,1]$.

RH-360 completes the deterministic route with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G}
 G_k(z)=\sum_{r=0}^{k-2}z^r\pi_k(r).
\end{equation}
For fixed $z<x$, the transform tends to
$(1-x^{-1})/(1-z/x)$.  In the critical window
$z_k=x\exp(\tau/k)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:critical-transform}
 \frac{G_k(z_k)}{k}\longrightarrow
 2(1-x^{-1})\int_0^1
 \frac{e^{(\tau+\log C_M)s}}{2-s}\,ds,
\end{equation}
while for fixed $z>x$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:super-transform}
 G_k(z)\sim
 \frac{2C_M(1-x^{-1})}{1-x/z}\left(\frac zx\right)^{k-2}.
\end{equation}
Thus the deterministic terminal-lag sequence closes through its entire
transform phase diagram.  The tilted laws remain normalized budget laws,
not eigenvalue distributions or noisy stochastic laws.

\section{The typed coefficient-fiber theorem}

The exact reason that the two branches do not glue is algebraic before it is
analytic.

\begin{definition}[finite typed coefficient information class]
Let $I$ be a nonempty finite order set and let $w_n>0$.  Fix two signed
arrays $(p_n)_{n\in I}$ and $(s_n)_{n\in I}$.  A typed coefficient fiber is
an array $(e_n)_{n\in I}$ together with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fiber}
 d_n^{[e]}=e_n,\qquad
 q_n^{[e]}=p_n+e_n,\qquad
 h_n^{[e]}=s_n+e_n.
\end{equation}
No operator, determinant, rank, root set, or cross-order realizability
condition is included in this information class.
\end{definition}

For an array $u$, write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weighted-norm}
 \|u\|_{1,w}=\sum_{n\in I}w_n|u_n|.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[exact typed fiber and nonpromotion]\label{thm:fiber}
Every array $e$ in \eqref{eq:fiber} satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fiber-exact}
 d^{[e]}=h^{[e]}-s,\qquad
 p=q^{[e]}-d^{[e]}
\end{equation}
coordinatewise.  If $v$ is any nonzero signed array, then along
$e^{(t)}=tv$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unbounded-fibers}
 \|q^{[e^{(t)}]}\|_{1,w}\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad
 \|h^{[e^{(t)}]}\|_{1,w}\longrightarrow\infty
 \quad (|t|\to\infty),
\end{equation}
while $p$ and $s$ remain fixed.  Moreover, the choices $e=-p$ and $e=-s$
give $q^{[-p]}=0$ and $h^{[-s]}=0$, respectively.  Consequently, as $e$
ranges over this coefficient information class, neither
$\|q^{[e]}\|_{1,w}$ nor $\|h^{[e]}\|_{1,w}$ has a finite uniform upper
bound determined by $p,s$, while the infimum of each norm is zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Substitution of \eqref{eq:fiber} gives
$h^{[e]}-s=e=d^{[e]}$ and
$q^{[e]}-d^{[e]}=(p+e)-e=p$.  Since $v\ne0$ and all weights are positive,
$\|tv\|_{1,w}=|t|\|v\|_{1,w}\to\infty$.  The reverse triangle inequality
gives
\[
 \|p+tv\|_{1,w}\ge |t|\|v\|_{1,w}-\|p\|_{1,w},
 \qquad
 \|s+tv\|_{1,w}\ge |t|\|v\|_{1,w}-\|s\|_{1,w},
\]
which proves \eqref{eq:unbounded-fibers}.  Finally,
$q^{[-p]}=p-p=0$ and $h^{[-s]}=s-s=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[logical scope]
The theorem does not say that every fiber is realized by a noisy quadratic
operator.  It says that the coefficient identities and the fixed arrays
$p,s$ do not by themselves imply the missing conclusion.  A physical
promotion requires an additional theorem restricting the actual defect
$d=h-s$.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[two forbidden promotions]\label{cor:forbidden}
The following deductions are unavailable from the batch premises alone.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A small selected or normalized actual direct budget for $p$ cannot be
promoted to the full-trace $q/E_{\rm off}$ budget without a same-clock bound
for $d$.
\item A large deterministic counterloop budget for $s$ cannot be promoted to
an actual-head budget, root set, rank law, or spectral-submultiset statement.
\end{enumerate}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The two budget nonpromotions follow from \cref{thm:fiber}: the first uses
$q=p+d$, and the second uses $h=s+d$.  The further $E_{\rm off}$, root,
rank, and multiset deductions are not encoded by the fiber premises and are
absent from the source-locked table.  They require a separate realization
or identification theorem that is unavailable in the batch.
\end{proof}

\section{The batch separation theorem}

\begin{theorem}[RH-352--RH-361 typed batch separation]
\label{thm:batch}
The ten layers satisfy all of the following.
\begin{enumerate}
\item RH-352--RH-354 give unconditional actual conclusions only for selected
natural/alias normalizations of $p$ and $Y$.  RH-353 also converts its
normalized gap into a selected signed-$Y$ supply, not a direct or full-prefix
lower bound.
\item RH-355--RH-360 give unconditional conclusions only for the
deterministic counterloop $s$ and normalized budgets derived from it.
\item Every actual-head transfer in RH-355--RH-360 assumes the same original
unnormalized, same-clock leaf $D_{4k}(R)\to0$.  No paper in the batch proves
that leaf.
\item The deterministic terminal-lag route is complete through
\eqref{eq:critical-transform}--\eqref{eq:super-transform}, but no actual
root, rank, spectrum, determinant, $q$, or $E_{\rm off}$ conclusion follows.
\item RH-241's moving noisy all-order envelope and coefficient bridge remain
open, and RH-288 remains inactive.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Items 1--3 follow from the exact object and hypothesis columns in the
source-locked table and from the defining statements of the nine upstream
papers.  The distinction is also enforced by
\eqref{eq:p-q-d}: RH-354 controls $p$, whereas the full-trace ledger uses
$q=p+d$.  By \cref{thm:fiber,cor:forbidden}, neither $q$ nor the actual head
is determined without a theorem for $d$.

Item 4 is the chain RH-358--RH-360 summarized in
\eqref{eq:geometric}--\eqref{eq:super-transform}.  The sources explicitly
define these probabilities from normalized deterministic absolute budget
weights, so no spectral interpretation is licensed.  Item 5 is the retained
frontier of RH-241 and the typed prefix requirement of RH-288
\citep{WangTraceFrontier2026,WangGluing2026,WangSignedReview2026}.
\end{proof}

\section{Executable audit and claim boundary}

The executable artifact reads the nine upstream result ledgers and checks
the exact forbidden-claim vector
\begin{equation}\label{eq:false-counts}
 (15,14,13,14,14,14,14,15,16),
 \qquad \sum=129.
\end{equation}
All $129$ values are false.  Each upstream file also contains five false
Gate values, for $45/45$ false values.  RH-361 adds five false Gate values
and twenty false forbidden claims, so the ten-paper totals are $50/50$ and
$149/149$, respectively.

The finite rows instantiate \cref{thm:fiber} with rational arrays and
positive rational weights.  They check both identities and exhibit different
$q/h$ budgets at fixed $p/s$.  They are formula-reproduction checks only:
they do not construct physical heads or noisy operators and are not
asymptotic evidence.

The individual archive contains twenty RH-361 publication files.  The nine
upstream manifests contain $156$, giving $176$ files in the batch manifest.
All manifests are SHA-256 rehashed by independent verification scripts.

The narrowest positive continuation is an actual theorem proving the
unnormalized same-clock $D_{4k}(R)\to0$ leaf, or a typed direct theorem for
$q/E_{\rm off}$ that bypasses it.  An unnormalized complete direct-prefix
theorem and the low orders below the RH-354 cut remain separate.  The
RH-241 moving noisy all-order envelope and no-over-extraction bridge remain
open.  RH-288 may be activated only after the complete direct prefix and
both analytic tails close in one physical determinant data type.

Gates A--E remain false/open.  This paper constructs no Hilbert--Polya
operator, identifies no Riemann zero, proves no von Mangoldt-weighted
prime-power trace, proves no completed-zeta divisor equality, and does not
prove the Riemann Hypothesis.
