# Roadmap after RH-361

The RH-352--RH-361 batch ends with an exact type separation.

The actual branch is RH-352--RH-354.  It proves selected normalized results
for the direct coefficient

    p = tau-a = q-d

and for actual signed completion remainders `Y`.  These theorems do not prove
the unnormalized complete direct prefix, the low-order prefix below the
RH-354 moving cut, or the full-trace `q/E_off` budget.

The deterministic branch is RH-355--RH-360.  It completely resolves the
graded upper-counterloop budget from the full strict-upper asymptotic through
mesoscopic and linear depth, terminal-lag localization, logarithmic accuracy,
and the exponential-transform phase diagram.  This branch is now closed at
its declared deterministic budget type.

There is no automatic promotion between the branches.  On one clock,

    d = h-s,                 p = q-d,

so the missing defect controls both the actual head and the full-trace
coefficient.  The narrowest theorem-backed continuations are:

1. Prove the original unnormalized same-clock head transport

       D_(4k)(R)=sum_(2<=n<4k)|h_(sigma,n)-s_(k,n)|R^n/n -> 0.

   This would transfer the deterministic budget profiles conditionally
   derived in RH-355--RH-360.  It would still not identify roots, rank, or a
   spectral submultiset without separate theorems.

2. Prove a typed direct theorem for the full-trace coefficient `q` or for the
   complete `E_off` aggregate, bypassing counterloop/head transport without
   replacing `q` by RH-354's `p`.

3. Close the unnormalized complete direct prefix.  RH-354 controls a
   normalized high-order tail only; low orders and removal of the `x^(-k)`
   normalization require new cancellation or actual spectral information.

4. Reopen the RH-241 moving noisy all-order envelope and its no-over-
   extraction coefficient bridge.  The deterministic numerator anchor and
   deterministic all-order envelope do not pay this noisy obligation.

5. Activate RH-288 only after the complete direct prefix and both analytic
   tails hold in one physical determinant data type.  Gate A still separately
   requires a canonical intrinsic determinant.

Do not continue the terminal-lag sequence by merely reparameterizing the
deterministic law.  A next numbered paper requires a new actual bridge, a
typed `q/E_off` theorem, a rigorous physical obstruction, or another
independent theorem edge.

Finite tables, coefficient fibers, deterministic shells, and conditional
inheritance statements are not reopening inputs.  Gates A--E remain
false/open.  No current result constructs a Hilbert--Polya operator,
identifies Riemann zeros, proves a von Mangoldt trace, proves completed-zeta
divisor equality, or proves RH.
