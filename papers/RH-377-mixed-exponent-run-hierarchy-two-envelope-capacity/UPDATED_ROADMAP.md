# RH-377 updated roadmap

RH-377 closes the algebraic reduction of the RH-371 eight-run hierarchy.
The sixteen signed densities reduce to thirteen higher mixed aggregates,
while the adaptive capacity reduces further to the single nonlinear
envelope `(U_N+abs(V_N))/N`.  No theorem in the frozen source corpus proves
that this envelope converges.

The next admissible routes are:

1. source-lock a genuine fixed-shift mixed-exponent Möbius theorem strong
   enough to control one or more of `A_k`, `B_k`, or their alternating
   envelope; ordinary raw Chowla statements must not be substituted for
   squarefree-masked correlations;
2. attack the envelope directly, allowing cancellation among aggregate
   levels, since full coordinatewise `o(N)` is sufficient but stronger than
   the exact capacity criterion;
3. isolate the first unresolved directional block at `k=3` and determine
   whether an available logarithmic or averaged theorem yields a rigorous
   scoped rigidity result without promoting it to an ordinary Cesàro limit;
4. if no such arithmetic input exists, retain `STOP_SCOPED` rather than
   extending the finite endpoint or fitting the conditional Euler constant.

The stationary witness excludes a purely algebraic derivation in the class
of all stationary ternary processes; it does not exclude special Möbius
identities.  A successor must therefore introduce arithmetic content, not
reinterpret the synthetic chain as a Möbius model.

No current route supplies a canonical operator, von Mangoldt prime-power
trace, completed-zeta divisor equality, Riemann-zero identification,
Hilbert--Pólya construction, or RH proof.
