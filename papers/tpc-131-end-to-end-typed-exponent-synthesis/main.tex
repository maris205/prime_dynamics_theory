\begin{abstract}
The fixed-shift TPC program now has exact local certificates for
physical frames, native grouping, localization, cover, reconnection,
determinant energy, and the distinguished zero mode.  A remaining
risk is not a missing local identity but an invalid global
composition: circular hypotheses, repeated physical losses,
energy--amplitude confusion, or reuse of one saving in two ledgers.
We give an end-to-end typed synthesis theorem that excludes those
errors.  A proof-carrying route is represented by an acyclic
dependency graph with a semantic target firewall and by an
occurrence-level physical-event registry.  Squarefree truncation,
zero-mode content, and physical high-frequency tails receive
different namespaces.  If every nonsoft literal packet has uniform
raw saving \(X^{-\sigma_{\rm raw}}\), the complete soft remainder is
\(o(X)\), and the physical synthesis cost is
\(X^{\Lambda_{\rm phys}+o(1)}\), then the original hard packet is
\[
 O\!\left(X^{1-\sigma_{\rm raw}+\Lambda_{\rm phys}+o(1)}\right)
 +o(X).
\]
Thus only the strict inequality
\(\sigma_{\rm raw}>\Lambda_{\rm phys}\) closes the route.  The
MVP1 threshold \(1/400\) is a specialization.  The
determinant--zero reserve is proved to be a separate admissibility
quantity and cannot be spent at the physical endpoint.  The paper is
an \(\Lzero/\Lone\) composition theorem and audit format.  It proves
no growing coefficient-specific arithmetic estimate.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} fixed shift; typed exponent ledger;
proof DAG; physical synthesis; determinant zero mode; endpoint
certificate.

\section{Why a final type check is necessary}

TPC-113--119 isolate the physical frame, grouping, localization,
complete tail, cover, endpoint, and reconnection interfaces
\citep{WangTPC113,WangTPC114,WangTPC115,WangTPC116,WangTPC117,
WangTPC118,WangTPC119}.  TPC-121 and TPC-122 isolate sufficient
determinant and zero-mode exponent ledgers
\citep{WangTPC121,WangTPC122}.  TPC-123--130 are designed to expose a
literal native archive and one arithmetic reduction chain
\citep{WangTPC123,WangTPC124,WangTPC125,WangTPC126,WangTPC127,
WangTPC128,WangTPC129,WangTPC130}.  These pieces do not compose
merely because their displayed powers look compatible.

There are four independent failure modes.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item A premise may be a renamed form of the desired conclusion.
\item The same physical event may be charged twice, or two separate
  occurrences of one formula may be merged and undercharged.
\item An energy exponent may be entered as an amplitude exponent.
\item An arithmetic saving, determinant reserve, or soft-remainder
  estimate may be recycled as physical endpoint slack.
\end{enumerate}
We prevent these errors before stating the synthesis theorem.

\section{Typed route records and three tail namespaces}

\begin{definition}[Literal scope]
\label{def:scope}
A route scope is a record
\[
 \mathfrak s_X=(h_0,W,\Omega_X,\nu_X,\mathfrak a_X,\mathfrak q_X),
\]
where \(h_0\ne0\) is prescribed, \(W\) is the fixed physical test
weight, \(\Omega_X\) is the actual active atom set, \(\nu_X\) is the
single physical normalization, \(\mathfrak a_X\) is a content-addressed
archive manifest, and \(\mathfrak q_X\) records the quantifier range.
Two estimates may be composed only after an exact crosswalk identifies
all six fields.
\end{definition}

The manifest is not cosmetic.  A final scalar equality cannot detect
a deleted atom paired with an inserted null pair.  The path archive
and physical reassembly proposed in TPC-123 and TPC-124 are therefore
ancestors of every downstream use, not conclusions inferred from the
value of the hard packet.

\begin{definition}[Disjoint tail roles]
\label{def:tails}
The certificate language has three different namespaces:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf{H3.sqtail}:&
 \text{the squarefree-divisor truncation remainder in the
 arithmetic packet},\\
\mathsf{H5.content}:&
 \text{the omitted content term in the distinguished zero mode},\\
\mathsf{Phys.hitail}:&
 \text{the physical high, ultra, boundary, and mask complement}.
\end{array}
\]
Two records from these namespaces are never exact duplicates merely
because both are called a tail.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[No cross-namespace cancellation]
\label{prop:tails}
Without a literal joint theorem, a saving recorded in one namespace
of \cref{def:tails} supplies no bound in either of the other two.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The three objects have different domains and roles.  The first is an
upstream arithmetic remainder inside a raw packet, the second is a
zero-mode summand, and the third is a physical outer reconstruction.
An inequality for one does not contain either of the other two as a
term.  Identifying their exponents therefore changes the bounded
object.  A joint theorem may of course replace the three records, but
it must display the common literal expression and list all absorbed
dependencies.
\end{proof}

Every complete certificate also carries the exact coverage map
\[
\begin{array}{ccl}
\mathsf{H3.sqtail}&\longrightarrow&
 \text{raw packet theorem or one separate arithmetic residual},\\
\mathsf{H5.content}&\longrightarrow&
 \text{the H5 zero-mode admission record},\\
\mathsf{Phys.hitail}&\longrightarrow&
 \text{the complete original-scale soft/physical return}.
\end{array}
\]
Each literal tail occurrence must appear exactly once in this map.
Different names are not by themselves a coverage proof, and one
occurrence may not be reused in two destinations.

\section{An acyclic proof DAG with a semantic firewall}

\begin{definition}[Proof record]
\label{def:proofrecord}
A proof record is
\[
 v=(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{statement},\mathrm{scope},
    \mathrm{source},\mathrm{parents},\mathrm{level}).
\]
The directed graph has an edge \(u\to v\) when the theorem recorded at
\(v\) invokes the statement recorded at \(u\).  Every source theorem
must precede its consumer in a displayed topological order.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Semantic target firewall]
\label{def:firewall}
Primitive records may not assume any of the following, or an
equivalent reformulation:
\[
 B_{h_0,\delta}(X)=o(X),\quad
 \text{the target H3 packet saving},\quad
 D_X\ge D_X^{\rm target},\quad
 |Z_X|\le Z_X^{\rm target}.
\]
In addition:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2em]
\item archive completeness may not be inferred from final scalar
  agreement;
\item a physical residual may not be called \(o(X)\) by invoking the
  final hard-packet estimate;
\item phase participation may not be defined from \(D_X/E_X\); and
\item a bilinear reduction may not use H3 itself to bound its
  residual terms.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Acyclic certificate composition]
\label{thm:dag}
Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite proof graph whose records have one literal
scope.  Suppose:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2em]
\item \(G\) has a topological ordering;
\item each nonprimitive record is obtained by a valid invocation of
  its parent records;
\item each primitive record has an independently proved source
  theorem; and
\item the semantic firewall of \cref{def:firewall} holds.
\end{enumerate}
Then the root statement is a noncircular consequence of the primitive
records.  Conversely, a directed cycle or a target-equivalent
primitive premise invalidates this certificate, even if every local
algebraic identity is correct.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Induct along the topological order.  Primitive nodes are justified by
(c).  When all parents of a nonprimitive node have been justified,
(b) justifies that node.  This reaches the root without using a later
node.  Condition (d) prevents the conclusion from being inserted as
an apparently primitive premise.  A directed cycle has no such
induction order.  A target-equivalent primitive premise makes the
implication formally true but circular, which is excluded by
definition.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The firewall is partly semantic]
A script can reject forbidden identifiers and graph cycles, but it
cannot decide mathematical equivalence of arbitrary theorem
statements.  The proof-carrying manifest must therefore include a
human-audited statement hash and a short independence note for every
primitive record.
\end{remark}

\section{Occurrence-level physical tokens}

TPC-118 introduced a nonduplicated physical registry
\citep{WangTPC118}.  Here we refine its identifier so that a repeated
application of one map is not silently merged.

\begin{definition}[Occurrence token]
\label{def:token}
An occurrence token is
\[
 \tau=(\mathrm{occ},\mathrm{map},\mathrm{domain},\mathrm{codomain},
 \mathrm{scope},\mathrm{scale},\lambda,\mathrm{source},\mathrm{deps}).
\]
Here \(\mathrm{occ}\) is a path-and-stage identifier and
\(\mathrm{scale}\in\{\mathsf A,\mathsf E\}\) records amplitude or
energy.  The exponent \(\lambda\) is unknown until a theorem supplies
it.  A joint token replaces, rather than supplements, the occurrence
identifiers in \(\mathrm{deps}\).  Occurrence identifiers are globally
unique, independently of scale.  The dependency graph of joint tokens
must be acyclic, and the maximal retained tokens must give a disjoint
exact cover of the primitive physical occurrences.  Self-dependency,
overlapping joint covers, and retaining both a joint token and one of
its dependencies invalidate the registry.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Safe exponent transport]
\label{prop:transport}
For upper bounds on a fixed route:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2em]
\item sequential amplitude losses add;
\item an energy loss \(X^{e+o(1)}\) contributes amplitude loss
  \(e/2\);
\item an \(\ell^1\) summation over \(X^{\mu+o(1)}\) branches
  contributes \(\mu\);
\item the replacement \(\mu\mapsto\mu/2\) requires a proved
  orthogonality or square-function inequality; and
\item two occurrences of the same map remain distinct unless a
  theorem bounds their literal composition.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Items (i) and (ii) follow from products of operator norms and taking a
square root.  Item (iii) is the triangle inequality.  Cauchy--Schwarz
gives item (iv) only after a square-sum bound is available; branch
count alone is insufficient.  For (v), applying the same operator at
two stages produces a product of two events.  Equality of formulas
does not identify their occurrences.
\end{proof}

For a valid retained registry \(\mathcal R\), the canonical amplitude
loss is therefore
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}
 =
 \sum_{\tau\in\mathcal R}
 \begin{cases}
  \lambda_\tau,&\mathrm{scale}(\tau)=\mathsf A,\\[0.2em]
  \lambda_\tau/2,&\mathrm{scale}(\tau)=\mathsf E.
 \end{cases}}
\label{eq:canonical-loss}
\end{equation}
The conversion in \eqref{eq:canonical-loss} is part of registry
aggregation, not a detached numerical example.

\begin{remark}[Actual composite versus quotient envelope]
The quotient condition number of TPC-113 is a sharp uniform envelope,
not automatically the norm of the actual H9 composite.  Likewise the
TPC-114 factor \(k_{\max}\) is a squared-energy factor and does not
automatically equal a max-packet-to-scalar cost.  The registry records
only the theorem-backed literal composite and its declared norm
\citep{WangTPC113,WangTPC114}.
\end{remark}

\section{The determinant--zero ledger is separate}

TPC-121 supplies the conditional determinant exponent
\[
 \lambda_D^{\rm cert}
 =(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+,
\]
while TPC-122 supplies, when
\(\delta_{\rm pre}\ge\ell_Z\),
\[
 \eta_Z^{\rm cert}
 =\min\{\delta_{\rm pre}-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}.
\]
These are callable only when their literal growing premises have been
proved on compatible archived fibers.

\begin{proposition}[Typed H5 admission test]
\label{prop:h5}
The TPC-121/122 certificates pass the determinant--zero
compatibility gate precisely when
\[
 \boxed{
 (\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+
 \le
 2\min\{\delta_{\rm pre}-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}}
\]
and \(\delta_{\rm pre}\ge\ell_Z\).  The nonnegative number
\[
 \rho_{\rm det}
 =
 2\min\{\delta_{\rm pre}-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}
 -
 (\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+
\]
is determinant reserve, not a negative physical token.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Substitute the two admissible exponents into the exact TPC-112 cone
\(\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z\) \citep{WangTPC112}.  The cone is an
admissibility condition between a lower determinant-energy bound and
an upper zero-mode bound.  Neither quantity is an operator norm in
the physical synthesis chain.  Hence their difference cannot be
subtracted from that chain without a new literal intertwining
theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Comparison-scale invariance]
In TPC-122 a rescaling \(A_f\mapsto X^cA_f\) changes
\((\delta_{\rm pre},\ell_Z)\) to
\((\delta_{\rm pre}+c,\ell_Z+c)\).  Only
\(\delta_{\rm pre}-\ell_Z\) is invariant.  Optimizing the two entries
separately is exponent bookkeeping, not a stronger zero-mode theorem.
\end{remark}

\section{The end-to-end synthesis theorem}

Let \(\cB_X^{\rm ns}\) be the retained nonsoft packet family.  Its
cardinality is allowed to be \(X^{o(1)}\), with every error term below
uniform in \(b\).  Let \(\mathscr S_X\) be the literal physical
synthesis after all selected route maps.

\begin{theorem}[Typed fixed-shift endpoint synthesis]
\label{thm:synthesis}
Fix one route scope \(\mathfrak s_X\).  Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item a complete native archive and exact reconnection identity
\[
 B_{h_0,\delta}(X)
 =
 R_{\rm soft}(X)
 +
 \mathscr S_X\bigl(\{\mathcal P_b\}_{b\in\cB_X^{\rm ns}}\bigr);
\]
\item \(R_{\rm soft}(X)=o(X)\) after its complete original-scale
outer summation;
\item for one fixed \(\sigma_{\rm raw}\ge0\),
\[
 \max_{b\in\cB_X^{\rm ns}}|\mathcal P_b|
 \le X^{1-\sigma_{\rm raw}+o(1)};
\]
\(\sigma_{\rm raw}\) is an \emph{amplitude} saving after every
energy-to-amplitude square root and the packet normalization have
been executed; a quadratic exponent may not be inserted unchanged;
\item the H5 admission test of \cref{prop:h5}, whenever that transfer
is used by the selected packet theorem;
\item an acyclic proof certificate satisfying
\cref{thm:dag}; and
\item one complete theorem-backed occurrence registry whose
canonical loss is \eqref{eq:canonical-loss}, and an actual
\(\ell^\infty(\cB_X^{\rm ns})\to\mathbb C\) synthesis theorem with
\[
 \left|
 \mathscr S_X\bigl(\{\mathcal P_b\}\bigr)
 \right|
 \le
 X^{\Lambda_{\rm phys}+o(1)}
 \max_b|\mathcal P_b|.
 \]
This norm includes the weighted packet census, packet multiplicity,
outer coefficients, branch aggregation, and every selected physical
occurrence exactly once.
\end{enumerate}
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 |B_{h_0,\delta}(X)|
 \le
 o(X)+
 X^{1-\sigma_{\rm raw}+\Lambda_{\rm phys}+o(1)}.}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{\sigma_{\rm raw}>\Lambda_{\rm phys}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
B_{h_0,\delta}(X)=o(X).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the exact identity in (i), the triangle inequality, and (vi).
Then insert (iii):
\[
 |B_{h_0,\delta}(X)|
 \le |R_{\rm soft}(X)|
 +X^{\Lambda_{\rm phys}+o(1)}
  X^{1-\sigma_{\rm raw}+o(1)}.
\]
The complete synthesis norm in (vi), rather than the bare statement
\(|\cB_X^{\rm ns}|=X^{o(1)}\), absorbs the weighted packet census and
outer aggregation.  Assumptions (iv) and (v) certify
that neither an incompatible H5 transfer nor a circular source was
used to obtain the two displayed bounds.  If
\(\sigma_{\rm raw}-\Lambda_{\rm phys}>0\), the power term and the soft
term are both \(o(X)\).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[MVP1 endpoint]
\label{cor:mvp1}
For the MVP1 raw packet target
\(\sigma_{\rm raw}=1/400\), the strict physical condition is
\[
 \boxed{\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400.}
\]
Equality produces only \(X^{1+o(1)}\) from the power term and is not a
pass.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Substitute \(1/400\) in \cref{thm:synthesis}.  At equality the
unspecified \(o(1)\) has no controlled favorable sign.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[A reduction is not item (iii)]
The exact Liouville pullback, squarefree expansion, quantifier
firewall, and Fej\'er four-sign reduction of TPC-127--130 may
identify a sufficient arithmetic target.  They satisfy (iii) only
after the resulting growing masked four-sign forms and every
truncation residual have been proved with the required uniform
power.
\end{remark}

\section{Route certificates and first-failure output}

For each affine parameter cell \(r\), TPC-118 writes
\[
 \Lambda_r(x)=c_r^{\mathsf T}x+d_r,\qquad
 A_rx\ge b_r,\quad x\ge0,
\]
after exact dependency replacement \citep{WangTPC118}.  The
arithmetic saving \(\sigma_r\) belongs in the right side of the strict
comparison, not as a negative physical token.

\begin{proposition}[Three endpoint outcomes]
\label{prop:outcomes}
For one selected route:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2em]
\item an exact theorem-backed primal point with
  \(\Lambda_r(x)<\sigma_r\) is a strict endpoint pass;
\item an exact dual lower certificate with optimum
  \(\Lambda_r^*\ge\sigma_r\) stops that fully specified route; and
\item an unknown token, unproved constraint, missing archive, or
  unavailable upper/lower certificate is
  \textnormal{\textsc{incomplete}}.
\end{enumerate}
These states are mutually exclusive.  A stop applies only to the
selected literal route and allowed replacement menu.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first two conclusions are weak duality with threshold
\(\sigma_r\).  They cannot both hold.  Every remaining state lacks a
certificate for either strict upper compatibility or a route lower
obstruction and is therefore incomplete.
\end{proof}

The manifest should emit the first node in topological order that is
absent, conditional, or scope-incompatible.  This ``first missing''
record is diagnostic; it cannot be replaced by a later consequence.

\section{Deterministic regression and present status}

The standard-library script
\texttt{experiments/tpc131\_typed\_synthesis\_audit.py} verifies:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item an acyclic example and rejection of a deliberate cycle;
\item a primitive-source firewall and rejection of target-as-premise;
\item occurrence-level tokens and exact joint dependency replacement;
\item energy-to-amplitude conversion;
\item nonmerging of the three tail namespaces;
\item the TPC-121/122 compatibility formula;
\item strict, equality, above-threshold, and incomplete endpoint
states; and
\item refusal to label a finite identity or reduction as \(\Ltwo\).
\end{enumerate}
The numeric values are certificate-format regressions, not estimates
of any actual growing TPC coefficient.

\section{Conclusion}

The central formula is not a new arithmetic estimate:
\[
 \text{raw packet saving}
 -
 \text{physical occurrence loss}
 =
 \text{final fixed-power slack}.
\]
Its value is that both terms are now typed and proof-carrying.
Determinant compatibility, squarefree truncation, zero-mode content,
and physical tails remain in their correct namespaces.  Equality at
the physical endpoint stops the selected route, while equality in
the determinant cone is allowed with zero determinant reserve.

No statement here proves H1--H9, a fixed-\(h_0\) M\"obius or
Liouville cancellation estimate, a Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic, a
prime-pair lower bound, or the twin-prime conjecture.  The result is a
strict end-to-end interface for deciding when such inputs would
actually compose.
