# TPC-291 paper plan

## Question

When a cross-prime Gram pair is coherent, how much signed two-component
cancellation is actually available, and what sign pattern does the optimal
coefficient require?

## Claim spine

1. Define the projection coefficient `rho*=G_(i,j)/d_j`.
2. Prove the exact Schur-complement identity
   `min_rho ||g_i-rho g_j||^2/d_i=1-Gamma_(i,j)`.
3. Relate the sign of `rho*` to the nonnegative/signed support distinction.
4. Record the normalized two-vector Rayleigh minimum
   `1-sqrt(Gamma)`.
5. Build a finite coherence-to-cancellation atlas on the complete TPC-289
   pair grid.

## Claim ceiling

The projection and Rayleigh identities are exact finite Hilbert-space facts.
The threshold counts are exact-rational finite certificates.  No multi-prime
null vector, growing-shell theorem, arithmetic `L2` estimate, or twin-prime
conclusion is claimed.
