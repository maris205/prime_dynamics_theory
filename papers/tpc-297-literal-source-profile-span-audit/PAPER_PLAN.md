# TPC-297 paper plan

## Question

Does a small, literal family of Möbius/Euler source profiles enlarge the
frozen source ray enough to reach the weighted shell target?

## Claim spine

1. Define the four cutoff profiles `beta_z`, the source matrix `U`, and the
   restricted correlation matrix `V=A^T U`.
2. Prove the exact least-squares projection formula and monotonicity under
   adding profiles.
3. Certify the image rank modulo two large primes on the inherited grid.
4. Replay the weighted, max-cut, and all-positive targets at 70 digits.
5. Separate the positive all-positive control from the weighted obstruction.
6. Keep growing uniformity, arithmetic `L2`, Gate B, and the twin-prime
   endpoint open.

## Evidence matrix

| claim | evidence | status |
|---|---|---|
| projection formula | orthogonal decomposition | PROVED_EXACT_FINITE |
| nested-span monotonicity | Pythagorean identity | PROVED_EXACT_FINITE |
| finite image ranks | two modular Gaussian eliminations | NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED_FINITE |
| residual atlas | 70-digit producer plus source-first replay | NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED_FINITE |
| growing native dimension | no asymptotic input | OPEN |

## Scope

The four cutoff profiles are an explicitly declared finite model family.  The
paper does not identify this family with every admissible V59 source profile,
and it assigns no exponent credit.
