\begin{abstract}
The current TPC physical route contains two distinct identifications.
Source records first collide at the literal residual label
\[
 q(u)=(\gamma_u,j_u,s_u),\qquad s_u=d(m_u)r_u,
\]
and residual labels may then collide after passage to a native
frequency label \(\pi(q(u))\).  Treating these operations as one
informal ``grouping'' hides both their kernels and their losses.

We separate them as a weighted residual coarea map
\[
 \Res_a e_u=a_u e_{q(u)}
\]
and an unweighted native forgetting map
\(\Nat e_y=e_{\pi(y)}\).  Three exact results follow.  First,
\(dr=d'r'\) has the complete coprime parametrization
\[
 d=ga,\quad d'=gb,\quad r=bt,\quad r'=at,
 \qquad (a,b)=1.
\]
Second,
\[
 \Res_a\Res_a^*=\operatorname{diag}(W_y),
 \qquad
 W_y=\sum_{q(u)=y}|a_u|^2,
\]
so every nonzero singular value of \(\Res_a\) is exactly
\(\sqrt{W_y}\).  Third, the residual and native Grams are
\[
 G_{\rm res}=\Res_a^*\Res_a,\qquad
 G_{\rm nat}=\Res_a^*\Nat^*\Nat\Res_a,
\]
and their difference is exactly the exchange between unequal
residuals sharing one native label.  For any upstream synthesis
\(\Syn\), the complete native observation satisfies
\[
 (\Nat\Res_a\Syn)^*(\Nat\Res_a\Syn)
 =\Syn^*G_{\rm nat}\Syn.
\]

The upper \(\ell^2\)-energy factor is the largest native multiplicity,
and the corresponding \(\ell^2\)-amplitude norm is its square root.
Neither is automatically the H9 max-packet-to-scalar cost.  When a
native fiber contains at least two residual labels, no positive lower
transfer exists on the full space without excluding the cancellation
kernel: two residuals may add or cancel exactly after native
forgetting.  Thus this paper supplies an exact \(\Lzero\) algebra and
an \(\Lone\) provenance dictionary, but no growing multiplicity
bound, fixed-\(h_0\) \(\Ltwo\) estimate, parity breakthrough, or
prime-pair theorem.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Literal convention.}
Every record \(u\), coefficient \(a_u\), divisor value \(d(m_u)\),
residual coordinate \(r_u\), and surviving label is inherited.
Neither a coefficient nor a collision multiplicity may be normalized
away after the fact.  All Hilbert spaces below use their declared
counting measures.

\section{Why two groupings are necessary}

TPC-46 proves that Bohr--Fourier orthogonality retains a literal
residual label and that positivity-only localization can spend the
full alias mass \citep{WangTPC46}.  TPC-58 introduces a native affine
alias Gram, while TPC-61 identifies the remaining cofactor/parity
kernel \citep{WangTPC58,WangTPC61}.  TPC-113 gives the correct
quotient condition number for any declared finite physical synthesis
\citep{WangTPC113}.  The missing dictionary is the exact map between
the source, residual, and native levels.

Let \(U\) be a finite set of source records.  A record carries
\[
 u=(m_u,r_u,\gamma_u,j_u,\xi_u),\qquad
 a_u\in\C,\qquad d_u=d(m_u)\in\mathbb N.
\]
The auxiliary label \(\xi_u\) records any remaining provenance but
does not enter the residual key
\[
 q(u)=(\gamma_u,j_u,d_ur_u)\in Y.
\]
Let \(\pi:Y\to I\) be the exact native label map supplied by the
application.  We do not assume it is injective.

\section{The divisor-collision normal form}

\begin{lemma}[Complete product-collision parametrization]
\label{lem:collision}
For positive integers \(d,d',r,r'\), the equality \(dr=d'r'\) holds
if and only if there exist \(g,a,b,t\in\mathbb N\) such that
\[
 d=ga,\qquad d'=gb,\qquad (a,b)=1,\qquad
 r=bt,\qquad r'=at.
\]
The parameters are unique with
\(g=(d,d')\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write \(d=ga\), \(d'=gb\), where \(g=(d,d')\) and \((a,b)=1\).
Then \(dr=d'r'\) is \(ar=br'\).  Coprimality gives \(b\mid r\)
and \(a\mid r'\), hence \(r=bt\) and \(r'=at\) for the same \(t\).
The converse is immediate, and the gcd choice proves uniqueness.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What the lemma does not count]
\Cref{lem:collision} identifies every arithmetic collision, but it
does not bound how many admissible quadruples occur in the actual
active support.  Such a bound must retain the masks on \(m,r,\gamma\)
and \(j\).  Replacing them by a rectangular ambient box would change
the physical fiber.
\end{remark}

\section{Weighted residual coarea}

Let \(\{e_u:u\in U\}\) and \(\{e_y:y\in Y\}\) be orthonormal bases.
Define
\[
 \Res_a:\C^U\to\C^Y,\qquad
 \Res_a e_u=a_u e_{q(u)}.
\]
Zero-weight records are allowed.  Put
\[
 W_y=\sum_{\substack{u\in U\\q(u)=y}}|a_u|^2.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact coarea singular values]
\label{thm:coarea}
The weighted grouping map satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \Res_a\Res_a^*=\operatorname{diag}_{y\in Y}(W_y).
 \quad}
\]
Consequently its nonzero singular values, with multiplicity, are
\(\{\sqrt{W_y}:W_y>0\}\), and
\[
 \|\Res_a\|^2=\max_yW_y,\qquad
 \|\Res_a^\dagger\|^2=\frac1{\min_{W_y>0}W_y},
\]
\[
 \kappa_{\rm grp}(\Res_a)^2
 =\frac{\max_yW_y}{\min_{W_y>0}W_y}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For \(y,y'\in Y\),
\[
 \langle e_y,\Res_a\Res_a^*e_{y'}\rangle
 =\sum_{u\in U}|a_u|^2
   \one_{\{q(u)=y\}}\one_{\{q(u)=y'\}}.
\]
The expression vanishes for \(y\ne y'\) and equals \(W_y\) on the
diagonal.  The remaining assertions follow from the singular-value
decomposition and the quotient convention of TPC-113.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Weighted, not cardinality-only]
If \(|a_u|\) varies, the fiber cardinality is not the coarea weight.
The exact quantity is \(W_y\).  A divisor-bound estimate for the
number of records is therefore insufficient unless the physical
weights are controlled on the same support.
\end{remark}

\section{The native Gram and alias exchange}

Define the native forgetting map
\[
 \Nat:\C^Y\to\C^I,\qquad \Nat e_y=e_{\pi(y)}.
\]
For \(i\in I\), write
\[
 k_i=\#\{y\in Y:\pi(y)=i\}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Native multiplicity spectrum]
\label{prop:native}
One has
\[
 \Nat\Nat^*=\operatorname{diag}_{i\in I}(k_i),
 \qquad
 \|\Nat\|^2=\max_i k_i.
\]
The nonzero singular values are \(\sqrt{k_i}\) for \(k_i>0\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The rows of \(\Nat\) have disjoint supports, and row \(i\) has exactly
\(k_i\) unit entries.  The result is \cref{thm:coarea} with all
weights equal to one and grouping map \(\pi\).
\end{proof}

The two source-side Grams are
\[
 G_{\rm res}=\Res_a^*\Res_a,\qquad
 G_{\rm nat}=\Res_a^*\Nat^*\Nat\Res_a.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact Gram dictionary]
\label{thm:dictionary}
For all \(u,v\in U\),
\begin{align*}
 (G_{\rm res})_{uv}
 &=\overline{a_u}a_v\,\one_{\{q(u)=q(v)\}},\\
 (G_{\rm nat})_{uv}
 &=\overline{a_u}a_v\,
   \one_{\{\pi(q(u))=\pi(q(v))\}},
\end{align*}
and hence
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 (G_{\rm nat}-G_{\rm res})_{uv}
 =\overline{a_u}a_v\,
  \one_{\{q(u)\ne q(v),\ \pi(q(u))=\pi(q(v))\}}.
 \quad}
\]
Thus the difference contains exactly the exchange between distinct
residual fibers that alias to the same native label.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first formula is the matrix coefficient of
\(\Res_a^*\Res_a\).  Since
\((\Nat^*\Nat)_{yy'}=\one_{\{\pi(y)=\pi(y')\}}\), insertion between
\(\Res_a^*\) and \(\Res_a\) gives the second.  Subtraction leaves
precisely equal-native, unequal-residual pairs.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The exchange is not a positive correction]
Although both \(G_{\rm nat}\) and \(G_{\rm res}\) are positive
semidefinite, their difference need not be.  The exchange terms can
add or cancel depending on the coefficient phases, so they may not be
entered as an automatic energy gain.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[Exact provenance intertwining]
\label{cor:intertwining}
Let \(\Syn:\C^\Theta\to\C^U\) be any upstream literal synthesis and
set \(T=\Nat\Res_a\Syn\).  Then
\[
 \boxed{\qquad T^*T=\Syn^*G_{\rm nat}\Syn.\qquad}
\]
In particular, every native quadratic estimate on the inherited
coefficient is a statement about this compressed physical Gram, not
about \(G_{\rm res}\) alone.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Expand \(T^*T\) and use the definition of \(G_{\rm nat}\).
\end{proof}

\section{Sharp lower-transfer obstruction}

\begin{proposition}[Addition and cancellation in one native fiber]
\label{prop:cancellation}
Suppose \(y_1\ne y_2\) and \(\pi(y_1)=\pi(y_2)\).  On their span,
\[
 \|e_{y_1}+e_{y_2}\|^2=2,\qquad
 \|\Nat(e_{y_1}+e_{y_2})\|^2=4,
\]
whereas
\[
 \|e_{y_1}-e_{y_2}\|^2=2,\qquad
 \Nat(e_{y_1}-e_{y_2})=0.
\]
Therefore the upper energy factor \(k_{\max}\) is sharp, while no
positive lower comparison
\(\|\Nat z\|^2\ge c\|z\|^2\) can hold on the full residual space.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both residual basis vectors map to the same native basis vector.
The displayed identities follow by addition and subtraction.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Collision is necessary for this obstruction]
If every native fiber is a singleton, then \(\Nat\) is an isometry
onto its range and the cancellation example is unavailable.  An
actual stop certificate must also show that the distinguished
coefficient, or the relevant range of \(\Res_a\Syn\), meets the
collision kernel.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Why separate losses must not be multiplied blindly]
\Cref{cor:intertwining} computes the complete Gram before any
condition-number estimate.  Bounding \(\Syn\), \(\Res_a\), and
\(\Nat\) separately can be convenient, but multiplying the three
worst-case costs may charge directions that never coexist.  The
native physical condition number should be measured from
\(\Syn^*G_{\rm nat}\Syn\) whenever that matrix is available.
\end{remark}

\section{Finite exact certificate}

The script
\texttt{experiments/tpc114\_grouping\_certificate.py} uses rational
arithmetic.  It enumerates product collisions, constructs
\(\Res_a,\Nat,G_{\rm res},G_{\rm nat}\), and checks:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item the coprime normal form in \cref{lem:collision};
\item \(\Res_a\Res_a^*=\operatorname{diag}(W_y)\);
\item \(\Nat\Nat^*=\operatorname{diag}(k_i)\);
\item both entrywise Gram formulas and the exchange identity; and
\item the sharp addition/cancellation pair.
\end{enumerate}
The JSON certificate is finite exact algebra.  It is not evidence
that actual TPC fibers have bounded multiplicity.

\paragraph{Established.}
\Cref{lem:collision,thm:coarea,prop:native,thm:dictionary,cor:intertwining,prop:cancellation}
give a complete finite-dimensional
dictionary.  The matrix identities and sharp obstruction are
\(\Lzero\).  Declaring \(q(u)=(\gamma,j,d(m)r)\) and retaining all
literal weights is the \(\Lone\) arithmetic interface anticipated by
TPC-MVP1 \citep{WangMVP1}.

\paragraph{Not established.}
We prove no asymptotic bound for \(W_y\), \(k_i\), or the positive
spectrum of the actual growing native Gram.  We do not identify a
protected complement to the cancellation kernel, recover one
prescribed \(h_0\), or prove a fixed-power arithmetic saving.  There
is no automatic conversion from the \(\ell^2\) norm
\(\sqrt{k_{\max}}\) to the H9 max-packet-to-scalar norm; that requires
the actual operator chain.  There
is no \(\Ltwo\) advance, parity breakthrough, Hardy--Littlewood
asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem.  Nothing is
specialized to \(h_0=2\).
