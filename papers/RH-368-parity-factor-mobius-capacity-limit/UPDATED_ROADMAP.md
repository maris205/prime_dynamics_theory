# Roadmap after RH-368

RH-368 closes one narrow constraint-graph route: the PCF three-cell factor
`A_{\{2\}}` has an exact Möbius capacity limit `4/π²`.

The result must not be reparameterized into the RH-366 four-state
distance-two capacity.  The latter still needs higher-order run-frequency
control and has no proved limit.

The shortest remaining independent candidates are:

1. the nonadaptive branch-symmetric Markov family `P_t` on the RH-366 graph,
   with exact covariance and fixed-`t` almost-sure Möbius orthogonality;
2. a general constraint-graph theorem separating parity-factor capacities from
   distance-two capacities;
3. the common strong-space projector/resolvent bridge for RH-367 Ulam data;
4. the original physical triggers and the RH-241 all-order bridge.

Route A is `GO` for the declared parity-factor theorem and Route B is
`STOP_SCOPED` at canonical data type.  Gates A--E remain false/open.
