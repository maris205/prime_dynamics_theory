## Question

After TPC-353 attached the finite V59 residual to the literal masked operator
on a low-origin panel, does its operator-level polarization transfer to a
genuinely higher-origin, disjoint holdout when every other protocol choice is
held fixed?

`beta(t)=Lambda(t+2)-b^(2)(t)` and the literal deleted-diagonal shell operator
with both divisibility masks retained.  The only protocol change from TPC-353

1. Prove the finite operator polarization identity for `beta=Lambda-b`.
2. Prove the normalized Cauchy envelope for the residual output fraction.
3. Rebuild all rows and compare both law summaries and the locked TPC-353 parent.
4. Make the parent comparison an origins-only, certificate-locked transfer test.
5. Use a separate reverse-shell implementation and mutation stress suite.
6. Keep the arithmetic gate, fixed-power credit, Route-B reassembly, and

## Expected decision rule

If positive alignment survives but the all-plus floor or mean drops, record a
finite transfer with a quantitative stability obstruction.  If signs vary or
the transfer fails, record the exact finite obstruction and move to a
position-aware bound.  Neither outcome is an asymptotic theorem or a
source-uniform `L2` estimate.
