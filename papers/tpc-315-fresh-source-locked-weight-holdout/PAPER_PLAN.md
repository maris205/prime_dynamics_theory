# TPC-315 paper plan

## Research question

Does the finite below/above-one separation survive when the weighting menu is
fixed in advance but the physical source interval, Gram matrix, and target
labels are all recomputed on a new panel?  If the class survives, does the
amplitude ordering remain stable?

## Claims to establish

1. Freeze the TPC-314 menu and its provenance before fresh target readout.
2. Define the literal rational physical outputs on `I={641,...,1280}` and
   rebuild all eight source-shell Gram matrices.
3. Enumerate every sign class modulo global sign and record the unique fresh
   minimum and all-positive maximum.
4. Enclose the three weighted laws, including `log(p)`, with rational
   directed intervals on the declared grid.
5. Certify all 48 finite cases and independently replay every endpoint,
   target, rank, digest, and law order.
6. Treat the surviving coarse class and changing order census as separate
   positive and negative results.

## Deliberate non-claims

The same TPC-268 arithmetic engine is used on both panels, so this is not an
external physical holdout.  The fresh minimum is still selected from its own
Gram matrix.  No law is shown canonical; no finite observation is promoted to
a growing theorem, arithmetic `L2` bound, fixed-power credit, Gate-B passage,
or twin-prime result.

## Planned artifacts

The producer writes a canonical JSON certificate after checking the locked
TPC-314 menu.  The independent checker loads only the TPC-268 engine and the
TPC-314 menu certificate, then copies the physical operator, sign enumeration,
and interval derivation.  The stress suite attacks directed rounding,
cancellation, range reduction, fresh geometry, and Gray-code enumeration.
