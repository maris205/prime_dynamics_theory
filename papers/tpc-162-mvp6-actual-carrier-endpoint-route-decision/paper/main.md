## The arithmetic corridor

TPC-159 imports the quantitative affine-correlation input of Tao–Teräväinen and its Möbius nonpretentiousness ancestry `\citep{TaoTeravainen2026,MatomakiRadziwillTao2015,WangTPC159}`. For the determinant-two periodic core, take $a,s,R\in\mathbb N$, $(a,s)=1$, $as$ odd, and $d,u\in\mathbb Z$ satisfying $su-ad=2$. For $z\in\mathbb Z$, write $$q=as,\qquad t(z)=ad+qz,\qquad
 c_z=\mu(d+sz)\mu(u+az),$$ and let $\rho$ be a bounded $R$-periodic function. TPC-159 proves that outside a dyadic shadow $\mathcal S_{X,J}$, $$\frac qT
 \left|
   \sum_{0<t(z)\leq T} c_z\rho(z)
 \right|
 \ll
 \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left\{
  (\log X)^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+\frac qT
 \right\},
\tag{1}\label{eq:almost-prefix}$$ provided $2^J\sqrt X\leq T\leq X$, while $$\frac1{\log X}\int_{\mathcal S_{X,J}}\frac{dT}{T}
 \ll J(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}.
\tag{2}\label{eq:shadow-measure}$$ The geometric dyadic decomposition incurs no multiplicative $J$ loss in [\[eq:almost-prefix\]](../main.tex#L187){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:almost-prefix"}. The level is $$\mathsf{PROVED\_L1\_ACTUAL\_PREFIX\_ALMOST\_ENDPOINT}.$$

TPC-160 then applies exact Abel summation `\citep{WangTPC160}`. If $A_i=\sum_{j\leq i}\sigma_j$ and $d_i=w_i-w_{i+1}$, with the terminal convention $d_m=w_m$, then $$\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sigma_iw_i=\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_id_i.
\tag{3}\label{eq:abel}$$ Splitting the variation into good and exceptional endpoint cells gives $$\frac qT\left|\sum_i\sigma_iw_i\right|
 \ll \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left\{
   \varepsilon_X V_{\rm good}(w)
   +(1+q/T)V_{\rm bad}(w)
 \right\},
\tag{4}\label{eq:weighted-interface}$$ where $\varepsilon_X=(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+q/T$. This is an exact weighted interface, but the literal physical weight, actual endpoint registry and production bad-variation estimate remain unavailable.

For a prefix cutoff, the Abel derivative is one unit atom at the chosen endpoint. A set of zero logarithmic measure may contain that atom. Thus [\[eq:shadow-measure\]](../main.tex#L193){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:shadow-measure"} alone cannot promote [\[eq:almost-prefix\]](../main.tex#L187){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:almost-prefix"} to every deterministic prefix.

# Endpoint V3

Endpoint V3 asks six literal questions before arithmetic and physical budgets may be combined:

1.  Are the coefficients the literal physical coefficients?

2.  Is the shift the fixed physical $h_0=2$?

3.  Is the atomic normalization the physical one?

4.  Is the representation canonical or proved minimal?

5.  Is the actual active support completely registered?

6.  Does the physical loss satisfy a strict upper bound below $1/400$?

An unknown answer is not zero cost.

The arithmetic ledger records the strongest achieved theorem and the target separately: $$\sigma_{\rm achieved}^{(X)}=0,
 \qquad
 \sigma_{\rm target}^{(X)}\geq\frac1{400}.
\tag{10}\label{eq:arith-ledger}$$ The zero in [\[eq:arith-ledger\]](../main.tex#L448){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:arith-ledger"} means that the proved saving is logarithmic, not that there is no arithmetic progress. The physical ledger requires $$\Lambda_{\rm phys}<\frac1{400},
\tag{11}\label{eq:physical-ledger}$$ with a complete independent registry. Full synthesis additionally requires compatible scopes and strict net slack. Current artifacts certify neither [\[eq:arith-ledger\]](../main.tex#L448){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:arith-ledger"}’s fixed-power target nor [\[eq:physical-ledger\]](../main.tex#L455){reference-type="ref" reference="eq:physical-ledger"}.

> **Proposition: No pseudo-endpoint** None of the following alone is an endpoint-V3 pass: an almost-endpoint log saving, continuous exceptional-set density, a conditional literal-weight interface, a finite empty ETO census, or an upper cost bound at or above $1/400$.

> **Proof** They respectively fail fixed-$X$-power, atomic endpoint, production weight, growing-scale coverage and strict inequality requirements.
