\begin{abstract}
TPC-153--161 sharpen both sides of the fixed-shift program.  On the
structural side, a canonical cut shadow exists, but the frozen
artifacts do not determine an actual conservative occurrence lift.
This stops only the current-artifacts-only route.  The
occurrence-augmented route remains unavailable at the first typed
object
\(\mathsf{H1.theorem\_backed\_occurrence\_provenance\_crosswalk}\);
the scalar-plus-eligible-tail route also remains open under its own
two-part contract.

On the arithmetic side, a determinant-two two-M\"obius periodic core
has been advanced from good dyadic blocks to cumulative prefixes
outside a sparse dyadic shadow.  Exact Abel summation then gives a
weighted almost-endpoint interface.  This is genuine
\(\Lone_{\rm actual}\) progress.  It is not a deterministic
all-prefix theorem, a literal physical-weight theorem, or a positive
fixed-\(X\)-power \(\Ltwo\) result.

We freeze these facts in MVP6.  Invalid data are rejected before seven
ordered route verdicts.  The audit separately records the typed
minimal \(\NT\) blocker antichain and the parent-ready \(\OPEN\)
frontier, preserves complete-route-universe and fresh-registry
requirements, and introduces an endpoint-V3 ledger that refuses to
charge unknown physical data as zero.  The current source-locked
decision is
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Verdict}_{153:161}=\textsc{not-testable}},
 \qquad
 \mathsf{FirstMissing}
 =
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathsf{H1.theorem\_backed\_occurrence\_}\\[-2pt]
 \mathsf{provenance\_crosswalk}
 \end{gathered}
 .
\]
No endpoint, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} fixed shift; occurrence provenance;
M\"obius correlation; almost endpoint; atomic prefix; route decision.

\section{The two corridors entering MVP6}

\subsection{The structural corridor}

TPC-156 identifies the production H1 contract
\citep{WangTPC156}.  Its occurrence-augmented map clause requires a
theorem-backed provenance crosswalk before the conservative
row-separated lift and the literal downstream maps can be evaluated.
Its independent scalar clause requires both a complete original-scale
frontier estimate and a theorem-backed disposition of every
eligible-tail-open path.  In symbols,
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{Map}_{H1}
={}&
\mathsf{Crosswalk}\wedge
\bigwedge_{\star\in\mathcal D} (D_\star=0)
\wedge\mathsf{RegistryComplete},\\
\mathsf{Scalar}_{H1}
={}&
\mathsf{FUM}_{o(X)}
\wedge\mathsf{ETO}_{\rm disposition},\\
\mathsf{H1Complete}
={}&
\mathsf{Map}_{H1}\vee\mathsf{Scalar}_{H1},
\end{split}
\]
where
\[
 \mathcal D=\{L,Q_D,Q_Z,G,P,DZ,GP,\mathrm{cover},\mathrm{rec}\}.
\]
The occurrence registry is an additional totality requirement, not a
tenth algebraic defect silently merged into this vector.

The finite archive has a canonical conservative cut shadow.  It also
has a rigorous nonuniqueness result for completions using only current
fields.  Consequently,
\[
 \mathsf{current\ artifacts\ only}
 \quad\longmapsto\quad
 \STOPR
\]
in that exact cell.  It does not imply that an enriched
theorem-backed occurrence carrier cannot exist.  Therefore neither
the occurrence-augmented map route nor the scalar-plus-ETO route is
globally stopped.

\subsection{The arithmetic corridor}

TPC-159 imports the quantitative affine-correlation input of
Tao--Ter\"av\"ainen and its M\"obius nonpretentiousness ancestry
\citep{TaoTeravainen2026,MatomakiRadziwillTao2015,WangTPC159}.
For the determinant-two periodic core, take
\(a,s,R\in\mathbb N\), \((a,s)=1\), \(as\) odd, and
\(d,u\in\mathbb Z\) satisfying \(su-ad=2\).  For \(z\in\mathbb Z\),
write
\[
 q=as,\qquad t(z)=ad+qz,\qquad
 c_z=\mu(d+sz)\mu(u+az),
\]
and let \(\rho\) be a bounded \(R\)-periodic function.  TPC-159 proves
that outside a dyadic shadow \(\mathcal S_{X,J}\),
\[
 \frac qT
 \left|
   \sum_{0<t(z)\leq T} c_z\rho(z)
 \right|
 \ll
 \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left\{
  (\log X)^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+\frac qT
 \right\},
\tag{1}\label{eq:almost-prefix}
\]
provided \(2^J\sqrt X\leq T\leq X\), while
\[
 \frac1{\log X}\int_{\mathcal S_{X,J}}\frac{dT}{T}
 \ll J(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}.
\tag{2}\label{eq:shadow-measure}
\]
The geometric dyadic decomposition incurs no multiplicative \(J\)
loss in \cref{eq:almost-prefix}.  The level is
\[
 \mathsf{PROVED\_L1\_ACTUAL\_PREFIX\_ALMOST\_ENDPOINT}.
\]

TPC-160 then applies exact Abel summation
\citep{WangTPC160}.  If \(A_i=\sum_{j\leq i}\sigma_j\) and
\(d_i=w_i-w_{i+1}\), with the terminal convention \(d_m=w_m\), then
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^{m}\sigma_iw_i=\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_id_i.
\tag{3}\label{eq:abel}
\]
Splitting the variation into good and exceptional endpoint cells gives
\[
 \frac qT\left|\sum_i\sigma_iw_i\right|
 \ll \|\rho\|_\infty
 \left\{
   \varepsilon_X V_{\rm good}(w)
   +(1+q/T)V_{\rm bad}(w)
 \right\},
\tag{4}\label{eq:weighted-interface}
\]
where
\(\varepsilon_X=(\log X)^{-\kappa_0}+2^{-J}+q/T\).
This is an exact weighted interface, but the literal physical weight,
actual endpoint registry and production bad-variation estimate remain
unavailable.

For a prefix cutoff, the Abel derivative is one unit atom at the
chosen endpoint.  A set of zero logarithmic measure may contain that
atom.  Thus \cref{eq:shadow-measure} alone cannot promote
\cref{eq:almost-prefix} to every deterministic prefix.

\section{A source-locked MVP6 snapshot}

TPC-161 supplies the typed integration manifest used below
\citep{WangTPC161}.

\begin{definition}[Valid snapshot]
An MVP6 snapshot is valid if:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2em]
\item the TPC-161 manifest, schema, audit and generating script match
  their canonical UTF-8/LF hashes;
\item direct TPC-156 and TPC-160 consistency anchors match the
  structural and arithmetic exports used by the classifier;
\item all hashes have integrity semantics only;
\item node identifiers, dependency modes, roles, scopes, carriers,
  normalizations and registries satisfy their schemas;
\item the active typed DAG is acyclic;
\item every stop, route-universe-completeness claim and reroute
  crosswalk resolves through the route-evidence registry to a typed
  source record with matching scope and route endpoints;
\item the route table exactly covers its declared universe, and every
  stopped cell carries source, scope, carrier, normalization,
  registry and complete-cell coverage;
\item every endpoint-V3 rational value has positive denominator and
  every pass/stop direction is consistent; and
\item all claim-boundary Booleans obey the achieved levels.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

Any failure returns the outer result \(\INVALID\).  Source drift makes
the certificate stale; it does not make a mathematical theorem false.

\section{Two frontiers that must not be merged}

Let \(\mathcal A\) be the active ancestor closure of the selected
root.  Define
\[
 \mathcal B_{\rm NT}
 =
 \min_{\preceq}\{v\in\mathcal A:
       \operatorname{status}(v)=\NT
       \text{ or }v\text{ is scope-incompatible}\}.
\tag{5}\label{eq:nt-antichain}
\]
This is a typed blocker antichain: descendants are suppressed by
their earliest missing ancestors.  The audit computes each recorded
transitive ancestor set directly from the source DAG rather than
trusting a submitted parent label.  A proved node with incompatible
scope is therefore also a blocker, exactly as in
\cref{eq:nt-antichain}.

Separately define the parent-ready open frontier
\[
 \mathcal F_{\rm open}
 =
 \{v\in\mathcal A:
   \operatorname{status}(v)=\OPEN,
   v\text{ is scope-compatible},\
   \text{every active parent of }v\text{ is proved}\}.
\tag{6}\label{eq:open-frontier}
\]
A node in \(\mathcal F_{\rm open}\) is a live mathematical target.
A node in \(\mathcal B_{\rm NT}\) needs a missing typed artifact before
its predicate is testable.  The two sets are disjoint by their
combined status-and-scope typing, and neither may be used as a
substitute for the other.

\begin{proposition}[Current typed frontiers]
\label{prop:blocker}
For the selected synthesis root,
\[
 \mathcal B_{\rm NT}
 =
 \left\{
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathsf{H1.theorem\_backed\_occurrence\_provenance\_crosswalk},\\
 \mathsf{H9.endpoint\_registry},\
 \mathsf{H9.literal\_weight\_registry},\\
 \mathsf{H9.normalization\_registry},\
 \mathsf{H9.phase\_cell\_registry}
 \end{gathered}
 \right\}.
\tag{7}\label{eq:current-blockers}
\]
The canonical selected first missing representative is the H1
crosswalk.  The separate parent-ready frontier is
\[
 \mathcal F_{\rm open}
 =
 \left\{
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathsf{O161.bad\_endpoint\_pointwise\_core},\\
 \mathsf{O161.direct\_additive\_twist\_core}
 \end{gathered}
 \right\}.
\tag{8}\label{eq:current-open}
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The canonical cut occurrence shadow, witness-verifier contract and
typed H1 decision are proved.  Their next node requires a
theorem-backed production crosswalk and is \(\NT\).  Independently,
the literal-weight, phase-cell, endpoint and normalization registries
are missing roots of the physical-return branch.  None of these five
nodes descends from another, while the lift, literal downstream maps,
physical cover, reconnection and complete physical registry descend
from them.  This proves \cref{eq:current-blockers}; canonical node
ordering selects the H1 entry first.  The two nodes in
\cref{eq:current-open} are status-\(\OPEN\), have ready artifacts and
proved parents, so they remain a distinct live frontier.
\end{proof}

\section{Route semantics and classifier}

The source-locked route universe contains five declared cells:
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\mathsf{R}_{\rm archive}&:&
\text{actual lift derivable from current fields only},\\
\mathsf{R}_{\rm map+}&:&
\text{occurrence-augmented theorem-backed map route},\\
\mathsf{R}_{\rm scalar+ETO}&:&
\text{complete FUM scalar theorem plus ETO disposition},\\
\mathsf{R}_{\rm twist}&:&
\text{direct additive-twist core route},\\
\mathsf{R}_{\rm bad\ endpoint}&:&
\text{pointwise control on exceptional prefix endpoints}.
\end{array}
\]
Only \(\mathsf{R}_{\rm archive}\) is stopped.  Since no independent
theorem proves these cells form a complete universe of possible
arguments, this cannot imply \(\AINF\).

A reroute is legal only when the selected route has an exact stop, the
alternative is open, a theorem-backed typed crosswalk connects their
objects and the alternative has a genuinely fresh registry.  Renaming
a registry or reusing the stopped route's rows makes the snapshot
\(\INVALID\), not \(\REROUTE\).

The production classifier runs in \(\mathsf{SOURCE\_LOCKED}\) mode.
Each route stop is resolved to its scoped source result, while any
future completeness theorem or crosswalk must be resolved by
canonical repository path and hash.  The reachability suite instead
runs in the disjoint
\(\mathsf{SYNTHETIC\_REACHABILITY}\) mode.  Its two file-backed
predicate fixtures and inline stopped-cell assumptions explicitly
carry
\(\mathsf{NONE\_SYNTHETIC\_REACHABILITY\_ONLY}\) semantics; they are
not theorem evidence and cannot be imported into the production mode.

\begin{definition}[Ordered valid-snapshot verdicts]
After validation, the first satisfied predicate in the following list
is returned:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\GO,\quad\AINF,\quad\REROUTE,\quad\STOPR,\\
\NT,\quad\AF,\quad\OPEN.
\end{gathered}
\tag{9}\label{eq:precedence}
\]
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.2em]
\item \(\GO\): every active requirement is proved in matching scope
  and the full endpoint has strict positive slack.
\item \(\AINF\): an independent theorem proves the route universe
  complete and every route has an exact complete-cell stop.
\item \(\REROUTE\): the selected route is stopped and a valid fresh
  typed alternative is selected.
\item \(\STOPR\): the selected route is stopped and no valid
  alternative is selected.
\item \(\NT\): the typed blocker antichain is nonempty.
\item \(\AF\): every structural node and the physical endpoint are
  proved, and all remaining unresolved nodes are scope-matched open
  positive-\(\Ltwo\) arithmetic targets.
\item \(\OPEN\): every other valid state.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Classifier totality]
Validation followed by \cref{eq:precedence} returns exactly one of
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \INVALID,\quad\GO,\quad\AINF,\quad\REROUTE,\\
 \STOPR,\quad\NT,\quad\AF,\quad\OPEN.
 \end{gathered}
\]
Every result is reachable on a finite typed synthetic
assumed-predicate regression fixture.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Validation either fails, giving the separate outer result, or produces
a finite valid state.  The ordered list has a final catch-all, hence
the first true predicate is unique.  The regression suite constructs
one logical witness for each verdict and one invalid route/registry
witness for \(\INVALID\).  This establishes classifier totality only;
it asserts none of the synthetic predicates as mathematics.
\end{proof}

\section{Endpoint V3}

Endpoint V3 asks six literal questions before arithmetic and physical
budgets may be combined:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textnormal{V3.\arabic*},leftmargin=2.8em]
\item Are the coefficients the literal physical coefficients?
\item Is the shift the fixed physical \(h_0=2\)?
\item Is the atomic normalization the physical one?
\item Is the representation canonical or proved minimal?
\item Is the actual active support completely registered?
\item Does the physical loss satisfy a strict upper bound below
  \(1/400\)?
\end{enumerate}
An unknown answer is not zero cost.

The arithmetic ledger records the strongest achieved theorem and the
target separately:
\[
 \sigma_{\rm achieved}^{(X)}=0,
 \qquad
 \sigma_{\rm target}^{(X)}\geq\frac1{400}.
\tag{10}\label{eq:arith-ledger}
\]
The zero in \cref{eq:arith-ledger} means that the proved saving is
logarithmic, not that there is no arithmetic progress.
The physical ledger requires
\[
 \Lambda_{\rm phys}<\frac1{400},
\tag{11}\label{eq:physical-ledger}
\]
with a complete independent registry.  Full synthesis additionally
requires compatible scopes and strict net slack.  Current artifacts
certify neither \cref{eq:arith-ledger}'s fixed-power target nor
\cref{eq:physical-ledger}.

\begin{proposition}[No pseudo-endpoint]
None of the following alone is an endpoint-V3 pass: an
almost-endpoint log saving, continuous exceptional-set density, a
conditional literal-weight interface, a finite empty ETO census, or
an upper cost bound at or above \(1/400\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
They respectively fail fixed-\(X\)-power, atomic endpoint, production
weight, growing-scale coverage and strict inequality requirements.
\end{proof}

\section{The MVP6 decision}

\begin{theorem}[Source-locked MVP6 verdict]
\label{thm:mvp6}
For the TPC-153--161 source-locked state,
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Verdict}_{153:161}=\NT,
 \qquad
 \mathsf{FirstMissing}
 =
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathsf{H1.theorem\_backed\_occurrence\_}\\[-2pt]
 \mathsf{provenance\_crosswalk}
 \end{gathered}
 .
 }
\]
The current-artifacts-only route is stopped in its exact declared
cell; the selected augmented map route and scalar-plus-ETO route are
not globally stopped.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
All source, type, DAG and endpoint validations pass, so the snapshot
is not \(\INVALID\).  The selected augmented map route has no stop.
The declared route universe has no completeness theorem, excluding
\(\AINF\), and no alternative is activated with a fresh registry,
excluding \(\REROUTE\).  By \cref{prop:blocker}, the selected active
set has a five-node minimal \(\NT\) antichain whose canonical first
entry is the H1 crosswalk.  The precedence therefore returns \(\NT\).

Independently, TPC-159 supplies an actual periodic-core
almost-endpoint \(\Lone\) theorem and TPC-160 a weighted interface,
but the literal physical weight, phase-cell, normalization and endpoint
registries, every atomic prefix and fixed positive \(X\)-power remain
unavailable.  Thus \(\AF\) and \(\GO\) would also be false even if they
were tested before the catch-all.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Precise progress label]
The current state contains structural \(\Lone\), actual
periodic-core almost-endpoint \(\Lone\), and an exact weighted-return
interface.  The achieved fixed-\(X\)-power level remains below
\(\Ltwo_{\rm actual\mbox{-}positive}\).
\end{corollary}

\section{Next forced objects}

\paragraph{Structural object.}
The selected route must next provide
\[
 \mathsf{H1.theorem\_backed\_occurrence\_provenance\_crosswalk}.
\]
It must cover every nonsoft ETO and FUM path, separate each target
occurrence, conserve source columns and preserve theorem source,
canonical parent, stage, multiplier, native value, fixed shift,
physical normalization and support lineage.  Only then can the actual
lift and nine defects be evaluated.

\paragraph{Arithmetic/physical-return objects.}
The parallel corridor must provide a source-locked literal physical
weight, phase-cell registry, actual endpoint registry and literal
normalization registry.  It must then prove either small bad variation
in \cref{eq:weighted-interface} or a pointwise theorem on the
exceptional atomic endpoints.  These are the next objects needed to
turn TPC-159/160 into a literal physical-return statement.  A further
theorem would still be required to replace the logarithmic saving by
a positive fixed power of \(X\).

\paragraph{Decision discipline.}
If the crosswalk is constructed, the first missing pointer moves
forward to the first nonzero or unevaluable literal map defect.  If a
theorem proves that no crosswalk exists on the actual carrier, the
augmented map cell may receive a typed stop.  If the scalar-plus-ETO
clause is completed, it becomes a legitimate reroute only with its
own fresh registry and crosswalk.  None of these future outcomes is
prejudged here.

\section{Reproducible audit}

The standard-library generator dynamically reads the TPC-161
manifest rather than embedding its hash.  It records canonical hashes
for the TPC-161 manifest, schema, audit and script, and directly checks
the TPC-156 scoped-stop and TPC-160 weighted-interface anchors.
It also locks the two synthetic predicate fixtures used only by the
reachability suite, then emits a strict snapshot and audit.

Mutation regressions reject:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item a malformed snapshot before mathematical classification;
\item an unregistered or fabricated theorem label, or classifier
  source-hash drift;
\item route-universe completeness without a registered independent
  theorem;
\item architecture infeasibility from stops on only the declared
  sample of routes;
\item rerouting without a fresh registry or registered typed
  crosswalk;
\item promotion of the current-artifacts stop to actual-carrier
  impossibility;
\item collapsing the five-node selected-root blocker antichain to its
  first reporting representative, or falsifying its transitive
  ancestry;
\item dropping a scope-incompatible blocker or merging an \(\NT\)
  blocker with a parent-ready \(\OPEN\) node;
\item assigning TPC-159 a deterministic all-prefix conclusion;
\item promoting logarithmic decay to a positive \(X\)-power;
\item treating exceptional logarithmic density as atomic avoidance;
\item promoting the TPC-160 interface to a production physical-weight
  theorem; and
\item an endpoint-V3 pass with any unknown literal field or without a
  strict \(1/400\) inequality.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

MVP6 finds that both corridors remain live but not yet joined.  The
selected structural first object is now one theorem-backed provenance
crosswalk, not an unspecified missing matrix.  Four independent
physical-return registries form the rest of the selected-root minimal
blocker antichain.  The arithmetic theorem now reaches almost every
cumulative endpoint of the actual periodic core, not merely one
dyadic block.  The remaining gap is explicit: construct the actual
carrier and return the arithmetic estimate through literal weights,
phases, normalizations and atomic endpoints.  Until that happens,
\(\NT\) is the only source-compatible verdict.
