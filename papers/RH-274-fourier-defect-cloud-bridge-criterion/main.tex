\begin{abstract}
The canonical pole counterloop is a rank-growing root-of-unity shell.  We
show that pointwise radial and phase convergence of its roots is not enough
to transport trace moments.  Fixed moments are controlled by aggregate
low-frequency Fourier defects.  A common phase shift tending to zero as
$(N+1)^{-1/2}$ makes the third moment grow like $\sqrt N$, giving an exact
counterexample to any maximum-phase-only bridge.  The example is logical and
is not asserted to describe the noisy cloud.
\end{abstract}
\section{Aggregate criterion}
Let $\theta_{N,j}=j\pi/(N+1)$ and consider conjugate pairs
$r_{N,j}e^{\pm i(\theta_{N,j}+\varepsilon_{N,j})}$.  Put
$r_{N,j}=\beta+\delta_{N,j}$, and let $S^*_{N,n}$ denote the moment with
$r_{N,j}=\beta$ and $\varepsilon_{N,j}=0$.
\begin{theorem}[fixed-moment stability]
For each fixed $n$, if $0\le r_{N,j},\beta\le B$ uniformly, then
\[
|S_{N,n}-S^*_{N,n}|
 \le 2nB^n\sum_{j=1}^N|\varepsilon_{N,j}|
 +2nB^{n-1}\sum_{j=1}^N|\delta_{N,j}|.
\]
Consequently aggregate $\ell^1$ phase and radial defects tending to zero
imply the RH-272 moment bridge at every fixed order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use $|e^{in\varepsilon}-1|\le n|\varepsilon|$ and the mean-value bound
$|r^n-\beta^n|\le nB^{n-1}|r-\beta|$, then sum both conjugate members.
\end{proof}
\section{Maximum-phase counterexample}
Take the exact radii $r_{N,j}=\beta$ and the common shift
$\varepsilon_{N,j}=\Delta_N=(N+1)^{-1/2}$.  The maximum phase error tends to
zero.  Since the unshifted third moment vanishes,
\[
 S_{N,3}=2\beta^3\sum_{j=1}^N
 \cos\!\left(\frac{3j\pi}{N+1}+3\Delta_N\right).
\]
The exact sine sum is
$\sum_{j=1}^N\sin(3j\pi/(N+1))=\cot(3\pi/(2(N+1)))$.  Hence
\begin{theorem}[common-shift divergence]
\[
 S_{N,3}=-\frac{4\beta^3}{\pi}\sqrt{N+1}+o(\sqrt N).
\]
Thus $\max_j|\varepsilon_{N,j}|\to0$ does not imply even a bounded third
moment.
\end{theorem}
\section{Boundary}
The theorem invalidates a logical extrapolation from finite phase fits.  It
does not prove that the archived family has a coherent shift or fails the
aggregate criterion.  No Gate status changes.
