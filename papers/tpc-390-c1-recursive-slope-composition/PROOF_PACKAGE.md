# TPC-390 proof package

## PROVED_EXACT_FINITE

* The candidate grid and selected indices are response-blind and fixed.
* Calibration/holdout roles and all count levels are fixed before readout.
* Current intervals are pairwise disjoint and disjoint from listed prior
  panels.
* The 13-point rational `Q=8` anchor has positive geometry and symmetric
  matrices for all four sign laws.
* The TPC-389 producer hash, certificate hash, schema, and frozen-slope
  interface are checked.
* The sequential and direct parent-composition formulas are the same declared
  finite power-law expression, with a separately audited numerical residual.

## NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED_FINITE

* The producer rebuilds a complete 256-row, 32-cell panel.
* The independent checker rebuilds it in descending prime-shell order without
  importing the producer.
* One-step parent, local-control, and recursive-composition forecasts are
  recomputed from the rows and certificate summary.
* The spectral and Schur failure censuses are recomputed, not trusted as
  metadata.
* The adversarial certificate stress rejects altered hashes, roles, rows,
  summaries, and claim-firewall fields.

## OPEN / NOT CLAIMED

This package does not prove a count-uniform or origin-uniform law, a
source-valid normalization, a growing operator bound, source-uniform
arithmetic `L2`, Route-A/Route-B closure, or any twin-prime theorem.  A finite
recursive pass would not be an asymptotic composition theorem; a finite
recursive failure is likewise only a scoped obstruction.
