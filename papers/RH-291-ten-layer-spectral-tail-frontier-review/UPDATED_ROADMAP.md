# Roadmap after RH-291

RH-292 is admissible only for a genuine weighted prefix input on the same
logarithmic clock as RH-282.  The preferred forms are:

1. a direct weighted estimate from the noisy modulus complement to the
   deterministic anchor that makes the RH-288 prefix vanish on `R=7/5`;
2. a synchronized pair consisting of a weighted total-noisy-trace bridge to
   counterloop plus anchor and weighted modulus-head transport to the exact
   finite-radius shell;
3. a contour or trace-aggregate theorem proving the same direct complement
   bridge without resolving ill-conditioned eigenvectors.

A root-`l1` theorem for the head can supply the second half of item 2 only;
it must be paired with the weighted full-trace bridge on the same clock.

Interval certification of `C_M != 1` would make the limiting-radius
root-`l1` obstruction strictly positive, but it would not by itself supply
noisy-cloud transport.  More finite endpoints, limiting-radius fits, or tail
certificates are not a reopening input.

Gates A--E remain false/open.
