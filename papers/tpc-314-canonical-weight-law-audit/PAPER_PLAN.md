# TPC-314 paper plan

## Research question

Does the finite minimum-versus-positive separation on the new TPC-312 panel
survive weighting laws that are fixed by an arithmetic motivation rather than
chosen from the target Gram?  If so, is the numerical amplitude independent of
that law?

## Claims to establish

1. Define the frozen TPC-312 physical outputs and the weighted Gram quotient.
2. State the three weighting laws before reading the inherited target labels.
3. Prove the weighted Gram identity, positivity of the diagonal normalizer,
   and scale invariance.
4. Give a fully rational range-reduction and atanh-tail enclosure for
   log(p).
5. Recompute all 48 finite target/law cases with outward interval endpoints
   and an independent replay.
6. Record the class robustness and the law-order crossovers as separate
   positive and negative results.

## Deliberate non-claims

The project does not claim that any law is canonical, that the target labels
are externally generated, that the source panel is an independent holdout, or
that a finite class extends to growing shells.  It pays no arithmetic L2
estimate or fixed-power credit and does not pass Gate B or prove a twin-prime
statement.

## Planned artifacts

The producer writes a canonical JSON certificate.  The independent checker
copies the physical operator and interval/logarithm derivation while loading
only the locked TPC-268 engine.  A small exact stress suite attacks signed
interval arithmetic, cancellation, range reduction, and the positive tail
bound.  The paper separates externally motivated modeling choices from exact
finite consequences.
