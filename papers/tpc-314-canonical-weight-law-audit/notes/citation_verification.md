# TPC-314 citation verification

The external motivation is deliberately modest.  The following primary or
authoritative research sources were checked on 2026-08-30:

1. Marco Cantarini, Explicit formula for the average of Goldbach and prime
   tuples representations, arXiv:1801.08475.  The abstract and introduction
   explicitly use the von-Mangoldt function in prime-tuple and Goldbach sums:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08475>.
2. Szilárd Gy. Révész, A Riemann-von Mangoldt-type formula for the
   distribution of Beurling primes, arXiv:2110.11463.  Its abstract states
   the prime-element value log|p| for the corresponding von-Mangoldt function:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.11463>.
3. William Banks, Kevin Ford, and Terence Tao, Large prime gaps and
   probabilistic models, Inventiones Mathematicae 233 (2023), 1471–1518,
   DOI <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-023-01199-0>.  The article discusses
   phi(Q)/Q as the density of integers coprime to Q, which motivates the
   reduced-residue normalization.  The identity phi(p)=p-1 for prime p is
   elementary.

These sources motivate a fixed finite menu only.  They do not justify a
canonical choice for this diagnostic, do not provide an external physical
holdout, and do not upgrade the finite certificate to a twin-prime result.
