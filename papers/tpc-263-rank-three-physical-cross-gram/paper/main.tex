\begin{abstract}
TPC-262 identified the literal signed cross-Gram as the correct growing-shell
observable, but left its actual source packets untreated.  We combine two
source-backed inputs on the same V59 clock.  The maximal-interval hybrid
estimate controls all four consecutive block sums, while the four-block Haar
construction supplies three adjoint coefficients with explicit asymptotics.
The resulting rank-three physical channel is
\(O_{M,K}(x^{5/3}/(\log x)^{M+3})\) for every fixed admissible \(K\) and
fixed logarithmic strength \(M\).  We prove the exact complementary split and
keep the orthogonal residual explicit.  Thus this is a genuine logarithmic
channel advance, with zero fixed-power credit; it is not a full \(L^2\) bound,
an endpoint payment, or a twin-prime theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and scope}
The Route-B object is the literal common-clock coupling
\(C_x=\ip{w}{A_x\beta}\), with the prime shell, unit masks, deleted diagonal,
and smooth kernel retained.  TPC-257 constructed a source-only four-block
frame \(z_0,z_1,z_2\) and proved
\[
 \ip{z_i}{A_x\beta}=-(9\kappa_i/2+o(1))\frac{x^{7/6}}{(\log x)^3}.
\]
TPC-254 controlled the hybrid residual on every consecutive interval to an
arbitrary fixed logarithmic order.  The question here is whether those two
facts can be multiplied on the same physical object, without replacing the
full coupling by a finite diagonal surrogate.

Our maximum claim is
\begin{equation*}
 \boxed{C_3(x):=\ip{\PP w}{\PP A_x\beta}
   =O_{M,K}\!\left(\frac{x^{5/3}}{(\log x)^{M+3}}\right)}.
\end{equation*}
The notation \texttt{x\string^(5/3)} in the release certificate refers to the
same power.  The theorem is for fixed \(M,K\), and its constants are not
claimed uniform in either parameter.

\section{The source-only rank-three frame}
Let \(I_x=(x/2,x]\cap\mathbb Z\), \(N=|I_x|\),
\(\ell=\lfloor N/2\rfloor\), and \(r=N-\ell\).  Split the two rank
children into consecutive blocks \(B_1,B_2,B_3,B_4\), with sizes
\(s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4\).  For adjacent sets of sizes \(p,q\), define
\[
 h(A,B)=\sqrt{\frac{pq}{p+q}}\left(\frac{1_A}{p}-\frac{1_B}{q}\right).
\]
Set
\[
 z_0=h(B_1\cup B_2,B_3\cup B_4),\qquad
 z_1=h(B_1,B_2),\qquad z_2=h(B_3,B_4).
\]

\begin{lemma}[exact frame geometry]
The vectors \(z_0,z_1,z_2\) are source-only and orthonormal for every
admissible real clock.  Consequently
\[
 \PP=\sum_{i=0}^2 z_i\otimes z_i
\]
is an orthogonal projection.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For \(h(A,B)\), its squared norm is
\(pq(p^{-1}+q^{-1})/(p+q)=1\).  The block-weighted product of \(z_0\) and
\(z_1\) is
\[
 s_1\frac1\ell\frac1{s_1}+s_2\frac1\ell\frac{-1}{s_2}=0.
\]
The same calculation applies to \(z_0,z_2\), while \(z_1,z_2\) have disjoint
support.  This also covers odd child cardinalities because the definitions
are made by rank, not by an integer-only macroscopic threshold.
\end{proof}

\section{Three hybrid moments}
Write \(W_j=\sum_{u\in B_j}w(u)\).  The maximal Type-I source theorem used in
TPC-254 is a nonnegative sum over active intervals.  Its \(m=1\) row therefore
controls each of these four consecutive sums, for every fixed \(M,K\):
\[
 |W_j|\ll_{M,K}\frac{x}{(\log x)^M}.
\]
Each frame vector is a normalized difference of two block means, and every
block size is comparable with \(x\).  Hence
\begin{equation}
 |\ip{z_i}{w}|\ll_{M,K}\frac{x^{1/2}}{(\log x)^M},
 \qquad i=0,1,2.                                      \label{eq:w}
\end{equation}
The order of operations matters: the four interval bounds are established
before a signed contrast is assembled.  A whole-shell mean would not imply
(\ref{eq:w}).

\section{Exact physical channel split}
Put \(g_x=A_x\beta\).  Since \(\PP\) is orthogonal,
\begin{equation}
 \ip{w}{g_x}=\ip{\PP w}{\PP g_x}+
 \ip{(I-\PP)w}{(I-\PP)g_x}.                            \label{eq:split}
\end{equation}
There are no mixed terms.  Expanding the first term in the orthonormal frame
gives the signed cross-Gram formula
\begin{equation}
 C_3(x)=\sum_{i=0}^2
 \overline{\ip{z_i}{w}}\,\ip{z_i}{A_x\beta}.             \label{eq:channel}
\end{equation}
This is the physical counterpart of the phase-typed object isolated in
TPC-262: the projection is applied to both actual vectors, not merely to their
packet diagonals.

\begin{theorem}[rank-three physical cross-Gram channel]
For every fixed admissible \(K\) and every fixed \(M>0\),
\[
 |C_3(x)|\ll_{M,K}\frac{x^{5/3}}{(\log x)^{M+3}}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The three source-backed coefficient asymptotics from TPC-257 are
\[
 \ip{z_i}{g_x}=-(9\kappa_i/2+o(1))
       \frac{x^{7/6}}{(\log x)^3},
\]
where
\[
 \kappa_0=\frac{\log(32/27)}{\sqrt2},\quad
 \kappa_1=\frac12\log(3456/3125),\quad
 \kappa_2=\frac12\log(884736/823543).
\]
Combine this with (\ref{eq:w}) in (\ref{eq:channel}) and sum three fixed
terms.  The powers multiply as
\[
 x^{1/2}x^{7/6}=x^{5/3},
\]
and the logarithmic orders add to \(M+3\).  The finitely many \(o(1)\) factors
are absorbed after \(M,K\) are fixed and \(x\) is sufficiently large.
\end{proof}

\section{What the theorem does not pay}
The second term in (\ref{eq:split}) is
\[
 C_\perp(x)=\ip{(I-\PP)w}{(I-\PP)A_x\beta}.
\]
TPC-263 supplies no estimate for it.  This is not a cosmetic qualification:
TPC-260 showed that finite packet marginals and a finite list of null/Haar
coordinates do not identify a four-packet residual.  The present paper pays
the rank-three channel while preserving the missing complement as a named
object for the next attack.

The channel is logarithmic only.  For any fixed \(\eta>0\),
\[
 \frac{x^{5/3}/(\log x)^{M+3}}{x^{5/3-\eta}}
 =\frac{x^\eta}{(\log x)^{M+3}}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Thus no fixed-power credit is generated, and TPC-261's strict \(1/400\)
endpoint obligation remains unpaid.

\section{Conclusion}
The natural next question is now sharply localized: estimate the orthogonal
complement after the rank-three logarithmic channel has been removed, or find
an explicit residual obstruction.  The reusable chain is
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \text{rank-three Haar frame}\to\text{blockwise hybrid control}\to\\
 \text{adjoint asymptotics}\to\text{exact projection split}\to\\
 \text{logarithmic channel}+\text{residual firewall}.
\end{gathered}
\]
