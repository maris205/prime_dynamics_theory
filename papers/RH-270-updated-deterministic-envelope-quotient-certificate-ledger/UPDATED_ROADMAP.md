# Updated roadmap after RH-270

RH-262--RH-268 close and sharpen the deterministic target envelope.  The
factorwise order-29 budget is substantially tighter than the coarse envelope
tail, while RH-268 proves that the geometric base itself cannot be improved
for the deterministic target.  These are target-side results and do not
identify coefficients selected from a noisy cloud.

RH-269 supplies the exact missing-hypothesis interface for a uniform quotient:
Hilbert--Schmidt convergence, a common finite-rank isolating contour, a
uniform resolvent bound, and a contractive power of the limiting quotient.
None is certified in the current archive (`0/4`).

The next reopening input must be genuinely new and legal: an anchored head
outside the audited classes together with a coefficient bridge, a continuum
proof of the RH-269 hypotheses, or an equivalent operator-derived quotient
certificate.  Repeated finite scans and finite-order fits do not reopen the
route.
