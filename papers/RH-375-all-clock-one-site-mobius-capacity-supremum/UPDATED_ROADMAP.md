# RH-375 updated roadmap

RH-375 closes the exact optimization over all fixed finite clocks for the
universally safe one-site phase/current-input class.  Its supremum is the
RH-374 Euler-product constant `B_infinity`, and no finite clock attains it.
This does not close the adaptive RH-366 capacity problem.

The shortest remaining source-backed routes are:

1. Prove a convergence or envelope theorem for the RH-371 alternating
   eight-run Möbius expression; this attacks adaptive capacity rather than
   another prescribed one-site certificate.
2. Obtain a genuine higher-order Möbius correlation theorem for an explicitly
   frozen memory-dependent safe-transducer class.
3. Compare finite-clock one-site optimization with a rigorously defined
   restricted growing-clock regime only after a uniform-in-clock Davenport
   estimate is available; no such passage is made here.
4. Reopen the strong-space cyclic-Ulam route only after a mesh-independent
   norm, deterministic projection estimate, and common isolating contour
   around `-1` exist.

No current route supplies a canonical operator, von-Mangoldt prime-power
trace, completed-zeta divisor equality, Riemann-zero identification,
Hilbert--Pólya construction, or RH proof.
