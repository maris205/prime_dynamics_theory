## 2. Exact character form with the deleted diagonal

For nonprincipal \(\chi\pmod q\), extended by zero on nonunits, define

\[
 B_{q,\chi}(v)=
 \sum_{\substack{t\in I_x\\q\nmid t}}
 \beta(t)\overline{\chi(t)}e(vt/H),
 \tag{2.1}
\]

\[
 D_{q,\chi}(v)=
 \sum_{\substack{u\in I_x\\q\nmid u}}
 w(u)\overline{\chi(u)}e(vu/H),
 \qquad
 Z_q=\sum_{\substack{n\in I_x\\q\nmid n}}\beta(n)w(n).
 \tag{2.2}
\]

Since \(w\) is real,

\[
 \overline{D_{q,\chi}(v)}=
 \sum_{\substack{u\in I_x\\q\nmid u}}
 w(u)\chi(u)e(-vu/H).
 \tag{2.3}
\]

The V36 character identity is therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak C_x=
 \int_{\mathbb R}\psi_+(v)
 \sum_{q\in\mathcal Q}\frac q{q-1}
 \left(
 \sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}B_{q,\chi}(v)
 \overline{D_{q,\chi}(v)}-(q-2)Z_q
 \right)\,dv.}
 \tag{2.4}
\]

The factor \(q-2\) is not cosmetic.  There are exactly \(q-2\)
nonprincipal characters modulo a prime \(q\), and every one contributes the
same \(t=u\) term.  Formula (2.4) deletes that physical diagonal before any
absolute value.

## 3. Exact four-packet polarization

For \(x,y\in\mathbb C\),

\[
 \boxed{x\overline y=
 \frac14\sum_{j=0}^{3}i^j|x+i^jy|^2.}
 \tag{3.1}
\]

Indeed, the \(|x|^2+|y|^2\) terms sum to zero, the
\(x\overline y\) terms sum to \(4x\overline y\), and the conjugate cross
terms sum to zero.

For a complex sequence \(a=(a_n)_{n\in I_x}\), put

\[
 \mathscr A_{q,\chi}[a](v)=
 \sum_{\substack{n\in I_x\\q\nmid n}}
 a_n\overline{\chi(n)}e(vn/H),
 \tag{3.2}
\]

and define its prime-weighted, kernel-localized off-diagonal remainder by

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal V_{\mathcal Q,H}^{\circ}(a)
 :={}&\int_{\mathbb R}\psi_+(v)
 \sum_{q\in\mathcal Q}\frac q{q-1}
 \Bigg(
 \sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}|\mathscr A_{q,\chi}[a](v)|^2\\
 &\hspace{38mm}-(q-2)
 \sum_{\substack{n\in I_x\\q\nmid n}}|a_n|^2
 \Bigg)\,dv.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{3.3}
\]

Now freeze the four literal packets

\[
 \boxed{a_n^{(j)}=\beta(n)+i^jw(n),\qquad 0\leq j\leq3.}
 \tag{3.4}
\]

Applying (3.1) first to the character transforms and then to the diagonal
coefficients gives the exact global identity

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak C_x=\frac14\sum_{j=0}^{3}i^j
 \mathcal V_{\mathcal Q,H}^{\circ}(a^{(j)}).}
 \tag{3.5}
\]

No error, envelope replacement, or extra diagonal occurs in (3.5).  In
particular,

\[
 \frac14\sum_{j=0}^{3}i^j
 |\beta(n)+i^jw(n)|^2=\beta(n)w(n)
 \tag{3.6}
\]

because \(\beta,w\) are real.  This is the V59 route advance: the old
joint-product typing obstruction is replaced by four instances of one
quadratic sequence functional.

## 4. Reduced-residue BDH interpretation

For \(r\in\mathbb F_q^\times\), let

\[
 A_{q,r}[a](v)=
 \sum_{\substack{n\in I_x\\n\equiv r\pmod q}}
 a_ne(vn/H),
 \qquad
 \overline A_q^\times[a](v)=\frac1{q-1}
 \sum_{r\ne0}A_{q,r}[a](v).
 \tag{4.1}
\]

Dirichlet-character orthogonality gives

\[
 \boxed{
 V_q^\times(a;v):=
 \sum_{r\ne0}|A_{q,r}[a](v)-\overline A_q^\times[a](v)|^2
 =\frac1{q-1}\sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}
 |\mathscr A_{q,\chi}[a](v)|^2.}
 \tag{4.2}
\]

The literal diagonal in (4.2) is

\[
 D_q^\times(a)=\frac{q-2}{q-1}
 \sum_{\substack{n\in I_x\\q\nmid n}}|a_n|^2.
 \tag{4.3}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal V_{\mathcal Q,H}^{\circ}(a)=
 \int_{\mathbb R}\psi_+(v)
 \sum_{q\in\mathcal Q}q
 \bigl(V_q^\times(a;v)-D_q^\times(a)\bigr)\,dv.}
 \tag{4.4}
\]

Thus the V35/V58 scalar is a signed combination of four *remainders after
the reduced-residue BDH diagonal*, not four positive variances.  Each
\(\mathcal V^\circ\) can have either sign, and (3.5) may use cancellation
between the four packets.

The strong source-shaped sufficient hypothesis

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf H_{4\mathrm{BDH}}(\delta):\quad
 \sum_{j=0}^{3}
 |\mathcal V_{\mathcal Q,H}^{\circ}(a^{(j)})|
 \ll x^{5/3-\delta+o(1)}}
 \tag{4.5}
\]

implies (1.7).  It is strictly stronger than the signed estimate consumed by
the endpoint; V59 does not assume that four outer absolute values are free.

## 5. Mesoscopic block compiler and natural scale

Choose once and for all a smooth bounded-overlap partition
\(\sum_b\eta_b(n)=1\) of \(I_x\), with physical block length \(H\).  Put

\[
 \beta_b(n)=\eta_b(n)\beta(n),\qquad
 w_c(n)=\eta_c(n)w(n),
 \tag{5.1}
\]

\[
 a_{b,c}^{(j)}(n)=\beta_b(n)+i^jw_c(n).
 \tag{5.2}
\]

Applying (3.1) to each ordered pair \((b,c)\), before any absolute value,
and using \(\sum_{b,c}\eta_b(t)\eta_c(u)=1\), gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak C_x=\frac14
 \sum_{b,c}\sum_{j=0}^{3}i^j
 \mathcal V_{\mathcal Q,H}^{\circ}(a_{b,c}^{(j)}).}
 \tag{5.3}
\]

Equation (5.3) is an exact pair partition.  The self-terms generated inside
each quadratic packet cancel only after the signed \(j\)-sum.  They may not
be multiplied by the number of blocks and estimated separately.

Schwartz decay of \(K_H(u-t)\) attaches a factor
\(O_A((1+|b-c|)^{-A})\) to blocks at separation \(|b-c|\).  For fixed
\(A>2\), these separation weights are absolutely summable.  Thus the
collective block-pair mass has the same power scale as one copy of the block
count (with the frozen divisor and partition losses absorbed into
\(x^{o(1)}\)):

\[
 J=\frac{x}{H}x^{o(1)}=x^{11/32+o(1)}.
 \tag{5.4}
\]

The natural unweighted reduced-residue BDH diagonal on one length-\(H\)
block is \(QH\).  The literal outer weight \(q\asymp Q\) raises the local
scale to

\[
 Q^2H=x^{127/96}.
 \tag{5.5}
\]

Collective reassembly over (5.4) therefore has natural scale

\[
 JQ^2H=xQ^2=x^{5/3}.
 \tag{5.6}
\]

The open arithmetic theorem is a fixed saving from (5.6), while retaining
the signed \(j\)-sum, all prime moduli, the diagonal subtraction, and the
same block partition:

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf H_{\mathrm{loc\mbox{-}pol}}(\delta):\quad
 \left|\frac14\sum_{b,c}\sum_{j=0}^{3}i^j
 \mathcal V_{\mathcal Q,H}^{\circ}(a_{b,c}^{(j)})\right|
 \ll xQ^2x^{-\delta+o(1)},\quad
 \delta>\frac1{400}.}
 \tag{5.7}
\]

Formula (5.7) is a source-facing normal form of the same open scalar, not a
claim that block decomposition itself saves a power.  A useful new theorem
must compile its left side collectively; per-block, per-prime, or per-packet
triangles return the natural scale.

## 7. Primary-source attachment matrix

### 7.1 Harper general-sequence BDH

[Harper, arXiv:2412.19644v1, Theorems 1--2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19644)
does treat a general complex sequence and its BDH variance.  This is the
closest source-native quadratic architecture.

It does not prove (4.5) or (5.7):

1. the stated range is \(\sqrt{2X}<Q\leq X\), whereas the unshifted literal
   sequence has ambient endpoint \(X\asymp x\) and \(Q=x^{1/3}\);
2. replacing \(X\) formally by the block length \(H\) makes the numerical
   inequality true by (6.1), but translating a physical block to
   \([1,H]\) changes the distinguished zero residue in the source's
   \((a,q)\)-grouped average;
3. the source averages every dyadic modulus, while the desired object is a
   signed remainder on the prime subset after subtracting its diagonal;
4. Progressions, Non-concentration on Multiples, and the additional sparse
   or integer-like hypotheses have not been proved for the four literal
   packets, uniformly in \(v\) and all blocks;
5. its stated error terms do not supply the required fixed power after the
   literal prime/block/polarization reassembly.

The identity (4.4) is therefore a real architecture crosswalk but not a
Harper theorem attachment.

### 7.2 Blomer--Pascadi and Pascadi

[Blomer--Pascadi, arXiv:2607.24311v1, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24311)
supplies the exact local critical saving in (6.2).  It accepts a fixed
modulus and already-emitted bilinear Kloosterman arrays.  The missing
operation is the whole compiler from (5.3) to those arrays, including axes,
units, smooth cutoffs, the prime shell, and one final signed reassembly.

[Pascadi, arXiv:2404.04239v3, Corollaries 17--18](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04239)
provides powerful post-emitter multilinear and incomplete Kloosterman
averages with sparse-Fourier input.  It likewise does not accept the literal
four-packet BDH remainder before transformation.

### 7.3 Short arithmetic progressions and convolution sources

[Klurman--Mangerel--Teräväinen, arXiv:1909.12280v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12280)
has short-progression variance results and stronger exceptional-set
information for prime moduli, but its coefficients are bounded
multiplicative functions with pretentious-character structure.  None of the
four \(\beta+i^jw\) packets is a source-native member of that class.

[Wright, arXiv:2604.25177v2, Corollary 2.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25177)
handles two \(q\)-independent divisor-bounded convolution arrays in a fixed
residue with a Siegel--Walfisz input and an outer modulus average.  It does
not prove a quadratic prime-BDH remainder for (3.4), nor the blockwise
diagonal-preserving compiler.

No screened primary source proves a power-saving version of (4.5) or (5.7).

## 8. Firewalls and finite falsifiers

### 8.1 Polarization and sign

For

\[
 x=2+3i,\qquad y=-1+2i,
 \tag{8.1}
\]

both sides of (3.1) equal

\[
 x\overline y=4-7i.
 \tag{8.2}
\]

At \(q=5\), put one coefficient in each unit residue and take

\[
 \beta=(1,-2,3,0),\qquad w=(2,1,-1,4).
 \tag{8.3}
\]

After multiplying the reduced-residue row by \(q-1\), its bilinear numerator is

\[
 \sum_r\beta_rw_r-\left(\sum_r\beta_r\right)
 \left(\sum_rw_r\right)=-15,
 \tag{8.4}
\]

and the four quadratic remainders reproduce \(-15\) exactly.  Replacing
\(q-2\) by \(q-1\) instead gives \(-12\); omitting the diagonal subtraction
gives \(-24\).

The one-sequence remainder has both signs:

\[
 \mathcal E(1,1,0,0)=-2,\qquad
 \mathcal E(1,-1,0,0)=2.
 \tag{8.5}
\]

Thus neither (3.3) nor (3.5) is a positivity argument.

### 8.2 Translation and prime-subset no-go

For residue masses \((10,1,0,0,0)\pmod5\), the variance over the four
residues excluding class \(0\) is \(3/4\), while excluding class \(1\)
gives \(75\).  This finite fixture proves that moving a physical block to
the origin does not preserve the reduced-residue variance used in (4.2).

An all-modulus signed remainder does not control a prime subfamily.  The
two-row scalar fixture

\[
 R_5=1,\qquad R_6=-1
 \tag{8.6}
\]

has total zero but prime-subset sum one.  This is a typing falsifier, not a
claim that (8.6) is produced by the physical coefficients.

### 8.3 Generic-sequence and reassembly firewalls

No theorem for arbitrary complex sequences can give (5.7) from divisor
envelopes alone: a coherent packet concentrated in one residue can retain
the natural BDH scale.  The theorem must use the literal Möbius/prime-hybrid
signs.

The following operations are forbidden:

1. changing \(q-2\) to \(q-1\), or restoring the diagonal;
2. replacing the signed \(j\)-sum by four outer absolute values without
   explicitly assuming the stronger hypothesis (4.5);
3. extracting the prime subset from an all-modulus signed asymptotic;
4. translating blocks while silently changing the distinguished zero
   residue;
5. triangulating over blocks, prime moduli, or transformed cells;
6. promoting the local \(q^{-1/32}\) clock match to a global theorem.
