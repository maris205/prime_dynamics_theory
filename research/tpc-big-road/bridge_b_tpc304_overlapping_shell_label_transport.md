TPC-304 reads the source-first weighted labels generated by TPC-302 and
restricts adjacent Q-shell pairs to their common primes.  Independent global
sign gauges are aligned before computing the exact binary-label correlation.
The six rows have group-mean correlations `1/2`, `1/11`, `1/2`; the middle
transition is the unique declared `rho<=1/3` fracture.  The locked TPC-303
census has budget descents `3,15,3` and same-prefix descents `0,9,0`, so the
minimum-correlation and maximum-descent transition coincide.

This is a finite localization crosswalk, not a causal theorem.  The physical
shell/operator and source-first target both change, and no counterfactual
transported-label budget is evaluated.  The Session-named Route-A/Route-B
evaluator files are absent from this checkout; the local proof package,
parent-locked certificate, independent replay, stress suite, PDF audit, and
Bridge-B checker are the fail-closed validation path.
