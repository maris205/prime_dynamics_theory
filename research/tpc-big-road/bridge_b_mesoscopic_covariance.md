For `a mod q`, define

```text
I_(q,a)={t in I_x : t=a mod q},
n_(q,a)=#I_(q,a),
B_(q,a)=sum_(t in I_(q,a))beta_x^raw(t),
```

```text

```

The actual `n_(q,a)` is retained throughout.  Replacing it by `x/q`, `X/q`
or a normalized-Haar weight is a different formula.

For every single `q`, direct finite algebra gives

```text
S_x=H_q+C_q,                                           (2.4)

S_x=sum_(t in I_x)beta_x^raw(t)w_x^(z)(t),
```

There are no empty fibers for sufficiently large `x`; in the finite algebra



## 3. Exact local profiles

Fix one `q in Q_x`.  For every fixed `K` and sufficiently large `x`, `q>z`.
The shifted-prime local profile is

```text
ell_q(a)=0,          a=-2 mod q,
         q/(q-1),    otherwise.                        (3.1)
```

The special row contains only the possible prime powers `t+2=q^j`; those
terms remain in the error and are never declared identically zero.

The committed tensor-local comparison has, for `q>z`, profile

```text
g_q(a)=q/(q-1),                  a=0 mod q,
       q(q-2)/(q-1)^2,           otherwise.            (3.2)
```

Therefore the residual profile `d_q=ell_q-g_q` is

```text
d_q(0)=0,
d_q(-2)=-q(q-2)/(q-1)^2,
d_q(a)=q/(q-1)^2,                a not in {0,-2},       (3.3)

sum_(a mod q)d_q(a)=0.                               (3.4)
```

The zero total in (3.4) is an Euler-profile statement.  Because the shell
fibers are ragged, `sum_a n_(q,a)d_q(a)` need not vanish.

Define literal AP errors by

```text
sum_(t in I_(q,a))Lambda(t+2)
  =n_(q,a)ell_q(a)+E_Lambda(q,a),

sum_(t in I_(q,a))b_x^(z)(t)
  =n_(q,a)g_q(a)+E_b(q,a),

E_w(q,a)=E_Lambda(q,a)-E_b(q,a).                      (3.5)
```

Then the finite identity is

```text
W_(q,a)=n_(q,a)d_q(a)+E_w(q,a).                       (3.6)
```

The comparison remainder in (3.5) is essential.  A local Euler factor is
not an exact finite-interval AP theorem.

## 4. Source-backed AP compiler for the low-frequency mean

This section distinguishes primary inputs from the corollary derived here.
The primary inputs are:

- the classical maximal Bombieri--Vinogradov theorem, originating with
  [Bombieri](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300005313) and
  [Vinogradov](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im3080), with a maximal-endpoint
  formulation in [Vaughan, Corollary 1.1.1](https://personal.science.psu.edu/rcv4/personal/Publications/s2-10-2-153.pdf);
- [Iwaniec's Rosser sieve and fundamental lemma, Theorem 1](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-36-2-171-202),
  *Acta Arithmetica* 36 (1980), 171--202;
- the already source-locked tensor-local Euler formula for `b_x^(z)` in
  `fm_local_comparison_compiler.md`;
- the V19 literal raw coefficient and (1.2).

The all-residue AP statement for `b_x^(z)` below is a derived corollary of
those inputs.  It is not attributed verbatim to Iwaniec or Vaughan.

### 4.1 The shifted-prime remainder

For `a!=-2`, `t+2` lies in the reduced residue `a+2 mod q`.  Applying the
maximal Bombieri--Vinogradov theorem to the two shell endpoints gives, for
every fixed `A_*`,

```text
sum_(q in Q_x) max_(a mod q)|E_Lambda(q,a)|
 <<_(A_*) x/log^(A_*)x+R_x log x.                     (4.1)
```

The `R_x log x` term pays all `a=-2` prime-power rows.  This is an averaged
modulus statement; it is not promoted to a prescribed single-modulus bound.

### 4.2 The hybrid-comparison remainder

The committed comparison can be written

```text
b_x^(z)(t)
 =1_(I_x)(t) C_z W_z 1_((t+2,P(z))=1)
  sum_(d|t)g_z(d),                                    (4.2)

C_z=product_(p>z)(1-1/(p-1)^2),
W_z=product_(p<=z)p/(p-1),

g_z(d)=mu^2(d)1_(P^-(d)>z)product_(p|d)1/(p-2).       (4.3)
```

For `q>z`, the rough-divisor sum must first be split explicitly.  On the row
`a=0`, write every squarefree rough divisor uniquely as either `d=e` or
`d=qe`, with `q` not dividing `e`; the two contributions combine by

```text
1+1/(q-2)=(q-1)/(q-2).                               (4.3a)
```

On every row `a!=0`, `q` cannot divide `t`, so only `d=e` occurs.  Multiplying
this forced/excluded factor by the `q` factor already present in `C_z` gives
exactly (3.2).  After this split, every divisor denoted `d` below is coprime
to `q`; there is no illegal invocation of CRT with `q|d`.

To turn that Euler identity into a finite AP estimate, choose

```text
E=log^(L_E)x,
D=x^(1/6).                                             (4.4)
```

Rename the coprime cofactor `e` as `d` and truncate that cofactor at `d<=E`;
on the row `a=0` the corresponding full rough divisor may be `qd`.  Since
`(qd,P(z))=1`, the conditions
`t=a mod q`, `d|t` and the one forbidden residue modulo every `p<=z` are
compatible CRT conditions.  Rosser--Iwaniec upper and lower weights at level
`D` give, uniformly in the complete `q,a` family,

```text
E_b(q,a)
 <<_(K) epsilon_x n_(q,a)
       +D log^C x
       +x/(qE)log^C x
       +log^C x,                                      (4.5)

epsilon_x<<_(A_*,K)log^(-A_*)x.                       (4.6)
```

The lattice remainder is `D log^C x`.  The two tail terms follow from

```text
g_z(d)<=3^omega(d)/d                                  (4.7)
```

and fixed divisor moments.  The fundamental-lemma parameter satisfies

```text
log D/log z -> infinity                               (4.8)
```

for fixed `K`.  First choose `L_E=L_E(A_*,K)`, then take `x` sufficiently
large.  Because `q asymp x^(1/3)`, (4.5) becomes

```text
max_(q in Q_x,a mod q)|E_b(q,a)|
 <<_(A_*,K) x/[q log^(A_*)x].                         (4.9)
```

The two power-scale errors are safely below the target:

```text
Q_mes D=x^(1/2),
x/Q_mes=x^(2/3).                                      (4.10)
```

This proof does not relabel the earlier multiplier Type-I theorem as an AP
theorem.  It reuses the source-backed sieve mechanism and supplies the new
CRT/lattice derivation explicitly.

## 7. Why centering is not automatic smoothing

Let `E_M` be counting-measure conditional expectation on the wrapped residue
fibers of a finite shell.  Then

```text
sum_t beta(t)w(t)
 =sum_t E_M beta(t) E_M w(t)
  +sum_t [beta(t)-E_M beta(t)][w(t)-E_M w(t)],         (7.1)
```

with exact energy Pythagoras.  But `I-E_M` has norm one: a mean-zero vector
inside one fiber is unchanged.  Therefore no universal energy contraction or
signed saving follows from the word "centered".

Literal V19 finite data at `x=166` make the warning physical.  The raw
coefficient has support `30` and energy

```text
sum_t|beta_166^raw(t)|^2=64177/1764.                  (7.2)
```

The centered-energy ratios are

```text
M=30: 16340/192531,
M=35: 3544/6639.                                      (7.3)
```

They are neither uniformly tiny nor monotone under a nonnested change of
modulus.  A literal-coefficient/synthetic-residual witness at `M=30` has

```text
beta(91)=-1,
beta(121)=-1/2,
w=e_91-e_121,
H_30=0,
C_30=-1/2.                                            (7.4)
```

This is a functional counterexample to automatic covariance smoothing, not a
claim that the synthetic `w` equals the actual prime residual.  Conversely,
`beta(90)=beta(120)=beta(150)=2` gives a fiber-constant synthetic witness with
the entire scalar `6` in the mean branch.  Neither branch is negligible by
algebra alone.

There is also no fixed-low-rank bypass hidden in centering.  If the shell has
`H=#I_x` points and `H>=q`, all `q` fibers are nonempty.  Their indicator
vectors are disjoint, so

```text
rank(E_q)=q,
rank(I-E_q)=H-q.                                      (7.5)
```

At `q=x^(1/3)` this is still `H-O(x^(1/3))`, asymptotically full physical
rank.  This is a rank theorem for the complete centered shell, not a claim
about the rank of one specially selected coefficient family.

Mean-only state also fails the physical deletion clock.  On
`I=(83,166]`, `M=30`, `p=7`, let

```text
f=e_84-e_114.                                         (7.6)
```

The three points in that residue fiber are `84,114,144`, so `E_30 f=0`.
After applying the pair-deletion mask for `p=7`, `84` is deleted and `114`
survives, hence

```text
E_30(D_7 f)(84)=-1/3.                                 (7.7)
```

Thus a closed exact dynamics must retain centered/martingale information; a
mean-only carrier does not commute with deletion.

## 9. Dynamical meaning of the new road

The wrapped decomposition gives a concrete target for a nonautonomous
symbolic/logistic/Hénon carrier: it would have to control the signed
fluctuation in (6.2), uniformly over the predeclared prime ensemble, while
preserving the exact arithmetic coding and the distinguished seed.

This is more precise than asking for positive measure or ordinary ergodicity.
Positive fiber mass, complete-frequency Parseval, almost-everywhere
genericity or recurrence do not prove the prescribed scalar (6.2).  A valid
dynamical bridge still needs a target-independent affine input-output law,
exact physical event coding, a deterministic distinguished-seed return and
the full arithmetic normalization.  Hénon remains a possible carrier/geometry
auxiliary, not an automatic source of prime cancellation.

The important progress is architectural: classical arithmetic now removes
the entire wrapped mean branch, so any proposed dynamics or dispersion engine
can be tested directly against one exact centered observable rather than the
unresolved mixture `S_x`.
