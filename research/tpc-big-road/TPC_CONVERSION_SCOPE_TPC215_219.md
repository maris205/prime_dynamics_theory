## Preserved discrepancies and source qualifications

- TPC215 has literal `^{,2}` in the [first harmonic expression](../../papers/tpc-215-short-quotient-mobius-majorant/paper/main.tex#L255)
  and [second expression](../../papers/tpc-215-short-quotient-mobius-majorant/paper/main.tex#L299),
  visible in PDF equations (12)/(18), page 4. Its [finite-energy display](../../papers/tpc-215-short-quotient-mobius-majorant/paper/main.tex#L493)
  has `,qquad` without the command backslash, also printed in equation (33),
  page 7. The package's harmonic squares are ordinary squares. The [setup](../../papers/tpc-215-short-quotient-mobius-majorant/paper/main.tex#L134)
  admits d≤U, whereas nearby prose and the package say d<U; the unit argument
  only needs d≤U<Q<q. These originals remain unchanged.
- TPC216's [route shorthand](../../papers/tpc-216-direct-sum-row-energy-envelope/paper/main.tex#L281)
  omits the displayed division by L. The precise theorem on PDF page 3 bounds
  E_dir/L, not unnormalized complete-period energy. Its explicit lack of a
  matching lower bound remains attached.
- TPC217's [equation (6)](../../papers/tpc-217-finite-window-rational-large-sieve/paper/main.tex#L151)
  and [derivation](../../papers/tpc-217-finite-window-rational-large-sieve/DERIVATION_PACKAGE.md#L126)
  replace (log U)³ with (log x)³ by literal equality, dropping the constant
  (133/400)³. This is scale equivalence, not exact equality. The parent
  spot-checked the printed TeX before archiving the qualification.
- TPC217's [finite fixture](../../papers/tpc-217-finite-window-rational-large-sieve/paper/main.tex#L288)
  describes a full squarefree band (2,35] but does not locally exclude divisors
  noncoprime to {11,13,17}, though the inverse notation requires units.
  TPC215's [fixture](../../papers/tpc-215-short-quotient-mobius-majorant/paper/main.tex#L448)
  supplies that exclusion. The [TPC217 README](../../papers/tpc-217-finite-window-rational-large-sieve/README.md#L54)
  calls pointwise agreement exact, whereas the manuscript qualifies it by
  floating-point rounding. No numerical artifact was opened to resolve it.
- TPC217 and TPC218 use e(t) without defining it locally. The interpretation
  exp(2πit) is inferred from their Fourier context and TPC215's explicit
  convention; it is not a newly supplied local definition or a source edit.
- TPC218's [active-cluster lemma](../../papers/tpc-218-prime-shell-packet-lift/paper/main.tex#L214)
  states a sum over all h≤U, but its [proof](../../papers/tpc-218-prime-shell-packet-lift/paper/main.tex#L224)
  restricts to active h≥H/(2Q). It also calls active h a subinterval where
  only containment is established. This is a statement/proof-scope mismatch,
  not a finding here that the stated bound is false. The parent spot-checked
  both ranges. The [derivation's Gram bound](../../papers/tpc-218-prime-shell-packet-lift/DERIVATION_PACKAGE.md#L68)
  omits the unit-vector qualification stated in the manuscript.
- TPC218 encodes “The large sieve” as a book/publisher entry in its
  [bibliography](../../papers/tpc-218-prime-shell-packet-lift/paper/references.bib#L1),
  whereas TPC217's [entry](../../papers/tpc-217-finite-window-rational-large-sieve/paper/references.bib#L1)
  uses article/journal fields. That local disagreement is preserved, not
  externally adjudicated. A proof-package pointer to an outside row record
  was not followed; the allowed manuscript supplies the needed row definition.
- TPC219's [Table 1](../../papers/tpc-219-prime-shell-longitudinal-ledger/paper/main.tex#L154)
  labels E_diag=E_perp=4,E_shell=0 “orthogonal” without giving the vectors.
  If it means pairwise orthogonal q-vectors, the label is incompatible with
  those energies. This remains an unresolved fixture label; neither code
  nor certificate was inspected. The parent confirmed the literal table row.

The literal coefficient is mu(d)log(d)/d with its integer cutoff masks and
profile weights. TPC218's rational fixture instead uses mu(d)/d and explicitly
identifies it as a surrogate. Neither source-reported finite result supplies
the final canonical signed coefficient/outer-weight attachment.
