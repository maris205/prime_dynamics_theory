# Bridge-B note: TPC-291 signed Schur cancellation atlas

TPC-291 is the next local bridge after the TPC-290 weighted-Gram firewall.
For every nonzero pair of physical prime-component vectors it proves the
exact projection identity
`min_rho ||g_i-rho*g_j||^2/d_i = 1-Gamma_(i,j)` and the normalized signed
two-vector Rayleigh minimum `1-sqrt(Gamma_(i,j))`.  The sign of the projection
coefficient records whether the minimizing direction needs opposite or
same signs.

The exact-rational finite replay covers all 1,380 unordered pairs in the
18-row TPC-289 grid.  It finds 1,377 positive and 3 negative cross terms;
the residual thresholds `1/2`, `1/4`, and `1/10` contain 1,074, 852, and 477
pairs.  The best tested pair is `(173,179)` at the late `(512,58,90,5,2)`
growth row, with residual approximately `0.0151239493`.  The three negative
pairs are `(29,53)`, `(31,53)`, and `(41,53)` at the early crossover row.

The atlas is pairwise and finite.  It does not claim that the low-residual
directions can be assembled into a multi-prime null vector, nor that their
coefficients are generated by the literal arithmetic source.  The
Session-named Route-A/Route-B evaluator files are absent from this checkout;
the project proof package, canonical certificate, reverse-order replay,
stress audit, and checker are the local fail-closed validation package.
