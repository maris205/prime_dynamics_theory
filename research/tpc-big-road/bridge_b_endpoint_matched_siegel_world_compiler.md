# Bridge A / Gates A--B V50: endpoint-matched moving conductor and the two Siegel-quality worlds

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: unnumbered big-road research artifact. V49 paid one fixed critical
conductor collar and exposed a local-centered principal/generic/exceptional
signed scalar. V50 replaces that one midpoint by an open family of
self-financing cuts. Every prescribed strict saving
\(0<\delta<1/9600\) chooses

\[
 D_\delta=x^{1/200+2\delta}
\]

and the V45 block estimate pays the complementary conductor range at exactly
the numerator exponent \(1997/1200-\delta\). The remaining endpoint core is
still an open arithmetic theorem. V50 then makes a second, genuinely global
route split. If primitive quadratic Siegel-zero qualities are unbounded,
Matomaki--Merikoski's Corollary 1.1 gives the twin prime conjecture directly.
If they are bounded, Bridge A only needs a family of signed-core estimates
whose saving and implied constants may depend on that one fixed global bound.
This is an exhaustive route compiler, not an unconditional arithmetic
advance. A Landau--Page singleton at one scale does not select the unbounded
world.

## 1. Frozen transition scalar and an open saving parameter

Retain the V45--V49 scales

\[
 H=x^{21/32},\qquad Q=x^{1/3},\qquad
 P=\frac{Q^2}{H}=x^{1/96},\qquad
 D_0=P^{1/2}=x^{1/192},
 \tag{1.1}
\]

and the transition numerator target

\[
 T_{\rm num}=\frac{1997}{1200}.
 \tag{1.2}
\]

Fix, before estimating any scalar,

\[
 \boxed{0<\delta<\frac1{9600}.}
 \tag{1.3}
\]

Define

\[
 \boxed{
 \beta(\delta)=\frac1{200}+2\delta,
 \qquad D_\delta=x^{\beta(\delta)}.}
 \tag{1.4}
\]

The interval in (1.3) is exactly the strict interior collar condition:

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac1{200}<\beta(\delta)<\frac1{192}.}
 \tag{1.5}
\]

Indeed,
\(1/192-1/200=1/4800\). The V49 cut is recovered at

\[
 \delta=\frac1{19200},\qquad
 \beta(\delta)=\frac{49}{9600}.
 \tag{1.6}
\]

For a cut \(Z\), let \(\mathfrak M_{<Z}^{\rm tr}\) and

## 2. The moving cut finances its own strict saving

For a dyadic primitive-conductor block \(c\asymp D\), V45 proved

\[
 \mathcal V_D^{(2)}(S)
 \ll P^2\left(\frac DQ+\frac1D\right)x^{o(1)}.
 \tag{2.1}
\]

Since \(D_\delta<D_0\) and \(D_0D_\delta<Q\), summing the collar
\(D_\delta\leq D<D_0\) yields

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal K_{[D_\delta,D_0)}
 &\ll P^2\left(\frac{D_0}{Q}+\frac1{D_\delta}\right)x^{o(1)}\\
 &\ll x^{1/48-1/200-2\delta+o(1)}\\
 &=x^{19/1200-2\delta+o(1)}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{2.2}
\]

The old range \(D\geq D_0\) has energy

\[
 \mathcal K_{\geq D_0}\ll P^{3/2}x^{o(1)}
 =x^{1/64+o(1)}.
 \tag{2.3}
\]

For the strict interval (1.3),
\(19/1200-2\delta>1/64\). Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal K_{\geq D_\delta}
 \ll x^{19/1200-2\delta+o(1)}.}
 \tag{2.4}
\]

Pairing (2.4) with the unchanged physical additive-large-sieve energy gives

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\mathfrak V_{\geq D_\delta}^{\rm tr}|
 &\ll x^{21/32+1+(19/1200-2\delta)/2+o(1)}\\
 &=\boxed{x^{1997/1200-\delta+o(1)}}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{2.5}
\]

Thus the cut is saving-matched: moving it upward by \(2\delta\) in conductor
exponent creates exactly \(\delta\) of numerator margin. At \(\delta=0\)
the strict margin vanishes. At \(\delta=1/9600\), the cut reaches the old
boundary \(D_0\); that V48 endpoint remains valid upstream but is outside the
open V50 interior family.

## 5. Siegel-zero quality and the two global worlds

For a primitive quadratic character \(\chi\pmod d\) with a real zero
\(\beta\), write

\[
 \boxed{
 \beta=1-\frac1{\eta(\chi)\log d},
 \qquad \eta(\chi)\geq10.}
 \tag{5.1}
\]

Let \(\mathscr E\) be the set of all such qualities. Pure logic gives the
exhaustive dichotomy

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathscr E\ \hbox{is unbounded}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \mathscr E\ \hbox{is bounded}.}
 \tag{5.2}
\]

The empty set belongs to the bounded world. This is a global statement over
all primitive quadratic characters, not the Landau--Page empty/singleton
alternative at one value of \(x\).

### 5.1 Unbounded-quality world: a source-backed direct exit

If \(\mathscr E\) is unbounded, choose primitive quadratic characters
\(\chi_j\pmod{d_j}\) with \(\eta_j:=\eta(\chi_j)\to\infty\). The conductors
also tend to infinity. Matomaki--Merikoski, Corollary 1.1(i), applied with
\(h=2\) and \(X_j=d_j^{10}\), gives for every fixed \(C\geq1\)

\[
 \sum_{n\leq X_j}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+2)
 =X_j\mathfrak S_2
 +O_C\!\left(X_j e^{-C\sqrt{\log\eta_j}}\right),
 \tag{5.3}
\]

because \(X_j\in[d_j^{10},d_j^{10\log\eta_j}]\). Here

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak S_2
 =2\prod_{p>2}\left(1-\frac1{(p-1)^2}\right)>0.}
 \tag{5.4}
\]

Terms for which \(n\) or \(n+2\) is a proper prime power contribute at most

\[
 O\!\left(X_j^{1/2}\log^3 X_j\right)=o(X_j).
 \tag{5.5}
\]

After removing them, (5.3)--(5.5) leave a positive weighted twin-prime sum of
order \(X_j\). Since each remaining weight is at most \(\log^2 X_j\), the
number of twin-prime pairs up to \(X_j+2\) tends to infinity. Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathscr E\ \hbox{unbounded}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \text{the twin prime conjecture}.}
 \tag{5.6}
\]

This implication is source-backed. Its hypothesis is not known, so it creates
no current TPC trigger.

### 5.2 Bounded-quality world: the reduced Bridge A theorem

Suppose instead that \(\mathscr E\) is bounded. Then there exists one finite
\(B\geq10\) such that \(\eta(\chi)\leq B\) for every member of
\(\mathscr E\). The weakest route-level family retained by V50 is

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathsf H_{\rm bd}(B):\quad
 \text{there exists }\delta_B\in(0,1/9600)\text{ such that}\\
 |\mathfrak C_{\delta_B}|
 \ll_B x^{1997/1200-\delta_B+o(1)}
 \quad(x\geq x_0(B)).
 \end{gathered}}
 \tag{5.7}
\]

The theorem family has the quantifier order

\[
 \boxed{
 \forall B<\infty\ \exists\delta_B\ \exists C_B,x_0(B)\
 \forall x\geq x_0(B).}
 \tag{5.8}
\]

The saving, threshold, and implied constant may depend on the fixed global
quality bound \(B\), but not on the later value of \(x\) or on a favorable
sign of the scalar. This is strictly weaker than a theorem uniform in
arbitrarily high exceptional quality. It remains an open arithmetic theorem.

Combining (5.2), (5.6), and (5.7) gives the V50 route compiler:

```text
unbounded global Siegel quality
  -> source-backed direct twin-prime exit;

bounded global Siegel quality by B
  -> prove one B-dependent endpoint-matched signed core H_bd(B)
  -> transition part of Bridge A is paid.
```

The bounded branch still leaves the long balanced and reverse-Type-I windows,
V42 Gate B, and the V43 A+B join. It is not itself a proof of TPC.

## 6. Quantifier and cancellation firewalls

Five distinctions are load-bearing.

1. A possible Landau--Page singleton at each scale does not imply that the
   corresponding qualities are unbounded. The singleton may be absent, may
   recur, or may have uniformly bounded quality.
2. A finite list of increasing qualities is not a proof of an unbounded
   sequence. The global world cannot be selected from a finite checker.
3. In the bounded world, \(B\) is fixed before \(\delta_B\), and
   \(\delta_B\) is fixed before all sufficiently large \(x\). Reversing those
   quantifiers makes (5.7) adaptive and circular.
4. The direct signed three-lane scalar remains primary. Triangle bounds for
   the three marginals are only a stronger fallback.
5. The exceptional lane is retained in the bounded world. A bound on its
   quality does not make its induced tower vanish.

## 7. Primary-source screen

The screen is primary-source-only and fail-closed as of 2026-08-11.

1. Bombieri--Friedlander--Iwaniec,
   [*Primes in arithmetic progressions to large moduli*](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/6385-11511_2006_Article_BF02399204.pdf),
   supplies the primitive-character large-sieve engine inherited through V45.
   It supports Section 2 after the repository's exact coefficient ledger; it
   does not prove (5.7).

2. Ford--Green--Konyagin--Maynard--Tao,
   [*Long gaps between primes*, Lemma 7.1,
   arXiv:1412.5029v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5029), supplies the
   Landau--Page empty/singleton primitive quadratic alternative used in
   Section 4. It says nothing about global unboundedness of the qualities.

3. Matomaki--Merikoski,
   [*Siegel Zeros, Twin Primes, Goldbach's Conjecture, and Primes in Short
   Intervals*, Corollary 1.1(i)](https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article/2023/23/20337/7111993),
   proves (5.3) for a fixed shift and
   \(X\in[d^{10},d^{10\log\eta}]\). This attaches literally to the
   unbounded-quality world and proves the conditional exit (5.6). It does not
   estimate the V50 signed core.

4. Sachpazis,
   [*Primes in arithmetic progressions under the presence of Landau--Siegel
   zeroes*, Theorem 1.1, arXiv:2511.16452v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16452),
   assumes \(x=D^V\) with \(V\geq200/\varepsilon\),
   \(0<\varepsilon<1/100\), and estimates one fixed-residue
   \(\psi(x;q,a)\). The V50 core extends up to
   \(D\asymp D_\delta=x^{1/200+2\delta}\); at that top edge one has
   \(V=(1/200+2\delta)^{-1}<200\), whereas the source requires
   \(V\geq200/\varepsilon>20000\). Thus it may inform a much smaller
   conductor subrange, but it cannot cover the whole V50 core. Its object is
   also not (4.2).

5. Wright,
   [*Primes in Arithmetic Progressions to Large Moduli and Siegel Zeroes*,
   Theorem 2.2, arXiv:2507.10780v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10780), assumes
   \(\log D=(\log x)^\kappa\) with \(\kappa<1\), hence
   \(D=x^{o(1)}\), and controls AP residue counts. It can therefore address
   only a subpower-conductor subrange, not the polynomial top edge
   \(D_\delta=x^{1/200+2\delta}\) of the whole V50 core; its object is also an
   AP residue count rather than the induced signed prime--hybrid scalar.

No checked primary theorem proves the bounded-quality family (5.7), either
directly or through all three stronger marginal lanes. This is the first
analytic fatal.

## 9. Route position

V50 advances the analytic-elimination map in two ways. First, every desired
strict margin below \(1/9600\) now has a canonical cut that pays its own
complement. Second, the exceptional obstruction is no longer treated as one
undifferentiated fog: globally unbounded quality has a source-backed TPC exit,
while globally bounded quality leaves a strictly weaker \(B\)-dependent
signed-core theorem. Neither world is currently known to hold in the form
needed to complete the program, and no arithmetic credit is booked.

The large-road order is

```text
global Siegel-quality dichotomy
  -> unbounded: direct source-backed TPC exit
  -> bounded: endpoint-matched B-dependent signed core
  -> long balanced Type II / reverse-Type-I windows
  -> V42 positive physical Gram Gate B in parallel
  -> V43 exact A+B zero-axis reassembly
  -> distinguished-seed dynamics reserve.
```
