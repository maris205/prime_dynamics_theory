## Bounded prerequisite and source-wording qualifications

- **TPC165, finite typed gluing:** main.tex L122–166 (p2) requires finite
  nonempty local row families, typed payloads, rational weights with exact
  column sum one, and typed weight-preserving overlap bijections satisfying
  identity, inverse and triple cocycle. Rational weights are not stated
  nonnegative. The imported five-field key at L98–109 (p1) is an archive
  address, not a physical coefficient. L171–251 (pp2–3) states finite
  gluing and matrix conservation, not natural-scale physical loss payment.
  L297 (p4) separates formal totality, actual active support and canonical/
  minimal representation. The two-patch/three-cut/four-copy/three-global-row
  fixture L273–293 (p3) is synthetic and not rerun; zero production edges
  L335–353 (p4) are an imported frozen-corpus statement. Source-record
  fields at L131 (p2) do not instantiate a complete local production
  path/hash/theorem/formula/derivation record in the allowed texts.
- **TPC166, a crosswalk sub-DAG:** main.tex L184–278 (pp2–3) refines only
  the monolithic crosswalk node into local edges, actual support and
  canonical/minimal representation. Full H1 remains larger at L140 (p2):
  map-side nine zero-defect conditions plus independent registry totality,
  or the scalar complete-FUM o(X) and theorem-backed ETO alternative.
  The selected root at L287–320 (p3) is work order/deterministic display,
  not a canonical-representation theorem. Future required payloads at
  L414–427 (p5) are requested, not supplied. Zero edges, nine source locks,
  NOT_TESTABLE and historical route-not-stopped language L324–369 (p4)
  are not fresh production verification or a change to TPC418. L349 and
  L393 (p4) do not enumerate nine exact path/hash pairs, and the source's
  both-certificate/audit-pairs wording is retained, not reconstructed.
- **TPC167, block phase mean square:** main.tex L64–93 (p1) explicitly
  requires a,s>=1, integral d,u, coprimality, odd as and su-ad=2, with
  q=as, t(z)=ad+qz and c_z=mu(d+sz)mu(u+az). For N>0 the block is
  I_N={z:N<t(z)<=2N}; F_N uses q/N and negative phase e(-alpha*z).
  The energy/count bound is E_N<=L_N<=N/q+1. The source identity and
  bad-phase measure bound L97–145 (p2) quantify over N>0 and lambda>0,
  not exceptional scales. The complete grid L149–175 (p2) uses r/M and
  M>=L_N without separately spelling out positive integral M or an empty-
  interval convention. The power envelope L179–201 (p3) states small
  polylogarithmic q and N>=sqrt(X), with no local N<=X requirement.
  Analytic phase-L2 is distinct from program-positive L2. The all-one
  phase-zero example L203–231 (p3) is coefficient-blind, not a literal
  Mobius realization. A determinant relation does not identify physical h0.
- **TPC168, finite separated registry:** main.tex L64–114 (pp1–2) uses
  a positive-phase polynomial, L coefficients, circular separation
  0<delta<=1 and factor delta^(-1)+4*pi*(L-1). The retained negative-phase
  block core L118–132 (p2) does not restate all TPC167 determinant-data
  restrictions. The application mentions a shift/unit modulus without
  separately documenting phase reversal; this is a bookkeeping omission,
  not a finding that the inequality is false. L134–176 (p2) requires
  lambda>0, delta>=c/M and M>=theta*L_N for fixed c,theta>0, with cost
  c^(-1)+4*pi/theta. Bad-density control as q/N tends to zero does not
  select a named good phase. The all-one selector fixture L201–229 (p3)
  is not asserted to be the literal core, and no actual production
  registry/selector theorem is provided at L231–246 (p3).
- **TPC169, cumulative finite-terminal prefixes:** main.tex L66–90 (p1)
