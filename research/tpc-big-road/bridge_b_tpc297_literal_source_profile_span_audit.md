## Interpretation

TPC-296 showed that an unrestricted finite source witness can be cheap while
the frozen one-ray proxy points in the wrong direction.  TPC-297 replaces
that proxy by the source-side span generated by the four literal cutoff
profiles
`beta_z(t)=lambda(t)-sum_{d<=z,d|t}mu(d)` for `z=3,5,7,11`.  With `U` the
profile matrix and `V=A^T U`, the exact restricted target problem is

```text
min_c ||Vc-b||^2 = b^T(I-P_V)b.
```

Two independent modular rank computations give rank 3 on the row with three
shell primes and rank 4 on all 17 other rows.  The 70-digit atlas then finds
the all-positive target within RMS `0.15` on all rows, but the weighted target
outside RMS `0.6` on all 17 large-shell rows.  The four-profile residual is
never worse than the TPC-296 one-ray residual, as it must be because the
frozen beta direction is included in the declared span.

The profile family is finite and explicitly declared.  It is not claimed to
exhaust the native V59 source class, and no asymptotic rank, principal-angle,
source-budget, arithmetic `L2`, Gate-B, or twin-prime conclusion follows.
The Session-named evaluator files are absent; the local proof package,
canonical certificate, independent replay, stress suite, PDF audit, and this
Bridge-B checker are the fail-closed validation path.
