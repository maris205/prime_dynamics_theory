## Preserved source discrepancies and qualifications

### TPC225–227: domain, scalar, clock, and target qualifications

- TPC225's [proof package](../../papers/tpc-225-cutoff-one-shared-clock-obstruction/PROOF_PACKAGE.md#L5)
  omits explicit integrality of Q and allows a common scalar C_h, while the
  manuscript fixes integer Q and C_h=1/h. Its complex-extension sentence
  must not extend the [affine formulas](../../papers/tpc-225-cutoff-one-shared-clock-obstruction/PROOF_PACKAGE.md#L144),
  printed with C_h², S_2, and S_1², to unrestricted complex scalars/slopes.
  The real manuscript setting avoids that extension issue. The inherited
  [PJ/(P+J) expression](../../papers/tpc-225-cutoff-one-shared-clock-obstruction/paper/main.tex#L49)
  also lacks a local definition of P,J at that point.
- TPC226's [signed-energy section](../../papers/tpc-226-first-primitive-collision-transition/paper/sections/4_signed_energy.tex#L10)
  uses h where the setup defines h_L. Its dilation-four normalization binds
  h=h_4=16Q, not physical h0. Its [package pointer](../../papers/tpc-226-first-primitive-collision-transition/PROOF_PACKAGE.md#L45)
  to a separate derivation package was not followed; the allowed manuscript
  setup itself contains the required row definition. The claimed census and
  “first stable” receipt were not replayed.
- TPC227's [aligned fixture](../../papers/tpc-227-packet-profile-axis-separation/paper/sections/4_collision_witness.tex#L24)
  is T=(1,1)/400, with Gram scale 1/160000. Calling it a physical target does
  not identify it with the literal V59 transform. No additional algebraic
  text discrepancy was located in this bounded Four-Gram reading.

### TPC228: complex conjugation and unresolved amplitude conventions

The [Q25 specialization](../../papers/tpc-228-source-native-polarized-collision-compiler/paper/sections/4_q25_block.tex#L10)
and [proof package](../../papers/tpc-228-source-native-polarized-collision-compiler/PROOF_PACKAGE.md#L49)
print beta-times-w products without conjugating the second channel. The
setup is complex and linear in the first slot, and no explicit real-amplitude
restriction or conjugated-w definition appears locally. With one directed
beta=1,w=i term, the printed product is +i/160000 whereas the corresponding
Hilbert term is −i/160000. This bounded convention check qualifies the
specialization; it is not a repair of the original or a new theorem. The
omission also occurs in PDF equation (4), page 2. The parent independently
noticed the missing conjugates in its reading-layer format spotcheck.

The indexed amplitudes and absorption of arbitrary common-profile weights
are not defined by the abstract row maps alone. Unit profile weights, or
their absorption into the amplitudes, must be stated before using the exact
four-product specialization. The [certification section](../../papers/tpc-228-source-native-polarized-collision-compiler/paper/sections/5_certification.tex#L11)
explicitly leaves the actual V59 coefficient-index crosswalk open.

The [single-coordinate fixture wording](../../papers/tpc-228-source-native-polarized-collision-compiler/paper/sections/4_q25_block.tex#L19)
also needs an orientation: one directed unit product gives 1/160000, while
both directed products on one shared coordinate give 1/80000. The numerical
artifact was not read to resolve that ambiguity. The README title omits the
initial “A” that appears in the TeX/PDF title; this minor variant is preserved.

### TPC229: literal cutoffs and matched-only energy

The [matching definition](../../papers/tpc-229-primitive-resonance-matching-spectrum/paper/sections/2_matching.tex#L3)
inherits both cutoffs and primitivity from TPC226. Equation plus gcd alone
is insufficient: Q=26,p=41,r=43 gives 7p+3r=416 and gcd(21,416)=1, but both
literal cutoffs are 6 and the required multiplier −7 is excluded. This
small arithmetic example illustrates a premise; it is not a census replay.

The [real-vector identity and ratio](../../papers/tpc-229-primitive-resonance-matching-spectrum/paper/sections/3_spectrum.tex#L3)
require their stated real-vector setting and a nonzero diagonal denominator.
Extending the cross-term identity to complex vectors requires a real part.
The [global criterion](../../papers/tpc-229-primitive-resonance-matching-spectrum/paper/sections/3_spectrum.tex#L25)
means a sum over matching blocks. If S,A are their symmetric/antisymmetric
energies and M_0 is unmatched diagonal mass, the corresponding full-object
inference is E_diag=S+A+M_0 and E_AP=2S+M_0. A full saving therefore needs
(1+δ)S+δM_0≤(1−δ)A, not just the printed matched-block inequality. The
manuscript conclusion retains the matched-mass limitation.

The block source bound has no explicit h^−2 factor. Reusing TPC228's raw
amplitudes needs an explicit normalization/weight convention. The abstract's
“first nontrivial shared-clock dilation” should be read with its intended
first primitive collision at L=4, not as the first cutoff enlargement.
