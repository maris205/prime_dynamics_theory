### TPC190–192: source wording and status do not supply physical data

and README call the kernels normalized, but its [theorem text](../../papers/tpc-190-parseval-to-atom-method-barrier/main.tex#L30)
displays unnormalized D_m and no explicit normalized family. The formula
is encoded as escaped linear text, also visible that way in PDF page 1;
it is preserved as source text rather than silently reconstructed as a new
display. The parent spot-checked both passages. Phase domain, measure
normalization, e(t) convention and integer range of m are not fully stated
locally. This audit does not fill those omissions or re-prove the witness.

All three papers declare the six physical target axes and data-only h0=2,

prefix order, X/N/q range, uniform constant or full loss calculation.

content, checker claims and payload/mutation assertions were not read or

### TPC193: domain discrepancy and two conditional analytic branches

The parent compared the [terminal and cumulative formulas](../../papers/tpc-193-literal-fixed-atom-candidate-mechanism-gate/main.tex#L91).
The q/N terminal block uses N<t(z)≤2N, while the q/T cumulative comparison
uses integral z and 0<t(z)≤T. The first display leaves integer indexing
implicit. N=T cannot identify these domains. The original explicitly calls
the direct-to-bad-endpoint implication unresolved, not false; this archive
review does not validate upstream attribution or create a third formula.

The [missing-field section](../../papers/tpc-193-literal-fixed-atom-candidate-mechanism-gate/main.tex#L141)
leaves production atom identifier/value/locator, packet schedule, common
ranges, positive exponent, uniform constant and complete losses absent.
The candidate skeleton is not an instantiated physical estimate. The
declared 7-primary/2-candidate/0-eligible corpus is finite source-reported
scope, not a new literature search or global nonexistence result.

The [first candidate record](../../papers/tpc-193-literal-fixed-atom-candidate-mechanism-gate/main.tex#L203)
uses fixed affine data, real bounded multiplicative functions, a
nonpretentious first function, fixed phase and reciprocal/logarithmic
normalization. Its substitution z=n+z0 retains gamma=−alpha_star and the
unit phase factor. Qualitative fixed-data o(1) does not imply natural,
uniform all-prefix fixed-X power. The
[second candidate record](../../papers/tpc-193-literal-fixed-atom-candidate-mechanism-gate/main.tex#L237)
keeps the exceptional set in [sqrt(X),X], logarithmic exceptional measure,
q/N normalization, sup-norm factor, qR≤(log X)^eta0 and good-scale
restriction. No production rational atom/denominator or all-scale theorem
is supplied. Neither cited external theorem was opened or verified here.

The [historical stop-before-TPC194 wording](../../papers/tpc-193-literal-fixed-atom-candidate-mechanism-gate/README.md#L45)
remains in the originals even though TPC194 is already in the locked
baseline. It is historical content, not current workflow authority.
