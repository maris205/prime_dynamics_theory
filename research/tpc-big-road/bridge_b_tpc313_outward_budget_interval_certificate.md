# Bridge B / TPC-313 — outward-rounded profile-budget certificate

## Route position

TPC-312 established a new finite source--shell Gram/sign atlas on
`I=(320,640]`.  TPC-313 takes the next minimal gate: it constructs the
literal 17-column source-profile image and certifies the native budget layer
with exact rational witnesses plus outward endpoint rounding.

For each of the eight `(Q,s)` rows, `k*` is the first profile prefix for which
the TPC-312 Gram-minimum sign target has normalized residual at most `1/2`.
The all-positive control is evaluated on that same prefix.  The exact ridge
system supplies a feasible primal upper bound and a weak-dual lower bound.
On the `10^-36` decimal grid, all eight weighted dual ratios are above
`5e-5`, and all eight positive-control primal ratios are below `1e-5`.

This is a finite source-first certificate.  The minimum label is selected from
the physical Gram under study; the panel is generated by the same locked
engine; and no external weighting law or independent physical holdout is
introduced.
