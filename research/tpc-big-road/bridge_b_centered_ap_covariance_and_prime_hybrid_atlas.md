## 2. Exact additive-zero-mode excision

For all sufficiently large \(x\),

\[
 d\le U<Q<q,
 \qquad
 0<|m|\le\frac{dq}{H}<d,
 \tag{2.1}
\]

because \(q/H\ll Q/H=x^{-31/96}\). Since \(q\) is invertible modulo
\(d\), a nonzero integer \(m\) with \(|m|<d\) cannot satisfy
\(m\overline q\equiv0\pmod d\). Hence

\[
 \boxed{A_d(0)=0.}
 \tag{2.2}
\]

This is not cancellation and uses no conjecture. It is a support identity in
the already frozen V46 emitter. Therefore (1.8) is exactly

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak R_x^{\rm AP}
 =-H\sum_{Y_0<d\le U}\sum_{\substack{r\bmod d\\r\ne0}}
 A_d(r)\widehat{\mathcal R}_d(r).}
 \tag{2.3}
\]

Define the residue average and centered residual by

\[
 \overline{\mathcal R}_d=\frac1d\sum_{a\bmod d}\mathcal R_d(a),
 \qquad
 \mathcal R_d^\circ(a)=\mathcal R_d(a)-\overline{\mathcal R}_d.
 \tag{2.4}
\]

For \(r\ne0\),

\[
 \widehat{\mathcal R}_d(r)=\widehat{\mathcal R_d^\circ}(r),
 \tag{2.5}
\]

and centered additive Parseval gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\substack{r\bmod d\\r\ne0}}
 |\widehat{\mathcal R}_d(r)|^2
 =d\sum_{a\bmod d}|\mathcal R_d^\circ(a)|^2.}
 \tag{2.6}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak R_x^{\rm AP}
 =-H\sum_d\sum_{r\ne0}
 A_d(r)\widehat{\mathcal R_d^\circ}(r).}
 \tag{2.7}
\]

Adding an arbitrary constant \(c_d\) to every residue class of
\(\mathcal R_d\) changes only its \(r=0\) transform and leaves (2.7)
unchanged. Thus V46's full all-residue energy paid a direction that the
physical pairing cannot see.

The scope of this deletion is deliberately narrow. It removes only the
constant additive residue direction. It does not delete the multiplicative
principal character on the unit group, any induced low-conductor character,
a possible exceptional real character, or any nonunit additive frequency.
Those modes remain mixed inside the nonzero additive spectrum.

## 3. Exact prime--hybrid local-error split

For primes \(p\), retain the V46 local factors

\[
 F_p(a)=\frac p{p-1}\mathbf1_{a\not\equiv-2\pmod p},
 \tag{3.1}
\]

\[
 G_{p,z}(a)=
 \begin{cases}
 F_p(a),&p\le z,\\
 p/(p-1),&p>z,\ a\equiv0\pmod p,\\
 p(p-2)/(p-1)^2,&p>z,\ a\not\equiv0\pmod p.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{3.2}
\]

Put

\[
 P_d(a)=\prod_{p\mid d}F_p(a),\qquad
 B_{d,z}(a)=\prod_{p\mid d}G_{p,z}(a),\qquad
 \Delta_{d,z}(a)=P_d(a)-B_{d,z}(a).
 \tag{3.3}
\]

Define the literal prime and hybrid local errors

\[
 \mathcal P_d(a)=
 \sum_{\substack{u\in I_x\\u\equiv a\pmod d}}
 \frac{\Lambda(u+2)-P_d(a)}{\log u},
 \tag{3.4}
\]

\[
 \mathcal H_d(a)=
 \sum_{\substack{u\in I_x\\u\equiv a\pmod d}}
 \frac{b_x^{(z)}(u)-B_{d,z}(a)}{\log u}.
 \tag{3.5}
\]

Since \(w=\Lambda(\cdot+2)-b_x^{(z)}\) and
\(\Delta=P-B\), the residue identity is exact:

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal R_d(a)=\mathcal P_d(a)-\mathcal H_d(a).}
 \tag{3.6}
\]

After centering,

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal R_d^\circ(a)=
 \mathcal P_d^\circ(a)-\mathcal H_d^\circ(a).}
 \tag{3.7}
\]

No lattice mesh or replacement of \(\log u\) occurs in (3.6)--(3.7).
All ordered \(+2/-1\), Möbius/log, MASTER/H2, unit/nonunit and hybrid data
remain in the same signed physical object.

Define

\[
 \mathcal E_{\mathcal P}^\circ
 =\sum_d d\sum_a|\mathcal P_d^\circ(a)|^2,
 \qquad
 \mathcal E_{\mathcal H}^\circ
 =\sum_d d\sum_a|\mathcal H_d^\circ(a)|^2,
 \tag{3.8}
\]

and

\[
 \mathcal C_{\mathcal P,\mathcal H}^\circ
 =\sum_d d\sum_a
 \mathcal P_d^\circ(a)\overline{\mathcal H_d^\circ(a)}.
 \tag{3.9}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal E_{\rm cov}^\circ
 :=\sum_d d\sum_a|\mathcal R_d^\circ(a)|^2
 =\mathcal E_{\mathcal P}^\circ+
 \mathcal E_{\mathcal H}^\circ-
 2\Re\mathcal C_{\mathcal P,\mathcal H}^\circ.}
 \tag{3.10}
\]

This identity is why the preferred new theorem is a signed covariance
theorem rather than two unrelated marginal estimates.

### 5.1 Lane A: signed centered covariance — preferred

\(\mathcal P_d^\circ-\mathcal H_d^\circ\). This is the smallest macro gate
identified here. It preserves cancellation between the prime error and the

A sufficient pair of hypotheses is

\[

nearly equal centered vectors may have a small difference. V47 records this

family realizes the extremal fixture.
